Privacy Policy: Spanish Language AP: Vozmere

EqualInformation, LLC

1. Introduction and scope

This policy explains how EqualInformation, LLC ("we") handles information in the app Spanish Language AP: Vozmere on iPhone, iPad and Android devices. Effective date: 26 November 2026.

2. Information we collect

A. Information you choose to give us. A diagnostic report you decide to send, with any note you add; feedback or suggestions you submit (category, message and, if you choose, your role, level, a source link and a rating); and, only if you tick the reply option, an email address.

B. Diagnostic and app-usage information, collected with your agreement or as permitted by applicable law: device and operating-system information (such as model, system version, processor, memory, display size, language, region, time zone and accessibility or display settings), app version, performance and memory indicators, crash and error information, a randomly generated app identifier, and how features are used. For learning features this includes the lessons, quizzes, tests, labs and productions you open, the items you answer, whether answers are correct, time taken, progress estimates for each learning objective, study sessions, the suggestions the app makes and how you respond to them, and purchase-screen interactions.

C. Information the app does not send to us. Your name, contacts, precise location, photographs, files, advertising identifiers, your essays, notes, production scripts or reference notes, and your voice recordings. Recordings are made only when you tap Record, are stored in the app's private storage on your device and are never uploaded by the app.

3. How we use information

To operate, maintain, secure and support the app; to diagnose problems; to provide and personalise the learning experience; to understand how the app is used; for research and development; to improve our apps and educational content; to read and act on feedback; and to comply with legal obligations.

4. Your choices

A notice is shown when you first open the app. Optional usage-information sharing is controlled by a switch there and at any time under More, Privacy and data. Where the law requires it, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the switch is off until you turn it on. A record that you accepted the notice, with the app version and region, is sent when you accept it. Diagnostics and feedback are sent only when you choose to send them. To request deletion, use "Delete my usage data" in the app or email diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

5. Storage and service providers

Information is stored in our Google Workspace services (Google Sheets and Drive) and notification email, processed by service providers acting on our behalf, and transmitted over encrypted connections. Purchases are processed by Apple or Google under their own terms; we do not receive your payment card details.

6. Retention

Usage and learning information is kept while it is useful for the purposes above. Deletion requests are carried out when we receive them. Information that stays only on your device remains until you delete it or remove the app.

7. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. International users and legal bases

Where applicable, we rely on your consent or on our legitimate interests in running and improving the app. Information may be processed in the United States.

9. Children

We design our apps to collect only the information described in this policy. If you believe information has been provided to us that you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

10. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our practices change. If the change is material, the in-app notice is shown again.

11. Contact

EqualInformation, LLC. support@equalinformation.com. Data and deletion requests: diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
