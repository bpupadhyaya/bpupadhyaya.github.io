App Support — PlainWeather

Get help with PlainWeather — a hyperlocal, offline-capable weather-safety app.

  • Home
  • /
  • App Support — PlainWeather

PlainWeather is a hyperlocal, offline-capable weather-safety app for iOS and Android. It gives severe-weather action guides for tornadoes, hurricanes, flash floods, wildfires, blizzards, heatwaves, lightning, and more, plus safety calculators (heat index, wind chill, a lightning flash-to-bang countdown), activity go/no-go checklists, and specialized modes for kids, elderly users, and people with chronic conditions — all usable with zero connectivity. It is 100% offline for every safety-critical feature, with no account required. PlainWeather is live now on Google Play, with the App Store version currently in review.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is PlainWeather available yet?

Yes on Android — download PlainWeather on Google Play now. The iOS version has been submitted and is currently in review with the App Store — check back here for the download link once it's approved.

Does PlainWeather work without internet?

Yes, for everything safety-critical. Every hazard action guide, safety calculator, and go/no-go checklist works fully offline. The one optional exception is forecast caching: when you're online, PlainWeather fetches and stores a forecast locally with a "last updated" timestamp, and keeps using that cached data once you lose signal.

Is my data private?

Yes. PlainWeather has no required account, no ads, and does not use analytics or tracking SDKs. Your family emergency plan, saved locations, and profile settings stay on your device. See our Privacy Policy for details, including the limited use of location for regional forecasts.

Is PlainWeather a replacement for official weather alerts?

No. PlainWeather provides general safety guidance and plain-language explanations of official terms, but it always defers to your local meteorological or emergency-management authority's alerts and evacuation orders. In a life-threatening situation, call your local emergency services.

What does PlainWeather cost?

The entire safety-critical layer — every hazard guide, calculator, and go/no-go checklist — is free for everyone, permanently, with no ads. An optional "PlainWeather Pro" unlock adds convenience-only depth, available either as a one-time purchase or a monthly subscription — whichever you prefer, both grant identical access.

What's included in PlainWeather Pro?

Pro covers convenience and depth features only, never anything safety-critical: a Historical Worst-Case Reference (how bad each hazard has actually gotten, with real examples), Pollen & Seasonal Allergy Notes, an Event & Wedding Weather Planner, and Gardening Frost Protection guidance. A lapsed or cancelled subscription never removes anything safety-critical.

What is the lightning countdown timer?

It's the built-in "30-30 rule" flash-to-bang timer: count the seconds between seeing lightning and hearing thunder, and PlainWeather tells you when conditions are unsafe and when it's safe to go back outside.

Does PlainWeather have modes for kids or vulnerable family members?

Yes. PlainWeather includes a simplified, visual Kids' weather-safety mode, an Elderly/heat-vulnerable mode with larger text and fewer taps, and guidance tuned for pregnancy, chronic-illness (asthma, COPD, cardiac), and pet safety. These are content-presentation modes, not medical tools, and are all free.

What languages does PlainWeather support?

PlainWeather follows your phone's language and falls back to English wherever a translation is still in progress, with more languages added progressively.

The app is crashing or not loading. What should I do?

Try these steps:
1. Force-close and reopen the app.
2. Restart your device.
3. Make sure you are running the latest version from the App Store or Google Play.
4. If the issue persists, contact us (see below) with your device model, OS version, and a description of the problem.

Availability

Android: live now on Google Play. iOS: submitted, currently in App Review — check back here once that completes.
