Intro to AI: Heurix - Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC.

Last updated: 2026-11-20.

What stays on your device

The app has no account and no server of ours. Your progress, credits and text-size setting are stored only on your device.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. With your consent (shown by a consent form where required) AdMob may use your device advertising identifier to show and measure ads. On iOS you can decline tracking when asked. Subscribers see no ads.

Source links

When you open a cited source, the app loads that public web page in a window inside the app. That site receives your request like any web visit. We do not keep cookies beyond that session.

Purchases

The optional monthly subscription is processed by Apple or Google; we receive no payment details.

Children

The app is a general-audience learning tool and is not directed to children under 13. On first launch it asks whether you are 18 or older. If you answer that you are under 18, ads are non-personalised, limited to teen-suitable content, tagged for the under-age-of-consent setting, and the iPhone tracking prompt is not shown.
