Support

Math Grade 3: Fennimo, from EqualInformation. Updated 27 November 2026.

Need help with Math Grade 3: Fennimo? Start here, or write to support@equalinformation.com and say which device and app version you have.

Restore a plan on a new device

Open the app, tap Grown-ups, answer the grown-up question, choose Plans, open the plan and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID or Google account that bought it.

Stop or change a plan

In the same Plans screen tap Manage subscription, or open your store's subscription settings (Apple ID, Subscriptions on iPhone and iPad; Payments and subscriptions in Google Play). Turning renewal off stops the next charge.

A plan does not open

Check the connection, then tap Restore purchases. A purchase waiting for a family approver opens when the store confirms it. Still locked after that? Email support with the day you bought the plan and the kind of device you are on.

Progress and privacy

Progress lives only on the device. "Erase all progress" is in the grown-up area under Settings. See the privacy page and the terms.

A question looks wrong

Tell us the stop name and the question text. We check every report.
