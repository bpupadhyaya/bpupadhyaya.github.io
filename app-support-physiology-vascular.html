Hemoflow support

Hemoflow is a blood-flow physics lab for physiology students and curious learners: Poiseuille flow, wall shear stress, stenosis and aneurysm models, pulse waves, Murray's law branching and the whole circulation, with every number traced to two sources inside the app (Sources tab).

Report an error

Found a wrong value, label or shape? Email support@equalinformation.com with the screen name and what you expected. Confirmed errors are corrected in the next update and listed in the release notes.

Ad-free subscription

Open More, then "Go ad-free". Manage or cancel in your Apple ID subscriptions. Restore purchases is in the same sheet. You can also watch a short video for seven hours without ads.

Text size

Use A- and A+ at the top of every screen. Hemoflow also follows your system text size.
