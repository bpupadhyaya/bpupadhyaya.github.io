NLP and LLMs: Tokora - Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-17. Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC.

What stays on your device

Your progress, answers, credits and settings are stored only on your device. There is no account and we operate no server that receives your learning data.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob at natural breaks. AdMob may collect an advertising identifier and device information to serve and measure ads. In regions that require it, a consent form appears at first launch; on iOS the system asks permission before tracking. You can decline and still use every lesson. The monthly ad-free plan or earned credits turn ads off.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple or Google; we receive no payment details.

Source links

When you open a cited source, the page loads inside the app from the publisher's website and is subject to that site's policies. The app keeps no cookies from those pages after the session.

Your age answer

Your age answer, asked on first launch and kept only on this device, decides how ads behave: under 13 sees no ads and no advertising code runs; 13 to 17 sees only non-personalised ads with a teen rating and is never asked about tracking; 18 and over sees ads according to consent choices. You can change it in the app.

Children

The app is educational and not directed at children under 13 for data collection; advertising settings follow platform rules.
