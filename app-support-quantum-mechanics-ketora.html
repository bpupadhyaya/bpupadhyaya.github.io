Quantum Mechanics: Ketora Support

Quantum Mechanics: Ketora is an undergraduate quantum mechanics course. Published by EqualInformation, LLC.

Report a content error

Found a wrong answer, a typo or an unclear explanation? Email bpupadhyaya@equalinformation.com with the topic and question text (a screenshot helps) and, if you can, the source you checked. We review every report and fix confirmed mistakes in an update.

Get help

Email bpupadhyaya@equalinformation.com with your device model, system version and a short description of the problem. We usually reply within a few days. Privacy questions and data requests go to the same address.

Manage or cancel the subscription

Subscriptions renew until you cancel. To cancel on iOS go to Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions; on Android open Google Play, then Payments and subscriptions. Refund requests go through Apple or Google, who process the payment.

Restore a purchase

On iPhone or iPad, open About in the app and choose Restore Purchases while signed in with the Apple Account that bought the subscription. On Android, use Restore in About with the same Google account.

Common questions

Are ads shown during exams? No. Ads appear only at natural breaks, never during timed tests or mock exams. Worked solutions are free and never hidden behind ads.

Does Ketora need an internet connection? No. Lessons, questions, worked solutions, mock exams and the wave packet, energy level and barrier tunnelling labs all run offline. A connection is only needed to show ads and to buy or restore the subscription.

Is this a replacement for my course? No. Ketora is a practice and study aid that goes alongside a course, textbook or instructor; it does not replace them.

How does the adaptive path work? On the Personalised path, Ketora tracks your mastery topic by topic, notices the pattern behind a wrong answer and chooses your next question and review from that, all on your device. Prefer a fixed order? Switch to the Traditional syllabus path at any time without losing progress.

What do the interactive labs do? They let you change parameters, predict what will happen and then see the result. Ketora includes the wave packet, energy level and barrier tunnelling labs.

Where do the further-reading links lead? To the original sources (for example MIT OpenCourseWare or OpenStax), which are cited and linked, not copied. Their licences remain with their owners.
