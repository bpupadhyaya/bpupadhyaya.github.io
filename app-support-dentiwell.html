Common questions

Does it work offline? Yes. Every tooth, chart and quiz works with no connection. Only ads and the subscription need the internet.

How do I make the text bigger? Use A+ at the top of any screen or on the More tab. Dentiwell also follows your iPhone or iPad text size setting.

How do I remove ads? More tab, then See ad-free options. Subscribe for $2.99 per month (price may vary by country), or watch short videos to earn ad-free time. Restore purchases is on the same screen.

How do I cancel? Settings, your name, Subscriptions, Dentiwell Ad-free, Cancel.

Is it medical advice? No. Dentiwell is for education only. Privacy policy.
