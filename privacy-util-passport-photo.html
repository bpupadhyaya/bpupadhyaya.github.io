Portlume Privacy Policy

Portlume: Passport Photo. Effective 13 November 2026. Publisher: Equal Information LLC.

Portlume turns a portrait into a correctly sized passport, visa or ID photo, or a print sheet, entirely on your phone.

What stays on your device

Your photos never leave your device. Portlume processes them locally: face detection, framing, background cleanup, checks and export all run on the phone (Apple's built-in vision tools on iPhone and iPad, and on-device models bundled in the app on Android). Photos are never uploaded to us or to anyone else, and there are no accounts. Exports are written to temporary app storage for sharing, or to your photo library only when you choose Save. A finished file leaves your device only when you tap Share, Email or Save (to Photos or Files) and choose where it goes, through your device's own share sheet, mail app or file picker, and we never receive it; nothing is sent to us. Custom sizes you create, settings and an export counter are stored on your device.

Permissions

If you choose to take a photo, the camera is used only to capture that picture, which stays on your device. On iPhone and iPad the app asks for add-only permission to save finished photos to your library when you choose Save. You can also pick an existing photo with the system picker. The app does not use the microphone, contacts or location.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob on non-critical screens. Your photos are never used for ads or sent to AdMob. An optional short video can be watched for one more export. On iPhone and iPad the system first asks whether the app may track you (App Tracking Transparency); in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some US states a Google consent message also appears (Google User Messaging Platform). On Android the Google consent message appears where the law requires it. You can decline and keep using every feature. With your permission, AdMob and its partners may use your device advertising identifier, approximate location derived from your IP address, and ad interaction and diagnostic data to choose, show and measure ads. See Google's advertising policies. You can change your choices later in the app under Settings (privacy choices for ads, where shown) and in your device settings. Ads are not served to people who have an ad-free purchase active.

Purchases

The optional ad-free monthly subscription and the one-time Unlock are handled by Apple or Google Play. We never see your payment details.

What is collected

Collected by us: nothing, and your photos are never collected. Collected by Google AdMob in the free version: device identifiers (advertising identifier), approximate location from IP address, and ad interaction and diagnostic data, used for third-party advertising and measuring ads.

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information, build profiles about you, run our own analytics, or ask you to create an account. No personal data is sent to us.

Children

Portlume is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. A parent or guardian may use the app to prepare a photo of a child; that photo is processed only on the device, exactly as any other, and is never sent to us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new effective date.
