App Support

App Support — Zoel AI

Your private AI — running entirely on your device.

Help and FAQs for Zoel AI (com.equalinformation.zoelai.app), the private, on-device AI for iOS and Android. For privacy details see the Privacy Policy.

Frequently asked questions

Does Zoel AI need an internet connection?

No. Zoel AI runs on your device. After you download a model (one time), chat, voice, and knowledge work fully offline. The internet is only used if you opt into internet enrichment or use cloud AI with your own API key.

How do I download an AI model?

Go to Settings → AI Engine & Models and download a model (for example a small Llama model, ~2 GB). Models are kept out of the install to keep the app small, so you download the one that fits your phone. The speech and semantic-search models work the same way.

Why does Zoel AI need microphone access?

Only for voice input. Speech recognition runs on your device; your audio is never uploaded. You can use Zoel AI entirely by typing if you prefer.

Is my health and personal data safe?

Yes. Connecting health, calendar, or files is opt-in, and that data is used only on your device to personalize answers. It is never sent to EqualInformation or any server.

What are the membership tiers?

  • Zoel Free — the Health module, plus AI conversation in every module.
  • Zoel Select ($4.99 one-time) — Health + any 4 knowledge modules you choose, forever.
  • Zoel Unlimited ($4.99/month or $49.99/year) — every module, current and future.

The AI conversation is available in every module on every tier; only a module’s knowledge library is gated.

How do I restore a purchase?

Reinstalled or switched devices? Open Settings → Membership → Restore Purchases. No account is needed — purchases are tied to your Apple ID or Google account.

Can I use cloud AI with my own key?

Yes — Bring Your Own Key. Add your own provider API key in Settings and your prompts go directly from your device to that provider, billed to your account. EqualInformation never sees your key or prompts.

What languages does Zoel AI support?

Zoel AI is multilingual for both voice and text, and replies in the language you write or speak.

Something isn’t working. What should I do?

Email us with your device model, OS version, and a short description (and a screenshot if you can) and we’ll help.
