Tortoise Derby Privacy Policy

Tortoise Derby, a game by EqualInformation, LLC

Last updated: 6 November 2026.

What the game stores

Tortoise Derby saves your race results, stars, clay coins, collected shells and settings on your device only. We run no account system and keep no server-side profile of you.

Advertising

The free game shows banner, full-screen and optional rewarded video ads served by Google AdMob. On first launch iOS asks whether the app may track you across other companies' apps; you can say no and still play everything. In regions that require it, Google's consent form then lets you choose how ad data is used, and you can reopen those choices in Settings. Ad partners may collect your device advertising identifier (if you allow it), coarse location derived from your network address, and ad interaction data. Please read Google's own privacy terms for details.

Subscription

An optional monthly subscription removes ads. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details. Cancel any time in Settings, then Apple ID, then Subscriptions.

Children

The game is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. Ads can be personalised only where the law and your choices allow it.

Your choices

Delete the app to remove all data stored on your device. Use the privacy choices entry in Settings to review ad consent. To ask a question or request help, see the support page.
