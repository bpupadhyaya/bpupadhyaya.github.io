Effective 28 November 2026. Published by EqualInformation.

Help for the iPhone and iPad app English Grade 4: Hesperquin.

Contact

Write to support@equalinformation.com. In the app, a grown-up can also use Parent, then Send feedback or Send diagnostics.

Common questions

How do I open the Parent area? Work out the multiplication written in words and type the answer in digits.

I bought a subscription on another device. Parent, then Restore purchases.

How do I cancel? Parent, then Manage subscriptions, or your device's account settings under Subscriptions.

Can I use British spelling? Parent, Settings, Spelling.

How do I make text larger? Use the A+ button at the top, up to 300%.

Is progress backed up? It is kept on the device only and is removed if the app is deleted.
