Support

Math Grade 10: Wrightarc · EqualInformation LLC · 27 November 2026

Getting help

Write to support@equalinformation.com. Tell us the device, what you were doing and, if you can, the question or commission title. A grown-up can write on a learner's behalf.

Diagnostics report

In the app open More, then the grown-up area, then Settings and privacy. You can read the whole report before choosing where to send it; diagnostics@equalinformation.com receives it. Nothing is sent automatically.

Subscriptions

Classical Plus and Real-World Plus are separate subscriptions that renew automatically every month until cancelled. Restore and Manage subscription are on the Plans page in the grown-up area. Cancel whenever you like in your app store account; Plus then lasts until the month you paid for is over. There is no free trial.

Something looks wrong in a question

The questions were checked by software and have not been reviewed by a teacher. Please send us the question text and what you think it should say.
