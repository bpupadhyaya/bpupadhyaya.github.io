App Support — Open Wallet

Get help with Open Wallet — the 100% open-source crypto wallet built on The Human Constitution.

  • Home
  • /
  • App Support — Open Wallet

Open Wallet is a 100% open-source crypto wallet built on The Human Constitution and Open Chain. It features 370 screens, 462 exchange paths, 13 chain modules, and support for 10 languages. Open Wallet uses multi-channel OTK (Open Token) to recognize and reward human contributions across six dimensions: Nurture, Education, Health, Community, Economic, and Governance. It is free and open source — no pre-mine, no fees.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data private?

Yes. Open Wallet is fully non-custodial. Your private keys, seed phrases, and passwords are stored only on your device. We never have access to your keys or funds. See our Privacy Policy for details.

What are OTK tokens?

OTK (Open Token) flows through 6 distinct value channels, each representing a dimension of human contribution: nOTK (Nurture — parenting, caregiving), eOTK (Education — teaching, mentoring), hOTK (Health — healthcare, wellness), cOTK (Community — volunteer work), xOTK (Economic — financial transactions), and gOTK (Governance — civic participation). There is zero pre-mine.

What is the Living Ledger?

The Living Ledger is a lifelong on-chain record of every value transfer — received, given, minted, and burned — broken down by channel. It tracks your contribution score, ring attribution (Self, Family, Mentors, Community, City, Country, Humanity), and gratitude transaction history.

What are Gratitude Transactions?

Gratitude Transactions let you thank parents, teachers, and mentors with on-chain gratitude messages. They are permanently recorded on Open Chain. There are four celebration tiers: Heartfelt, Great, Epic, and Legendary.

Is Open Wallet free?

Yes. Open Wallet is 100% free and open source. There are no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, and no fees charged by the app.

Does the app require internet?

Yes. Open Wallet requires an internet connection for blockchain operations including fetching balances, broadcasting transactions, and syncing with Open Chain. Your private keys remain on your device at all times.

The app is crashing or not loading. What should I do?

Try these steps:
1. Force-close and reopen the app.
2. Restart your device.
3. Make sure you are running the latest version.
4. Check your internet connection — blockchain operations require connectivity.
5. If the issue persists, contact us (see below) with your device model, OS version, and a description of the problem.

I lost my seed phrase. Can you recover my wallet?

No. Open Wallet is fully non-custodial, meaning we never have access to your seed phrase or private keys. If you lose your seed phrase, there is no way for anyone to recover your wallet. Always store your seed phrase in a safe, offline location.
