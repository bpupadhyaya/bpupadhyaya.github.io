How to play

Tap a vial to lift it, then tap another vial to pour the top liquid into it. Finish when every vial is empty or full of one liquid. The density rule: a liquid may be poured onto the same liquid, into an empty vial, or on top of a heavier liquid, never onto a lighter one. The strip above the vials lists the liquids from heaviest to lightest.

Hints and undo

Undo is unlimited. Hints are calculated by a solver that sees the whole puzzle. Every level and every daily puzzle was checked by it, so each one can be finished. If you are stuck, restart the level; nothing is lost.

Text size

Use the A- and A+ buttons at the top of any screen, or Settings, to make all text larger. The app also follows the Larger Text setting of your device.
