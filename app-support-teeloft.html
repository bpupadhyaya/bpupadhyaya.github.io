Teeloft: Support

Teeloft is a golf scorecard with an estimated handicap index, made by EqualInformation, LLC. Email support@equalinformation.com with your device, iOS version and what you saw.

Getting started

Open Courses and add the course you play: name, rating and slope from the scorecard, then par and stroke index for each hole. You can also import a CSV (columns: course,place,tee,tint,rating,slope,hole,par,stroke_index,yards, one row per hole) from the Courses menu. Then tap Start a round on Today.

Questions

Is the index official? No. It is an estimate using the World Handicap System calculation, shown with its working in Insights. For an official handicap ask your club or national golf association.

What can I say in the Say it box? Things like “birdie on 4, bogey on 5”, “pars on 1 through 3”, “6 on 9”, “two putts on 7”, “missed the fairway on 8” or “par the rest”. Teeloft shows what it understood and you press Apply. Tap the microphone on the iOS keyboard to dictate.

My estimate has not appeared. It needs three 18-hole rounds (or six nine-hole rounds), each with at least 14 holes recorded (7 for nine holes).

Why did a round not count? Open the round: the handicap card shows how it counted, or why not. You can also switch Count toward my estimated index off for casual rounds.

Where is my data? Only on your device. Use More, Export rounds (CSV) or Back up everything (JSON) before deleting the app or changing phones.

Ads. More, Go ad-free: a monthly subscription, or earn ad-free time with rewarded videos. Restore purchases is on the same screen. Cancel in your Apple Account subscriptions.
