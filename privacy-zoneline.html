Zoneline privacy policy

Effective 7 November 2026. Zoneline is published by EqualInformation, LLC.

What stays on your phone

The places, names and sleeping or working hours you enter, your chosen call type and calendar, and your earned ad-free time are stored only on your device. We do not run a server for Zoneline and we cannot see this information. Time zone rules come from your device, so the app works without a connection.

What we do not collect

Zoneline has no account, no sign-in and no analytics of its own. It does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or health data.

Ads

Zoneline is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, coarse location derived from your network address, and how you interact with ads, in order to show and measure ads. If you allow tracking when iOS asks, ads can be personalised; if you decline, ads are not personalised. Where the law requires it (for example the EU, the UK and some US states), Google's consent message lets you choose, and you can change that choice later under More, Ad privacy choices. See how Google uses data from ads.

Subscription and purchases

The optional monthly ad-free subscription is handled entirely by Apple. We receive no payment details. Manage or cancel it in your Apple Account settings.

Sharing a meeting card

When you share a card or text, it goes where you send it using the iOS share sheet. Zoneline does not upload it anywhere.

Children

Zoneline is not directed at children under 13 and is not a kids app.

Your choices and deleting data

Use More, Reset places and settings to erase what the app stores, or delete the app. You can turn off tracking for Zoneline in iOS Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking.

Contact

Questions: privacy@equalinformation.com. Changes to this policy will be posted on this page.
