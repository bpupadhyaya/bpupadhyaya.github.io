Anatomy Muscles: Myoloom Support

EqualInformation, LLC

Myoloom is an offline 3D muscle anatomy lab for the shoulder, arm, forearm and wrist: a live simulation of muscle pull, origin, insertion, action and nerve supply cards, drills, cases and a glossary. It works offline and is for education only.

Report an error

Found a wrong value, label or shape? Email support@equalinformation.com. Every claim in the app lists its sources under More, Accuracy and sources.

Ad-free, trial and restore

Open More (or the Ad-free button), then choose the monthly Myoloom Ad-free subscription ($2.99 per month, 7-day free trial for new subscribers, renews until cancelled in your Apple Account settings) or the one-time Remove Ads purchase. Use Restore purchases on a new device. You can also earn ad-free time by watching an optional video.

Text size

Use A- and A+ in the app. Myoloom also follows your system text size.
