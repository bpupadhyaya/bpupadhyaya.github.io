Turnwick: Encounter Tracker. Support

Turnwick keeps initiative order, hit points, conditions and dice for tabletop role-playing sessions on iPhone and iPad.

Quick answers

How do I undo a mistake? Tap Undo on the Table screen. Every change is recorded in the Journal and can be stepped back.

Which dice can I roll? Standard notation such as 2d6+3, 2d20kh1 (keep highest) or 4d6kh3, plus saved rolls.

Where is my data? Only on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

How do I remove ads? Open More, then choose the monthly ad-free pass, or watch rewarded videos to earn ad-free time. Use Restore Purchases on the same screen on a new device.

How do I cancel the subscription? iOS Settings, your name, Subscriptions, Turnwick, Cancel. Cancel at least a day before renewal.

Contact

Email support@equalinformation.com with your device model and iOS version. We reply within a few business days.
