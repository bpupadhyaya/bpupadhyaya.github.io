Quillcase help

Daily case-file puzzles by Equal Information.

How a case works

Every case seats a row of people. Read the numbered evidence slips, then tap a cell on the board and choose what belongs there. Stamp what you are sure of; cross out what cannot be. Each case has exactly one answer that can be reached by reasoning alone. If you are stuck, the hint button first points to a slip, then names what it settles; a short video (or an ad-free plan) reveals the stamp.

Daily streak and archive

Close any of the three daily case files to keep your streak seal. Today's files are the same for everyone on the same date. The archive offers more cases at your own pace.

Ads, videos and the Ad-free plan

Quillcase shows banner ads and occasional full-screen ads. Open the About sheet (the circle with dots on the desk screen) to earn ad-free time by watching videos, to subscribe to the monthly Ad-free plan, or to restore a previous purchase.

Troubleshooting

  • A purchase does not show: open About and tap Restore purchases while signed in to the Apple account that bought it.
  • An ad will not load: check your connection and try again in a moment.
  • Cancel the subscription: Settings, your name, Subscriptions, Quillcase.
