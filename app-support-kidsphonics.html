Phonics for Kids: Soundwick Support

How does it work?

Tap Play. Soundwick introduces a few new letter sounds or words, then practises them with short activities: listen and pick the letter, pick the picture that starts with a sound, build a word, find the written word, rhyme, read a sentence. Things your child misses come back later in a different way; things they know come back less often.

Credits, ad-free plan and the grown-ups area

Tap the lock icon at the top of the home screen and answer the grown-up question. Level 1 and the daily Quick review are always free. One credit opens 24 hours of play. You start with 2 credits; watching a short ad in the grown-ups area earns 1 more. If no ad can be shown you receive 1 free credit, at most once every 24 hours. The ad-free monthly plan (7-day free trial) removes ads and credits entirely, and can be restored from the Ad-free plan screen.

Moving to a new device

Grown-ups area > Settings > Export progress, then Import progress on the new device.

Cancelling the subscription

iPhone and iPad: Settings > your name > Subscriptions. Android: Google Play > Payments and subscriptions > Subscriptions.

Sounds

Voices and letter sounds are computer-generated. If a sound seems unclear, tell us which one.
