Clockridge Support

Clockridge: Timesheet Overtime is a clock in/out tracker with breaks, pay periods, overtime rules and earnings estimates. It works fully offline.

Common questions

Where is my data? Only on your device. Export a CSV from the Pay period tab to back it up; deleting the app deletes the data.

An overtime figure looks wrong. Open Settings, then Accuracy and sources, and compare with the rule set you selected. Rules differ by state and employer, and your pay records prevail. Use Report an error on that page to tell us.

Restore or manage the Ad-free subscription. Open the Ad-free sheet in the app and tap Restore, or use Apple ID subscription settings.
