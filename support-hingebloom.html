How to play

Each bed holds one or more chains of petals joined by hinges. Tap a petal, or use its numbered chip in the dock, to bend that hinge; a chain cannot pass through stones or through itself. Cover every gilt pad to bloom the bed. Fewer bends earn more stars. The lightbulb shows the next useful bend and is always free.

Ads and the ad-free subscription

The game is free with ads. Open the No ads button on the home screen to subscribe monthly, or to restore a purchase made earlier on the same Apple ID. Manage or cancel in iOS Settings, your name, Subscriptions. You can also earn bonus coins by watching an optional rewarded video.

Consent and tracking

Change your ad consent in the in-app Settings, and your tracking permission in iOS Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking. See the privacy policy.

Progress

Progress is stored on the device only. It is not synced between devices.
