IELTS Practice Test: Bandholm support

Common questions

Speech recognition does not work. Allow Microphone and Speech Recognition in Settings. If no offline pack is installed, add English in Settings > General > Keyboard > Dictation, or choose online recognition for one session. You can always self-check instead.

How do credits work? You start with 2. Watching a rewarded video to the end gives 1. One credit opens one test or pack once. Practice review, the mistake notebook and every full solution are always free.

Cancel the subscription. Cancel any time in your App Store or Google Play account settings. If you cancel, you keep ad-free until the end of the period you already paid for, and you will not be charged again.

Are scores real? No. Band numbers are rough practice estimates, never predicted scores. Listening audio is computer-generated.
