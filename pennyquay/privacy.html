Privacy Policy

Pennyquay: GCSE Economics · PrivacyTermsSupport

Effective 26 November 2026.

EqualInformation, LLC publishes Pennyquay, a revision app for GCSE Economics on iPhone, iPad and Android. This policy explains what information leaves your device when you use it, why, and what you can control. There is no account to create.

What stays on your device

Your answers, topic strength estimates, study plan, pinned items, exam date, mission briefs and judgement exercises are kept in the app on your device. What you type into answer boxes and the topic search box stays there too: it is not part of usage information.

The acceptance record

When you accept the notice shown on first opening, the app sends a short record: a random identifier created by the app, an acceptance reference and time, the notice text and its version, your sharing choice, the app version, your platform, and your device's region setting, locale and time zone. If sharing is on, the record also holds device model, operating system version, languages, processor and memory details, screen and window size, text size, and how long the notice was open. We receive a copy by email.

Optional usage information

If the sharing switch is on, we receive diagnostic and app-usage information that is permissible under applicable law: sessions and their length; screens and features used; lessons opened; for each question answered, which question and topic, the kind of practice, which option was chosen (or that a figure was typed, without the figure), whether it was right, how long it took and the attempt number; topic strength estimates; the suggestions the planner makes and whether you take them; switches between the Syllabus and Real-World views; missions, labs and paper sets used; use of the subscription screen; and script errors and crash reports. It carries the random identifier, app and content versions, details of how the app was installed, and device information. It does not carry your name.

The switch starts off where your device region is in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, or is unknown. Elsewhere it starts on, and you see it before accepting. You can change it at any time under More, then Privacy and data.

Information you choose to send

Diagnostics. If you press Send on a diagnostic report, we receive what the preview shows: app and device details, region and time zone, memory and storage figures, settings, a recent log of screens and answers by reference code, crash signatures and any note you add.

Feedback. If you send feedback, rate an explanation or report a question, we receive your category, message, optional role, the question or topic concerned, and your email address only if you tick the box asking for a reply.

Deletion requests sent from the app carry the random identifier. Please leave personal details out of notes and messages.

Information we do not ask for

The app does not request your name, contacts, precise location, photographs, files, microphone, camera or an advertising identifier. It does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Purchases

Pennyquay Plus is sold by Apple or Google. They process payment under their own policies. The app learns whether a subscription is active; we do not receive your payment card details. Usage information may record whether the app was on the free or Plus level.

Why we use information

To operate, secure and support the app; to find and fix faults; to provide and personalise learning; to understand how the app and its explanations work for learners; for research and development and to improve our apps and teaching material; to read and answer feedback; and to meet legal obligations. Information may also be transferred as part of a financing, merger, acquisition or similar business transaction, subject to this policy.

Where it goes and how long it is kept

Information is sent over encrypted connections to a service we run on Google Workspace (Apps Script, Sheets, Drive and email), and is handled by service providers acting for us, including Google. It may be processed in the United States. Usage and learning information is kept while it remains useful for the purposes above. Diagnostics, feedback and acceptance records are kept as long as needed for support, our records and legal obligations. We act on valid deletion requests.

Legal bases and your rights

Where data protection law requires a legal basis, optional usage information relies on your consent, and operating the service, keeping the acceptance record and answering your requests rely on our legitimate interests or legal duties. Depending on where you live, you may ask to access, correct, delete or restrict information, object to its use, withdraw consent, or complain to your data protection authority. Because there is no account, include your install identifier (shown on the Privacy and data screen).

Security

We use reasonable safeguards. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Children

We design the app to collect only what this policy describes. If you believe information has reached us that you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

Changes

We will update this page when practice changes. A material change to the first-open notice means the app shows it again.
