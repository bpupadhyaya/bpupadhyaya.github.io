Pennyquay is a GCSE Economics study app for iPhone, iPad and Android, made by EqualInformation, LLC. Here we set out what the app sends off your device, our reasons, and the choices you have. You can study without signing up for anything.

Kept on the device you study on

The app stores your quiz history, its estimate of how well you know each topic, your plan and pins, your exam date, finished mission briefs and judgement exercises on your own device. Words and figures that you key into answer boxes or the topic search are held there as well and are left out of usage reports.

When you accept the opening notice

Pressing the agree button sends us a small receipt. It contains a random code the app made for this install, a reference and timestamp for your acceptance, the wording and version of the notice, whether reporting was left on, which release and kind of device system is running, and the region, locale and time-zone settings. With reporting on, the receipt adds the hardware model, system release, language list, processor and memory figures, screen and window dimensions, the text size you chose and the seconds the notice stayed on screen. A copy of the receipt reaches our mailbox.

Study and usage reports (your switch)

While the reporting switch is on, we collect technical and usage information where the law allows it: how long study sessions last; which pages and tools you open; lessons viewed; for each answer, the question and topic codes, the type of practice, the option you chose (a keyed-in figure is noted only as having been keyed in), whether it was correct, the time taken and how many tries you have had; the app's estimate of each topic; what the study planner proposed and what you did about it; moves between the Syllabus and Real-World views; activity in missions, labs and paper sets; visits to the Plus screen; and faults and crashes. Each report carries the install code, release numbers for the app and its question bank, facts about how the app was installed and about the device. Your name is not in it.

If your device is set to a country in the European Economic Area, to the United Kingdom or to Switzerland, or if the country cannot be read, the switch begins in the off position. For other regions it begins on, and the opening notice shows it to you before you agree. To move it later, go to More and then Privacy and data.

Things you decide to send us

A fault report. The app first shows you the whole report. It lists app and device details, region and time zone, memory and storage readings, your settings, a short recent history of screens and answered questions by code, crash markers and anything you write in the note box. It leaves only when you press Send.

Comments. A comment, a thumbs rating or a problem report gives us the kind of comment, your words, a role if you pick one, and the question or topic it refers to. We get an email address only when you tick the box asking us to reply.

An erasure request made inside the app includes the install code. Whatever you write to us, please keep personal details out of free-text boxes.

What the app never asks your device for

It does not ask for your name, address book, exact position, pictures, documents, microphone, camera or any advertising identifier, and it does not follow your activity in other companies' apps or websites.

Paying for Plus

Apple or Google takes payment for Pennyquay Plus and applies its own privacy terms. Our app is told whether a Plus plan is running. Card numbers do not come to us. A usage report can say whether the app was being used with or without Plus.

What we do with it

We use these records to keep the app and its service working and secure; to track down errors; to fit lessons and practice to the learner; to learn which features and explanations help; to research and develop better apps and course material; to answer you; and to meet duties the law places on us. If our business or this app is financed, merged or sold, these records may pass with it and remain under the promises on this page.

Storage, processors and keeping periods

Records travel by encrypted connection to a collection service we operate with Google Workspace tools (Apps Script, Sheets, Drive and email). Google and any other supplier handle them on our instructions, possibly in the United States. Study and usage records stay for as long as they still serve the purposes listed above. Acceptance receipts, fault reports and comments stay as long as support, record keeping or the law require. A valid erasure request is carried out.

Your legal position

Where the law asks us to name a legal ground, the optional reports rest on your consent. Running the service, holding acceptance receipts and replying to you rest on our legitimate interests or our legal duties. Local law may give you rights to see, correct, erase or limit data about you, to object, to take back consent, or to complain to a data protection regulator. Without accounts, we find your records by the install code printed on the Privacy and data page of the app.

Keeping it safe

We limit who can open these records and send them only over encrypted links. Even so, we cannot promise that any system will never be breached.

Children

Pennyquay asks for nothing beyond what is set out on this page. A learner, parent, guardian or school who wants a record erased can write to the data address below, and we erase it once we can confirm the request relates to that install.

When this page changes

If our handling of data changes, this page changes with it. Should the opening notice change in a way that matters, the app puts it in front of you again.
