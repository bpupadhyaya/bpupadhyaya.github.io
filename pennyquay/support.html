Common questions

What is free? Twenty of the 39 topics, two quayside missions, the Market maker and Trader's till labs, one paper set, two judgement exercises, the coverage screen and the curriculum map.

How do I restore Plus? Open More, then Settings and Plus, and press Restore purchases while signed in to the store account that bought it.

How do I cancel? Press Manage subscription, or use your Apple or Google account subscription settings. Cancelling takes effect from the next billing period.

The text is too small or too large. Use A- and A+ at the top of any screen, from 50% to 300%.

I think a question is wrong. Open its full explanation and press Report this question.
