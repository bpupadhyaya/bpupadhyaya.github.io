Stuck? Email support@equalinformation.com and tell us which device you use, its system version and what went wrong. Inside the app, More and then Send diagnostics lets you read a fault report before deciding to send it.

Questions learners ask

How much can I use without paying? Free study covers 20 of the 39 topics, the first two quayside missions, two labs (Market maker and Trader's till), one timed set, two judgement exercises, plus the coverage screen and the course map.

My Plus plan has not appeared on this device. Check that the store account is the one that paid, then open More, Settings and Plus, and tap Restore purchases.

How do I stop paying? Tap Manage subscription in the app, or open subscriptions in your Apple or Google account, and switch renewal off. You keep Plus until your paid month runs out.

Can I change the text size? Yes. The A- and A+ buttons at the top shrink or enlarge text between half and three times the normal size.

One of the questions looks wrong. Open that question's full explanation and use Report this question.

I want reporting off, or my records erased. Both controls are in the app under More, Privacy and data.
