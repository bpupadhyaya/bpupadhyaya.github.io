Help us improve the health of every human being — at near zero cost.

NutriSize is a health intelligence app that provides science-backed nutrition and exercise guidance referencing WHO, ACSM, and NASEM standards. It is built and maintained by a single developer who believes that access to quality health information should not depend on income or background.

Every feature is designed with privacy at its core — your health data stays on your device. NutriSize is available on iOS, Android, and Web, and all essential features are free. Your support helps us continue improving the app, adding new features, and reaching more people around the world.
