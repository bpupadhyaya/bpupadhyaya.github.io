Corpolume: Human Anatomy 3D

A living 3D anatomy atlas: a beating heart with valves and coronary arteries, breathing lungs with a bronchial tree, the eye and the ear at work, and one continuous zoom from the whole body down to a single molecule. Works offline.

Help

  • Look around: drag to orbit, pinch to zoom. In Body to Molecule, pinch or use the scale slider to zoom through the levels.
  • Peel layers: use the Layers tab; the chest wall and lung opacity sliders are under Controls. The Section tab cuts the model open.
  • Name a structure: tap it. Labels shows names on every visible structure.
  • Replay motion: play, pause, change speed and scrub the timeline under the 3D view.
  • Bigger text: A- and A+ at the top of every screen; the app also follows your system text size.
  • Colour-blind palette: Legend, then Colour-blind safe, or About.
  • Remove ads: the sparkle button on the home screen: monthly subscription, or earn ad-free time with rewarded videos. Restore Purchases is on the same sheet. Cancel in Settings, your name, Subscriptions.

Report an error

Accuracy matters. Open About, then Report an error, or email bpupadhyaya@equalinformation.com with the region, the structure and a source for the correction. Fixes ship in the next update.
