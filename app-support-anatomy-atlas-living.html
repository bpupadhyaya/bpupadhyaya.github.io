Anatomy Atlas: Corpolume Support

EqualInformation, LLC

Corpolume is an offline living 3D anatomy atlas: a beating heart, a breathing chest and the eye, with cut-away views, labels and quizzes. It works offline and is for education only.

Report an error

Found a wrong value, label or shape? Email support@equalinformation.com. Every claim in the app lists its sources under the Accuracy and sources page.

Ad-free, trial and restore

Open More (or the Ad-free button), then choose the monthly Corpolume Ad-free subscription ($2.99 per month, 7-day free trial for new subscribers, renews until cancelled in your Apple Account settings) or the one-time Remove Ads purchase. Use Restore purchases on a new device. You can also earn ad-free time by watching an optional video.

Text size

Use A- and A+ in the app. Corpolume also follows your system text size.
