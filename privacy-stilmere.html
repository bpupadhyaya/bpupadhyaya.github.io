Latin AP: Stilmere Privacy Policy

Effective 26 November 2026. This policy explains how EqualInformation, LLC handles information in the app Latin AP: Stilmere.

What stays on your device

Your translations, essays, build notes, pins, study plan, word-card boxes and settings are stored on your device. The app does not collect the text you write in those places. No account is needed, and the course works without a connection.

Information you choose to send

Diagnostics: when you use Send diagnostics, the app shows a preview first. The report contains device and app details, a technical log, performance and error information and any note you add. Feedback and suggestions: the category and message you submit and any optional role, level, rating or source link; an email address only if you ask for a reply. One-tap ratings and content reports on a lesson, explanation or studio are also sent when you tap them.

Usage and learning information

If the usage switch is on, the app records how its features are used: screens, lessons and readings opened; questions attempted, the option chosen, whether it was correct and the time taken; study sessions; studio and build activity; estimates of your progress; the adaptive suggestions the app makes and what happens next; purchase-screen interactions; device and operating-system details, app version, language, region and time zone, display and accessibility settings; and performance and crash information. These records carry a randomly generated app identifier rather than your name or contact details.

When you accept the first-run notice, the app records that acceptance (time, notice version, app and platform version, the random identifier, language, region, time zone and your switch setting) even if the usage switch is off.

We do not collect your name, contacts, precise location, photographs, files or the device identifier that phone makers provide for marketing.

How we use information

To run, secure and support the app; to find and fix faults; to adapt and improve learning features and content; to understand how the app is used; for research and development and analytics; to read and act on feedback; and to meet legal obligations. Information may be transferred as part of a merger, acquisition or similar transaction, subject to the commitments in this policy.

Your choices

The usage switch is off by default in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and where the region is not known, and you can change it at any time under More, Settings, Privacy and data. Diagnostics and feedback are sent only when you act. To request deletion, use Delete my usage data in the app or write to diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple or Google under their terms. We do not receive your payment card details.

Storage, providers and retention

Information sent to us travels over encrypted connections and is kept in our Google Workspace services (including Google Sheets and Drive) and notification email, handled by providers acting for us. It may be processed in the United States. We keep usage and learning information while it is useful for the purposes above, and we act on deletion requests when we receive them. Where the law requires a legal basis, we rely on your consent or on our legitimate interest in operating and improving the app. We take reasonable measures to protect information; no method is completely secure.

Children

We design our apps to collect only the information described in this policy. If you believe information has been provided to us that you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

Changes and contact

We will post changes here, and where a change is material the in-app notice is shown again. EqualInformation, LLC: support@equalinformation.com. Data requests: diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
