Privacy Policy: GCSE English Lit: Quillara

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Contact: support@equalinformation.com. Last updated 2026-11-25.

1. Introduction and scope

This Privacy Policy describes how EqualInformation, LLC ("we", "us") handles information in connection with GCSE English Lit: Quillara (the "app"), version 1.0.0 and later. It applies to the app only. The app has no account system and no sign-in.

2A. Information you choose to provide

Diagnostic reports: under Settings, Privacy and data, you may choose Send diagnostics. The app shows you the content first and sends it only after you tap Send. Feedback and suggestions: if you choose to send feedback, a suggestion or a problem report, we receive its category, your message (up to 2,000 characters, with email addresses, telephone numbers and links removed automatically) and, if you provide them, a role, context, a source link and a rating. We receive an email address only if you choose to let us contact you about your feedback.

2B. Diagnostic and app-usage information

While the optional usage setting is on, or where the law permits, the app can send: app version and build; device model; operating system and version; processor and memory characteristics; display size, language, region and time zone; accessibility and display settings such as text size; performance, memory and crash information; a random app identifier; and how the app is used. For this learning app that includes the lessons and labs opened, questions attempted, whether answers were correct, time taken, progress estimates, the study suggestions made by the app and what you did next, and activity in labs. Answers typed into question fields, if any, are shortened and automatically cleaned of recognisable personal identifiers.

2C. Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, contacts, precise location, photographs or files. Text you write in the Essay Planner stays on your device and is never sent. The app uses no advertising identifier.

3. How we use information

We use information to operate, maintain, secure and support the app; to diagnose problems; to provide and personalise the learning experience; to understand how the app is used; for research and development and analytics to improve our apps and educational content; to read and act on feedback; and to comply with legal obligations.

4. Your choices

When you first open the app you see a notice and an optional switch for sharing anonymous usage and device information. The switch is off by default in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and where the region cannot be determined, until you turn it on. You can change it at any time under Settings, Privacy and data, where you can also delete your usage data. Diagnostics and feedback are sent only when you choose. To request deletion you can use the in-app delete request or write to diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

5. Storage and service providers

Information is stored in our Google Workspace services (Google Sheets and Drive) and notification email, and is processed by service providers acting on our behalf. It is transmitted over encrypted connections. Purchases are handled by Apple or Google; we do not receive your payment details.

6. Retention

Usage and learning information is kept for as long as it is useful for the purposes above. Deletion requests are handled on request.

7. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. International users and legal bases

Where applicable law requires a legal basis we rely on your consent or on our legitimate interests in operating and improving the app. Information may be processed in the United States.

9. Children

We design our apps to collect only the information described in this policy. If you believe information has been provided to us that you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

10. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, the notice shown when the app first opens will change version and will be shown again.

11. Contact

EqualInformation, LLC, support@equalinformation.com. Deletion and data requests: diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
