Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC.

Last updated: 2026-11-20.

What stays on your device

Arrayo has no accounts and no server of its own. The code you type, your exercise drafts, quiz results, credits and your text-size setting are stored only on your device. Programs you write are run by an interpreter inside the app and are never sent anywhere.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, only between activities. Where required, a Google consent form (and on iPhone the tracking prompt) asks for your choice first. If you agree, advertising partners may use your device advertising identifier to personalise ads; if you decline you can still use every lesson. People who subscribe see no ads.

Cited sources

When you open a source in the in-app window, that web page is loaded from its publisher like any web page; the window keeps no cookies after it closes.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple or Google. We receive no payment details.

Children

Arrayo is a general-audience learning tool and is not directed to children under 13.
