Privacy

Math Grade 3: Fennimo, from EqualInformation. Updated 27 November 2026.

The short version. The app keeps its practice progress on the device and sends nothing about you to us or to anyone else. It has no accounts, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on the device

Answers, points, the learner's plan, text size and settings are saved in the app's own storage on the phone or tablet. We cannot see them. Deleting the app, or using "Erase all progress" in the grown-up area, removes them. Your device's own backup may copy app data, under your device settings.

What we do not collect

We do not collect names, email addresses, ages, locations, photos, contacts, identifiers, usage statistics or crash reports. The app does not use the microphone or camera, and it does not send notifications. Questions are read aloud with the voice already built into the device; nothing is recorded.

Buying a plan

Two optional monthly plans are bought through Apple's App Store or Google Play, always behind a grown-up check. The store handles the payment, the receipt and your subscription. We never see your card, your name or your store account. The app only asks the store on your device whether a plan is active.

The one outside connection

A grown-up can open the source pages we cite (for example a curriculum or a health organisation) inside the app. That page is run by its publisher and follows its own privacy rules. Nothing else in the app connects to the internet.

Young learners

A grown-up nearby is a good idea. Because the app collects nothing, there is no information about a young learner for us to keep, share or delete. Anything that could cost money or leave the app is behind a question meant for grown-ups.

Changes and contact

If this ever changes, the app update and this page will say so before the change starts. Questions: privacy@equalinformation.com.
