Privacy

Math Grade 3: Fennimo, from EqualInformation. Updated 27 November 2026.

The short version. The app keeps its practice progress on the device and sends nothing by itself. It has no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. We receive something only if a grown-up chooses to email us.

What stays on the device

Answers, points, the learner's plan, text size and settings are saved in the app's own storage on the phone or tablet. We cannot see them. Deleting the app, or using "Erase all progress" in the grown-up area, removes them. Your device's own backup may copy app data, under your device settings.

What we do not collect

The app does not collect names, ages, locations, photos, contacts, identifiers, usage statistics or crash reports. The app does not use the microphone or camera, and it does not send notifications. Questions are read aloud with the voice already built into the device; nothing is recorded.

If a grown-up emails us

In the grown-up area, "Send diagnostics" shows a short problem report (app version, device model and system version, screen and text size, a few settings and the last app store message) and then opens a draft email to diagnostics@equalinformation.com. "Send feedback" opens a draft to support@equalinformation.com. Nothing is sent until you send it from your own mail app or share options, and you can change or delete any of it first.

If you send it, we receive your email address, what you wrote and anything you left in the draft. We use it only to answer you and to fix or improve the app. We do not sell it, share it for advertising or use it to track anyone. Please leave out a child's name and other details about a child. To have your message deleted, write to privacy@equalinformation.com.

Buying a plan

Two optional monthly plans are bought through Apple's App Store or Google Play, always behind a grown-up check. The store handles the payment, the receipt and your subscription. We never see your card, your name or your store account. The app only asks the store on your device whether a plan is active.

The one outside connection

A grown-up can open the source pages we cite (for example a curriculum or a health organisation) inside the app. That page is run by its publisher and follows its own privacy rules. Apart from that and purchases through the app store, the app does not connect to the internet.

Young learners

A grown-up nearby is a good idea. The app itself gathers no information about a young learner. Anything that could cost money, send an email or leave the app is behind a question meant for grown-ups. If you believe a message holds details about a child, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes and contact

If this ever changes, the app update and this page will say so before the change starts. Questions: privacy@equalinformation.com.
