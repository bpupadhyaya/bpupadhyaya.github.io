Signaro Privacy Policy

Signaro: Driving Test Prep. Effective 13 November 2026. Publisher: Equal Information LLC.

Signaro helps learner drivers prepare for the written driving knowledge test with original practice questions, road-sign flashcards and 3D right-of-way scenes. It is an independent study aid and is not affiliated with any government agency.

What stays on your device

Your practice history, mistakes, readiness estimate, pack choice, text size and settings are stored only on your device. There are no accounts and no servers of ours; we do not collect your name, email, location or contacts. The app works offline and does not use the camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob on non-critical screens. Ads never appear during questions or mock tests, or in your first session. An optional short video can be watched for extra practice. On iPhone and iPad the system first asks whether the app may track you (App Tracking Transparency); in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some US states a Google consent message also appears (Google User Messaging Platform). On Android the Google consent message appears where the law requires it. You can decline and keep using every feature. With your permission, AdMob and its partners may use your device advertising identifier, approximate location derived from your IP address, and ad interaction and diagnostic data to choose, show and measure ads. See Google's advertising policies. You can change your choices later in the app under Settings (privacy choices for ads, where shown) and in your device settings. Ads are not served to people who have an ad-free purchase active.

Purchases

The optional monthly subscription and the one-time Unlock are handled by Apple or Google Play. We never see your payment details.

What is collected

Collected by us: nothing. Collected by Google AdMob in the free version: device identifiers (advertising identifier where available), approximate location from IP address, and ad interaction and diagnostic data, used for third-party advertising and measuring ads.

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information, build profiles about you, run our own analytics, or ask you to create an account. No personal data is sent to us.

Children

Signaro is for learner drivers and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and we do not collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new effective date.
