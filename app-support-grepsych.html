GRE Psychology Test: Psychnook Support

Help for the GRE Psychology Subject Test practice app by EqualInformation, LLC.

Contact

Write to support@equalinformation.com. Please include your device model, system version and the app version shown in Settings.

Restore a subscription

Open Settings, then Ad-free plan, then Restore purchases. Subscriptions are managed in your Apple ID or Google Play account settings, where you can also cancel.

Move your progress

Settings has Export progress and Import progress. The file holds your learning data only, not credits or purchases.

Report a content error

Open the Full solution sheet and the References for the question, then email the question number shown in the sheet and what you believe is wrong to support@equalinformation.com. Content is written by the publisher and has not yet been reviewed by an outside psychology expert; the app says so.
