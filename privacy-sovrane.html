Privacy Policy: Comparative Gov AP: Sovrane

This page explains what information the app handles and the choices you have.

1. Who we are and what this covers

This policy is issued by EqualInformation, LLC and applies to the Comparative Gov AP: Sovrane app for iPhone, iPad and Android devices. It takes effect on 27 November 2026.

2. Information we collect

Acceptance record. When you continue past the first-run notice, the app sends a short record that the notice was accepted: a record identifier, the time, the app, platform, version and build, the notice version and a fingerprint of its text, how you accepted it, whether usage sharing was on, a random app identifier, and your country or region setting, language region and time zone. If usage sharing is on, the record also includes the device facts described below.

Usage and diagnostic information, with the sharing switch on. Device and operating system information (such as model, operating system version, processor, memory, screen size, language, region, time zone, text size and display settings), app version, performance and memory indicators, crash and error information, a random app identifier, and how the app and its learning features are used: screens, lessons, comparison tools, labs and builds opened and the settings you move in them; questions attempted, whether answers were correct and how long they took; topic-level progress estimates; study sessions; choices made in your study plan; the next-step decisions the app makes and what followed; and views of and actions on the subscription screen.

What you choose to send. A diagnostic report, shown to you before you send it, with any note you add; feedback, ratings and suggestions you submit, with any optional details such as your role; and an email address only if you choose to give one for a reply.

What stays on your device. Your free-response drafts, your build notes and documents, your study progress and your plan are stored on your device. The text of drafts and notes is not sent by the app. We do not collect your name, contacts, precise location, photographs, files or advertising identifiers.

3. How we use information

To operate, support and secure the app; to find and fix faults; to shape and improve how the app adapts to learners; to understand which features help; for research and development of our apps and educational content; to read and act on feedback; to handle deletion requests; and to meet legal obligations.

4. Your choices

The usage sharing switch is shown on the first-run notice and under More, Privacy and data. It is off by default in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and where your region cannot be determined, until you turn it on. You may change it at any time. With it off, usage information and automatic crash reports are not sent; the acceptance record described above, and anything you choose to send, still are. Under the same settings you can ask us to delete your usage data, or write to diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

5. Subscriptions

Purchases are processed by Apple or Google under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not receive your payment card details.

6. Links to sources

Source pages open inside the app or in your browser. When you open one, that website receives your request and applies its own privacy practices.

7. Storage, service providers and security

Information is sent over encrypted connections and stored in our Google Workspace services (Google Sheets and Drive) and notification email, using service providers that act on our behalf. If a copy was installed from an app store, that email may repeat the record: the acceptance record, usage and learning batches, crash information, diagnostic reports, feedback and deletion requests, addressed to our mailboxes and open to forwarding to another that is ours. We take reasonable measures to protect it; no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

8. Retention

Usage and learning information, acceptance records, diagnostic reports and feedback are kept while they remain useful for the purposes above or while the law requires. Deletion requests are handled when we receive them. The work is done by hand, as nothing in our system deletes automatically, and any emailed copy is retained unless it is deleted by hand too.

9. International users and legal bases

Information may be processed in the United States. Where a legal basis is required, we rely on your consent for optional usage sharing and for what you choose to send, and on our legitimate interests or legal obligations for the acceptance record. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete information; contact us to exercise them.

10. Children

We design our apps to collect only the information described in this policy. If you believe information has been provided to us that you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

11. Changes

If we make a material change, we will update this page, and where appropriate the app will show an updated notice.

12. Contact

Questions: support@equalinformation.com. Data and deletion requests: diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
