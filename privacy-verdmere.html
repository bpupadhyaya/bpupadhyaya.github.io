Environmental Sci AP: Verdmere: Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Contact: support@equalinformation.com. Last updated 2026-11-25.

1. Introduction and scope

This policy describes how EqualInformation, LLC (we, us) handles information in connection with Environmental Sci AP: Verdmere, version 1.0 and later (the app). It applies to the app only. The app needs no account. Lessons, questions, labs and your progress are stored on your own device, and the free part of the app works without a network connection.

2. Information we collect

A. Information you choose to provide. Diagnostic reports: under Settings, Privacy and data, Send diagnostics displays the complete report before anything is transmitted, and it is transmitted only when you tap Send. Feedback and suggestions: a category, your message (limited to 2,000 characters, with email addresses, telephone numbers and links removed automatically) and, if you provide them, a role (AP Environmental Science learner, teacher or tutor, parent or guardian, other), a context, a source link and a rating. We receive an email address only if you tick the option to be contacted, and we use it only to reply.

B. Diagnostic and app-usage information, collected with your agreement or as permitted by applicable law while the optional usage setting is on: app version and build; device model; operating system and version; processor and memory details; screen size; language, region and time zone; text-size, accessibility and display settings; network type; whether free access or Verdmere Plus is active; a randomly generated app identifier; session timing; crash and error information; performance indicators; the screens, topic notes, quizzes, mock exams, labs and missions you use; the questions you attempt, whether your answers are correct, the time you take and whether you open an explanation; estimates of your progress by topic; the choices the adaptive engine makes and their outcomes; plan and text-size changes; and interactions with the subscription screen. Numeric answers typed into lab goals and text typed into free-response boxes may be included in shortened form, with recognisable email addresses, telephone numbers, links and card or identity numbers removed.

When you accept the first-run notice, a short acceptance record is always sent: a consent identifier, the time, the app, platform, version and build, the notice version and a hash of its text, the state of the usage setting, the random app identifier, and your country, locale and time zone.

C. We do not collect your name, contacts, precise location, photographs, files, identifiers used to track you across other companies' apps and websites, or other text you write in the app, such as personal notes.

3. How we use information

To operate, maintain, secure and support the app; to diagnose problems; to provide and personalise the learning experience; to understand how the app is used; for research and development; for analytics and to improve our apps and educational content; to read and act on feedback; and to meet legal obligations.

4. Your choices

When you first open the app, a notice explains these practices and offers a separate usage switch. The switch starts off in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and wherever your region cannot be determined, until you turn it on; elsewhere it starts on. You can change it at any time under Settings, Privacy and data, where you can also read the notice again. Diagnostics and feedback are sent only when you tap Send. To request deletion of your usage data, choose Delete my usage data in the app or email diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

5. Storage and service providers

Information travels over encrypted (HTTPS) connections to a web application operated by EqualInformation, LLC, which records it in our Google Workspace services (Google Sheets and Drive); diagnostic reports and acceptance notices are also delivered by email. Google acts for us as a service provider. Apple or Google processes payments for Verdmere Plus, and we do not receive your payment details. Reference links open public web pages in a sheet inside the app; those sites follow their own privacy practices.

6. Retention

Usage, learning and diagnostic information is kept for as long as it remains useful for the purposes described above. Deletion requests are handled on request.

7. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures, including encryption in transit. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. International users and legal bases

Where a legal basis is required, we rely on your agreement (the usage switch, and your decision to send diagnostics or feedback) and on our legitimate interest in operating and improving the app. Information may be processed in the United States.

9. Children

We design our apps to collect only the information described in this policy. If you believe information has been provided to us that you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the version of the first-run notice changes and the notice is shown again.

11. Contact

EqualInformation, LLC. Questions: support@equalinformation.com. Deletion and data requests: diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
