GRE Psychology Test: Psychnook Privacy Policy

GRE Psychology Subject Test practice. Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Effective 15 November 2026.

What the app stores

Your answers, levels, review schedule, mistake notebook, mock results, streak days, credits and settings are saved only on your device. There is no account, no sign-up and no server of ours that receives this data. You can export your progress to a file you control, import it again, or reset it in Settings. Deleting the app deletes the data.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on some screens, an occasional full-screen ad after a finished practice block, and optional rewarded videos that earn credits. There are no ads in timed mocks or labs. AdMob may use your device advertising identifier, approximate location and device information to choose and measure ads. On iOS the app asks permission before tracking across other companies' apps and websites, and where the law requires it, shows a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform. You can refuse and keep using the app. The first practice session has no ads. See policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

References and web pages

The References control lists the sources behind each topic and can open the cited page inside the app, limited to the cited website. This happens only when you tap a reference and you are online; that website then receives an ordinary web request from your device. The citation text itself is stored in the app and works offline.

Purchases

The optional ad-free plan is an auto-renewing monthly subscription handled by Apple or Google. We receive no payment details. Manage or cancel it in your store account settings.

Independence

Psychnook is independent: it is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by ETS. GRE is a registered trademark of ETS, used here only to describe the kind of test this practice suits. DSM-5-TR is a publication of the American Psychiatric Association; Psychnook uses diagnostic terminology only and does not reproduce the manual. All questions and explanations are original and written for this app, with sources listed under References. Score ranges are rough practice estimates from your own answers, not predictions of any GRE score. Psychnook is an education tool and gives no medical, psychological or clinical advice.

Children

The app is an education tool for graduate-school applicants and is not directed to children under 13.
