Fixwick Support

Getting started

On first launch answer the questions about your home. Fixwick then builds a calendar of only the jobs that apply. Tap any part of the house drawing to see its jobs, tap a job for steps and sources, then tap Done today.

Common questions

A job does not apply to me. Open it and choose "I do not have this, hide it", or change what your home has under Home profile.

How do I make text bigger? Use A+ at the top of any screen. Fixwick also follows the system text size.

How do I remove ads? Open the menu, then Go ad-free: watch 100 short videos in a month, or subscribe for the monthly price shown there. Manage or cancel in Settings, your Apple Account, Subscriptions. Use Restore purchases on a new device.

Is my data backed up? It is stored on your device and is included in your normal iOS backup.

I found an error in a maintenance interval. Use Report an error under Accuracy and sources, or write to us below. Your appliance manual always takes priority over general guidance.
