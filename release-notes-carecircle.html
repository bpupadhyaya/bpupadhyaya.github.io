Release Notes

What's new in CareCircle — version history and milestones.

CareCircle is under active development. This page documents each production release with what changed, what's new, and what to expect.

These release notes are maintained by AI agents. While we strive for accuracy, limitations may apply.

v1.0.0-android Stable Current May 2026

CareCircle v1.0.0 approved and live on Google Play. First Android release — full feature parity with iOS.

What's included

  • All 20 modules available: 7 free core + 13 premium
  • 17 data models with full local persistence (Room)
  • Companion Mode for care receivers
  • 10 reference guides
  • Google Play Billing v7 in-app purchase integration
  • Dark theme home page with gradient module cards
  • 100% offline — all data stored locally, no accounts, no tracking
Android
v1.0.0-ios Stable April 2026

CareCircle Caregiving v1.0.0 approved and live on the App Store. First iOS release — caregiver coordination app for families caring for elderly parents, disabled loved ones, or patients. 35 screens with full feature parity.

What's included

  • 7 free core modules: Tasks, Medications, Calendar, Notes, Emergency Contacts, Vitals, Companion Mode
  • 13 premium modules: Appointments, Care Schedule, Expenses, Meal Planner, Documents, Wellness, Symptoms, Daily Summary, Care Team, Activity Log, Wellbeing, Comm Log, Care Guides
  • 17 data models with full local persistence (SwiftData)
  • Companion Mode — simplified interface for care receivers with large text and one-tap check-ins
  • 10 reference guides covering safety, legal, medication, emergency, mobility, sundowning, hygiene, nutrition, pain management, and community resources
  • Premium content with one-time in-app purchase ($5.99)
  • StoreKit 2 in-app purchase integration
  • Dark theme home page with gradient module cards
  • 100% offline — all data stored locally, no accounts, no tracking
iOS
v1.0.0 Stable April 2026

Initial release of CareCircle — caregiver coordination app for families caring for elderly parents, disabled loved ones, or patients. 35 screens across iOS and Android with full feature parity.

What's included

  • 7 free core modules: Tasks, Medications, Calendar, Notes, Emergency Contacts, Vitals, Companion Mode
  • 13 premium modules: Appointments, Care Schedule, Expenses, Meal Planner, Documents, Wellness, Symptoms, Daily Summary, Care Team, Activity Log, Wellbeing, Comm Log, Care Guides
  • 17 data models with full local persistence (SwiftData / Room)
  • Companion Mode — simplified interface for care receivers with large text and one-tap check-ins
  • 10 reference guides covering safety, legal, medication, emergency, mobility, sundowning, hygiene, nutrition, pain management, and community resources
  • Premium content with one-time in-app purchase ($5.99)
  • StoreKit 2 (iOS) and Google Play Billing v7 (Android) integration
  • Dark theme home page with gradient module cards
  • 100% offline — all data stored locally, no accounts, no tracking
  • Full cross-platform feature parity between iOS and Android
