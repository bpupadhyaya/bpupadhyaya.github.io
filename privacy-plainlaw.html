Privacy Policy — PlainLaw

Effective Date: April 26, 2026

  • Home
  • /
  • Privacy Policy
  • /
  • PlainLaw

1 Who We Are

PlainLaw is provided by EqualInformation, LLC.

If you have privacy questions or requests, you may contact us at:
Email: privacy@equalinformation.com
Contact page: equalinformation.weebly.com/contact.html | bpupadhyaya.github.io#contact

2 Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the PlainLaw app (iOS bundle identifier: com.equalinformation.plainlaw.app; Android package name: com.plainlaw.app). PlainLaw is available on iOS (App Store) and Android (Google Play).

3 Information PlainLaw May Access or Store

PlainLaw is a legal education and reference app serving both the general public and legal professionals. Depending on how you use the app, PlainLaw may store information on your device, including:

  • In-app purchase status (Premium and Pro subscription state)
  • User role selection (general public, attorney, judge, law student, etc.)
  • Selected jurisdiction and language preferences
  • Pinned jurisdiction setting
  • App settings and preferences
  • User-configured data source URLs (RSS feeds, API endpoints)
  • Locally cached content from public legal APIs
  • Bookmarks and reading history
  • Evidence organizer notes and photos (stored locally only)
  • Developer mode state (debug builds only, stripped from production)

PlainLaw does not collect or store personal identification data, legal case details, or confidential client information.

4 How We Use Information

PlainLaw uses information to:

  • Provide legal education and reference content
  • Display appropriate content based on purchase status (Free, Premium, or Pro)
  • Auto-detect and configure jurisdiction-specific legal content based on device locale
  • Display content in the user's preferred language
  • Cache legal data from public APIs for offline access
  • Maintain bookmarks, reading history, and app preferences locally

5 Local-First Design

PlainLaw is designed with a local-first architecture. The vast majority of content is bundled within the app and works completely offline. PlainLaw does not require an account, login, or registration. There is no cloud sync of personal data, and no user profiles are created on remote servers.

6 External Data Sources

PlainLaw Pro users may optionally connect to external legal data sources to fetch publicly available legal information (e.g., congressional records, federal regulations, court opinions from government APIs). When external sources are configured:

  • Data is fetched directly from the source to your device — PlainLaw servers are not involved
  • Fetched data is cached locally on your device for offline access
  • User-provided API credentials (if any) are stored only in the device's secure keychain/keystore and are never transmitted to EqualInformation servers
  • Sync occurs only on WiFi by default (configurable by the user)

7 No Analytics or Tracking

PlainLaw does not use any analytics SDKs, crash reporters, or tracking tools. No data is sent to third-party analytics services. No advertising SDKs are included.

8 App Store and Google Play Billing

PlainLaw may use Apple App Store In-App Purchase (on iOS) or Google Play Billing (on Android) for Premium (one-time purchase) and Pro (annual subscription) functionality. Apple or Google may process payment and transaction information under their own respective policies. PlainLaw may receive limited purchase-status or product information needed to unlock purchased content on your device. EqualInformation, LLC does not receive your full payment card number through PlainLaw.

9 Legal Content Disclaimer

All legal content in PlainLaw is for educational purposes only and does not constitute legal advice. PlainLaw is not a law firm, does not provide legal services, and no attorney-client relationship is created by using the app. Content covers multiple jurisdictions worldwide but may not reflect the most current state of the law. Users should consult a licensed attorney for specific legal problems.

10 Jurisdiction Detection

PlainLaw may use your device's locale setting or, with your permission, approximate location to auto-detect your jurisdiction and display relevant legal content. This information is processed entirely on your device and is not transmitted to any server. You can manually select or pin any jurisdiction at any time.

11 Permissions

PlainLaw may request the following device permissions:

  • Internet: Used for in-app purchase verification and optional data source sync
  • Location (optional): Used only for jurisdiction auto-detection if user grants permission
  • Camera/Photos (optional): Used only within the Evidence Organizer feature to attach photos to cases, stored locally

PlainLaw does not require contacts, microphone, or other sensitive permissions.

12 Sharing and Sale of Data

EqualInformation, LLC does not sell personal or sensitive user data. We do not share users' data with data brokers or for advertising purposes. The company does not intentionally transmit personal or sensitive PlainLaw user data to its own servers or unrelated third parties.

13 Data Retention and Deletion

Purchase-related state may be restorable through App Store or Google Play for eligible purchases. Uninstalling PlainLaw removes all locally stored data from your device, including bookmarks, cached content, evidence organizer data, and preferences.

14 Security

PlainLaw relies on iOS and Android platform protections and app sandboxing as part of its local-first design. User-provided credentials for external data sources are stored in the platform's secure keychain (iOS) or keystore (Android). However, no method of storage or electronic transmission is completely secure, and the company cannot guarantee absolute security.

15 Your Choices

You may choose to use the free tier, purchase Premium, or subscribe to Pro. You may configure or disable external data source connections at any time. You may change your jurisdiction and language preferences at any time. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data from your device.

16 Children's Privacy

PlainLaw is not intended for or directed to children under 13. PlainLaw does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information inappropriately, please contact the company.

17 International Users

PlainLaw serves users worldwide with jurisdiction-specific legal content. Regardless of your location, your data remains on your device. No personal data is transferred across borders by PlainLaw. The privacy protections described in this policy apply equally to all users regardless of jurisdiction.

18 Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the updated version on this page and revise the Effective Date above.
