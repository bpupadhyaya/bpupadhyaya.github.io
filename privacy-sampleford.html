Sampleford: AP Statistics - Privacy Policy

Statistics exam practice for the revised five-unit AP(R) Statistics course, with practice questions, free-response practice, mock tests and labs. Publisher: Equal Information LLC. Effective 15 November 2026.

What the app stores

Your answers, levels, review schedule, mistake notebook, mock results, free-response scores, streak days, credits and settings are saved only on your device. There is no account, no sign-up and no server of ours that receives this data. You can export your progress to a file you control, import it again, or reset it. Deleting the app deletes the data.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on some screens, an occasional full-screen ad after a finished practice block, and optional rewarded videos that earn credits. There are no ads in timed mocks, labs or free-response timers. AdMob may use your device advertising identifier, approximate location and device information to choose and measure ads. On iOS the app asks permission before tracking across other companies' apps and websites, and where the law requires it, shows a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform. You can refuse and keep using the app. The first session has no ads. Many learners are under 18, so ads are limited to a general-audience rating and the app is not directed to children under 13. See policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Purchases

The optional ad-free plan is an auto-renewing monthly subscription handled by Apple or Google. We receive no payment details. Cancel any time in your App Store or Google Play account settings. If you cancel, you keep ad-free until the end of the period you already paid for, and you will not be charged again.

Sources and independence

Sampleford is independent. All questions and explanations in the app are original. Score ranges are rough practice estimates from your own answers, not predictions of any exam score.

Web pages in the app

When you open a source reference, the page loads from the publisher's website (OpenStax or OpenIntro) inside the app, or in your browser for College Board pages, and is governed by that site's own policy.
