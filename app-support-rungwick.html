Rungwick support

Rungwick is a daily word-ladder game: change one letter at a time, and every step has to be a real word.

How a ladder works

Each day brings one ladder, the same for everyone, with a start word and a target word. Tap a tile, pick a new letter, and if the result is a word in the list it becomes a rung. You may build down from the top word, up from the bottom word, or both; the ladder is finished when the two ends are one letter apart. Par is the length of the shortest route through everyday words. Finishing under par means you found a shorter bridge.

Bridge hints

Each ladder starts with three bridge hints. The first tap marks the tile to change; a second tap shows the letter. When they run out you can watch a short video for more, or carry on without.

A word is missing, or one looks wrong

The word lists are built from an open dictionary and filtered, so a few fair words may be absent and an odd one may slip in. Write to support@equalinformation.com with the word and the ladder number (shown at the top of the puzzle) and we will review it.

Ads, ad-free time and the monthly plan

Rungwick is free and supported by ads. Under More you can watch a video to turn ads off for 30 minutes at a time, or subscribe to the monthly ad-free plan. To cancel, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. To restore a plan on a new device, use Restore purchase in the same sheet. Questions about charges are handled by Apple at support.apple.com/billing.

My streak or progress is gone

Progress is stored only on the device and is removed when the app is deleted. Rungwick has no account, so it cannot be restored from our side.
