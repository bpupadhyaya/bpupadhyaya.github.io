Support

Questions, content corrections and subscription help: support@equalinformation.com. Please include your device model, the app version and the theme, topic or question you are asking about.

Send diagnostics

Open Settings, Privacy and data and choose Send diagnostics. The app shows exactly what the report contains; nothing is sent until you tap Send. The report is delivered to diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

Feedback and suggestions

Use Feedback and suggestions under Settings, the thumbs and Report a problem controls under answers and lessons, or Suggest content. Nothing is sent until you tap Send. An email address is included only if you tick that we may reply.

Usage information switch and deletion

The optional usage-information switch is under Settings, Privacy and data and can be changed at any time. To remove usage data, choose Delete my usage data there, or email diagnostics@equalinformation.com with your request.

Subscription

Manage or cancel Cartovane Plus in your App Store or Google Play account settings. Use Restore purchases on the Cartovane Plus screen if a purchase does not appear.

Found an error?

Send the theme, topic and question and, if you can, a reliable source that disagrees. Each cited source opens inside the app.
