App Support — Anything Assistant

Get help with Anything Assistant — your all-in-one personal super app.

  • Home
  • /
  • App Support — Anything Assistant

Anything Assistant is an all-in-one personal super app that bundles 9 independent tools into a single download: MediSight (pill identifier), PlainHealth, CareCircle, Kindly, PlainDental, PlainLaw, PlainAuto, RepairMate, and ChronoTrack. All data stays on your device — no accounts, no cloud sync, no tracking.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data private?

Yes. Anything Assistant stores all data locally on your device. There are no accounts, no cloud sync, and no analytics. We never collect, transmit, or share any personal information. See our Privacy Policy for details.

What apps are included?

Anything Assistant includes 9 built-in tools: MediSight (pill identifier & drug reference), PlainHealth (health tracker & medical reference), CareCircle (caregiver coordination), Kindly (mental wellness), PlainDental (dental health guide), PlainLaw (legal reference), PlainAuto (auto repair guide), RepairMate (home repair guide), and ChronoTrack (medication & appointment tracker). All apps are available in a single download.

What features are free?

Core features across all 9 apps are free and always will be. Premium unlocks advanced features for power users and professionals — see the in-app Premium screen for full details per app.

What does Personal Premium include?

Personal Premium ($14.99, one-time purchase) unlocks premium features across all 9 apps — enhanced tools, offline content, and power-user capabilities. This is a one-time payment with no recurring charges and includes future app updates.

What does Professional Premium include?

Professional Premium ($29.99/month or $199.99/year, auto-renewing subscription) includes everything in Personal Premium plus clinical assessments, professional legal references, institutional-grade data management, and advanced medication interaction analysis. Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation. Subscriptions automatically renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the renewal date. Manage or cancel in your App Store account settings.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Open Anything Assistant → More tab → Premium → Restore Purchases. Your purchase is linked to your Apple ID and restores automatically.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → Anything Assistant → Cancel. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period. You keep access until then.

Does the app require an internet connection?

No. Anything Assistant works 100% offline. All content and databases are bundled with the app. No internet connection or account is ever required.

How does the MediSight camera pill scanner work?

MediSight uses on-device OCR (Apple Vision framework on iOS) to read pill imprint codes from your camera in real time. No images are transmitted to any server. Camera access is optional — you can always search manually by name, imprint, color, or shape. The scanner matches detected imprint text against the bundled FDA/NLM drug database.

Where does the medical information come from?

Medical content in MediSight and PlainHealth references authoritative public health databases: FDA openFDA (National Drug Code Directory & DailyMed), NIH/NLM DailyMed, RxNorm, MedlinePlus, NIH PubChem, WHO Essential Medicines List, AGS Beers Criteria, and USP standards. This information is for educational purposes only — always consult a qualified healthcare professional before making medical decisions.

Is Anything Assistant a replacement for medical, legal, or professional advice?

No. All content in Anything Assistant is for informational and educational purposes only. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, legal advice, or any other professional advice. Always consult a qualified professional before making any decisions. In a medical emergency, call 911. Poison Control: 1-800-222-1222.

The app is crashing or not loading. What should I do?

1. Force-close and reopen the app.
2. Restart your device.
3. Make sure you are running the latest version from the App Store.
4. If the issue persists, contact us below with your device model, iOS version, and a description of the problem.
