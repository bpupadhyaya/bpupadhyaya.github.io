ROADWREN

Privacy policy

Updated 7 November 2026. Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC.

No account, no server of ours

Roadwren does not ask you to sign in and we run no server that receives your answers. The state you choose, your test date, every answer you give, your streak and your settings are kept in the app's private storage on your iPhone or iPad. Deleting the app deletes them.

Read-aloud

If you switch on read-aloud, questions are spoken by the speech engine built into iOS. The text is not sent to us.

Not a government service

Roadwren is an independent study aid. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by any state motor vehicle agency or other government body. Always confirm rules in your official state driver handbook.

Advertising

The free app shows ads served by Google AdMob. On iOS the app first asks for App Tracking Transparency permission, then, where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK, or under certain US state laws), shows Google's consent or opt-out form, and only after that loads ads. If you allow tracking and consent, Google and its advertising partners may process your advertising identifier and information about how you interact with ads to show and measure ads. If you decline, ads are not personalised. You can change the tracking permission in iOS Settings, and reopen the consent choices from Settings inside the app where your region requires it. See how Google uses data from ads.

Ad-free options

An optional monthly subscription removes ads. It is bought and managed through your Apple Account, and we receive no payment card details. You can also earn ad-free time by watching a short rewarded video. Counters for these limits stay on your device.

Children

Roadwren is meant for people old enough to hold a learner's permit in their state. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

Turn off tracking in iOS Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking. Cancel a subscription in Settings, Apple Account, Subscriptions. For anything else, write to us using the address on the support page.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes and the new text is published here.
