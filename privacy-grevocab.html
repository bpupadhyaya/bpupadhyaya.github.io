GRE Vocab Cards: Wordgrove - Privacy Policy

GRE vocabulary flashcards and word drills. Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Effective 15 November 2026.

What stays on your phone

Your progress, answers, notes, credits and settings are stored only on your device. There is no account and no server that receives your practice data. You can export or delete your progress in Settings.

Audio

Word pronunciations are pre-recorded computer-generated clips bundled in the app. The app does not use your microphone.

Ads and purchases

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob. Where required, you are asked for consent first (Google User Messaging Platform and, on iPhone, App Tracking Transparency). Ad providers may collect device identifiers and advertising data under their own policies. Your practice answers, writing and voice are never used for ads. The optional monthly ad-free plan is processed by Apple or Google; we do not receive your payment details.
