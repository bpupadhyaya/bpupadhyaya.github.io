Statistics Course: Sigmora Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-16. Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. App: Statistics Course: Sigmora.

Short version

Using Sigmora requires no account. We run no server that stores your study data, and your progress, settings and wallet remain on your device.

What is not collected

We do not collect or ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone, health information or any personal identifiers, and we do not sell personal data.

Local storage

The app keeps its working data (your scores, the topics you have mastered, your review queue, theme and text-size settings, and earned ad-free time) on the device so that an introductory college statistics course remains usable offline. We have no access to that data.

Third-party advertising

Sigmora is supported by Google AdMob. The App Tracking Transparency prompt on iOS and Google's consent message (where required) come first, and only then are ads loaded. Where you have allowed it, the advertising identifier and data about how you interact with ads go to Google for advertising and measurement. Timed tests are always ad-free. Learn more at Google's page on ad data.

Children under 13

Sigmora is not aimed at children under 13. We knowingly collect nothing from them.

Optional subscription

An optional auto-renewing monthly subscription removes ads. The price is shown by the App Store or Google Play before you buy, a free trial is included where offered, and the subscription renews until you cancel it. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google; we never see your card details and the app only reads whether your subscription is active. Manage or cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account settings. See the terms of use.

Your choices and data requests

Change tracking permission any time in iOS Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking, and reopen the ad consent message from the app's About screen where available. To ask a privacy question or to request access to, or deletion of, any data we might hold about you, write to bpupadhyaya@equalinformation.com; the support page has more help.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it.
