Statistics Course: Sigmora - Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC.

Last updated: 2026-11-24.

Statistics Course: Sigmora is an adaptive introductory statistics course. It is an independent product of EqualInformation, LLC.

What stays on your device

The app has no account and no backend of ours. Your answers, mastery, review queue, notes and settings are stored only on your device. We collect no personal data and receive none of your study data. Deleting the app deletes it.

Tracking and analytics

The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It does not read or use your advertising identifier and shows no tracking prompt.

Sources

When you open a cited source, the page loads in your browser or in the app, and that site receives your request like any web visit.

Purchases

The optional monthly subscription, Sigmora Plus, is processed by Apple or Google; we receive no payment details.

Audience

The app is for learners aged 13 and over.
