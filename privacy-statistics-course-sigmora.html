Statistics Course: Sigmora - Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC.

Last updated: 2026-11-27.

1. Introduction and scope

This Privacy Policy describes how EqualInformation, LLC ("we", "us") handles information in connection with the app Statistics Course: Sigmora (the "app"). It applies to the app on iOS and Android.

2. Information we collect

A. Information you choose to provide. Diagnostic reports that you send, together with any note you add; and feedback and suggestions that you submit (category, message, and optionally your role, course or year, a source link and a rating). If you tick the contact option, the email address you enter is used to reply.

B. Diagnostic and app-usage information collected with your agreement or as permitted by applicable law: device and operating-system information (model, operating-system version, processor, memory, display size, language, region and time zone, accessibility and display settings), the app version, performance and memory indicators, crash and error information, and a randomly generated identifier of the installation. We also collect information about how the learning features are used and perform: lessons and topics viewed, questions attempted, whether answers are correct, time taken, hints and solutions opened, topic-level progress estimates, practice sessions and mock examinations, adaptive-learning decisions and their outcomes, and activity in laboratories and simulations. Answers you type into question fields, if any, are shortened and automatically cleaned of recognisable personal identifiers.

C. Information we do not collect: your name, contacts, precise location, photographs, files, advertising identifiers, or other text you enter in the app.

3. How we use information

To operate, maintain, secure and support the app; to diagnose problems; to provide and personalise the learning experience; to understand how the app is used; for research and development; for analytics and improvement of our apps and educational content; to read and act on feedback; and to comply with legal obligations.

4. Your choices

When you first open the app a notice is shown with a separate switch for usage information. The switch is off by default in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland until you turn it on, and you can change it at any time under More, Privacy and data. Diagnostics and feedback are sent only when you choose to send them. You may request deletion with the in-app "Delete my usage data" request or by email to diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

5. Storage and service providers

Information is stored in our Google Workspace services (Google Sheets and Drive) and notification email, and is processed by service providers acting on our behalf. Statistics work and app activity from a store-installed Sigmora may reach us by email in batches; the acceptance record, crash records, diagnostic reports, feedback and deletion requests may too. Each goes to mailboxes of ours, which can forward to another of ours. It is transmitted over encrypted connections. Purchases are processed by Apple or Google; we receive no payment details.

6. Retention

Usage and learning information is kept for as long as it is useful for the purposes described above; deletion requests are processed manually. The in-app request does not itself delete data, and email copies are unaffected unless we remove them by hand.

7. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information; no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. International users and legal bases

Where applicable, we rely on your consent or on our legitimate interests. Information may be processed in the United States.

9. Information you ask us to remove

We design our apps to collect only the information described in this policy. If you believe information has been provided to us that you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

10. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, the notice shown in the app receives a new version and is displayed again.

11. Contact

EqualInformation, LLC, support@equalinformation.com. Deletion and data requests: diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
