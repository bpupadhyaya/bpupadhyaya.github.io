What stays on your device

Your answers, mastery, test results, notes, settings and text size are stored only on your device. There is no account and no analytics.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: banners on browsing screens, occasional full-screen ads at natural breaks and optional rewarded videos. No ads appear during a timed mock paper or while a question is open. A consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) is shown where required. Because many learners are under 18, ad requests are marked for under-age users and are non-personalised.

Subscription

An optional monthly plan removes ads and unlocks every feature. Apple or Google process the payment; we never see your payment details. There is no free trial. Cancel any time in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions.

Links you open

Source links open public web pages inside the app. Those sites apply their own privacy practices.
