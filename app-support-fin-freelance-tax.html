Common questions

How are the numbers calculated? In the app, open More, then Sources and method. It lists the IRS tables used, the date of the data and what is not modelled.

Where is my data? Only on your device. Deleting the app deletes it. Use Log, Export, to keep a CSV copy first.

Remove ads or cancel the subscription. More, Go ad-free. To cancel: iPhone Settings, your name, Subscriptions, Quarterwick. You can also earn an ad-free month by watching videos.

Text too small? Use the A- and A+ buttons at the top of any screen, or your system text size.

Ad choices. More, Privacy and ad choices (shown when required in your region).
