Hanquill Privacy Policy

Chinese Language AP: Hanquill · EqualInformation, LLC · Effective 26 November 2026

Scope

This policy explains how EqualInformation, LLC handles information in the Hanquill study app for Apple and Android devices.

What stays on your device

Your progress, learner estimates, study plan, settings, exam date and the text you write in practice tasks are kept in the app on your device. Voice recordings are made only when you tap Record. They are stored in the app's private folder, are not uploaded, and can be deleted one at a time or all at once. Removing the app removes this information.

Listening audio is produced by your device's own text-to-speech service. That service, and your keyboard, are provided by your device maker or by software you chose and follow their own terms.

Information you choose to send

Diagnostic reports: you see a preview and send a report only by tapping Send. A report contains app, device, performance and error details, recent app event names and any note you add.

Feedback and suggestions: the category, your message, and optional details such as your role, a rating or a source link. An email address is included only if you ask for a reply.

Diagnostic and app-usage information

When the usage-information switch is on, we collect diagnostic and app-usage information that is permissible under applicable law:

  • device and operating-system details (model, system version, processor, memory, display size, languages, region, time zone, accessibility and display settings), app version and network type;
  • performance, crash and error information;
  • a random identifier created by the app, which is not your name, an advertising identifier or a hardware serial number;
  • how features are used: screens and lessons viewed, questions attempted, answers chosen, whether they were correct, time taken, help opened, progress estimates, self-check totals, what the app recommended and what happened next, and purchase-screen actions.

When you accept the first-run notice, a short record of that acceptance (notice version, time, a random identifier, app version, region and time zone) is sent even if the switch is off.

The app does not collect your name, contacts, precise location, photographs, files, voice recordings or advertising identifiers.

How we use information

To run, secure and support the app, find and fix problems, personalise the study path, understand how the app is used, carry out research and development, improve our apps and learning material, answer messages, and meet legal obligations.

Your choices

The first-run notice has a separate usage-information switch. It starts off in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and where the region is unknown, and on elsewhere. You can change it at any time in the app under Me, then Privacy and data. There you can also ask us to delete your usage data, or write to diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

Storage and service providers

Information is stored in our Google Workspace services (Sheets, Drive and notification email) and processed by providers acting on our behalf. It is sent over encrypted connections. Subscriptions are handled by Apple or Google; we do not receive your payment card details. Links to external sources open in a browser, where the other site's policy applies.

Retention and security

We keep information while it is useful for the purposes above and delete it on a valid request unless the law requires us to keep it. We use reasonable safeguards, although no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

International users

Information may be processed in the United States. Where the law requires a legal basis, we rely on your consent or on our legitimate interests in running and improving the app. Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object; contact us to use them.

Children

We design our apps to collect only the information described in this policy. If you believe information has been provided to us that you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes in a material way, we update this page and, where the first-run notice changes, show it again. Contact: EqualInformation, LLC, support@equalinformation.com.
