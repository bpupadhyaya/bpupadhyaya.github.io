USMLE Step 1 Prep: Stethari: Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Last updated 2026-11-24.

What stays on your device

Your answers, review schedule, mistake notebook, flags, lesson progress, timed-block results, streak and settings are stored only on your device. We run no account system and no server that receives this data. Uninstalling the app or using Reset in Exam & Me erases it.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not collect or share any personal data. The app is for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed to children.

Subscription

The optional monthly Stethari Plus plan is processed by Apple or Google; we never see payment details. You can cancel in your store account settings and keep Plus until the paid period ends.

Sources you open

Source pages open inside the app but load from their publishers (for example NCBI Bookshelf, OpenStax, CDC), which apply their own privacy practices.

Your choices

You can erase local data at any time with Reset in Exam & Me.
