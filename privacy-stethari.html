USMLE Step 1 Prep: Stethari: Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Effective 2026-11-24. Last updated 2026-11-27.

1. Introduction and scope

This Privacy Policy explains how EqualInformation, LLC ("we", "us") handles information in connection with the USMLE Step 1 Prep: Stethari app. It applies to the app and to the first-run notice shown when you first open it.

2. Information we collect

A. Information you choose to provide. Diagnostic reports that you choose to send, with any note you add. Feedback and suggestions that you submit, with their category, message, optional role, level, source link and rating, and an email address only if you choose to give one for a reply.

B. Diagnostic and app-usage information, collected with your agreement or as permitted by applicable law. Device and operating-system information (model, system version, processor, memory, display size, language, region and time zone, accessibility and display settings), app version, performance and memory indicators, crash and error information, a randomly generated app identifier, and how features of the app are used. Because this is a learning app, this includes how learning features are used and perform: lessons viewed, questions attempted, whether answers are correct, time taken, hints used, progress estimates by topic, study sessions, adaptive-learning decisions and their outcomes, and activity in labs and simulations. The words you type into the app's search fields, and anything else you type in the app, are not included in this information: what you type stays on your device. Text leaves the app only when you choose to send it as feedback, a suggestion, a problem report or a note with a diagnostic report (A above).

C. Information we do not collect. Your name, contacts, precise location, photographs, files, advertising identifiers, or, in usage information, search words or other text that you enter in the app, such as notes.

Your study progress, answers and settings are also stored on your device so that the app works offline.

3. How we use information

To operate, maintain, secure and support the app; to diagnose problems; to provide and personalise the learning experience; to understand how the app is used; for research and development; for analytics and improvement of our apps and educational content; to read and act on feedback; and to comply with legal obligations.

4. Your choices

When you first open the app a notice is shown. The optional usage-information switch is under Exam & Me, Privacy and data; it is off by default in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland until you turn it on. To ask for your usage information to be deleted, use "Delete my usage data" in the same place or write to diagnostics@equalinformation.com. The button switches usage sharing off and sends us the request; it does not itself delete what we already hold. Diagnostic reports and feedback are sent only when you choose to send them.

5. Storage and service providers

Information is stored in our Google Workspace services (Google Sheets and Drive) and notification email, and processed by service providers acting on our behalf. When the app was installed from the App Store or Google Play, the notification email can contain the record in full. This covers the notice acceptance, usage and learning batches, crash records, feedback, diagnostic reports and deletion requests. It goes to our own mailboxes and may be passed on to another mailbox that is also ours. Information is transmitted over encrypted connections. Subscription payments are processed by Apple or Google; we do not see payment details.

6. Retention

Usage and learning information is kept for as long as it is useful for the purposes above. Deletion is a manual job. Nothing expires or is removed automatically: we receive your request as a logged entry and an email, and records are taken out by hand. Doing so in our spreadsheets or files does not touch emailed copies, which remain unless they too are removed by hand.

7. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. International users and legal bases

Where applicable we rely on your consent or on our legitimate interests. Information may be processed in the United States.

9. Children

We design our apps to collect only the information described in this policy. If you believe information has been provided to us that you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

10. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes the first-run notice version changes and the notice is shown again.

11. Contact

EqualInformation, LLC, support@equalinformation.com. Deletion and data requests: diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
