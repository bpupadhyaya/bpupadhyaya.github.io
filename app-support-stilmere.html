Latin AP: Stilmere Support

Help for Latin AP: Stilmere. Write to support@equalinformation.com and tell us your device and what you were doing.

Common questions

How do I switch views? Use the Classical and Real world switch at the top. Your progress and pins are shared.

How do I change the text size? Use A- and A+ at the top, from 50% to 300%.

I subscribed but Plus is locked. Open More, Settings and choose Restore purchases while signed in to the store account you used.

How do I cancel? Choose Manage subscription in the app, or open subscriptions in your Apple or Google account. You keep Plus until the paid month ends.

I found a mistake in the content. Use the report control under the explanation or lesson, or write to us with the question or line.

Data requests. Use Delete my usage data in Settings or write to diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
