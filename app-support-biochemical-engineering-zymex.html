Biochemical Engineering: Zymex Support

Biochemical Engineering: Zymex is a Biochemical Engineering course study app. Published by EqualInformation, LLC.

Restore a purchase

After a reinstall or a new device, use the same Apple Account (or Google account on Android), open About and choose Restore Purchases. You are not charged again.

Manage or cancel the subscription

Apple or Google manages the subscription. On iOS go to Settings, your name, Subscriptions; on Android open Google Play, Profile, Payments and subscriptions. Cancel at least a day before the renewal date to skip the next charge; the store handles refunds.

Get help

Email bpupadhyaya@equalinformation.com with your device model, system version and a short description of the problem. We usually reply within a few days. Privacy questions and data requests go to the same address.

Report a content error

Found a wrong answer, a typo or an unclear explanation? Email bpupadhyaya@equalinformation.com with the topic and question text (a screenshot helps) and, if you can, the source you checked. Confirmed mistakes are fixed in an update.

Common questions

How does the adaptive path work? The adaptive path tracks what you know topic by topic and chooses what to practise or review next from your mistakes, using only data on your device. You can move to the course-order path at any time and keep your progress.

Can I choose how long a quiz is and how hard? You set the quiz length and the level yourself. Timed tests and mock exams never show ads while they run.

What are Plus features and how do I unlock them? Plus features are the extra labs, levels, modes or tools that are marked with a lock in the app; the free core course stays free. Two ways to get them: watch an optional rewarded video for 24 hours of access, or subscribe monthly to remove ads and keep everything unlocked. The free core course stays usable without either.

Does the app need an internet connection? No. Lessons, questions, worked solutions, mock exams and the interactive labs work offline. A connection is needed only to show ads, watch rewarded videos and buy or restore the subscription.

Are ads shown during exams? No. Ads appear only at natural breaks, never during timed tests or mock exams, and worked solutions are not hidden behind ads.

Where do the further-reading links lead? To the original sources, which are cited and linked, not copied. Their licences stay with their owners.

Is this a replacement for my course? No, Zymex is a companion for study and practice, not a substitute for a class or instructor.
