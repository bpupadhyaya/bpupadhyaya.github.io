What stays on your device

Your answers, topic scores, review schedule, notes, finished builds and settings are stored only on your device. The app has no account and does not upload your study data.

The app does not track you and does not collect your personal data. It has no account and no analytics.

Subscription

The optional monthly plan, Histmere Plus, unlocks the Plus content. Payments are handled by Apple or Google; we never see your payment details. Cancel any time in your store account settings; cancelling takes effect at the end of the billing period.

Links

Source and legal links open in your browser. Those sites apply their own privacy practices.

Audience

The app is made for GCSE History learners, who are usually 14 to 16. Parents or guardians with questions can contact us.
