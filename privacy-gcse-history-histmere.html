Privacy Policy: GCSE History Prep: Histmere

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Contact: support@equalinformation.com. Last updated 2026-11-22.

What stays on your device

Your answers, topic scores, review schedule, notes, finished builds and settings are stored only on your device. The app has no account and does not upload your study data.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: banners on browse screens, a full-screen ad after some finished quizzes, and optional rewarded videos that unlock premium features for 24 hours. On first launch the app asks whether you are 18 or older. If you are under 18, only non-personalised ads are requested and no tracking permission is asked. Adults are asked for tracking permission (iOS) and for consent where required by law; you can change your choice in Settings, About, privacy and terms, Ad and privacy choices.

What ad partners may collect

Google may process your device advertising identifier and approximate interaction data to serve and measure ads, subject to your choices. We do not receive that data.

Subscription

The optional monthly plan removes ads and unlocks every feature. Payments are handled by Apple or Google; we never see your payment details. There is no free trial. Cancel any time in your store account settings.

Links

Source and legal links open in your browser. Those sites apply their own privacy practices.

Audience

The app is made for GCSE History learners, who are usually 14 to 16. Under-18 users receive non-personalised ads only. Parents or guardians with questions can contact us.
