Polityloom privacy policy

In short

This policy covers the Polityloom app on iPhone, iPad and Android devices, published by EqualInformation, LLC. It applies from 26 November 2026. Polityloom has no accounts and does not ask who you are.

What leaves your device, and when

When you accept the first page, whatever you choose there, we receive a short record: the app and its version, when you accepted, which version of the page and a fingerprint of its wording, whether reporting was on, random numbers the app made for this record and this installation, and your language region, country setting and time zone, with basic technical details that show whether the copy came from a store. If reporting was on, the record also carries the page wording, how long you spent on it and the device facts listed next.

While the reporting switch is on, the app sends:

  • how you use the study tools: screens, lessons, labs, builds, plan changes, sources opened and the subscription page
  • quiz activity: which questions, which option number, right or wrong, time taken, the app's estimate for each topic and the next step it proposed
  • visits: when the app was opened and left, and for how long
  • device and app facts: model, system version, processor, memory and storage figures, screen and window size, languages, region, time zone, text size, app version, plan type and the random installation number
  • crash details

Only when you press Send, we receive a message, rating, thumbs-up or thumbs-down, error report, optional role or source link, or a fault report. A fault report is shown to you in full first; it holds device facts, settings, a short list of recent app actions and any note you add. An email address reaches us only if you tick the box asking for a reply.

What the reports leave out

Automatic reports do not carry the words you type as answers, build notes or searches. Written responses and notes are saved on your device. Anything you type into a message or fault report is sent as written, so leave out personal details. The app does not read your name, contacts, location services, photos or advertising identifier.

Why we use it

To run, protect and repair the app; to see which study tools help and to improve how the app adapts; to develop our teaching material and apps; to answer you; to act on deletion requests; and to meet legal duties.

Your switches

Reporting is chosen on the first page and can be changed under More, then Privacy and data. It starts off in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and wherever the region is unknown, and starts on elsewhere. Switching it off stops automatic reports and discards those still waiting on your device. The same screen can send a request to delete your usage records; you may also write to diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

Where it goes

Reports travel over encrypted connections to storage and mail services that Google provides to us. Some reports also produce a notification email to us. Service providers act on our instructions. No system can promise complete security.

How long we keep it

We keep records while they serve the purposes above or the law requires them, and we handle deletion requests when they reach us.

Legal grounds and transfers

Information may be processed in the United States. Where the law calls for a legal ground, reporting and the things you send rest on your consent, and the acceptance record rests on our legitimate interest in showing what was agreed and on legal duties. Local law may give you rights to see, correct or erase information; write to us to use them.

Purchases and other websites

Apple or Google processes subscriptions under its own policy; we do not receive card details. A source you open comes from another website with its own policy.

Children

We limit collection to what this policy describes. If you believe we hold information that should be removed, write to us and we will delete it after a valid request.

Changes and contact

We will change this page when our practice changes, and show a new first page in the app when the difference matters. Data requests: diagnostics@equalinformation.com. Other questions: support@equalinformation.com.
