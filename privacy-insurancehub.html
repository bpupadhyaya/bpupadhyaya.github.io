Privacy Policy — Insurance Hub

Effective Date: July 10, 2026

  • Home
  • /
  • Privacy Policy
  • /
  • Insurance Hub

1 Who We Are

Insurance Hub is provided by EqualInformation, LLC.

If you have privacy questions or requests, you may contact us at:
Email: privacy@equalinformation.com
Contact page: bpupadhyaya.github.io#contact

2 Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Insurance Hub website at bpupadhyaya.github.io/insurance-site/, the Insurance Hub iOS and Android apps, and related EqualInformation, LLC pages that link to this policy. See Section 9 for practices specific to the mobile apps.

3 Information We Do Not Collect

Insurance Hub is designed so that nothing about you is stored. The website:

  • Has no user accounts, sign-ups, or logins
  • Does not ask for your name, email, address, date of birth, driver history, health information, or any other personal details
  • Does not store your browsing selections on any server
  • Does not use advertising, ad networks, or data brokers
  • Does not sell, rent, or share personal data — because it does not collect any

4 Information Processed in Your Browser

Comparison inputs you choose on the site — such as an insurance domain (auto, home, health, and so on), a state, or coverage options — are processed in your browser to display comparative prices. These selections are not transmitted to or stored on EqualInformation, LLC servers.

5 Hosting and Technical Data

The Insurance Hub website is hosted on GitHub Pages, operated by GitHub, Inc. As with any website, GitHub's servers may process technical request information (such as your IP address and browser user agent) to deliver pages, under GitHub's own privacy policies. EqualInformation, LLC does not receive or retain this information.

6 Public Data Sources

The prices and facts shown by Insurance Hub are computed from public, authoritative regulatory data, including state rate filings (SERFF), state Departments of Insurance, CMS / Healthcare.gov, OpenFEMA / NFIP, USDA RMA, and NAIC reports. These sources contain carrier and regulatory information — they do not contain personal data about Insurance Hub visitors.

7 Cookies, Analytics, and Tracking

Insurance Hub does not use tracking cookies, third-party analytics, advertising identifiers, or cross-site tracking of any kind.

8 External Links

Insurance Hub links to public regulatory filings and official government or regulator websites so you can verify every price against its source. When you follow those links, your device connects directly to those third-party sites, which operate under their own privacy policies and terms, not this Privacy Policy.

9 Mobile Apps (iOS and Android)

The Insurance Hub iOS and Android apps are designed the same way as the website: nothing about you is collected or stored on our servers.

  • No accounts. The apps have no sign-up, login, or user profile of any kind.
  • Your watchlist stays on your device. Insurance comparisons you choose to save are stored only in the app's local, on-device database. They are never transmitted to EqualInformation, LLC or any third party, and are deleted if you uninstall the app.
  • Public data, downloaded securely. The apps periodically download public insurance rate and coverage datasets from our own server (equalinformation.com) over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Each download is checksum-verified before use. This is a one-way delivery of public data to your device — no personal information is sent as part of it.
  • Optional Wi-Fi sync. A Settings toggle lets you turn on automatic daily data refresh over Wi-Fi only; it never uses cellular data, and manual refresh is always available regardless of this setting.
  • No analytics, tracking, ads, or crash reporting. The apps do not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs, and do not use advertising identifiers or cross-app/cross-site tracking.
  • Minimal permissions. The Android app requests only internet access and network-state access (to know if a connection is available before attempting a data refresh) — no location, camera, contacts, or storage permissions.
  • Payment. Insurance Hub is a one-time paid download. Your purchase is processed entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play Store under their own privacy and payment policies — EqualInformation, LLC never receives or stores your payment card details.

10 Children

Insurance Hub does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe information has been provided inappropriately, please contact the company.

11 Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the updated version on this page and revise the Effective Date above.
