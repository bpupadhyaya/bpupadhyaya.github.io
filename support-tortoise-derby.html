Tortoise Derby, a game by EqualInformation, LLC

How the game works

Your gold-caped Champion must cross the finish line after every other tortoise. Tap a lane card to hand out Hurry, Nap or Swap orders, then ring the bell. Undo a round or restart any time; nothing is ever lost.

Common questions

I cannot see ads or the ad-free offer. Open Settings in the game, choose No ads, and use Restore purchases if you subscribed before.

How do I cancel the subscription? On your iPhone or iPad open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Tortoise Derby.

How do I change my ad privacy choices? In the game open Settings and tap Privacy choices (shown where required), or Settings on iOS then Tortoise Derby then Tracking.
