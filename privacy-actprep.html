ACT Prep & Practice: Actvane - Privacy Policy

Effective 14 November 2026. Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC.

About

Actvane helps students prepare for the enhanced ACT with original ACT-style English, Math, Reading and Science practice, timed section practice, full mock tests, interactive labs, worked solutions and a mistake notebook. ACT is a trademark of its owner. Actvane is not affiliated with or endorsed by ACT. Questions are original practice items, not real exam questions.

What stays on your device

Actvane works offline and has no account or sign-up. Your answers, mistakes, review schedule, streak, credits, goal score, test date and settings are stored on your device only and are never sent to us. You can export your progress to a file you choose, import it again, or erase everything in Settings. Deleting the app removes it too.

Permissions

Actvane does not use the camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location, and asks for no storage permission. Export and import use the system file picker, which only gives Actvane the one file you choose.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob: a banner on the Plan, Tests, Labs and Progress screens, a short full-screen ad after some finished practice sets, study-break cards with an optional rewarded video you can watch to earn a credit, and optional rewarded videos. No ads are shown during a question, a timed section, a mock test, a lab, a diagnostic or the review of a solution, and none in your first sessions. On iPhone and iPad the system asks whether the app may track you (App Tracking Transparency) only for users who say they are 18 or older; in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some US states a Google consent message also appears (Google User Messaging Platform). On Android the Google consent message appears where the law requires it. You can decline and keep using Actvane. Ads are not shown to people with an active ad-free subscription.

Students under 18

Actvane is made for high-school students aged 13 and older and for adults. At first launch it asks, without collecting a birth date, whether you are 18 or older. If you say you are under 18, Actvane requests only non-personalised ads and limits ad content to the teen rating, and it does not ask for tracking permission. The answer is stored on your device and you can change it in Settings. Actvane is not directed to children under 13.

Credits and subscription

Actvane is free to use with credits: new users start with 2 credits, a watched rewarded video earns 1 (up to 12 a day), streak milestones add bonus credits, the first four practice sets each day are free, a timed section costs 1 credit and a full mock costs 3. If no ad can be shown, one free action is granted at most once every 24 hours. The optional ad-free monthly subscription (7-day free trial, then the monthly price shown in the app and store) is handled by Apple or Google Play; we never see your payment details. Credits, purchase status and settings are stored on your device.

What is collected

Collected by us: nothing. Your answers and progress are never collected. Collected by Google AdMob in the free version, with your permission where required: device identifiers (advertising identifier), approximate location from IP address, and ad interaction and diagnostic data, used for third-party advertising and measuring ads. For users under 18 the ads are non-personalised.

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information, build profiles about you, run our own analytics, or ask you to create an account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new effective date.
