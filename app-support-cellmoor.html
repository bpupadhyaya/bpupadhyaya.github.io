Biology AP: Cellmoor: Support

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Contact: support@equalinformation.com. Last updated 2026-11-25.

Support

Questions, content corrections and subscription help: support@equalinformation.com. Please include your device model, the app version and the topic and question number or screen concerned.

Send diagnostics

Open Settings, Privacy and data and choose Send diagnostics. The app shows you what the report contains and nothing is sent until you tap Send. Reports are delivered to diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

Feedback and suggestions

Use Feedback and suggestions under Settings, or the thumbs and Report a problem controls beside answers, topics and labs. An email address is included only if you tick that you would like a reply.

Delete my usage data

Open Settings, Privacy and data and choose Delete my usage data, or email diagnostics@equalinformation.com with your request.

Subscription

Manage or cancel Cellmoor Plus in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings, or use Manage subscription on the Cellmoor Plus screen. Cancelling takes effect at the end of the current billing period. Use Restore purchases if a purchase does not appear on a new device.

Found an error?

Send the topic, the question or card concerned and, if you can, a reliable source that disagrees. Corrections are reviewed and fixed in an update.

Contact

support@equalinformation.com. Privacy and deletion requests: diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
