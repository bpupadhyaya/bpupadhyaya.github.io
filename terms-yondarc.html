Using Yondarc

Your licence

You may use Yondarc for your own study and to help people you teach or look after. On Apple devices Apple’s standard licence agreement for apps also applies.

The two passes

Classical costs US$2.99 a month and Real-World US$9.99 a month, or the local price your store shows before you confirm. Each is a separate pass that repeats monthly until you end it in your store account. Ending a pass stops later charges; the month you have bought stays usable until it finishes. There is no trial.

Refunds

Apple and Google take the payment and decide refund requests, so ask them first. Nothing here removes a right to a refund that the law where you live gives you.

Purpose

Yondarc helps you practise. It does not teach a licensed course, grant credit or stand in for an exam, and no standards body, school or exam board has had anything to do with it. Nobody outside the author has gone through the questions, so measure what you learn against your own syllabus.

Pretend scenarios

Flight and engineering pictures are simplified and must never guide a real operation. Money exercises use made-up figures and are not advice about your finances, tax or insurance.

Mistakes and liability

We work to keep the content right and the app running, but cannot promise there are no errors. If you spot one, tell us. Where the law lets us, we do not accept responsibility for loss that comes from relying on the app; where the law will not let us limit something, we have not tried to.
