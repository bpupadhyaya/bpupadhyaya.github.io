Privacy Policy

African American AP: Griotly · EqualInformation, LLC · Effective November 26, 2026

What this page is

Griotly (African American AP: Griotly) is a study app from EqualInformation, LLC for Apple and Android phones and tablets. Below we set out, for Griotly alone, what leaves your device, why it does, and what you can control.

The short version

Griotly needs no sign-up. Your scores, plan, drafts and case-file writing live on your own device. What can be sent to us falls into four groups: a receipt for the opening notice, study and technical records, reports you send by hand, and comments you send by hand.

The notice receipt

Tapping through Griotly's opening notice sends us a small receipt: a random code for that acceptance, the moment it happened, which Griotly build you run, and the language, region and time zone your device is set to.

Study and technical records

If the sharing switch is on, or where the law otherwise lets us, Griotly records how a study session goes and how the software behaves. On the study side: which Griotly lessons and case-file steps you open, the answer you pick on a question and whether it matched the key, seconds spent, mastery estimates for course topics, mock sittings, what Griotly suggested you do next and what you then did, and your taps on the subscription screen.

On the technical side: handset or tablet type, system release, chip, memory, screen measurements, locale and clock settings, text-size and accessibility settings, Griotly's release number, speed and memory readings, and details of crashes or faults. A randomly made installation code ties these records together. Your name is not part of them.

Reports and comments you send

The diagnostics button shows a preview first and transmits only after you press Send, along with any remark you typed for it. The comment forms transmit your chosen topic and message and, only if you want to give them, your role, study level, a link or a rating. Should you ask us to write back, the email address you enter is kept for that answer and is the single item we can connect to a person.

Things Griotly leaves alone

Griotly does not ask for your name, address book, exact whereabouts, camera roll or documents, and reads no advertising identifier. Words you type into practice answers, essays and case files are never part of the records described above.

Why we want it

These records help us keep Griotly running and secure, trace faults, shape each learner's study suggestions, see which parts of the course earn their place, carry out research and development, improve our teaching material and software, act on what you tell us, and satisfy duties the law places on us.

Your controls

Open Settings, then Privacy and data, to flip the sharing switch whenever you like. For devices set to a country in the European Economic Area, to the United Kingdom or to Switzerland, or whose region Griotly cannot tell, the switch starts in the off position. The same screen offers Delete my usage data. You may instead email diagnostics@equalinformation.com with the request.

Where it goes

Records travel over encrypted links into spreadsheets, file storage and mail that EqualInformation holds with Google Workspace; companies providing such services handle the data on our instructions. Apple or Google, not Griotly, take payment for Griotly Plus and keep your card details. A web source opened from Griotly is served by its own publisher, who sees a normal page visit.

How long, and how safe

We hold study and technical records for as long as they remain useful for the reasons listed above, and act on removal requests when they reach us. We protect the data with sensible safeguards, though nobody can promise that any system is beyond attack.

Legal footing and location

Depending on where you live, our basis is your agreement or our legitimate interest in running and improving Griotly. Processing may take place in the United States.

Children

Griotly is built to gather no more than this page describes. If something has reached us that you want taken out, write to us; when the request is valid we remove it.

Updates and contact

When we change this policy in a way that matters, Griotly's opening notice is revised and presented once more. General questions go to support@equalinformation.com; questions about your data go to diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
