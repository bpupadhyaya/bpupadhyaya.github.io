Privacy Policy

African American AP: Griotly · EqualInformation, LLC · Effective November 26, 2026

1. Introduction and scope

This policy explains how EqualInformation, LLC handles information in the app African American AP: Griotly, for iPhone, iPad and Android devices.

2. Information you choose to provide

A diagnostic report that you send, with any note you add to it. Feedback and suggestions that you submit: a category, your message and, if you choose, a role, level, source link or rating. An email address is collected only if you tick the option asking for a reply, and it is used for that reply.

3. Diagnostic and app-usage information

With your agreement, or as permitted by applicable law, the app collects diagnostic and app-usage information. This includes device and operating-system details (model, system version, processor, memory, display size, language, region and time zone, accessibility and display settings), the app version, performance and memory indicators, crash and error information, a random app identifier, and how features are used.

Because this is a study app, usage information also covers how the learning features are used and how they perform: lessons viewed, questions attempted, which option was chosen and whether it was correct, time taken, progress estimates by topic, case-file steps completed, mock exams, purchase-screen interactions, and the study recommendations the app makes together with what happens after them.

When you first open the app, a record of your acceptance of the notice is sent, with a random acceptance identifier, the time, the app version, and your language, region and time zone.

4. Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, contacts, precise location, photographs, files or advertising identifier. Text that you write in practice answers, essays and case-file entries stays on your device and is not collected. There is no account.

5. How we use information

To operate, maintain, secure and support the app; to find and fix problems; to provide and personalise study; to understand how the app is used; for research, development, analytics and improvement of our apps and educational content; to read and act on feedback; and to meet legal obligations.

6. Your choices

A notice is shown when you first open the app. The usage-information switch is under Settings, Privacy and data. It is off by default in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and where the region is unknown, until you turn it on. Diagnostics and feedback are sent only when you choose to send them. To ask for deletion, use Delete my usage data in the app or write to diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

7. Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple or Google under their own terms. We do not receive your payment card details.

8. Storage and service providers

Information is stored in our Google Workspace services (Google Sheets and Drive) and notification email, and is processed by service providers acting for us. It is sent over encrypted connections. When you open a cited source inside the app, the publisher of that page receives an ordinary web request under its own policy.

9. Retention

Usage and learning information is kept while it is useful for the purposes above. Deletion requests are handled when we receive them.

10. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

11. International users

Where applicable we rely on consent or legitimate interests. Information may be processed in the United States.

12. Children

We design our apps to collect only the information described in this policy. If you believe information has been provided to us that you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

13. Changes

If this policy changes in a material way, the first-run notice is updated and shown again.

14. Contact

EqualInformation, LLC: support@equalinformation.com. Deletion and data requests: diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
