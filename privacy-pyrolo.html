Python Coding for AI: Pyrolo - Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC.

Last updated 2026-11-17.

Pyrolo has no account, no server of its own and no chat feature. Your progress, the code you type and your settings stay on your device. We do not collect your name, e-mail, birth date or answers. The app asks only whether you are 18 or older; the answer is stored on the device and is used to decide how ads are shown.

Ads. The free version shows ads through Google AdMob at natural breaks. For adults, with your permission where required (tracking prompt and consent form), Google may collect device identifiers and approximate location to show relevant ads. If you say you are under 18, no tracking prompt is shown, ads are non-personalised and limited to teen-appropriate content, and consent is requested for under-age users.

Subscription. An optional monthly subscription removes ads. Payment is handled by the App Store or Google Play; we receive no payment details.

Links. Citations open the cited website inside the app. Those sites have their own privacy practices. The app does not set cookies of its own, and the in-app window keeps no cookies after it closes.

Age. The app is meant for ages 13 and up. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
