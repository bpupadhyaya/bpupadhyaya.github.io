Privacy policy

Math Grade 2: Marlowisp is made by EqualInformation, LLC. This policy is dated 27 November 2026.

The app sends nothing by itself. It has no accounts, no analytics and no crash reporting of its own.

Answers, progress, settings and the grown-up's real-object check marks are stored only on this device. "Erase progress" in the grown-ups area removes them, and so does deleting the app. Your device may include app data in its own backup, under your device settings.

In the grown-ups area, opening a cited source loads that one page or document from the publisher's website in a window inside the app, with scripts and content from other sites switched off, and keeps no cookies, history or copy of it. The publisher's server receives an ordinary web request, including your internet address, under its own privacy policy. "Open in browser" hands the page to your web browser.

Spoken prompts use the voice built into the device. The app does not use the microphone, camera, location, contacts or photos.

Buying and billing happen inside the app store on your device, under that store's own privacy policy. The app only asks the store which subscriptions are switched on, and it remembers that answer here. Your name and payment details never reach us through the app.

The app store may give us store statistics and crash reports according to your device settings.

In the grown-ups area, the Support page can start an email in your own mail app. A diagnostics report holds the app version, system version, device model, screen size, language and the app's display and sound settings, and is shown to you in full first. A feedback message holds what you write. Neither holds an identifier or anything your child did. Nothing is sent until you press send in your mail app.

If you email us, we receive your email address, your name if your mail app adds it, and what the message holds. We use them only to answer you and to fix and improve the app. Email is handled by our email provider. To ask us to delete your messages, write to diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
