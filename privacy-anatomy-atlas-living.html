Anatomy Atlas: Corpolume Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026. Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC.

In short

Corpolume is an offline study app. It needs no account and has no servers for your study data. Your progress, bookmarks, notes, quiz results and text size stay on your device and are removed when you delete the app.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob on menu and result screens, never inside the 3D study view or on a quiz question. To choose and measure ads, Google may use your device's advertising identifier and information about how you interact with ads. On iPhone and iPad the app first asks whether it may track you across other companies' apps and websites; where required by law it then shows a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform. You can say no to both and keep using every feature; ads are then less personalised. See policies.google.com/technologies/ads. You can remove ads with the monthly Corpolume Ad-free subscription, with the one-time Remove Ads purchase, or earn ad-free time by watching an optional rewarded video.

Purchases

The subscription and the one-time Remove Ads purchase are sold and billed by Apple through your Apple Account. We never see your payment details.

What we do not collect

Corpolume does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, health data, camera, microphone or precise location, has no sign-in and contains no analytics SDK of ours. We do not sell personal data.

Children

Corpolume is a study tool for students and adults and is not directed to children under 13.

Contact

Questions or corrections: support@equalinformation.com. See also the support page and the terms of use. We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.

Education only. Corpolume does not diagnose or treat any condition and is not medical advice.
