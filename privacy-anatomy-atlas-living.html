Corpolume: Human Anatomy 3D

Privacy Policy. Effective 8 November 2026. Publisher: Equal Information LLC.

In short

Corpolume is an offline anatomy study app. It needs no account and runs no servers for your study data. Your quiz scores, which structures you have studied, text size and colour settings stay on your device.

Data kept on your device

Quiz best scores, a list of structures you have opened, your A-/A+ text zoom, the colour-blind palette choice and your ad-free state are stored in the app's local storage. Deleting the app removes them.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob on menu and result screens, never inside the 3D study view. To choose and measure ads, Google may use your device's advertising identifier, approximate location from your network address and information about how you interact with ads. On iOS the app first asks whether it may track you across other companies' apps and websites; where required by law it then shows a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform. You can say no to both and keep using the app with less personalised ads. See policies.google.com/technologies/ads. You can remove ads with the monthly Ad-free subscription or by earning ad-free time with rewarded videos.

Subscription

The optional monthly Ad-free subscription is sold and billed by Apple through your Apple Account. We never see your payment details. Manage or cancel it in your Apple Account subscription settings.

What we do not collect

Corpolume does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, health data, camera, microphone or precise location. It contains no analytics SDK of ours and no AI features. We do not sell personal data.

Children

The app is an educational reference. Ads shown are those allowed by the consent and age settings of the platform.
