Privacy Policy — Sristi

Effective Date: June 27, 2026

  • Home
  • /
  • Privacy Policy
  • /
  • Sristi

1 Who We Are

Sristi is provided by EqualInformation, LLC. Sristi is an AI-first personal life optimizer — "Live by design, not by default."

If you have privacy questions or requests, you may contact us at:
Email: privacy@equalinformation.com
Contact page: equalinformation.weebly.com/contact.html | bpupadhyaya.github.io#contact

2 Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Sristi app (iOS bundle identifier: com.equalinformation.sristi.app; Android package name: com.equalinformation.sristi.app). Sristi is available now on Google Play, and in review on the Apple App Store.

3 Information Sristi May Access or Store

Sristi plans, executes, and evaluates the essentials of a healthy life across 13 cited life domains. Depending on how you use the app, Sristi may store information on your device, including:

  • Your profile from onboarding (age, weight, height, sex, activity level, dietary preferences, goals — all optional)
  • Daily logs (hydration, sleep, movement, energy, mood, meals)
  • A light on-device mood journal that keeps your own words
  • Daily-plan completion state
  • Reminders setting
  • In-app purchase status (Sristi Plus unlock state)

Sristi does not collect or transmit any of this data. All information remains on your device.

4 How We Use Information

Sristi uses locally stored information to:

  • Generate a daily plan that adapts to your logged energy
  • Track your hydration, sleep, movement, energy, mood, and meals
  • Produce an evening recap and weekly/monthly reflection
  • Display appropriate content based on purchase status (free vs Sristi Plus)

5 Local-First Design

Sristi is 100% on-device. It does not collect, transmit, or store any user data on servers. There is no account, no servers, no cloud sync, and no user tracking. The core experience works fully offline.

6 No Analytics or Tracking

Sristi does not use any analytics SDKs, crash reporters, advertising SDKs, or tracking tools. No data is sent to third-party analytics services.

7 App Store and Google Play Billing

Sristi may use Apple App Store In-App Purchase (on iOS) or Google Play Billing (on Android) for the optional Sristi Plus purchase. Apple or Google may process payment and transaction information under their own respective policies. Sristi may receive limited purchase-status or product information needed to unlock purchased content on your device. EqualInformation, LLC does not receive your full payment card number through Sristi.

8 Health Content

Sristi is evidence-based, with tappable citations (WHO, IOM/NASEM, EFSA, ACSM, NSF, AASM, ACC/AHA, APA, CFPB, CDC, NHTSA, and peer-reviewed studies). The content provided is for educational purposes only and is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. In an emergency, contact your local emergency services. Always seek the advice of your physician or other qualified health provider with any questions regarding a medical condition.

9 Permissions

Sristi may request the following device permissions:

  • Microphone and Speech Recognition (optional): Used only for voice input. Sristi is voice-first (voice, tap, or text).
  • Health data (read-only): Via Apple HealthKit (iOS) or Android Health Connect. Sristi reads exercise minutes and sleep to keep the day's metrics accurate. This data stays on your device.
  • Notifications: Local reminders for your plan steps.
  • Billing: Used only for in-app purchase verification.

Sristi does not require location, camera, or contacts, and does not need internet for its core functionality.

10 Sharing and Sale of Data

EqualInformation, LLC does not sell personal or sensitive user data. We do not share users' data with data brokers or for advertising purposes. The company does not intentionally transmit personal or sensitive Sristi user data to its own servers or unrelated third parties.

11 Data Retention and Deletion

Purchase-related unlock state may be restorable through App Store or Google Play for eligible purchases. Uninstalling Sristi removes all locally stored data from your device, including your profile, daily logs, mood journal, plan history, and settings.

12 Security

Sristi relies on iOS and Android platform protections and app sandboxing as part of its local-first design. However, no method of storage or electronic transmission is completely secure, and the company cannot guarantee absolute security.

13 Your Choices

You may choose to purchase Sristi Plus or not — the free core, including Safety & Emergency and the health essentials, is always free. Voice input and health-data reads are optional. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data from your device.

14 Children's Privacy

Sristi is designed for adults seeking to optimize their daily life. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe information has been provided inappropriately, please contact the company.

15 Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the updated version on this page and revise the Effective Date above.
