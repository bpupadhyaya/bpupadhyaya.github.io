Rivetally privacy policy

Effective 27 November 2026

Who we are

Rivetally is a grade 8 maths study app published by EqualInformation, LLC. Contact: support@equalinformation.com.

The short version

The app does not send us anything automatically. It has no account, no analytics service, no third-party tracking code and no remote settings. It does not ask for a name, email address, photograph, contacts, location, camera or microphone.

What stays on the device

Answers, progress, the learner record used to choose next steps, pinned items, works-order designs, text size and the date the first-run notice was accepted are stored on the device. We cannot see them. If you use your device maker’s backup service, the app’s stored data may be included under that service’s terms.

What you may choose to send

In the grown-up area an adult can preview a problem report and share it with an app of their choice, for example to mail it to diagnostics@equalinformation.com. It lists app version, device model and system version, window and text size, language, time-zone offset, whether each subscription is active, a store status message, whether 3D drawing works and recent app error messages. It contains no name, answers or learning record. If you mail it, we also receive your email address and anything you add. We use these only to answer and to fix the problem, and delete the message when it is no longer needed, normally within 12 months.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple or Google. We do not receive card or bank details. The store tells the app on your device whether a subscription is active. Apple and Google give developers sales reports under their own policies, and handle your data under their own privacy policies.

Google Play on Android

For subscriptions, the Android version includes Google Play’s billing library. Google’s components inside it may send technical information about that service, such as diagnostic logs, to Google, which handles it under the Google privacy policy. We do not receive it.

Platform information

Apple and Google may give developers aggregated store figures and crash information that you have agreed to share with them. That is collected by the platform, under its settings, not by code we add.

Children

Rivetally is for young learners, and nothing in it asks them to tell us who they are. Anything that spends money or sends something out of the app sits on the grown-up side of a check, which keeps out stray taps but cannot tell anyone’s age. If you think a young person has mailed us details about themselves, tell us at support@equalinformation.com and we will remove them.

Your choices and rights

You can erase all progress in Settings, or remove the app. For information you have mailed to us, you may ask for access, correction or deletion at support@equalinformation.com. Depending on where you live, other legal rights may apply.

Changes

If a later version handles data differently, we will update this policy and the store privacy information before that version is released.
