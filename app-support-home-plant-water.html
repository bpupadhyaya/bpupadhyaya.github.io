Leafwell support

How do I change the watering rhythm?

Open a plant, tap Edit, and change where it lives (light) or its pot width. Leafwell recalculates the rhythm. Seasons follow your hemisphere under Me.

Reminders do not appear

Open Me, switch Daily watering reminder on and allow notifications. Also check iOS Settings, Notifications, Leafwell.

Remove ads or restore a subscription

Open Me, Go ad-free. You can subscribe, restore purchases, or watch short videos to earn ad-free time. Manage the subscription in your Apple Account settings.

Report an error in the care ranges

Care ranges are general guidance. Use Me, Accuracy and sources, Report an error, or write to bpupadhyaya@equalinformation.com.
