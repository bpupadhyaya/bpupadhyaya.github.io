Data Center Siting: Wattmere - Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC.

Last updated: 2026-11-23.

Data Center Siting: Wattmere is a planning tool and adaptive course for comparing candidate data center sites. It is an independent product of EqualInformation, LLC.

What stays on your device

The app has no account and no backend of ours. Your candidate sites, scores, notes, risk register, diligence tracker and settings are stored only on your device. We collect no personal data and receive none of your project data. Deleting the app deletes it.

Imports, photos and location

Files you import (CSV or similar) are read locally and used only inside the app. Field photos you attach and GPS positions you capture in field mode stay on your device and are included only in exports that you create and share yourself. We do not upload them.

Public data and sources

The app bundles indicative public data for map layers. When you open a cited source, the app loads that public web page inside the app, and that site receives your request like any web visit.

Tracking and analytics

The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It does not read or use your advertising identifier and shows no tracking prompt. Your learning progress (practice answers, mastery and mock results) is stored only on your device.

Purchases

The optional monthly subscription, Wattmere Plus, is processed by Apple or Google; we receive no payment details.

Audience

The app is a professional tool for adults and is not directed to children.
