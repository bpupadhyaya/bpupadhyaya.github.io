FELLWICK

Support

Fellwick turns each habit into a footpath on its own map. Questions, bug reports and ideas are welcome at support@equalinformation.com. Please say which iPhone or iPad and which iOS version you use.

How do camps work?

Every six steps on a trail earns a camp, up to three. If a due day goes quiet you have three days to pitch a camp on it, and your stretch carries on. You can also borrow a camp by watching a short video, or at no cost if ads are off.

Widgets do not show my trails

Open Fellwick once, then long-press the Home Screen, tap the plus button and add a Fellwick widget. On the Lock Screen choose Customise, then add the widget. If a widget looks empty, remove it and add it again.

Ad-free and restoring a purchase

Open Pack in the app, then Restore purchases. The subscription is managed in your Apple Account settings, where you can cancel it at any time.

Reminders do not arrive

Check Settings, Notifications, Fellwick, and make sure the trail has a reminder time. A reminder is skipped for a day once you have logged a step.
