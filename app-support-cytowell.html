Cytowell support

Cytowell: Cell Biology 3D, by Equal Information LLC.

Using the app

  • Cell: tap an organelle for its notes, drag to orbit, pinch to dive inside, press Fly-through for a guided tour. Switch between animal and plant cell.
  • Transport: pick a mode, change temperature and gradients and watch the particles.
  • Division: step or play through mitosis and meiosis.
  • Signals: raise the ligand and follow the cascade.
  • Study: quizzes and flashcards with spaced repetition.
  • Text size: use A- and A+ at the top of any screen. System text size is honoured too.

Subscription

The optional Cytowell Ad-free monthly subscription removes ads. Restore it from Study, then the Ad-free button. Cancel any time in Settings, Apple ID, Subscriptions.
