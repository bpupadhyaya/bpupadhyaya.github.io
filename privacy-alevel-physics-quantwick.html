A Level Physics: Quantwick: Privacy Policy

Quantwick is an A Level and AS physics revision app by EqualInformation, LLC. It has no account. Your progress (topics practised, answers, readings from the virtual lab bench) is stored only on your device and is never sent to us. The app collects no personal data, has no analytics and does not track you. The optional Quantwick Plus subscription is bought and managed through Apple (the App Store, using your Apple ID) or Google Play on Android; we do not see or store your payment details. Source links open public web pages inside the app, which apply their own privacy practices.
