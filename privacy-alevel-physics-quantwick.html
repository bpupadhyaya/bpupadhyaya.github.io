A Level Physics: Quantwick: Privacy Policy

Quantwick is an A Level and AS physics revision app by EqualInformation, LLC. It has no account. Your progress (topics practised, answers, readings from the virtual lab bench) is stored only on your device and is never sent to us. The app shows ads through Google AdMob; AdMob may collect a device or advertising identifier, IP address and ad interaction data according to your consent choices. Users under 18 get non-personalised ads; users under 13 see no ads. You can change ad choices in Me, Settings. Subscriptions are handled by Apple or Google; we do not see payment details. Source links open public web pages inside the app, which apply their own privacy practices.
