Calculus AB AP: Derivane - Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Last updated 2026-11-15.

What the app stores

Your answers, levels, review schedule, mistake notebook, simulation results, mock results, free-response self-scores, streak days, credits and settings are saved only on your device. There is no account, no sign-up and no server of ours that receives this data. You can export your progress to a file you control, import it again, or reset it. Deleting the app deletes the data.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on some screens, an occasional full-screen ad after a finished practice block, and optional rewarded videos that earn credits. There are no ads in timed mocks, simulations or while you answer a question. AdMob may use your device advertising identifier, approximate location and device information to choose and measure ads. On iOS the app asks permission before tracking across other companies' apps and websites, and where the law requires it, shows a consent form from Google. On Android the consent form is shown where required. If you decline, you see non-personalized ads. The app is intended for learners aged 13 and over, is not directed to children, and requests ads suitable for teens.

Subscription

The optional ad-free monthly subscription is handled by Apple or Google. We do not see your payment details. Cancel any time in your App Store or Google Play account settings. If you cancel, you keep ad-free until the end of the period you already paid for, and you will not be charged again.

Reference pages

When you open a reference online, the page loads from its own publisher (for example OpenStax) which has its own privacy practices. College Board pages open in your browser and are not embedded in the app.

Your choices

Change ad personalization in Settings (Ad privacy options, where shown) and in your device settings. Cancel or restore a subscription in your store account. Delete all local data with the reset option or by uninstalling the app.
