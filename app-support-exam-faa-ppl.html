Pilotwick support

Private pilot written test prep: study route, practice tests, instrument trainer, 3D airspace, METAR lab and flight computer. Need help? Write to support@equalinformation.com and tell us your device and iOS version.

Found a wrong answer or outdated rule?

Tap "Report an error" under any explanation, or in Settings. Include the question text. We check it against the current regulation or handbook and fix confirmed errors in an update.

How do I remove ads?

Tap the airplane button at the top of any screen. You can subscribe monthly, or watch short videos to earn ad-free time. Use "Restore purchases" on a new device. To cancel, open Settings, your name, Subscriptions.

Is my progress backed up?

Progress lives on your device only. If you delete the app or erase the device it is gone, unless your device backup includes app data.

Is this the real FAA test?

No. All questions are original. Pilotwick is not affiliated with the FAA. Always use current official publications and your instructor, and never use the weight-and-balance or airspace models for real flights.
