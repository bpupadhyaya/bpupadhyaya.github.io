Pourlume: Liquid Sort Puzzle

Privacy Policy. Effective 9 November 2026. Publisher: Equal Information LLC.

In short

Pourlume is a puzzle game that works fully offline. There is no account, and we run no server for the game, so we never receive your progress, scores or settings. They stay on your device.

What is stored on your device

Stars, best move counts, your daily puzzle streak, text size, haptics and colour-assist choices, and the state of your ad-free time or subscription are saved in the app's own storage. Deleting the app deletes them.

Advertising

The free game shows ads from Google AdMob. To choose and measure ads Google may use your device advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking when the app asks), your approximate location derived from your network address, and information about how the ads perform. Where the law requires it, a consent message asks for your choice before any ad is requested, and you can change that choice at any time. If you decline tracking, ads are still shown but are not personalised. Google's policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Ad-free subscription

The optional monthly subscription is handled by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment details. The app only learns whether your subscription is active.

Children

Pourlume is a general audience game and is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

You can refuse tracking in the iOS prompt or in Settings, reset all progress inside the app, or delete the app. For questions write to support@equalinformation.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new date appears at the top of this page.
