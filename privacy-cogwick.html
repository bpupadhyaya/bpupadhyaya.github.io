AI Literacy for Teens: Cogwick - Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC.

Last updated: 2026-11-20.

Cogwick does not collect your name, e-mail, birth date or answers. Your progress stays on your device. There is no account.

Advertising: the free version shows ads through Google AdMob. With your permission where required, Google may collect device identifiers, approximate location from your IP address and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. If you say you are under 18, Cogwick requests only non-personalised ads, limits ad content to a teen rating and does not ask for tracking permission. Cogwick is not directed to children under 13.

Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.

You can change your age answer in More. Questions: see the support page linked on our website.
