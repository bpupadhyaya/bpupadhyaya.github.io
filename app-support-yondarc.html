Getting help with Yondarc

Bring a pass back

Press Adults, answer the compass check, then Restore purchases. The app tells you whether an active pass was found.

End a pass

Press Adults, then Manage subscription, or open the subscription list in your store account.

A question looks wrong

Press Adults, open the diagnostic report, share it to the address in the footer and add the text of the question.

Reading comfort

The Text size button opens a slider from 50 to 300 percent. Read aloud uses the voice installed on your device.
