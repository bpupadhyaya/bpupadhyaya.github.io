Yondarc: help

The app “Math Grade 12: Yondarc” is published by EqualInformation LLC. This text dates from 27 November 2026.

Getting a subscription back

Tap Adults, answer the compass check, then Restore purchases. The app says whether an active subscription was found.

Stopping a subscription

Tap Adults, then Manage subscription, or open the subscriptions list in your Apple or Google account.

A question looks wrong

Tap Adults, open the diagnostic report, share it to the address below and add the question text.

Reading comfort

The Text size button opens a slider from 50 to 300 percent. Read aloud uses the voice installed on your device.
