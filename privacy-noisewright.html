Generative Models: Noisewright - Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC.

The app has no account and no server. Your progress and settings are stored only on your device. We do not collect personal data ourselves.

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. With your consent (and, on iOS, your tracking permission) ads may use the advertising identifier; you can decline and still use every lesson. Purchases are handled by Apple or Google; we receive no payment details. When you open a cited source, that website receives your request like any browser visit and its own privacy policy applies.

The app is intended for adults and older students; it is not directed to children under 13. On first launch the app asks whether you are 18 or older. If you answer that you are under 18, ads are non-personalised, limited to teen-suitable content and tagged for the under-age-of-consent setting, and the iPhone tracking prompt is not shown.
