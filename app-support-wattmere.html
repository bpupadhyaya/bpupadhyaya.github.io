Support

Questions, data corrections and subscription help: support@equalinformation.com. Include your device model and the app version.

Subscription

Wattmere Plus is a monthly subscription. To cancel or manage it, use Manage subscription in the app (More, or the Plus screen) or open your App Store or Google Play account settings. If you cancel, Plus stays active until the end of the month you paid for. Use Restore purchases in the app if a purchase does not show.

Your data

Everything you enter, import or capture stays on your device, so deleting the app deletes it. Export to PDF or CSV to keep a copy. The app works offline; only opening a cited source needs a connection.

Found a mistake?

Send the layer, region or source and what disagrees. Public data is indicative; confirm site-critical facts with utilities and authorities.
