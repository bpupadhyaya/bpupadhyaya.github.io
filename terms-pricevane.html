2. Pricevane Plus

Pricevane Plus is an optional auto-renewing monthly subscription priced at $2.99 per month (the local price is shown in the app), billed through your Apple or Google account. It adds units 4 to 6, the remaining graph labs and missions, full mock exams and more free-response sets; units 1 to 3, six graph labs, the Coverage screen and explanations after every answer remain free. There is no free trial and no introductory offer. The subscription renews each month until you cancel it in the subscription settings of your App Store or Google Play account; cancelling stops the next renewal and you keep Plus until the end of the period you have paid for. Use Restore purchases in the app if a purchase does not appear on a new device.
