Getting started

Take the short starting check, then follow the daily plan on the Today tab. Flashcards, drills in the sentence equivalence and text completion formats, interactive word labs and timed mini mocks help you build GRE vocabulary.

Credits and the ad-free plan

You start with 2 credits. Watch a short video to earn 1 more. Credits open mini mocks 2 to 6 and three advanced labs; practice, reviews, the mistake notebook and every full solution are always free. The ad-free monthly plan includes a 7-day free trial and can be cancelled in your Apple ID or Google Play subscription settings. Use Restore purchases on the plan screen after reinstalling.

No sound on a word?

Check that the phone is not on silent, the volume is up and Pronunciation sound is on in Me, Settings. Pronunciations are computer-generated clips.
