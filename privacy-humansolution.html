Privacy Policy — Human Solution

Effective Date: April 26, 2026

  • Home
  • /
  • Privacy Policy
  • /
  • Human Solution

1 Who We Are

Human Solution is provided by EqualInformation, LLC.

If you have privacy questions or requests, you may contact us at:
Email: bpupadhyaya@equalinformation.com
Contact page: equalinformation.weebly.com/contact.html | bpupadhyaya.github.io#contact

2 Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Human Solution app (iOS bundle identifier: com.equalinformation.anything; Android package name: com.anything.app). Human Solution is a composite super app that includes multiple modules covering health, wellness, legal, auto, and home topics. Each module operates under this same privacy policy.

3 What Human Solution Is

Human Solution is a personal assistant super app that brings together multiple specialized modules in one place:

  • PlainHealth — Health education and literacy
  • CareCircle — Caregiver coordination
  • ChronoTrack — Chronic condition tracking
  • Kindly — Mental wellness companion
  • MediSight — Medicine identification
  • PlainDental — Dental health education
  • PlainLaw — Legal rights education
  • PlainAuto — Vehicle maintenance guide
  • RepairMate — Home repair guides

Additional modules may be added over time. All modules follow the same privacy principles described in this policy.

4 Information Human Solution May Access or Store

Depending on which modules you use, Human Solution may store information on your device, including:

  • Health tracking data (symptoms, vitals, medications, check-ins)
  • Caregiver and care receiver information (names, tasks, schedules)
  • Mental wellness entries (mood logs, journal entries, breathing exercises)
  • Vehicle information (year, make, model, service history)
  • Home repair progress and tool inventory
  • Legal checklists, evidence notes, and document drafts
  • App preferences and settings per module
  • In-app purchase status (premium unlock state)
  • Module installation preferences

Important: All of this information is stored exclusively on your device. Human Solution does not transmit, upload, or sync any of this data to external servers.

5 How We Use Information

Human Solution uses information to:

  • Provide the functionality of each module you use
  • Display appropriate content based on purchase status (free vs premium)
  • Remember which modules you have installed or removed
  • Maintain your preferences and progress within each module

6 Local-First Design

Human Solution does not collect, transmit, or store any user data on servers. All data is stored locally on your device using SwiftData (iOS) or Room/SharedPreferences (Android). There is no cloud sync, no user accounts, and no user tracking. Human Solution does not require an internet connection to function (except for initial module download on Android via Google Play Feature Delivery, and in-app purchase verification).

7 Module Download (Android)

On Android, Human Solution uses Google Play Dynamic Feature Delivery. When you choose to install a module, it is downloaded from Google Play. When you remove a module, it is uninstalled from your device. Google Play may process download-related metadata under its own privacy policy. No personal data from the app is sent to Google during this process.

8 Module Content (iOS)

On iOS, Human Solution may use Apple's On-Demand Resources to download module content when you first open a module. You can free up space by removing module content from within the app. Apple may process download-related metadata under its own privacy policy. No personal data from the app is sent to Apple during this process.

9 No Analytics or Tracking

Human Solution does not use any analytics SDKs, crash reporters, or tracking tools. No data is sent to third-party analytics services. No advertising identifiers are collected.

10 App Store and Google Play Billing

Human Solution may use Apple App Store In-App Purchase (on iOS) or Google Play Billing (on Android) for eligible in-app purchase functionality. Apple or Google may process payment and transaction information under their own respective policies. Human Solution may receive limited purchase-status or product information needed to unlock purchased content on your device. EqualInformation, LLC does not receive your full payment card number through Human Solution.

11 Health and Care Information

Several modules within Human Solution allow you to record health-related data such as symptoms, vital signs, medications, mood, and wellness check-ins. This information is stored exclusively on your device and is never transmitted to external servers. Human Solution is not a medical device and is not intended to replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

12 Legal Information

The PlainLaw module provides legal education content. It is not legal advice and does not create an attorney-client relationship. Always consult a qualified attorney for legal matters.

13 Permissions

Human Solution may request the following device permissions:

  • Billing (Android): Used only for in-app purchase verification
  • Camera (if MediSight module is used): Used only for pill identification scanning, processed on-device

Human Solution does not require location, contacts, microphone, or other sensitive permissions for core functionality.

14 Sharing and Sale of Data

EqualInformation, LLC does not sell personal or sensitive user data. We do not share users' data with data brokers or for advertising purposes. The company does not intentionally transmit personal or sensitive Human Solution user data to its own servers or unrelated third parties.

15 Data Retention and Deletion

All data in Human Solution is stored locally on your device. Purchase-related unlock state may be restorable through App Store or Google Play for eligible purchases. Uninstalling Human Solution removes all locally stored data from your device, including all module data, preferences, and records. On Android, removing an individual module removes that module's data from your device.

16 Security

Human Solution relies on iOS and Android platform protections and app sandboxing as part of its local-first design. Each module's data is stored within the app's private sandbox and is not accessible to other apps. However, no method of storage or electronic transmission is completely secure, and the company cannot guarantee absolute security.

17 Your Choices

You may choose which modules to install and use. You may purchase premium content or not. You control what information you enter into each module. Uninstalling the app or individual modules removes all associated locally stored data from your device.

18 Children's Privacy

Human Solution is intended for adults. It is not designed for or directed at children under 13. Human Solution does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe information has been provided inappropriately, please contact the company.

19 Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, including when new modules are added. When we do, we will post the updated version on this page and revise the Effective Date above.
