Terms: Math Grade 5: Brisklet

Applies to the Brisklet app. Written 27 November 2026.

What Brisklet is

Brisklet is practice material for Grade 5 mathematics, with a story world to explore. It is a learning aid to use alongside school or home teaching. It is not a school, a teacher or an exam, and it cannot promise any result in a test.

Free and paid parts

About half of each view is open to everyone, with no purchase. Two monthly plans open the rest: the Survey Log plan and the Expedition plan. They are separate, and you may buy either or both. What was open before you bought stays open if a plan ends.

How a plan is billed

A plan is a monthly subscription. It renews each month, at the price the store shows when you buy, until it is cancelled in the store's subscription settings. There is no free trial and no introductory price. The Apple App Store or Google Play takes payment from the account signed in on the device and sets the exact renewal and cancellation steps.

When a plan is cancelled it will not renew again, and it stays open until the end of the period already paid for. Refunds are decided by the store under its own rules and by the law where you live; ask the store.

Restoring a plan

The same store account can restore a plan on another device: open the grown-ups’ area, then Settings, then “Restore plans bought with this store account”.

Using the app

You may use Brisklet on your own devices for your family’s learning. Please do not copy, resell or take apart the app, or use its content to build another product. The app, its artwork, questions and wording belong to Equal Information.

Accuracy

We work hard on the mathematics, but the questions are generated and the course has not yet been reviewed by a teacher, so a mistake is possible. Tell us at the address below and we will fix it. Progress shown in the app is an estimate for guidance and is not a grade.

We provide the app as it stands and cannot promise it will always run without interruption on every device. These terms do not take away any right you have under consumer law that cannot be waived.
