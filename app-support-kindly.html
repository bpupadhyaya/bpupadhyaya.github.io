App Support — Kindly

Get help with Kindly — your AI mental wellness companion.

  • App Support — Kindly

Kindly is an AI mental wellness companion available on iOS and Android. All data stays on your device — no accounts, no cloud sync, no tracking.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data private?

Yes. Kindly stores all data locally on your device. There are no accounts, no cloud sync, and no analytics. We never collect, transmit, or share any personal information. See our Privacy Policy for details.

What features are free?

The free tier includes 9 fully functional features: Mood Tracking, AI Chat, Journal, Breathing Exercises, Affirmations, Coping Toolkit, Gratitude Journal, Daily Check-In, and Crisis Safety Resources. These are not limited trials — they are complete, production-quality tools available forever at no cost.

What does Kindly Pro include?

Pro ($4.99/month or $39.99/year) unlocks: Mood Insights with analytics, Sleep Log, Habit Tracker, Worry Journal, Emotion Wheel, Weekly Reflection, Self-Care Planner, Mindfulness Timer, Data Export, and Safety Plan.

What does Kindly Clinical include?

Clinical ($19.99/month or $149.99/year) is designed for healthcare professionals and includes everything in Pro plus: Patient Dashboard, Clinical Notes, Outcome Tracking (PHQ-9, GAD-7, PCL-5, AUDIT), Session Management, Client Mood Overview, Treatment Plans, Progress Reports, and Institutional Analytics.

How do I restore my subscription on a new device?

iOS: Open Kindly → Settings → Restore Purchases. Your subscription is linked to your Apple ID and restores automatically.
Android: Open Kindly → Settings → Restore Purchases. Your subscription is linked to your Google account and restores automatically.

How do I cancel my subscription?

iOS: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → Kindly → Cancel.
Android: Google Play Store → Menu → Subscriptions → Kindly → Cancel.
Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period. You keep access until then.

Is Kindly a replacement for therapy?

No. Kindly is a wellness companion, not a substitute for professional mental health care. It uses evidence-based techniques (CBT, DBT, ACT) conversationally but does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or mental health condition. If you are experiencing a crisis, please contact a qualified professional or call 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline).

The app is crashing or not loading. What should I do?

Try these steps:
1. Force-close and reopen the app.
2. Restart your device.
3. Make sure you are running the latest version from the App Store or Google Play.
4. If the issue persists, contact us (see below) with your device model, OS version, and a description of the problem.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Data Export is available with Kindly Pro. Go to Data Export in the app to export your mood logs, journal entries, and other tracked data.
