GCSE Maths Prep: Algebrix: Privacy Policy

Algebrix is a GCSE maths revision app by EqualInformation, LLC. It has no account. Your progress (which topics you have practised and your answers) is stored only on your device and is never sent to us. The app shows ads through Google AdMob; AdMob may collect a device or advertising identifier, IP address and ad interaction data according to your consent choices. Users under 18 get non-personalised ads; users under 13 see no ads. You can change ad choices in More, Privacy and ad choices. Subscriptions are handled by Apple or Google; we do not see payment details. Contact: support@equalinformation.com.
