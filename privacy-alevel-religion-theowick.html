A Level Religion: Theowick: Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Last updated 2026-11-26.

1. Introduction and scope

This policy explains how EqualInformation, LLC (we, us) handles information in connection with A Level Religion: Theowick, version 1.0 and later (the app). It covers the app only. There is no account. Lessons, questions, labs, essay drafts, notes and study plans are stored on your device.

2. Information we collect

A. Information you choose to provide. Diagnostic reports: under More, Privacy and data, Send diagnostics shows the full content of the report before anything is sent, and it is sent only when you tap Send. Feedback and suggestions: a category, your message, an optional role, an optional rating and, only if you tick the contact option, an email address for a reply.

B. Diagnostic and app-usage information that is permissible under applicable law, collected with your agreement while the optional usage setting is on: app version and build; device model; operating system and version; processor and memory details; screen size; language, region and time zone; accessibility and display settings; performance and memory indicators; crash and error information; a random app identifier; which features and screens are used; and how learning features are used and perform: lessons viewed, questions attempted, whether answers are correct, time taken, progress estimates, adaptive-learning decisions and their outcomes, and activity in labs.

When you accept the first-run notice, a short acceptance record is always sent: a consent identifier, the time, the app, platform, version and build, the notice version and a hash of its text, the state of the usage setting, the random app identifier, and your country, language region and time zone.

C. We do not collect your name, contacts, precise or approximate location, photographs, files, advertising identifiers, or the text you write in the app, such as notes, mission answers and essay drafts, which stay on your device.

3. How we use information

To operate, maintain, secure and support the app; to diagnose problems; to provide and personalise the learning experience; to understand how the app is used; for research and development; for analytics and improvement of our apps and educational content; to read and act on feedback; and to comply with legal obligations.

4. Your choices

On first opening, a notice explains these practices and offers a separate usage switch. It starts off in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and wherever your region is unknown, until you turn it on. You can change the switch at any time under More, Privacy and data, where you can also request deletion of your usage data. Diagnostics and feedback are sent only when you choose to send them.

5. Storage and service providers

Information travels over encrypted (HTTPS) connections to a web application run by EqualInformation, LLC that records it in our Google Workspace services (Google Sheets and Drive); diagnostic reports and acceptance notices also generate a notification email. Subscription payments are handled by Apple or Google; we do not receive your payment details.

6. Retention

Usage, learning and diagnostic information is kept for as long as it remains useful for the purposes above; deletion requests are handled on request.

7. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures, including encryption in transit. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. International users and legal bases

Where a legal basis is required we rely on your agreement (the usage switch, and your decision to send diagnostics or feedback) and on our legitimate interest in operating and improving the app. Information may be processed in the United States.

9. Children

We design our apps to collect only the information described in this policy. If you believe information has been provided to us that you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the version of the first-run notice changes and the notice is shown again.

11. Contact

EqualInformation, LLC. Questions: support@equalinformation.com. Deletion and data requests: diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
