Zoneline support

Zoneline shows when people in different time zones are awake, ranks the best hours to talk, and makes a shareable card for the time you pick.

Getting started

Tap the plus button, search for a city, and optionally type the person's name. Drag the rail at the top of the board to move the time. Gold on the rail marks hours when everyone is awake.

Common questions

A time looks wrong. Zoneline uses your device's time zone data. Update iOS if a government changed its clock rules recently, and check the place you added is the right zone (open it from the board).

Someone sleeps at different hours. Tap their row, then change Wakes up, Goes to sleep and the work hours. Weekends follow each country's own calendar.

How do I remove ads? Open More, Go ad-free. You can watch short clips to earn ad-free time, or subscribe monthly. Restore purchases is on the same screen.

Cancel the subscription. iOS Settings, your name, Subscriptions, Zoneline.

Offline. Everything except ads and the subscription price works without a connection.
