Unitwick support

Unitwick is a fast offline unit converter for iPhone and iPad: length, mass, volume, temperature, speed, area, energy, data, cooking, fuel economy, pressure, power, time and angle.

Using it

  • Pick a converter from the ribbon, type on the keypad (sums such as 2 1/2 + 3 work), and every unit updates at once.
  • Tap a row to make it the target, press and hold a row to copy it, tap the star to keep a pair in Favourites.
  • A- and A+ change the text size in the app; Unitwick also follows your system text size.

Ad-free

Tap the crown to subscribe monthly, restore a purchase, or earn ad-free time by watching short videos. Manage or cancel in Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions.

Accuracy

Conversion factors are the exact definitions published by NIST (SP 811, Handbook 44) and the BIPM. The app lists its sources under More > Accuracy and sources. Found a wrong value? Email us with the units and the number you expected.
