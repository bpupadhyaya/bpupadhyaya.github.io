Privacy policy

Math Nursery: Pomwillow · EqualInformation, LLC · 27 November 2026

In short

Math Nursery: Pomwillow is a maths app that a grown-up and a child use together. The app sends nothing by itself. We do not receive your child's name, answers, progress or your notes, and the app has no accounts and no tracking. We receive something only if a grown-up chooses to send it from their own mail app.

What stays on your device

The app saves these in its own storage on your phone or tablet, so that it can choose what to offer next:

  • which pages and jobs have been played, and how they went
  • what the grown-up records about what the child could do
  • pins, text size, sound and other settings, and that the first-run notice was accepted

Marks drawn on the cloth are not saved. Your device's own backup may include app data, according to your device settings. You can erase progress in the grown-up area (Settings, then Hold to erase progress), or remove the app to remove its data.

What the app does not use

The app does not ask for the camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos. It has no typing fields for personal details, no chat, no social features and no notifications. Spoken words use a voice built into the device.

Monthly plans

If you buy a plan, the purchase is made through the App Store. Apple handles your payment under Apple's own privacy terms. The app asks the App Store only whether a plan is active on the account, and for its price. We do not receive your name, payment card or email from the purchase. Apple gives us sales and subscription reports that we use for accounting.

Feedback, diagnostics and other messages

In the grown-up area, Send feedback and Send diagnostics open a draft in your own mail app. You can read it, change it or discard it; nothing is sent unless you press send there. If you send it, or email us at support@equalinformation.com or diagnostics@equalinformation.com, we receive:

  • your email address and name as your mail app shows them
  • what you write (customer support messages and feedback); a feedback draft also carries the app version and build
  • for a diagnostics message, the technical details in the draft: app version and build, device model, system version, language and region, time zone, screen size and pixel ratio, text size, the voice, sounds, calm and spoken-words settings, whether the 3D drawing is working, which plans are active and the last error the app met

We use these only to answer you and to fix and improve the app, and not for tracking. They are held in our email service. Please do not send details that identify your child. Write to us to ask for our copy to be deleted.

Source pages

In a source window, Read the original here loads the cited organisation's own page over the internet. Like any website, that organisation receives ordinary technical details such as your internet address, under its own privacy terms. Scripts on the page are switched off in this window, nothing from it is kept, and its links cannot be followed.

This website

The links in the app open pages on this website in your browser, outside the app. The company that hosts the website may record ordinary technical details of a visit, such as an internet address, under its own terms.

Children

The app is made to be used by a child together with a grown-up. Purchases, outside links, settings and the privacy information are placed behind a question meant for grown-ups. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

If a later version handles information differently, this page and the app store listing will be updated first. Questions and requests: support@equalinformation.com.
