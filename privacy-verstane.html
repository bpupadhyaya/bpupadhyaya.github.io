Privacy Policy: A Level Sociology: Verstane

1. Introduction and scope

EqualInformation, LLC (we, us) publishes A Level Sociology: Verstane (the app). This policy sets out what information is handled when you use the app, version 1.0.0 onward. It covers the app only. The app has no user accounts. Contact: support@equalinformation.com. Last updated 2026-11-25.

2. Information you choose to send

Diagnostic report: under Me, Settings, Privacy and data you can choose Send diagnostics. The app displays the report before anything leaves your device and sends it only when you tap Send. Feedback: if you send feedback, a suggestion or a problem report, we receive its category, your message (up to 2,000 characters, with email addresses, telephone numbers and links removed automatically) and, when you supply them, your role, a source link and a rating. An email address is received only if you ask us to reply to you.

3. Diagnostic and usage information

Where the optional usage setting is on, or where the law allows, the app can send: app version and build; device model; operating system and version; processor and memory details; display size; language, region and time zone; text-size and accessibility settings; network type; whether the free or Plus entitlement is active; a random app identifier that is not an advertising identifier; session timing; the screens, lessons and labs you open and for how long; the practice questions you answer, whether each answer was correct, the time taken and the option chosen; topic progress estimates; the study suggestions the app makes and what you do next; how you use labs; interactions with the subscription screen; performance measures; and crash and error reports. When you accept the first-run notice a short acceptance record is always sent: a consent identifier, the time, the app, platform, version and build, the notice version and a hash of its text, the state of the usage setting, the random app identifier, and your country, locale and time zone.

4. Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, contacts, precise location, photographs, files or an advertising identifier. Notes and essay plans you write stay on your device and are not sent, apart from feedback you choose to submit.

5. How we use information

To operate, secure and support the app; to find and fix problems; to personalise your study path; to understand how the app is used; for research and development; to improve our apps and educational content; to read and act on feedback; and to meet legal obligations.

6. Your choices

At first launch a notice explains these practices and offers a separate usage switch. The switch is off by default in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and wherever your region cannot be determined, and on elsewhere. You can change it at any time under Me, Settings, Privacy and data. To ask for deletion of your usage data, use Delete my usage data in the app or write to diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

7. Storage and service providers

Information is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections to a web application operated by EqualInformation, LLC, which records it in Google Workspace services (Google Sheets and Drive). Diagnostic reports and acceptance notices are also delivered by email. Google acts as a service provider on our behalf. Subscription payments are handled by Apple or Google and we do not receive your payment details.

8. Retention and security

Usage, learning and diagnostic information is kept for as long as it remains useful for the purposes above; deletion requests are handled on request. We use reasonable technical and organisational measures, including encryption in transit. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

9. International users and legal bases

Where a legal basis is required we rely on your consent (the usage switch and your decision to send diagnostics or feedback) and on our legitimate interest in maintaining and improving the app. Information may be processed in the United States.

10. Children

We design our apps to collect only the information described in this policy. If you believe information has been provided to us that you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

11. Changes and contact

If we make a material change, the version of the first-run notice changes and the notice is shown again. Questions: support@equalinformation.com. Data and deletion requests: diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

Specification names and codes (AQA, OCR) and the Department for Education are used only to describe course content. This independent app is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by any of them and does not predict or guarantee any grade.
