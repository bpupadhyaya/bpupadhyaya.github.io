Data Center Course: Rackmere - Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Last updated: 2026-11-22.

What stays on your device

The app has no account and no server of ours. Your progress, quiz results, saved scorecards, age answer and text-size setting are stored only on your device. We collect no personal data. Lessons, labs, maps and the course data are bundled in the app and work offline.

Advertising

The free app shows ads through Google AdMob at natural breaks, and offers optional rewarded videos. With your consent (shown by a consent form where required) AdMob may use your device advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, advertising ID on Android) to show and measure ads, and only for adults. On iOS you can decline tracking when asked (App Tracking Transparency). Subscribers see no ads.

Source links

When you open a cited source the app loads that public web page in a window inside the app. That site receives your request like any web visit and applies its own privacy practices.

Purchases

The optional monthly subscription that removes ads is processed by Apple or Google. We receive no payment details.

Children

The app is a general-audience learning tool for ages 13 and up and is not directed to children under 13. On first launch it asks whether you are 18 or older; the answer stays on your device. If you answer that you are under 18, ads are non-personalised, no advertising identifier is used and the iPhone tracking prompt is not shown.
