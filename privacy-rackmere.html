Data Center Course: Rackmere - Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Last updated: 2026-11-23.

What stays on your device

The app has no account and no server of ours. Your progress, quiz results, saved scorecards and text-size setting are stored only on your device. We collect no personal data and the app does not track you. Lessons, labs, maps and the course data are bundled in the app and work offline.

Source links

When you open a cited source the app loads that public web page in a window inside the app. That site receives your request like any web visit and applies its own privacy practices.

Purchases

The optional monthly subscription, Rackmere Plus, is processed by Apple or Google. We receive no payment details.

Children

The app is a general-audience learning tool for ages 13 and up and is not directed to children under 13. It collects no personal data from anyone.
