Who this covers

The Wiremoot app for iPhone, iPad and Android is published by EqualInformation, LLC. You do not create an account or give a name to use it.

Kept on your device only

Your answers, strength estimates, pinned items, exam date, self-marks and settings stay on the device. Words typed into the search box in the app are never sent.

Optional usage sharing

The first screen asks whether usage records may be shared. The choice is shown with a switch and with the wording of the button you press. For devices set to a country in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, or to no known country, it begins switched off; elsewhere it begins switched on. You may flip it before agreeing, and later under More, then Privacy and data.

With sharing on, the app sends:

  • learning activity: which questions and cases you attempted, the option chosen, right or wrong, time taken, strength estimates, and which benches and papers you used;
  • app activity: screens, topics and sources opened, view and level choices, text size, plan changes, quiz and paywall events, and whether Plus is active;
  • faults: error messages and crash notes;
  • device and region facts: platform, app version and build, device model, system name and version, install channel, whether it runs in an emulator, language region, country setting and time zone;
  • a record of your agreement to the first-screen notice: its wording, the time, how long it stayed open and whether you scrolled to its end;
  • random numbers identifying this installation and this session.

The app does not read precise location, contacts, photos, files or an advertising identifier.

Reports you send yourself

A diagnostic report leaves only when you press Send, after a preview. It carries app and device facts, region and time zone, settings, a short log of recent actions, recorded faults and your optional note.

Feedback leaves only when you press Send: your message, the subject and role you chose and, if you type one, a reply address. Addresses, links and long numbers are cut from messages and notes before they leave the device.

A deletion request leaves when you press Request deletion. These three work with sharing off.

Where records are stored

Records travel over an encrypted connection to a collection service that we operate within Google Workspace, and are held there in our spreadsheets and files. Google handles them as our service provider.

For installs made through the App Store or Google Play, records of every kind above (usage, learning, agreement, diagnostics, crashes, feedback and requests) may also be sent to mailboxes we own, and that mail can be forwarded on to another of our mailboxes.

Apple or Google process subscription payments under their own terms. We never receive card details.

What we use them for

To run and protect the app, locate and repair faults, reply to you, learn which parts of the course help, and improve the material and the way it responds to each learner. Where a legal basis is required, usage records rest on your choice of the switch, and reports and requests on your choice to send them.

Keeping and deleting

Nothing is removed on a timer; records stay until we remove them by hand.

To request deletion, use Request deletion in the app or write to diagnostics@equalinformation.com. We handle requests manually, so they are not instant, and copies already mailed to our own mailboxes stay there. Switching sharing off stops new automatic records and drops those still queued on the device, but does not remove what we already hold. Uninstalling the app clears only what is stored on the device.

Your rights

Depending on where you live you may ask to see, correct, delete, restrict or receive a copy of information about you, object to its use, withdraw agreement, or complain to a data protection authority. With no account, we may need the installation number shown in the diagnostic preview to find your records. Records may be handled in the United States and elsewhere.

Families

The app is a revision aid for a school course. If a parent or guardian believes a child has sent us information that should go, write to support@equalinformation.com and we will act on it.

Changes and contact

A revised policy will replace this page with a new date. Questions: EqualInformation, LLC, support@equalinformation.com.
