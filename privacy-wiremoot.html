Privacy Policy for IB Digital Society: Wiremoot

EqualInformation, LLC. Effective 27 November 2026.

Scope

This policy covers the Wiremoot app for iPhone, iPad and Android, published by EqualInformation, LLC. The app has no account and asks for no name.

What stays on your device

Your progress, strength estimates, pins, exam date, self-marks and settings are stored on your device. Words you type into the in-app search are not sent anywhere.

Usage records, controlled by a switch

The first screen shows a switch for sharing usage records. It starts off for devices set to a country in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, or where no country is known, and starts on elsewhere. You can see it before you agree and change it at any time under More, Privacy and data.

While it is on, the app sends:

  • learning activity: the questions and cases attempted, the option chosen, whether it was correct, time taken, strength estimates for course points, and benches and practice papers used;
  • app activity: screens, topics and sources opened, view and level choices, text size, plan changes, quiz and paywall events, and whether Plus is active;
  • faults: error messages and crash notes;
  • device and region details: platform, app version and build, device model, system name and version, how the app was installed, whether it runs on an emulator, language region, country setting and time zone;
  • a record that you accepted the notice, with its text, the time, how long it was open and whether you reached its end;
  • random identifiers for this installation and this session.

No precise location, contacts, photos, files or advertising identifier is read.

Things you choose to send

A diagnostic report goes only when you press Send, after a preview. It holds app and device details, region and time zone, settings, a short log of recent actions in the app, recorded faults and your optional note.

Feedback goes only when you press Send. It holds your message, the topic and role you picked and, only if you enter one, a reply address. Addresses, links and long numbers are removed from the message and note before they leave the device.

A deletion request goes when you press Request deletion. These three work even when the sharing switch is off.

Where records go and who handles them

Records are sent over an encrypted connection to a collection service we run on Google Workspace, and are kept in our spreadsheets and files there. Google processes them as our service provider.

Records from an installation made through the App Store or Google Play, including usage and learning records, consent records, diagnostic reports, crash notes, feedback and requests, can also be emailed to our own mailboxes, and that mail may be forwarded to another mailbox of ours.

Subscriptions are processed by Apple or Google under their own policies. We do not receive your payment card details.

Why we use them

To run and protect the app, find and fix faults, answer you, understand which parts help learners, and improve the course material and the way it adapts. Where the law asks for a legal basis, usage records rest on your agreement through the switch, and reports and requests on your decision to send them.

How long, and deletion

Nothing is deleted automatically. We keep records until we remove them by hand.

To ask for deletion, use Request deletion in the app, or write to diagnostics@equalinformation.com. We act by hand, so it is not immediate. Copies already emailed to our mailboxes remain there. Turning the switch off stops new automatic records and discards those still waiting on the device; it does not remove records already received. Removing the app erases what is stored on the device only.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or a copy of information about you, object to its use, withdraw agreement, or complain to a data protection authority. Because there is no account, we may need the installation identifier shown in the diagnostic preview to find your records. Records may be processed in the United States and other countries.

Children and families

The app is a study aid for a school course. A parent or guardian who believes a child has sent us information that should be removed can write to support@equalinformation.com and we will deal with it.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version appears here with a new date. Questions go to EqualInformation, LLC at support@equalinformation.com.
