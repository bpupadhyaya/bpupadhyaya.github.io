Privacy Policy: CS Principles AP: Bitfern

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Contact: support@equalinformation.com. Last updated 2026-11-27.

1. Introduction and scope

This Privacy Policy explains how EqualInformation, LLC ("we", "us") handles information in connection with CS Principles AP: Bitfern (the "app"), version 1.0.0 and later. It applies to the app only. The app has no account system.

2A. Information you choose to provide

In Settings, Privacy and data, you may choose Send diagnostics; the app shows you the content first and sends it only after you tap Send. If you send feedback or a suggestion, we receive its category, your message (up to 2,000 characters, with email addresses, telephone numbers and links removed automatically) and, if you provide them, a role, context and a rating. We receive an email address only if you choose to let us contact you.

2B. Diagnostic and app-usage information

While the optional usage setting is on (or where the law permits), the app can send: app version and build; device model; operating system and version; processor, graphics and memory characteristics; screen size; language, region and time zone; text-size and accessibility display settings; network type; a battery-level range; whether the free or Plus entitlement is active; a randomly generated app identifier (not an advertising identifier); session timing; screens and labs opened and for how long; lesson activity; practice-question attempts (the question, its learning objective and topic, whether the answer was correct, time taken and option chosen); learning-objective progress estimates; the study suggestions made and what followed; lab control settings; subscription-screen interactions; performance indicators; and crash and error information. When you accept the first-run notice, a short acceptance record is always sent: a consent identifier, the time, app, platform, version and build, the notice version and a hash of its text, the state of the usage setting, the random app identifier, and your country, locale and time zone.

2C. Information we do not collect

The app does not collect your name, contacts, precise location, photographs or files, an advertising identifier, or other text you enter in it, except feedback you choose to send. Your progress, plan and settings are stored on your device.

3. How we use information

To operate, maintain, secure and support the app; to diagnose problems; to personalise the learning experience; to understand how the app is used; for research and development and to improve our apps and educational content; to read and act on feedback; and to comply with legal obligations.

4. Your choices

The first-run notice explains the optional usage setting, which is off by default in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland until you turn it on. You can change it at any time under Settings, Privacy and data. You can send diagnostics and feedback only when you choose. To request deletion, use Delete my usage data in the app or write to diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

5. Storage and service providers

Information is stored in our Google Workspace services (Google Sheets and Drive) and notification email, and processed by service providers acting on our behalf. The mail part includes record contents. A Bitfern from the App Store or Google Play may have its acceptance record, usage batches, crashes, diagnostic reports, feedback and deletion requests emailed to us, to mailboxes that can forward to one another and are all ours. It is transmitted over encrypted connections. Subscription payments are handled by the store you use; we do not receive your payment details.

6. Retention

Usage and learning information is kept for as long as it is useful for the purposes above; deletion requests are worked through by hand. Records are not removed automatically, and a copy already in our email stays unless deleted there too.

7. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. International users and legal bases

Where applicable we rely on your consent or on our legitimate interests in operating and improving the app. Information may be processed in the United States.

9. Children

We design our apps to collect only the information described in this policy. If you believe information has been provided to us that you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

10. Changes to this policy

If we make a material change, the version of the first-run notice changes and it is shown again.

11. Contact

EqualInformation, LLC, support@equalinformation.com. Deletion and data requests: diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
