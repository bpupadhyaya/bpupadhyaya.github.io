EqualInformation is built and maintained by a single developer — not a corporation, not a venture-backed startup. Every app is crafted with care, backed by science, and offered free of charge because we believe powerful tools for health and well-being should be accessible to everyone.

Behind every feature, every update, and every platform release is one engineer dedicating his time and expertise to make a meaningful difference in people's lives. Your support helps sustain this mission and ensures these applications can continue to grow and improve.

How Your Support Helps

  • Continued development of new features and improvements across iOS, Android, and Web
  • Keeping all essential features free for every user, everywhere
  • Covering infrastructure costs including development tools, testing devices, and app store fees
  • Building new intelligent applications for health, finance, and daily life
  • Maintaining privacy-first, ad-free experiences across all platforms

All essential features in our apps are free and will always remain free. Some apps may offer optional advanced features through in-app purchases.
