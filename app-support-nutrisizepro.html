App Support — Nutrisize Pro

Get help with Nutrisize Pro — your comprehensive nutrition, exercise, and health tracking companion.

  • Home
  • /
  • App Support — Nutrisize Pro

Nutrisize Pro is a comprehensive nutrition, exercise, and health tracking app available on iOS and Android. It features five major sections — MyHealth, Nutrition, Exercise, System, and Nutrisize — with personalized plans, extensive food and exercise databases, and physiology tracking. All data stays on your device.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data private?

Yes. Nutrisize Pro stores all health data locally on your device. There are no accounts and no cloud sync. We never collect, transmit, or share any personal information. See our Privacy Policy for details.

What features are free?

The free tier includes: basic health parameter tracking and history, nutrition types browser with knowledge base, exercise types browser with age-based guidance and knowledge base, physiology overview, diagnostics, zoom accessibility, feedback, and full privacy and data management controls.

What does Premium include?

Nutrisize Pro offers four one-time purchase tiers:
• Physiology Complete — Unlocks all 201+ physiology parameters and disease maps with physician-grade content and clinical reference ranges.
• Nutrition Premium — Unlocks 5,000+ foods from the USDA database, personalized meal plans, and full nutrient tracking.
• Exercise Premium — Unlocks the full exercise library with form cues, age modifications, recovery plans, and mental health exercise prescriptions.
• Nutrisize Premium (Bundle) — Unlocks everything above plus all future premium features. Buy once, access forever.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

iOS: Open Nutrisize Pro → System → Upgrade to Premium → Restore Purchases. Your purchase is linked to your Apple ID and restores automatically.
Android: Open Nutrisize Pro → System → Upgrade to Premium → Restore Purchases. Your purchase is linked to your Google account and restores automatically.

Does the app require internet?

No. Nutrisize Pro is fully offline. All health data, food databases, exercise guides, and physiology content are bundled with the app. No internet connection is required for any feature.

What is the MDConnect feature?

MDConnect is designed to help you connect with healthcare providers by preparing health summaries from your tracked data. It helps you have more informed conversations with your doctor.

The app is crashing or not loading. What should I do?

Try these steps:
1. Force-close and reopen the app.
2. Restart your device.
3. Make sure you are running the latest version from the App Store or Google Play.
4. If the issue persists, contact us (see below) with your device model, OS version, and a description of the problem.

Is Nutrisize Pro a replacement for medical advice?

No. Nutrisize Pro provides health and nutrition information for educational purposes only. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. Always consult a licensed healthcare provider for medical advice.
