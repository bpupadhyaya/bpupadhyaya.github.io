Tabwick support

Tabwick splits restaurant bills, tax and tip between friends and settles group costs with the fewest payments.

Common questions

Why does one person pay a cent more? Some totals do not divide evenly. The extra cents are given out one at a time by the largest remainder method, rotating between people, so the shares add up to the exact total.

How do I remove ads? Open More, then Go ad-free. You can subscribe monthly or earn ad-free time by watching short videos. Manage or cancel the subscription in your Apple ID settings.

Is my data uploaded? No. Bills and groups stay on your device. See the Privacy Policy.

Text too small? Use the A- and A+ buttons at the top of any screen, or your system text size.
