Chorewick Support

Chorewick: Family Chore Wheel is a spinning 3D wheel that hands out household chores fairly, tracks points on a weekly leaderboard and lets you set prizes. Everything works offline.

Common questions

How does the wheel decide who gets the chore? The wheel picks the chore. The chore goes to the person with the lightest week so far: points earned this week plus open jobs count. Ties go to whoever has waited longest. You can change the person on the result card.

When does a week start? It follows your device calendar and region.

The text is too small. Use the A- and A+ buttons at the top of any screen, or the Text size row in More. The app also follows your system text size.

How do I remove ads? More, then Go ad-free: a monthly subscription, or earn ad-free time by watching rewarded videos. Restore purchases is on the same screen. Cancel any time in your Apple Account settings.

Do you store my family's information? No. It stays on your device. See the privacy policy.
