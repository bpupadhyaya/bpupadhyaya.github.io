Get help with Insurance Hub — every insurance, one hub, the honest price.

Insurance Hub is AI-native insurance intelligence for the USA. It compares every kind of insurance — auto, home, renters, health, life, flood, pet, motorcycle, umbrella, crop, workers’ comp, travel, and more — using prices computed from what carriers legally file with state regulators. Evidence, not ads. Nothing about you is stored.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where do the prices come from?

Every price is computed from public, authoritative regulatory data: state rate filings (SERFF), state Departments of Insurance, CMS / Healthcare.gov for ACA marketplace plans, OpenFEMA / NFIP for flood, USDA RMA for crop, and NAIC reports. Every price links to the public filing behind it, so you can verify the evidence yourself.

Are these real quotes?

They are comparative prices computed from carriers’ filed rates — not partner bids, not ads, and not binding quotes. Your final premium is always determined by the carrier based on your specific situation. Use Insurance Hub to see the honest landscape first, then confirm with the carrier or a licensed agent.

Is anything about me stored?

No. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, and no personal questions. Your comparison selections are processed in your browser and never stored on our servers. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How fresh are the rates?

Prices are refreshed as carriers file changes with regulators. Each price shows its source filing and effective date, so you always know exactly which filed rate you are looking at.

Which insurance domains are covered?

Auto, home, renters, health (ACA marketplace, county by county), life, flood (NFIP and private), pet, motorcycle, umbrella, crop, workers’ compensation, and travel — with disability, title, and business insurance in the hub as well. Start from the full landscape, then drill down to the coverage, state, and situation that matter to you.

When will the iOS and Android apps be available?

The apps are coming soon. They will include the same domains as the website with the complete provider list. Check the Insurance Hub site for availability updates.

A price or fact looks wrong. How do I report it?

Please email us (see below) with the domain, state, carrier, and the filing reference shown next to the price. Because every number traces to a public filing, corrections can be verified against the source and shipped quickly.
