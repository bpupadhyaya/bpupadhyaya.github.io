Support Nutrisize Health

Help us build advanced health intelligence accessible to everyone.

  • Support Nutrisize Health

Nutrisize Health brings advanced health intelligence to everyone — personalized nutrition, exercise, and physiology tracking with 4,995 foods, 5,404 exercises, and 200+ physiological parameters across 16 body systems.

Nutrisize Health is built and maintained by a single developer who believes that comprehensive health tools should be accessible to everyone — from farmers to physicians. Every feature is designed with privacy at its core: all health data stays on your device. No cloud servers, no accounts, no tracking.

The app provides science-backed guidance referencing WHO, ACSM, and NASEM standards, with individualized nutrition and exercise plans, KPI dashboards covering 10 health dimensions, and multi-language support. Your support helps us continue improving the app and reaching more people around the world.
