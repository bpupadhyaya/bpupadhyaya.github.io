Privacy Policy: A Level English Lit: Proselind

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Contact: support@equalinformation.com. Last updated 2026-11-27.

1. Introduction and scope

This Privacy Policy explains how EqualInformation, LLC ("we", "us") handles information in connection with A Level English Lit: Proselind (the "app"), version 1.0.0 and later. It applies to the app only. The app has no account system.

2A. Information you choose to provide

Diagnostic reports: in Settings, Privacy and data, you may choose Send diagnostics. The app shows you the content first and sends it only after you tap Send. Feedback and suggestions: if you send feedback, a suggestion or a problem report, we receive its category, your message (up to 2,000 characters, with email addresses, telephone numbers and links removed automatically) and, if you provide them, a role, context, a source link and a rating. We receive an email address only if you choose to let us contact you about your feedback.

2B. Diagnostic and app-usage information

While the optional usage setting is on (or where applicable law permits), the app can send: app version and build; device model; operating system and version; processor and memory details; screen size; language, region and time zone; text-size setting and a summary of accessibility and display settings; network type; battery-level range; whether the free or Plus entitlement is active; a randomly generated app identifier (not an advertising identifier); session timing; the screens and labs you open and for how long; lesson activity such as scroll depth and time; practice-question and mock attempts (the question, its topic, whether the answer was correct, the time taken and the option chosen); topic-level progress estimates; the study suggestions the app makes and what follows them; use of labs; purchase-screen interactions; and crash and error information. A short acceptance record is sent when you accept the first-run notice.

2C. Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, contacts, precise location, photographs, files or advertising identifiers.

3. How we use information

To operate, maintain, secure and support the app; to diagnose problems; to provide and personalise the learning experience; to understand how the app is used; for research and development; for analytics and improvement of our apps and educational content; to read and act on feedback; and to comply with legal obligations.

4. Your choices

The first-run notice; the usage-information switch under Settings, Privacy and data (off by default in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland until you turn it on); sending diagnostics and feedback only when you choose; and deletion requests through the in-app Delete my usage data request or by email to diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

5. Storage and service providers

Information is stored in our Google Workspace services (Google Sheets and Drive) and notification email, and processed by service providers acting on our behalf. Notification mail for store-installed Proselind can hold writing-course activity batches and usage records, and also the acceptance, diagnostics, crashes, feedback and deletion requests. It lands in our mailboxes and may be forwarded to another mailbox in our own hands. It is transmitted over encrypted connections. Subscriptions are processed by the platform store; we do not receive your payment details.

6. Retention

Usage and learning information is kept while it is useful for the purposes above. Removing records after a request is done by hand and not by any automatic function. Whatever was emailed stays in email unless somebody deletes it there as well.

7. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. International users and legal bases

Where applicable we rely on your consent or on our legitimate interests. Information may be processed in the United States.

9. Children

We design our apps to collect only the information described in this policy. If you believe information has been provided to us that you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

10. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, the first-run notice version changes and is shown again.

11. Contact

EqualInformation, LLC, support@equalinformation.com. Deletion and data requests: diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
