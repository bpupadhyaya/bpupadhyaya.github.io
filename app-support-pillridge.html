Pillridge: Drug Class Cards, Support

Pillridge teaches drug classes, name stems and mechanisms with flashcards, drills and real 3D protein structures. It is a study aid only and gives no dosing, diagnosis or treatment advice.

Common questions

  • Text too small or large? Use A- and A+ at the top of any screen, or change the system text size in iOS Settings, Display & Brightness, Text Size.
  • How do I remove ads? Open More, then Go ad-free: subscribe monthly, or watch a short video to earn ad-free time. Use Restore purchases if you changed devices.
  • Cancel the subscription? iOS Settings, your name, Subscriptions, Pillridge.
  • Does it work offline? Yes. Lessons, cards, drills and 3D structures are inside the app.
  • Where do the 3D structures come from? The RCSB Protein Data Bank (public domain). See More, Sources and attributions.
  • Found a mistake? Tell us the class and the line and we will review it.
