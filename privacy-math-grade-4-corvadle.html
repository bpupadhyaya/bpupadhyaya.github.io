Math Grade 4: Corvadle is a maths practice app that children use. This page says what it keeps, what it shares and who to ask.

Short version

The app does not collect anything about your child. It has no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no microphone, camera or location use, and it never sends progress or answers anywhere.

What stays on the device

Your child’s progress, settings and the choices made in builds are saved in the app’s own storage on the phone or tablet. Deleting the app, or using “Erase all progress” in the grown-ups area, removes them. Your phone’s own backup may copy app data if you have backup switched on; that is between you and the company that provides the backup.

What is sent out

Nothing about your child. When a grown-up buys a monthly plan, your app store takes the payment and tells the app only which plan is active. We do not receive card details, names or email addresses from the store through the app.

Children

The learner screens ask for no personal details and have no links, chat or purchase prices. Anything involving money, outside pages or the plan screen sits behind a check meant for grown-ups. That check is not proof of age.

Reading aloud

Read aloud uses a voice already installed on the device and works without a connection.

Your rights

We hold no personal data about you or your child, so there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. You can see everything the app keeps in its Progress tab. For any question, write to bpupadhyaya@equalinformation.com.

Store services

Your app store and your device maker may record downloads, crashes and purchases under their own settings and policies.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes. A change that affects children’s data would be shown in the app before it takes effect.
