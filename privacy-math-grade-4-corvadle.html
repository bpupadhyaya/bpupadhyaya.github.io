Math Grade 4: Corvadle is a maths practice app that children use. This page says what it keeps, what it shares and who to ask.

Short version

The app sends nothing on its own. It has no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no microphone, camera or location use, and it never sends progress or answers anywhere. A grown-up may choose to email us feedback or a diagnostic report.

What stays on the device

Your child’s progress, settings and the choices made in builds are saved in the app’s own storage on the phone or tablet. Deleting the app, or using “Erase all progress” in the grown-ups area, removes them. Your phone’s own backup may copy app data if you have backup switched on; that is between you and the company that provides the backup.

Feedback and diagnostic reports a grown-up chooses to send

The grown-ups area has Send feedback and Send diagnostics. Each prepares a draft in your own mail app, addressed to diagnostics@equalinformation.com. You can read, change or discard it, and nothing leaves the device unless you press Send.

If you send it, we receive your email address and what the message says. An email you choose to send also shows whatever sender name your mail account uses. A diagnostic report lists: the app version, device model and system version, language and time zone, window and text size, the app’s sound and display settings, which plans are active, whether the 3D picture works and the last few error messages. It holds no answers, progress or child’s name. Please leave personal details about children out of anything you write.

We use these messages only to reply, to fix faults and to improve the app. We do not use them for tracking or advertising profiles. We keep them only as long as needed for those purposes, and delete them when you ask.

What else is sent out

Nothing about your child on the app’s own initiative. When a grown-up buys a monthly plan, your app store takes the payment and tells the app only which plan is active. We do not receive card details, names or email addresses from the store through the app.

Cited source pages

In the grown-ups area, behind the adult check, a grown-up can open the official pages the app cites (the Department for Education, the California Department of Education, the World Health Organization and the American Academy of Pediatrics). The page is read from that organisation’s own website inside the app. As with any website visit, that website receives your internet address and device type and applies its own privacy policy. The app switches scripts off, blocks other companies’ pictures and requests, keeps no cookies or history from the visit, and adds nothing about you or your child. We receive nothing from these visits.

Children

The learner screens ask for no personal details and have no links, chat or purchase prices. Anything involving money, outside pages or the plan screen sits behind a check meant for grown-ups. That check is not proof of age.

Reading aloud

Read aloud uses a voice already installed on the device and works without a connection.

Your rights

We hold no data about you unless you have written to us. You may ask to see, correct or delete a message you sent by writing to diagnostics@equalinformation.com. You can see everything the app keeps about learning in its Progress tab. For any other question, write to bpupadhyaya@equalinformation.com.

Store services

Your app store and your device maker may record downloads, crashes and purchases under their own settings and policies.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes. A change that affects children’s data would be shown in the app before it takes effect.
