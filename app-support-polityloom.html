Polityloom help

Write to us

support@equalinformation.com. Name your device, its system version and the screen you were on. In the app, More, then Privacy and data, can also send a fault report or a message.

Two ways through the course

Use the Syllabus and Real world control at the top of the main screens. Your estimates, pins and plan stay with you when you switch.

Bigger or smaller text

Press A- or A+ at the top of a screen.

Plus: restore or cancel

Open More. Restore purchases brings a subscription back; Manage subscription opens the store page where you can cancel.

A source stays blank

Sources are web pages, so check your connection. On Android a PDF source shows the publisher's course page and a button that opens the PDF.

Removing usage records

Open More, then Privacy and data, and ask for deletion, or write to diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
