Cytowell Privacy Policy

Effective 8 November 2026. Publisher: Equal Information LLC.

What stays on your device

Cytowell is a cell biology study app. Your quiz history, flashcard schedule, text size and explored organelles are stored only on your device. There are no accounts and we cannot see this information. The app works fully offline.

Ads

The free app shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device advertising identifier, approximate location and basic interaction data to choose and measure ads. On iOS the app first asks whether it may track you across other companies' apps and websites, and where the law requires it shows a Google consent form so you can choose how ad data is used. You can decline and still use everything; ads are then less personalised. See policies.google.com/technologies/ads. You can turn ads off with the optional subscription or by watching a video to earn ad-free time.

Subscription

The optional Cytowell Ad-free monthly subscription is processed entirely by Apple. We receive no payment details. Manage or cancel it in your Apple ID subscription settings.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect names, emails, health data or contacts.
  • We do not sell personal data.
  • Cytowell contains no artificial intelligence features and sends no study data to any server.

Children

Cytowell is a general-audience education app and is not directed to children under 13.

Education only

Cytowell teaches cell biology. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.
