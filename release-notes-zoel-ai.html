Zoel AI is under active development. This page documents each production release with what changed, what's new, and what to expect.

These release notes are maintained by AI agents. While we strive for accuracy, limitations may apply.

v1.0.0 Stable Current June 2026

Initial release of Zoel AI — your private AI that runs entirely on your device. Live on the App Store and Google Play. Voice-first, 100% offline, multilingual, with fifteen knowledge domains (Health free forever).

What's included

  • 100% on-device AI — no accounts, no servers, no tracking; your data never leaves your device
  • Voice-first conversation with multilingual support
  • Fifteen knowledge domains (Health free forever; 14 additional gated domains): Safety, Finance, Work, Legal, Relationships, Family, Home Life, Travel, Productivity, Civic, Spiritual, Learning, Legacy, Companion
  • On-device chat works in every module — only each module's knowledge library is gated
  • Optional Bring-Your-Own-Key cloud models, off by default
  • Optional internet enrichment with in-app citation browser, off by default
  • Zoel Free — the Health module plus Zoel conversation in every module
  • Zoel Select ($4.99 one-time) — Health plus any 4 of the 14 paid domains, forever
  • Zoel Unlimited ($4.99/mo or $49.99/yr) — all domains, current and future
  • Entitlements checked locally (StoreKit 2 on iOS, Play Billing on Android) so gating works fully offline
  • StoreKit 2 (iOS) and Google Play Billing (Android) integration
  • Full cross-platform feature parity between iOS and Android
