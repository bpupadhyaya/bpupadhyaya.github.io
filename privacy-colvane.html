Ivy League Admissions: Colvane: Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Contact: support@equalinformation.com. Last updated 2026-11-22.

What stays on your device

Your progress, quiz answers, school lists, plan choices, checklist ticks, essay drafts, notes, text-size setting and age band are stored only on your device. The app has no accounts and no server that receives this data. Deleting the app erases it. We do not collect your name, email address or contact details.

Age band and children (COPPA)

On first launch the app asks whether the user is an adult (18 or older), a teen (13 to 17) or a child under 13 (a parent chooses). The answer is stored only on the device and decides advertising: under 13, the app shows no advertising and requests no tracking permission; the app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ages 13 to 17 receive only non-personalised ads, limited to teen-appropriate content, with no tracking prompt. Adults are asked for permission before tracking across other companies' apps, and where the law requires it Google shows a consent form.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner, occasional full-screen ads at natural breaks, and an optional rewarded video that turns ads off for 30 minutes. AdMob may use device and usage data to select and measure ads under Google's own privacy policy.

Subscription

The optional monthly plan only removes ads. Purchases are processed by Apple or Google; we never see payment details. Cancel any time in your store account settings and keep the benefit until the paid period ends.

Sources you open

Source links open public web pages inside the app. Those sites apply their own privacy practices.
