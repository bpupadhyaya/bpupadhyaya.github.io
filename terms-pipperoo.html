What the app is

Pipperoo is practice for early number, shape, pattern and measuring ideas. It is not a test, a school assessment or a judgement of a child, and it does not promise any result. A grown-up should be near, and real objects and talk away from the screen matter at least as much as the app. No teacher has reviewed the content yet.

Free part and plans

About half of the skills in each of the two ways of playing are free, with no time limit. The Counting Mat plan (US$2.99 a month) opens the rest of the Counting Mat. The Maker Jobs plan (US$9.99 a month) opens the rest of Maker Jobs. The two are separate. Prices differ by country; the app shows the App Store price before you confirm.

Renewal and cancelling

Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm. A plan renews every month at the price shown until you cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current month to stop the next charge, in your device Settings, under your name, then Subscriptions. You keep the plan until the month you paid for ends. There is no free trial. Refunds are decided by Apple.
