IB Biology Prep: Genemere: Support

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Contact: support@equalinformation.com. Last updated 2026-11-24.

Support

Questions, content corrections and subscription help: support@equalinformation.com. Please include your device model, the app version and the question number or screen.

Send diagnostics

Open Settings, Privacy and data and choose Send diagnostics. The app shows you exactly what the report contains; nothing is sent until you tap Send. The report is delivered to diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

Feedback and suggestions

Use Feedback and suggestions under Settings, the thumbs and Report a problem controls under answers and lessons, or Suggest content. Nothing is sent until you tap Send. An email address is included only if you tick that we may contact you about it.

Delete my usage data

In the app, open Settings, Privacy and data and choose Delete my usage data, or email diagnostics@equalinformation.com with your request.

Subscription

Manage or cancel Genemere Plus in your App Store or Google Play account settings. Use Restore purchases on the Genemere Plus screen if a purchase does not appear.

Found an error?

Send the topic and question and, if you can, a reliable source that disagrees. Each cited source opens inside the app.
