Funcrest: AP Precalculus - Support

Help with the Funcrest AP Precalculus exam prep app.

Contact

Email support@equalinformation.com. For a content error, include the question number shown in the solution sheet and the app version from Settings.

Subscription

Manage or cancel in your App Store or Google Play account settings. Restore purchases is in Settings and on the subscription screen.

Your progress

Progress is stored on your device. Export a backup from Settings. Reinstalling without a backup starts a new plan.
