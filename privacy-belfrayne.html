European History AP: Belfrayne: Privacy Policy

EqualInformation, LLC. Effective 27 November 2026.

1. Introduction and scope

EqualInformation, LLC publishes European History AP: Belfrayne, a study application for iPhone, iPad and Android devices. This policy describes the information the application handles. The application requires no account. Lessons, questions and your study record are held on your device, and the course works without a network connection.

2. Information we collect

Information you choose to send. If you select Send diagnostics, the application first shows you the report and transmits it only when you confirm. It contains application and device details, recent crash signatures, a short technical event log and any note you add. If you submit feedback or a suggestion, we receive the category, your message and, where you provide them, your role, course context, a source link and a rating. Email addresses, telephone numbers and links typed into a message are removed automatically. We receive your email address only if you enter it in the contact field and ask us to reply.

Diagnostic and app-usage information. With your agreement, or as permitted by applicable law, while the optional usage setting is on, the application transmits diagnostic and app-usage information, including: application version; device model, operating system, processor, memory and display characteristics; language, region, time zone, text-size and accessibility settings; battery range and network type; whether free access or Belfrayne Plus is active; a random identifier created by the application; session timing; crash, error and performance information; the screens, topics, sets, timed papers, labs and commissions you use; the questions you attempt, the option selected, whether it is correct and the time taken; progress estimates for each learning objective; the study suggestions the application makes and what follows them; and interactions with the subscription screen.

Acceptance record. When you accept the first-run notice, a record of that acceptance is sent, whatever the position of the usage setting. It includes an identifier, the time, the application version and platform, the notice version and its text, the position of the usage setting, device and operating-system details, language, country and time zone, and the random application identifier.

What stays on your device. Essays, short answers, document notes, commission entries, search words, pins, your exam date and your study record are not transmitted to us. We do not collect your name, contacts, precise location, photographs, files or advertising identifier.

3. How we use information

We use this information to operate, secure and support the application, to diagnose faults, to provide and improve the learning experience, to understand how features are used, for research and development of our educational applications, to read and act on feedback, and to meet legal obligations.

4. Your choices

The first-run notice includes a separate switch for sharing usage and device information. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and where the region cannot be determined, it is off until you turn it on. Elsewhere it is initially on. You may change it at any time under Settings, Privacy and data. To request deletion of usage information, choose Delete my usage data in that section or write to diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

5. Subscription

Belfrayne Plus is an optional monthly subscription. Apple or Google processes payment under its own privacy terms. We do not receive your payment card details.

6. Storage and service providers

Information is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored in our Google Workspace services, including spreadsheets, files and notification email. When Belfrayne is installed from an app store, an emailed notification may reproduce the record, including usage and learning batches, your acceptance, diagnostic and crash reports, feedback and deletion requests. These go to our mailboxes, with possible forwarding to another of our own. Google processes it on our behalf. Reference links open third-party web pages, which follow their own privacy practices.

7. Retention

We keep diagnostic, usage and feedback information for as long as it is useful for the purposes above. We handle deletion requests when we receive them, by manual removal; no system step erases data for us. Emailed copies are not covered by deleting a stored record and stay unless we delete them by hand.

8. Security

We use reasonable safeguards. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

9. International users and legal bases

Where a legal basis is required, we rely on your consent or on our legitimate interests in operating and improving the application. Information may be processed in the United States.

10. Children

We design our apps to collect only the information described in this policy. If you believe information has been provided to us that you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

11. Changes

We will update this page when our practices change. A material change also changes the version of the first-run notice, which is then shown again.

12. Contact

EqualInformation, LLC, support@equalinformation.com. Data and deletion requests: diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
