Video Compressor: Reelwick: Support

Reelwick is a video compressor and resizer app for iPhone, iPad and Android. Reelwick shrinks videos to fit chat and email limits, or a size you choose, entirely on your phone. No account is needed.

Common questions

  • Are my videos uploaded anywhere? No. Compression happens on your phone and the app works offline. Your original is never modified.
  • How do I fit a size limit? Choose Chat (16 MB), Email (25 MB), your own MB value, or a resolution (1080p, 720p, 480p). Reelwick shows an estimated size before it starts and the real before and after sizes when it finishes.
  • Can I compress several videos? Yes, up to 10 at once.
  • Can I trim or mute a video? Yes. Set a start and end, mute the sound and optionally strip metadata such as location.
  • What are credits? You start with 2 credits. Each export run (a batch of up to 10 videos counts as one run) uses 1 credit. Watch a short ad to earn another, or go ad-free.
  • How do I remove ads? Open the purchase screen in Reelwick (Settings or More, or the ad-free prompt) and choose the ad-free monthly subscription or the one-time Unlock. Both remove ads and credit limits.
  • How do I restore a purchase? Open Settings (or the purchase screen) and tap Restore purchases, using the same Apple Account or Google account you bought with. This is needed after reinstalling or changing device.
  • How do I cancel the subscription? iPhone and iPad: Settings, your name, Subscriptions. Android: Google Play, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel it.
  • Text too small? Use A- and A+ on the screens, or change the system text size.
  • Can I change my ad choices? Yes. Use the privacy choices for ads in the app where shown, or your device's privacy settings.
