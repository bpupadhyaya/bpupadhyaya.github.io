App Support — Human Solution

Get help with Human Solution — your personal assistant super app.

  • Home
  • /
  • App Support — Human Solution

Human Solution is a personal assistant super app available on iOS and Android. It combines 9 independent apps into one unified experience across 5 categories: Health (PlainHealth, MediSight), Wellness (Kindly, ChronoTrack), Life Skills (PlainLaw, PlainDental), Home & Auto (RepairMate, PlainAuto), and Caregiving (CareCircle). Install only the modules you need — each works independently with its own data. All data stays on your device.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data private?

Yes. Human Solution and all its modules store data locally on your device. There are no accounts, no cloud sync, and no analytics. Each module manages its own local storage independently. We never collect or transmit any personal information. See our Privacy Policy for details.

What modules are included?

Human Solution includes 9 modules across 5 categories:
• Health: PlainHealth (health literacy), MediSight (pill identification and drug reference)
• Wellness: Kindly (mental wellness), ChronoTrack (medication and health tracking)
• Life Skills: PlainLaw (legal education), PlainDental (dental health education)
• Home & Auto: RepairMate (home repair guides), PlainAuto (vehicle maintenance)
• Caregiving: CareCircle (caregiver coordination)

Do I have to install all modules?

No. You can install and remove individual modules at any time. On iOS, modules use On-Demand Resources so uninstalled modules use zero storage. On Android, modules use dynamic feature delivery through Play Store. Only install what you need.

What features are free?

Each module has its own generous free tier. The Human Solution shell (home screen, module browser, search, and settings) is always free. See the individual app support pages for details on what each module offers for free.

How does Premium work across modules?

Human Solution offers two premium tiers that unlock features across all 9 modules:

  • Personal Premium — $14.99 one-time: Unlock advanced features across all 9 modules. No subscription, no recurring charges. Includes offline access to all content and future app updates.
  • Professional Premium — $29.99/month or $199.99/year (Save 44%): Everything in Personal, plus clinical assessments & diagnostic tools, professional legal references & SCOTUS cases, institutional-grade data management, and advanced medication interaction analysis.

All core features in every module are free. Purchase from Settings → Upgrade to Premium inside the app.

How do I restore my purchases on a new device?

iOS: Open Human Solution → Settings → Restore Purchases. All purchases are linked to your Apple ID.
Android: Open Human Solution → Settings → Restore Purchases. All purchases are linked to your Google account.

Does the app require internet?

The core app and most modules work 100% offline. Module installation/removal requires an internet connection. Some individual modules may require internet for specific features (check each module's support page for details).

The app is crashing or not loading. What should I do?

Try these steps:
1. Force-close and reopen the app.
2. Restart your device.
3. Make sure you are running the latest version from the App Store or Google Play.
4. If a specific module is crashing, try removing and reinstalling that module.
5. If the issue persists, contact us (see below) with your device model, OS version, which module is affected, and a description of the problem.

How is Human Solution different from installing the apps individually?

Human Solution combines all 9 apps into one unified interface with shared search, consistent navigation, and a single All-Access premium tier. It saves home screen space and provides a cohesive experience. Each module retains full functionality identical to its standalone version.
