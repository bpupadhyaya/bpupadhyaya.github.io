Contact

Email support@equalinformation.com. To send technical details, use More, Settings, Privacy and data, Send diagnostics: you see a preview and nothing is sent until you tap Send.

Subscription help

Restore: More, Settings, Restore purchases. Manage or cancel: More, Settings, Manage subscription, or your Apple or Google account subscription page. Cancelling keeps Enjambra Plus until the end of the paid period.

Study help

Switch between the Classical view and the Real-World view at the top of any main screen. Use A− and A+ to set text from 50% to 300%. More, Coverage lists every course point the app covers.

Corrections

If you think an answer or explanation is wrong, use Report under the explanation, or email us with the question text.
