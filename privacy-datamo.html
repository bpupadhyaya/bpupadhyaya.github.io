Datamo privacy summary (last updated 2026-11-17)

Who we are: Equal Information. Contact: support through equalinformation.com/app-support-datamo.html.

What stays on your device: your age answer, text size, scores, answers, replay shelf and credits are stored only on your device. We have no accounts and no servers that receive them.

Ads: Datamo is free with ads. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. If you say you are 13 to 17, ads are not personalised, we do not ask to track you across apps, and ad content is limited to a teen rating. If you say you are 18 or older, you may be asked for consent; you can say no and still use everything. Ad partners may collect device identifiers and approximate usage as described in their policies.

Subscription: purchases are handled by Apple or Google Play. We receive no payment details.

Sources: tapping a source opens the cited web page inside the app. That page's own privacy rules apply, and no cookies are kept after you close the window.

Children: Datamo is for ages 13 and up. If you are under 13, please do not use it.

Your choices: you can reset your progress in More, change ad privacy choices there when your region offers them, and cancel a subscription in your app store account.
