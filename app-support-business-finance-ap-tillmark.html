Common questions

How do I restore Tillmark Plus? Open Me and tap Restore purchases while signed in to the store account you bought it with.

How do I cancel? Open Me and tap Manage subscription, or use your store account's subscription settings. Cancelling takes effect from the next billing period.

What works without a connection? Lessons, questions, labs, builds and papers. Buying, restoring, opening cited pages and sending messages need a connection.

How do I change the text size? Use A- and A+ at the top of the screen (50% to 300%).

I think a question is wrong. Tap "Report a problem" under its explanation, or email us the question text.

Can the app tell me what to do with my money? No. It is a study aid and gives no personalised financial, investment, tax or legal advice.
