Computer Vision: Pixelwright - Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC.

Last updated: 2026-11-17.

On your device

Pixelwright has no account and no server of ours. Your progress, missed questions, credits and text size are stored only on your device. The pictures used in the labs are drawn by the app; the app never reads your photo library or camera.

Advertising

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob at natural breaks. Where required, a consent form asks for your choice; on iOS you may decline tracking. AdMob may use the device advertising identifier to show and measure ads. Subscribers see no ads.

Cited sources

When you open a cited source, the app loads that public page in a window inside the app; that site sees your request like any visit. No cookies are kept after the window closes.

Purchases

The optional monthly subscription is handled by Apple or Google. We receive no payment details.

Children

The app is for learners aged 13 and over. On first launch the app asks whether you are 18 or older. If you answer that you are under 18, ads are non-personalised, limited to teen-suitable content and tagged for the under-age-of-consent setting, and the iPhone tracking prompt is not shown.
