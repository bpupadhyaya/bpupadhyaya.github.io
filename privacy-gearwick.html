First Robotics - Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC.

Last updated: 2026-11-20.

First Robotics is an independent introductory robotics course. It is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by FIRST (For Inspiration and Recognition of Science and Technology), the FIRST Robotics Competition, FIRST Tech Challenge or FIRST LEGO League.

What stays on your device

The app has no account and no server of ours. Your progress, your age answer and your text-size setting are stored only on your device. We collect no personal data. The lessons, 3D robot labs, build guides and quizzes are bundled in the app and work offline.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. With your consent (shown by a consent form where required) AdMob may use your device advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, advertising ID on Android) to show and measure ads, and only for adults. On iOS you can decline tracking when asked (App Tracking Transparency). Subscribers see no ads.

Source links

When you open a cited source, the app loads that public web page in a window inside the app. That site receives your request like any web visit. We do not keep cookies beyond that session.

Purchases

The optional monthly subscription is processed by Apple or Google; we receive no payment details.

Children

The app is a general-audience learning tool for ages 13 and up and is not directed to children under 13. On first launch it asks whether you are 18 or older; the answer stays on your device. If you answer that you are under 18, ads are non-personalised, limited to teen-suitable content, tagged for the under-age-of-consent setting, no advertising identifier is used, and the iPhone tracking prompt is not shown.
