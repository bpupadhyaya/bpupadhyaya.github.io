Heapmerge privacy policy

Last updated 7 November 2026. Heapmerge is a puzzle game published by Equal Information LLC.

What stays on your device

Your stars, coins, discovered gems, daily-puzzle history and settings are saved only on your device. Heapmerge has no account, no sign-in and no server of ours that receives your play data. Erasing the app, or using "Erase my progress" in More, removes it.

Ads

Heapmerge is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may use your device's advertising identifier and similar device information, and may collect coarse location derived from your IP address, ad interaction data and diagnostics. On iOS we ask for your permission before any tracking. If you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalised. In regions that require it, a consent message from Google lets you choose how data is used, and you can change that later under More, Ad privacy choices. See how Google uses data from partner sites and apps.

Subscription

The optional monthly ad-free pass is sold and managed by Apple. We receive no payment card or Apple account details; the app only learns whether a pass is active. Manage or cancel it in your Apple account settings.

Offline use

Every puzzle is built into the app. Without a connection the game works normally; only ads and the subscription need the internet.

Children

Heapmerge is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.
