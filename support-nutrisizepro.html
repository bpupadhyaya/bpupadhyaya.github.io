Maintaining and improving human health via Nutrition and Exercise at near-zero cost.

Nutrisize Pro is a comprehensive health intelligence platform with 5,000+ foods, 5,400+ exercises, and 201 physiological parameters across 16 body systems. It features NutriGuide — an on-device AI chat assistant for nutrition, exercise, and health questions with voice input. Available on iOS, Android, and Web.

Built with a dual audience in mind: accessible enough for anyone to understand their health, yet detailed enough for physicians and medical professionals. Physiology content is available in 10 languages serving the global population. Privacy-first — all health data stays on your device.

Why Nutrisize Pro Exists

Healthcare is expensive. The average American spends $12,530 per year on healthcare, yet most chronic diseases are preventable through proper nutrition and regular exercise. Nutrisize Pro puts comprehensive, science-backed health knowledge in everyone's hands at near-zero cost.

The app serves both the general public and top-notch doctors from a single platform. A patient can understand their blood work using the "Simply Put" explanations, while their physician can look up differential diagnoses, drug interactions, and lab methodology from the same parameter page.

All core features remain completely free forever. Premium features (expanded databases, advanced analytics) are available through affordable one-time purchases — no subscriptions, no recurring charges.

If you find Nutrisize Pro valuable, your support helps us continue improving the app, adding new features, and reaching more people worldwide. Every contribution, large or small, is deeply appreciated.

Disclaimer: Nutrisize Pro provides health intelligence for educational and personal wellness purposes. It is not a substitute for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical decisions.
