Bindwell privacy policy

In effect from 7 November 2026. Bindwell, the pharmacology receptor and kinetics lab, is published by EqualInformation, LLC.

In short

Bindwell has no accounts and no servers of ours. Your study progress stays on your device. The only outside company that handles data is Google, through the advertising that keeps the app free.

Kept on your device

Drill scores, daily streak, the classes you have browsed, your bench settings and any ad-free time you have earned are stored in the app's private storage. We cannot see, copy or restore them. Deleting the app removes them.

What the app does not use

No location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, health or fitness data, and no sign-in. You never enter patient information, because the app has no place for it. Bindwell is for learning and is not medical advice.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Before any ad is requested, iOS asks whether the app may track you across other companies' apps (App Tracking Transparency). In regions where it applies, Google's consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) then lets you accept or limit ad personalisation. If you decline, ads are not personalised. Google may receive your device identifier (when you allow tracking), IP address and basic device and ad-interaction details to deliver and measure ads. See how Google uses data from ads.

You can remove ads by earning ad-free time through optional rewarded videos, or with the monthly ad-free subscription.

Subscription

Purchases are processed by Apple. We receive no payment details and no personal identity. The app only reads the entitlement that Apple reports on your device. Manage or cancel in Settings, Apple Account, Subscriptions.

Children

Bindwell is a study tool for older students and adults and is not aimed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your choices

Change tracking in iOS Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking. Revisit ad consent in the app's About screen where the consent form is available.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new date appears above. Questions: support page or bpupadhyaya@equalinformation.com.
