Support for the light path puzzle game by Equal Information LLC.

How do I play?

Tap a part in the tray, tap an empty square to place it, tap a placed mirror again to turn it. Light every receiver with exactly the colour it shows. The How to play page in the app has the full steps.

Ads and the ad-free subscription

Open Go ad-free on the home screen. You can subscribe for $2.99 per month, or earn six ad-free hours per short video. Manage or cancel a subscription in iOS Settings, your name, Subscriptions. Use Restore purchases if you reinstall.
