Eosinwell Support

Virtual microscope for histology

Getting help

Email bpupadhyaya@equalinformation.com with your device model, iOS version and a short description. We aim to reply within three working days.

Common questions

  • How do I zoom? Pinch, double-tap, or use the objective buttons (4x to 100x).
  • The image is blurry. Use the fine focus slider or tap Refocus. At high power the depth of field is shallow.
  • How do I name a structure? Tap it. The name, function and how to recognise it appear.
  • Does it work offline? Yes. All slides and notes are built in. Ads and the subscription need a connection.
  • Restore my subscription from Progress, Go ad-free, Restore purchases.
