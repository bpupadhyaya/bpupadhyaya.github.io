Pilotwick privacy policy

Last updated 9 November 2026. Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC.

The short version

Pilotwick has no account and no sign-in. Your study progress, answers, settings and chosen test date stay on your iPhone or iPad. We do not collect your name, email, location or flight details, and we do not run our own servers for the app.

What is stored on your device

Which questions you answered and how, practice test scores, streak, text size, the optional test date, and the count of videos you watched toward ad-free time. You can erase all of it in Settings, "Erase my study progress", or by deleting the app.

Ads in the free version

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier and technical information such as IP address and app usage to select and measure ads. Before any ad is requested, Pilotwick asks for App Tracking Transparency permission on iOS and, where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and some US states), shows Google's consent form. If you decline, you still get every study feature; ads may be less relevant. You can change the choice in iOS Settings. See Google's advertising policy.

Subscription

The optional ad-free subscription is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the app whether a subscription is active. Manage or cancel it in your Apple ID settings.

Children

Pilotwick is a study aid for adults and students preparing for a pilot knowledge test. It is not directed to children under 13.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. The only third party that receives data from the app is Google (advertising) while ads are shown, as described above. Because we keep no personal data on our side, there is nothing for us to retain or delete; your on-device data is removed when you erase progress or delete the app.

Reporting an error or contacting us

Email support@equalinformation.com. Pilotwick is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Federal Aviation Administration.
