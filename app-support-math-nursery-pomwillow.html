Help and support

Math Nursery: Pomwillow · EqualInformation, LLC · 27 November 2026

Contact us

In the app, Grown-ups, Settings has Send feedback and Send diagnostics, which open a draft in your own mail app. Or write to support@equalinformation.com. Please do not send details that identify your child.

Opening the grown-up area

On the first screen of either view press Grown-ups and type the answer to the sum written in words. There you will find progress, real-object checks, ideas for home, screen-time guidance, coverage, plans, level names and settings.

Two views

Storybook follows five chapters of pages in order. At home offers jobs in which the child builds something and then does it for real. Switch with the two buttons at the top of the first screen.

No spoken words or sounds

Check the volume, then Grown-ups, Settings, and make sure Spoken words and Soft sounds are on. The voice is the one built into your device. Show spoken words as text is also available there.

Text size

Grown-ups, Settings, Text size: from 50% to 300%.

Plans

Plans are in Grown-ups, Plans. To restore, press Restore purchases. To cancel, use your Apple account subscription settings.

Erasing progress

Grown-ups, Settings, then press and hold Hold to erase progress. Removing the app also removes its data from the device.
