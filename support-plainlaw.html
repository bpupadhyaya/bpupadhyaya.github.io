Legal knowledge explained in plain language — so everyone can understand their rights, navigate legal situations, and make informed decisions, no matter where they are in the world.

PlainLaw is a global legal knowledge platform that serves both the general public and legal professionals. It auto-detects your jurisdiction and shows relevant domestic law, international law, and UN treaties — all in plain language. It is built and maintained by a single developer who believes that understanding your legal rights should not require a law degree or an expensive attorney.

Every feature is designed with privacy at its core — all content works offline with no data collection, no accounts, and no tracking. PlainLaw is available on iOS and Android. Core features are free, with optional Premium ($5.99 one-time) for consumers and Pro ($59.99/year) for legal professionals. Your support helps us expand to more countries, more languages, and more legal content.

Why PlainLaw Exists

Lawyers charge $200–$500 per hour. Most people can't afford even a 30-minute consultation for basic questions like "Can my landlord do this?" or "What are my rights if I'm arrested?" PlainLaw provides that basic legal literacy — for free.

Legal systems vary enormously across the world's 195 countries. A person traveling from the US to Japan, or an immigrant learning a new country's laws, needs to understand how the local legal system works. PlainLaw auto-detects your jurisdiction and adapts instantly — showing you the laws that matter where you are.

Legal professionals — attorneys, judges, clerks, law students — need quick-reference tools they can use mid-hearing or in chambers. PlainLaw's Pro tier provides annotated constitutions, landmark cases, legal standards, citation formats, and cross-jurisdictional comparisons — all offline, all in 2 taps. Content rich enough for a Supreme Court justice, clear enough for a first-year law student.

PlainLaw is not a substitute for professional legal advice. It is an educational platform that helps people understand their rights and helps professionals do their work more efficiently.

Disclaimer: PlainLaw provides legal information for educational purposes only. It is NOT legal advice. Laws vary by jurisdiction. For specific legal problems, consult a licensed attorney in your area.
