Wallet Safety Guide

Everything you need to know about keeping your Open Wallet safe.

Updating Open Wallet

✅ SAFE — your wallet data is preserved
✅ Update from App Store / Play Store — wallet data preserved
✅ Update your phone's operating system — wallet data preserved
✅ Change your PIN in Settings — old PIN replaced, wallet preserved
✅ Enable/disable biometrics — wallet preserved
❌ DANGEROUS — your wallet data is DELETED
❌ Uninstall the app — ALL wallet data deleted permanently
❌ Clear app data in phone Settings — ALL wallet data deleted
❌ Factory reset your phone — ALL wallet data deleted
❌ Lose your recovery phrase — funds UNRECOVERABLE

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens when I update Open Wallet from the App Store / Play Store?
Your wallet, seed phrase, PIN, and all settings are PRESERVED. App updates only replace the app code — your encrypted vault data stays intact. You do NOT need to re-enter your seed phrase or set up your wallet again. Just update and unlock with your PIN or biometrics as usual.
What happens if I uninstall and reinstall Open Wallet?
⚠️ WARNING: Uninstalling the app DELETES all wallet data permanently. Your seed phrase, PIN, settings, and transaction history will be lost. You MUST have your 24-word recovery phrase written down to restore your wallet. To reinstall safely: 1. Make sure you have your recovery phrase written on paper 2. Uninstall the app 3. Install from App Store / Play Store 4. Tap "Restore Existing Wallet" 5. Enter your 24-word recovery phrase 6. Set a new password and PIN
Is my wallet safe during phone updates (iOS/Android)?
Yes. Operating system updates do NOT affect your wallet data. Your encrypted vault is stored in the device's secure keychain, which persists across OS updates.
What if my phone is lost or stolen?
Your wallet is protected by: • PIN (6 digits, locked after 5 failed attempts) • Biometrics (Face ID / Fingerprint) • AES-256-GCM encryption with Argon2id key derivation • Auto-lock after 5 minutes of inactivity To recover on a new phone: 1. Install Open Wallet on the new phone 2. Tap "Restore Existing Wallet" 3. Enter your 24-word recovery phrase
What should I NEVER do?
• NEVER share your 24-word recovery phrase with anyone • NEVER screenshot your recovery phrase • NEVER store your recovery phrase digitally (no photos, no cloud, no notes app) • NEVER uninstall the app without your recovery phrase written down • NEVER clear app data / cache manually in phone settings
How do I back up my wallet?
Your 24-word recovery phrase IS your backup. As long as you have it written on paper and stored safely, you can restore your wallet on any device. Go to Settings > Account & Security > Backup Verification to verify your recovery phrase is correct.
