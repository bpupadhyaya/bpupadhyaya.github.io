Need-Blind Aid: Aidmere: Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Contact: support@equalinformation.com. Last updated 2026-11-23.

What stays on your device

Your progress, answers, the figures you type into the tools (income, savings, award letters, loan amounts), your appeal drafts, checklist ticks and settings are stored only on your device. The app has no account system and no server that receives this data. Deleting the app erases it. We do not collect your name, email address or contact details, and the app does not track you or use analytics.

Subscription

Aidmere Plus is an optional monthly subscription. Purchases are processed by Apple or Google; we never see your payment details, and we receive no personal data from the purchase. You can cancel any time in your App Store or Google Play account settings: cancelling takes effect from the next billing period, and you keep Plus until the period you already paid for ends.

Sources you open

Source and school-page links open public web pages inside the app. Those sites apply their own privacy practices.

Children

The app is designed for students and families. It does not ask for personal information.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@equalinformation.com.

Names such as FAFSA, College Board, CSS Profile and school names are used only descriptively. This independent app is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by any college, those organisations or the US Department of Education.
