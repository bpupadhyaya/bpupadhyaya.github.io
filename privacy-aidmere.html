Need-Blind Aid: Aidmere: Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Contact: support@equalinformation.com. Last updated 2026-11-22.

What stays on your device

Your progress, answers, the figures you type into the tools (income, savings, award letters, loan amounts), your appeal drafts, checklist ticks, age band and settings are stored only on your device. The app has no account system and no server that receives this data. Deleting the app erases it. We do not collect your name, email address or contact details.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: banners and occasional full-screen ads at natural breaks, and an optional rewarded video. AdMob may use device and usage data to select and measure ads, under Google's own privacy policy. Adults (18 and over) on iOS are asked for permission before tracking across other companies' apps, and where the law requires it Google shows a consent form. Users aged 13 to 17 receive non-personalised ads, with content limited to teen-appropriate ratings, and no tracking prompt. Users under 13 see no ads at all.

Subscription

The optional monthly plan only removes ads. Purchases are processed by Apple or Google; we never see your payment details. You can cancel any time in your App Store or Google Play account settings and keep the benefit until the paid period ends.

Sources you open

Source and school-page links open public web pages inside the app. Those sites apply their own privacy practices.

Audience

The app is designed for students and families and asks an age band on first launch (stored on the device only) so that advertising rules for under-18 and under-13 users are applied.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@equalinformation.com.

Names such as FAFSA, College Board, CSS Profile and school names are used only descriptively. This independent app is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by any college, those organisations or the US Department of Education.
