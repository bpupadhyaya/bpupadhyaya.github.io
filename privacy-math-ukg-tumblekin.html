Privacy: Math UKG: Tumblekin

Math UKG: Tumblekin is a children's maths practice app published by EqualInformation LLC. Last updated 28 November 2026.

The short version

The app sends nothing automatically. It has no account, no analytics and no tracking, and the practice works offline. We receive information from the app only if a grown-up chooses to send us an email from it. A connection is needed to send that email, to buy or restore a plan through the app store, to read the official documents the app cites, and to open our web pages in your browser.

What you may choose to send us

Both choices sit behind the grown-up question, under About. Each opens a draft in your own email app, which you can read, change, send or delete.

  • Send diagnostics (to diagnostics@equalinformation.com). The whole report is shown on screen first: app version, kind of device and system version, screen and text size, device language, voice and sound settings, whether a plan is switched on, and any program errors since the app opened. It has no child's name, answers or progress.
  • Feedback (to support@equalinformation.com). Whatever you write.

If you send either one, we receive your email address, your message and the report text if included. We link these to your email address, use them only to answer you and to keep the app working and improve it, and do not use them for tracking or advertising. Please leave out your child's name. The emails are kept in our Google Workspace mailboxes for as long as we need them for those purposes. To ask us to delete them, write to support@equalinformation.com.

What stays on the device

Your child's answers and progress, a grown-up's checks and notes of what they saw, and settings such as text size and sound. This is saved only on the phone or tablet. We cannot see it. Removing the app removes it, and the grown-up area has an Erase progress button.

What the app does not use

It does not ask for or use a name, a photo, a location, the microphone, the camera or contacts, and it has no box for typing an email address. It contains no analytics code.

Official documents the app cites

Behind the grown-up question, you can open the school and health publications the app cites. The app shows each one inside the app, straight from its publisher's website (GOV.UK, the California, New York State and Texas education sites). As with any visit to a website, that publisher receives your internet address and basic device information under its own privacy notice. The app sends it nothing about your child, switches page scripts off, and does not keep what the page stores.

Spoken words

When the app speaks, it uses the voice built into your device. Nothing is recorded or listened to.

Plans

Payment for the two optional monthly plans goes through the app store on your device, and that store keeps its own records under its own privacy terms. All the app ever asks the store is whether a plan is switched on; it never learns your name or how you paid.

Children

The app is meant to be used by a child with a grown-up nearby, and the grown-up area sits behind a question for adults. The app collects nothing from a child on its own; the two email choices are for grown-ups only.

Changes and questions

If this ever changes, the new version will be on this page and the app will tell grown-ups before it collects anything more. Questions: support@equalinformation.com.
