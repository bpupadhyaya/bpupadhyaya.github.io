Privacy: Math UKG: Tumblekin

Math UKG: Tumblekin is a children's maths practice app published by EqualInformation LLC. Last updated 27 November 2026.

The short version

The app does not collect your child's information. It has no account, no advertising, no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet.

What stays on the device

Your child's answers and progress, a grown-up's checks and notes of what they saw, and settings such as text size and sound. This is saved only on the phone or tablet. We cannot see it. Removing the app removes it, and the grown-up area has an Erase progress button.

What the app does not use

It does not ask for or use a name, an email address, a photo, a location, the microphone, the camera, contacts, or an advertising identifier. It contains no advertising and no analytics code.

Spoken words

When the app speaks, it uses the voice built into your device. Nothing is recorded or listened to.

Plans

The two monthly plans are bought through Apple (App Store) or Google Play. Apple or Google handles your payment and keeps its own records under its own privacy terms. The app only asks the store whether a plan is active; it is not told your name or payment details.

Reports you choose to share

The grown-up area can prepare a short text report, made of counts only, with no name. The app does not send it anywhere. It hands the text to your device's share sheet, and you decide whether to pass it on and to whom.

Children

The app is meant to be used by a child with a grown-up nearby, and the grown-up area sits behind a question for adults. Because we collect nothing from children, there is nothing for us to share, sell or delete on our side.

Changes and questions

If this ever changes, the new version will be on this page and the app will tell grown-ups before it collects anything. Questions: privacy@equalinformation.com.
