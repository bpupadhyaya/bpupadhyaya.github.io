Privacy Policy — ChronoTrack

Effective Date: April 26, 2026

  • /

1 Who We Are

ChronoTrack is provided by EqualInformation, LLC.

If you have privacy questions or requests, you may contact us at:
Email: bpupadhyaya@equalinformation.com
Contact page: equalinformation.weebly.com/contact.html | bpupadhyaya.github.io#contact

2 Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the ChronoTrack app (iOS bundle identifier: com.equalinformation.chronotrack.app; Android package name: com.chronotrack.app). ChronoTrack is available on iOS (App Store) and Android (Google Play).

3 What ChronoTrack Does

ChronoTrack is a chronic condition tracker with on-device pattern analysis designed for two audiences:

  • Patients (Free + Plus tier): Symptom logging, trigger tracking, medication schedules, calendar view with pain heatmap, daily wellness check-ins, health journal, emergency card, PDF and FHIR report generation, flare timeline, medication effectiveness analysis
  • Healthcare Professionals (Pro tier): Multi-patient dashboard, SOAP clinical report generator, Data Hub with configurable data source connectors, Connector Store, API Key Vault, API call audit log, FHIR R4 integration

Important: ChronoTrack is a health tracking and data management tool. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or medical condition. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider for medical advice.

4 Information ChronoTrack May Access or Store

Depending on how you use the app, ChronoTrack may store information on your device, including:

  • Symptom entries (condition, pain level, body location, pain type, duration, timestamps)
  • Trigger entries (food, weather, sleep, stress, activity, and other triggers)
  • Medication logs (medication name, dosage, effectiveness rating)
  • Medication schedules and dose tracking
  • Journal entries (content, mood, tags)
  • Daily check-in data (overall feeling, sleep quality, energy, stress levels)
  • Wellness goals and completion records
  • Emergency card information (conditions, allergies, medications, emergency contacts)
  • Onboarding preferences (name, selected conditions)
  • In-app purchase status (subscription tier)

For Pro tier users, ChronoTrack may additionally store:

  • Connector configurations (endpoint URLs, sync schedules, field mappings)
  • API keys you provide (encrypted, stored on-device only)
  • API call audit logs (timestamps, API names, actions, costs)
  • Patient share records (patient names, share codes, data summaries)
  • Cached data from external APIs you configure (weather, drug info, research articles)

Important: All of this information is stored exclusively on your device. ChronoTrack does not transmit, upload, or sync any of this data to external servers unless you explicitly configure a data connector to do so.

5 How We Use Information

ChronoTrack uses information to:

  • Display your symptom history, calendar, and pain trends
  • Run on-device pattern analysis (frequency, trends, trigger correlations, medication effectiveness)
  • Generate PDF and FHIR reports for healthcare provider appointments
  • Track medication schedules and adherence rates
  • Display appropriate content based on subscription tier (Free, Plus, Pro)
  • For professionals: manage patient data, generate SOAP notes, and execute user-configured data connectors

6 Local-First Design

ChronoTrack does not collect, transmit, or store any user data on our servers. All data is stored locally on your device using SwiftData (iOS) or Room database (Android). There is no cloud sync, no user accounts, and no user tracking. ChronoTrack does not require an internet connection to function for core features (except for in-app purchase verification and optional external API data fetching).

7 External API Data Sources

ChronoTrack can optionally fetch data from external APIs to enrich your health tracking experience. These include:

  • Free APIs (built-in): Open-Meteo (weather), OpenFDA (drug information), PubMed (research articles). These are free, public APIs that do not require user accounts.
  • User-configured APIs (Pro tier): You may add your own data source connectors with your own API keys. ChronoTrack does not select, recommend, or profit from any external data provider.

Important: When external APIs are used, the data fetched is cached locally on your device. ChronoTrack never sends your personal health data to these APIs — it only fetches reference data (weather conditions, drug information, research articles). You control which APIs are enabled and can disable them at any time.

API keys you enter are encrypted and stored exclusively on your device. ChronoTrack does not transmit your API keys to our servers or any third party. ChronoTrack has no financial relationship with any external data provider.

8 Pattern Analysis

ChronoTrack's pattern analysis engine runs entirely on your device. Your symptom data is analyzed locally to identify trends, trigger correlations, and medication effectiveness. No data is sent to any external AI service, cloud provider, or third-party server for analysis.

9 No Analytics or Tracking

ChronoTrack does not use any analytics SDKs, crash reporters, or tracking tools. No data is sent to third-party analytics services. No advertising identifiers are collected.

10 App Store and Google Play Billing

ChronoTrack uses Apple App Store In-App Purchase (on iOS) or Google Play Billing (on Android) for subscription functionality (ChronoTrack Plus and ChronoTrack Pro tiers). Apple or Google may process payment and transaction information under their own respective policies. ChronoTrack may receive limited purchase-status or product information needed to unlock purchased content on your device. EqualInformation, LLC does not receive your full payment card number through ChronoTrack.

11 Sensitive Health Information

ChronoTrack stores symptom data, pain levels, trigger information, medication logs, and wellness tracking data that may be considered sensitive health information. This data is stored exclusively on your device and is never transmitted to any server (unless you explicitly configure a data connector to share data with a healthcare provider). ChronoTrack does not access Apple Health, Google Health Connect, or any other health platform without your explicit permission.

12 Patient Data Sharing (Pro Tier)

Pro tier users (healthcare professionals) may receive patient data through QR codes, share codes, or file transfers. This data sharing is initiated entirely by the patient and requires the patient's explicit consent. All shared data is stored locally on the receiving device. ChronoTrack does not operate a cloud relay or intermediary server for data sharing.

13 Permissions

ChronoTrack may request the following device permissions:

  • Notifications (iOS/Android): Used only for optional medication reminders and check-in notifications that you control
  • Location (optional): Used only for automatic weather correlation when logging symptoms. Location data is not stored or transmitted — it is used only to fetch local weather conditions.
  • Camera (optional, Pro): Used only for scanning QR codes when accepting patient data shares
  • Billing (Android): Used only for in-app purchase verification

ChronoTrack does not require microphone, contacts, or other sensitive permissions.

14 Data Export

ChronoTrack allows data export in multiple formats: PDF reports, CSV files, and FHIR R4 JSON. All exports are generated locally on your device and shared using your device's native share sheet. No data is uploaded to any server during the export process.

15 Sharing and Sale of Data

EqualInformation, LLC does not sell personal or sensitive user data. We do not share users' data with data brokers or for advertising purposes. The company does not intentionally transmit personal or sensitive ChronoTrack user data to its own servers or unrelated third parties.

16 Data Retention and Deletion

All data in ChronoTrack is stored locally on your device. Subscription-related unlock state may be restorable through App Store or Google Play for eligible purchases. Uninstalling ChronoTrack removes all locally stored data from your device, including all symptom entries, medication logs, journal entries, cached API data, connector configurations, and preferences.

17 Security

ChronoTrack relies on iOS and Android platform protections and app sandboxing as part of its local-first design. App data is stored within the app's private sandbox and is not accessible to other apps. API keys stored in the API Key Vault are encrypted using platform-native encryption (Keychain on iOS, KeyStore on Android). However, no method of storage or electronic transmission is completely secure, and the company cannot guarantee absolute security.

18 Your Choices

You may choose to subscribe to Plus or Pro content or not. You control what information you enter into the app. You can enable or disable external API data sources at any time. You can add, pause, or remove data connectors at any time. You can set your own API budgets and spending limits. You can export your data at any time. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data from your device.

19 Children's Privacy

ChronoTrack is intended for adults (18+). It is not designed for or directed at children under 13. ChronoTrack does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe information has been provided inappropriately, please contact the company.

20 Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the updated version on this page and revise the Effective Date above.
