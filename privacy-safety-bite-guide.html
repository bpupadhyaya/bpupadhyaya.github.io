Fangwell Privacy Policy

Fangwell: Snake Bite First Aid. Effective 13 November 2026. Publisher: Equal Information LLC.

Fangwell is an offline first-aid guide for snake, spider, scorpion, animal, insect and marine bites and stings. It is general information only and is not a substitute for emergency services or a health professional.

What stays on your device

Your settings, region choice, study progress, streak and the hospital handover note you may fill in (bite time, symptoms, first aid given, allergies) are saved only on your device. There are no accounts and no servers of ours, so we cannot see any of it. The call buttons open your phone's dialer with the emergency number for your region; the call is placed by your phone, not by us. The app does not use the camera, microphone, contacts, photos or precise location, and it does not identify animals from pictures.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob on practice screens only. Ads are never shown on emergency, call, step-by-step guide or hospital handover screens. An optional short video can be watched for extra practice. On iPhone and iPad the system first asks whether the app may track you (App Tracking Transparency); in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some US states a Google consent message also appears (Google User Messaging Platform). On Android the Google consent message appears where the law requires it. You can decline and keep using every feature. With your permission, AdMob and its partners may use your device advertising identifier, approximate location derived from your IP address, and ad interaction and diagnostic data to choose, show and measure ads. See Google's advertising policies. You can change your choices later in the app under Settings (privacy choices for ads, where shown) and in your device settings. Ads are not served to people who have an ad-free purchase active.

Purchases

The optional ad-free monthly subscription and the one-time Unlock are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google Play. We receive confirmation that a purchase is active and never see your payment details.

What is collected

Collected by us: nothing. Collected by Google AdMob in the free version: device identifiers (advertising identifier), approximate location from IP address, and ad interaction and diagnostic data, used for third-party advertising only.

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information, build profiles about you, run our own analytics, or ask you to create an account. No personal data is sent to us.

Children

Fangwell is a general-audience safety guide and is not directed to children under 13. Apple rates it 12+ because it contains medical information. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and we do not collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new effective date.
