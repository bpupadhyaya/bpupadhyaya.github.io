Privacy Policy

IB Maths AI Prep: Modelwick. Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Last updated 2026-11-27.

1. Introduction and scope

This Privacy Policy explains how EqualInformation, LLC ("we", "us") handles information in connection with IB Maths AI Prep: Modelwick (the "app"), a study aid for the International Baccalaureate Diploma Programme course Mathematics: applications and interpretation. It applies to the app only. The app has no user account and its lessons, quizzes, labs and builds work offline.

2. Information we collect

A. Information you choose to provide. Diagnostic reports: under Settings, Privacy and data, you may choose Send diagnostics. The app shows the full content of the report and sends it only when you tap Send. Feedback and suggestions: when you choose to send feedback, we receive the category, your message (up to 2,000 characters, from which email addresses, telephone numbers and links are removed automatically) and, if you provide them, a role, a level or context, a source link and a rating from 1 to 5. We receive an email address only if you tick the option allowing us to reply to you.

B. Diagnostic and app-usage information that is permissible under applicable law, collected with your agreement or as permitted by law while the optional usage setting is on: app version and build; device model; operating system and version; processor type; memory; screen size; language, region and time zone; text-size and accessibility settings; network type; a battery-level range; whether the free or Plus entitlement is active; a randomly generated app identifier (not an advertising identifier); session timing; the screens, labs and builds you open and the time spent in them; lesson activity such as scroll depth and time; your attempts at quiz, test and mock-paper questions (the question, its topic, whether the answer was correct, the time taken and whether a hint was used); topic-level progress estimates; the adaptive-learning decisions the app makes for you and their outcomes; paywall and purchase-screen interactions; and performance, crash and error information.

C. Information we do not collect. We do not collect your name, contacts, precise location, photographs or files, an advertising identifier, or the text you type into the app, such as answers in answer boxes or Exploration planning notes, except the feedback you choose to submit as described above. Payments are handled by your store account; we do not receive your payment card details.

3. How we use information

To operate, maintain, secure and support the app; to diagnose problems; to provide and personalise the learning experience; to understand how the app is used; for research and development; for analytics and improvement of our apps and educational content; to read and act on feedback; and to comply with legal obligations.

4. Your choices

When you first open the app, a notice explains these practices and offers a separate switch for usage information. The switch is off by default in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and where your region cannot be determined, until you turn it on. You can change it at any time under Settings, Privacy and data. Diagnostics and feedback are sent only when you choose to send them. To request deletion of your usage information use Delete my usage data in the same place, or email diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

5. Storage and service providers

Information is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections to a web application operated by EqualInformation, LLC, which records it in our Google Workspace services (Google Sheets and Drive). Our mailboxes receive record contents from copies of Modelwick installed through a store. This can be any of the following: acceptance records, diagnostic reports, deletion requests, feedback, crash records, or batches showing what you used and how you did on questions. We may have that mail forwarded to a second mailbox belonging to us. Service providers process information on our behalf for these purposes. Purchases are processed by Apple or Google under their own terms and policies.

6. Retention

Usage, learning and diagnostic information is kept for as long as it is useful for the purposes described above. Deletion requests (section 4) are acted on by hand; no software of ours deletes records automatically, and what was emailed remains in mail unless also deleted manually.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information, including encryption in transit. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. International users and legal bases

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we rely on your consent (the usage switch and the actions you take to send diagnostics or feedback) and on our legitimate interest in maintaining and improving the app. Information may be processed in the United States.

9. Children

We design our apps to collect only the information described in this policy. If you believe information has been provided to us that you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If a change is material, the version of the first-run notice changes and the notice is shown again.

11. Contact

EqualInformation, LLC. Questions: support@equalinformation.com. Deletion and data requests: diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

Not affiliated with or endorsed by the International Baccalaureate Organization. IB is named in plain text only to describe the course the app supports.
