Support PlainLife

Help us bring the human generational health loop — womb to next womb — to everyone, for free.

Biology doesn't work in slices. PlainLife unifies the whole human health loop — womb to next womb — into one plain-language, offline app, so every stage of life and the next generation gets the guidance it deserves.

PlainLife is a mobile app that guides a person through the entire arc of human health as one continuous, intergenerational cycle: embryology and conception → pregnancy and birth → infancy and childhood → adolescence → fertility → conception again. It covers six chapters — Origins, Birth, Childhood, Adolescence, Fertility, and Generations — and is built and maintained by EqualInformation, LLC.

Every feature is designed with privacy at its core — PlainLife is 100% on-device with no accounts, no servers, no cloud sync, and no tracking, with your data kept in encrypted local storage that only you can unlock, and the core works fully offline. PlainLife is available now on Google Play and the Apple App Store. It is free for everyone. Your support helps us continue improving the app and building more tools that help people live well across generations.

Why PlainLife Exists

Most health apps cover a single slice — a pregnancy app here, a parenting app there, a period tracker somewhere else. But the conditions of the womb shape the child, childhood shapes adolescence, and adolescent and preconception health shape the next pregnancy — rippling forward across generations.

PlainLife unifies that whole loop into one app, with the womb recognized as the single most powerful intervention point in a human life. It is the consumer-facing front end of the Human Engineering project — where end-to-end models of human biology become practical, daily guidance for real people and their future children.

Guidance should be trustworthy and within reach. PlainLife is plain-language and evidence-based with cited sources, includes urgency / triage tags so you know when to seek timely care, and offers "Ask PlainLife" — offline question-and-answer right on your device.

PlainLife is not a substitute for professional medical advice. It is an educational companion that helps you understand each stage of the health loop and make better decisions for yourself, your children, and the generations to come.

Disclaimer: PlainLife is for educational purposes only. The information provided is not intended to replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always seek the advice of a qualified health professional with any questions regarding pregnancy, a child's health, or a medical condition. If you think you may have a medical emergency, contact your local emergency services immediately.
