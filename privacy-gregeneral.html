GRE Prep Verbal Quant: Gremont Privacy Policy

GRE General Test practice with Verbal Reasoning, Quantitative Reasoning and Analytical Writing. Publisher: Equal Information LLC. Effective 14 November 2026.

What the app stores

Your answers, levels, review schedule, mistake notebook, essay drafts, mock results, streak days, credits and settings are saved only on your device. There is no account, no sign-up and no server of ours that receives this data. You can export your progress to a file you control, import it again, or reset it in Settings. Deleting the app deletes the data.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on some screens, an occasional full-screen ad after a finished practice block, and optional rewarded videos that earn credits. There are no ads in timed mocks, labs or the essay editor. AdMob may use your device advertising identifier, approximate location and device information to choose and measure ads. On iOS the app asks permission before tracking across other companies' apps and websites, and where the law requires it, shows a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform. You can refuse and keep using the app. The first practice session has no ads. See policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Purchases

The optional ad-free plan is an auto-renewing monthly subscription handled by Apple or Google. We receive no payment details. Manage or cancel it in your store account settings.

Practice content and independence

Gremont is independent: it is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by ETS. GRE is a registered trademark of ETS, used here only to describe the kind of test this practice suits. All questions, passages and explanations are original and written for this app. Score ranges are rough practice estimates from your own answers, not predictions of any GRE score.

Essays

Essay drafts you type stay on your device. The writing feedback is produced on your device by simple counting rules and is approximate.

Children

The app is an education tool for graduate-school applicants and is not directed to children under 13.
