MCAT Prep: Premedra - Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC.

Premedra (MCAT Prep: Premedra) is published by EqualInformation, LLC. Last updated 2026-11-19.

What stays on your device. Your answers, review schedule, mock history, text-size setting, mastery per topic and unlock timers are stored only on your device. We do not run accounts and we do not receive this data.

Ads. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob between practice sets and offers optional rewarded ads. Google may collect your advertising identifier and device information to serve and measure ads. On iOS you are asked for App Tracking Transparency permission and shown Google's consent form where required; you can decline and still use the whole app. Premedra Ad-Free removes ads.

Purchases. Subscriptions are processed by Apple or Google. We receive no payment details.

Sources. Opening a cited source loads that publisher's web page inside the app; that site's own policy applies.

Children. Premedra is for learners aged 18 and over and is not directed to children.
