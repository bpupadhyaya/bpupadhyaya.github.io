Privacy Policy

Business Finance AP: Tillmark. EqualInformation, LLC. Effective 27 November 2026.

What this policy covers

This policy explains what the Business Finance AP: Tillmark app for phones and tablets handles. EqualInformation, LLC publishes the app and is responsible for the information described here. The app has no user account.

What stays on your device

Your progress record, study plan, lab settings, the words you type into build steps and free-response boxes, and your text size are stored on your device. Erasing progress inside the app, or deleting the app, removes them. What you type into build steps and free-response boxes is not sent to us.

Information we receive

The first-run acceptance record. When you accept the first-run notice we receive a record of that acceptance: the notice version and a fingerprint of its text, the time, a random identifier created by the app, the app version, platform, country and language settings, time zone and whether the usage switch was on.

Diagnostic and app-usage information, when the usage switch is on. This can include device model, operating system, processor, memory, screen and window size, language, region, time zone, accessibility and display settings, app version, performance and memory indicators, network type, crash and error details, the random app identifier, and how the app and its learning features are used: screens and lessons viewed, questions attempted, the option chosen and whether it was right, time taken, papers, labs and builds used, progress estimates, the suggestions the app made and what happened next and purchase-screen interactions. What you type into the coverage-map search box is used on your device and is not sent.

Information you choose to send. A diagnostic report (shown to you before you send it) with any note you add; feedback, suggestions or problem reports with the category, your message and any optional details you pick; and, only if you add one so that we can reply, your email address.

We do not ask the app to collect your name, contacts, precise location, photographs, files or an advertising identifier.

How we use it

To run, secure, support and fix the app; to personalise and improve the learning experience; to understand which parts help; for research, development and analytics concerning our apps and educational content; to answer and act on what you send; and to meet legal obligations. Information may also pass to a successor as part of a financing, merger, acquisition or similar transaction, subject to the commitments in this policy.

Your choices

  • The usage switch is under Me, then Privacy and data. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, and where the region cannot be determined, it starts off and stays off until you turn it on.
  • Diagnostics and feedback are sent only when you choose to send them.
  • To ask us to delete usage data, use "Delete my usage data" in the same place, or write to diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

Purchases and cited pages

Subscriptions are handled by Apple or Google under their own terms. We do not receive your payment card details; the app learns from the store whether a subscription is active. When you open a cited source, the other organisation's website loads inside the app and its own privacy practices, including cookies, apply.

Storage, service providers and security

What we receive is sent over encrypted connections and kept in our Google Workspace services (including Google Sheets, Drive and email) and processed by providers acting on our behalf. In particular, for copies from either store, our server can email to our own mailboxes the acceptance record, usage and learning batches, crash records, diagnostic reports, feedback and deletion requests, with what they contain. We may forward such mail to another mailbox of ours. We use reasonable safeguards, but no method is completely secure.

How long we keep it

Usage, learning and diagnostic information is kept while it is useful for the purposes above. Feedback and messages are kept while we may need them to answer or to improve the app. We handle deletion requests when we receive them, except where the law requires us to keep something. Handling means deleting by hand, because nothing is erased automatically; email copies stay unless that manual step is taken for them as well.

Where it is processed and legal grounds

Information may be processed in the United States. Where the law requires a legal ground, we rely on your consent or on our legitimate interests in operating and improving the app. Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object; write to us to use them.

Children

We design our apps to collect only the information described in this policy. If you believe information has been provided to us that you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

Changes

If this policy changes in an important way we will update this page and, where appropriate, show the first-run notice again.

Contact
