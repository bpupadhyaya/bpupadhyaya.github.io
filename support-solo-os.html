Support

Support SoloOS

Your business OS — 100% on your device. Zero cloud. Zero fees.

SoloOS is built by a solo developer and funded by the people who use it — not by ads, data sales, or tracking. Your business data stays on your device, always — no server, no cloud, no subscription lock-in. Your support is what keeps it that way and funds new features.

Support the development

The most direct way to support SoloOS is to upgrade to Pro. Every dollar goes straight into development — better AI, more features, and ongoing improvements. No ad networks, no data brokers, nothing about you is ever sold.

  • SoloOS Pro — $6.99/month or $49.99/year. Unlimited clients and invoices, full AI Business Advisor, contract templates, recurring invoices, and all future features. Cancel anytime.

Pro undercuts HoneyBook ($29-109/mo), Bonsai ($9-49/mo), and FreshBooks ($23-70/mo) — at a fraction of the cost, with zero data exposure.

Other ways to help

Support doesn't have to be financial. Each of these genuinely helps an independent, privacy-first app:

  • Rate & review SoloOS on the App Store and Google Play — honest reviews are how privacy tools get discovered.
  • Tell a fellow freelancer or solopreneur. Word of mouth is how an app with no ad budget grows.
  • Share feedback. Feature ideas and bug reports directly shape what gets built next.

Where your support goes

  • On-device AI Business Advisor with real LLM inference (llama.cpp integration)
  • Encrypted local storage (SQLCipher) for maximum business data security
  • Recurring invoices and iCloud/Google Drive backup sync
  • More contract templates and proposal intelligence
  • Keeping the Free tier genuinely useful for early-stage solopreneurs
