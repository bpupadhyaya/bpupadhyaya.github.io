Subscriptions

Restore: open You, then Restore purchases. Cancel or change: open You, then Manage subscription, or use your store account's subscription settings. Cancelling takes effect at the end of the paid period.

Text size and tablets

A- and A+ at the top of the screen change text size from 50% to 300%. Tablets use wider layouts in both orientations.

Privacy and diagnostics

Under You, then Privacy and data, you can change the usage switch, send diagnostics (you see them first), send feedback or ask us to delete usage data.

A mistake in a question?

Use Report under the explanation, or email us with the question text.
