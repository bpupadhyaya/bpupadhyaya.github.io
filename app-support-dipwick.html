Dipwick: Support

Dipwick is a car-care logbook for iPhone and iPad from EqualInformation, LLC. Email support@equalinformation.com and tell us your device, iOS version and what happened.

Common questions

Where is my data? Only on your device. If you delete the app, the logbook goes with it. Use Settings, Export, to save a CSV or PDF first.

The suggested interval differs from my manual. The manual wins. Open Service plan, tap Edit on the line, and set your own distance and months.

How do I log something quickly? On the Garage screen, type a sentence such as "oil change 52,300 mi $89" and press go. Check the filled-in form, then save.

How is fuel economy worked out? From two full tanks in a row: distance between them divided by the fuel added. Mark each fill-up as a full tank for best results.

Reminders do not arrive. Turn on Service reminders in Dipwick Settings and allow notifications for Dipwick in iOS Settings.

Remove ads. Settings, Remove ads: a monthly subscription, or watch rewarded videos for ad-free time. Restore purchases is on the same screen. Manage or cancel the subscription in your Apple ID settings.
