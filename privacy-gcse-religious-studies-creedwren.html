Privacy Policy

GCSE Religious Studies: Creedwren

1. Introduction and scope

EqualInformation, LLC ("we") publishes GCSE Religious Studies: Creedwren ("the app"), an independent revision companion for GCSE Religious Studies. This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled when you use the app from version 1.0.0. The app has no user accounts. Effective 25 November 2026.

2. Information we collect

Information you choose to provide: a diagnostic report that you decide to send, with any note you add, and feedback or suggestions you submit (category, message, and optionally a role, source link and rating). An email address is received only if you ask us to reply to you. Diagnostic and app-usage information, collected with your agreement or as permitted by applicable law: device and operating-system information (model, system version, processor, memory, display size, language, region, time zone, accessibility and display settings), app version, performance and memory indicators, crash and error information, a random app identifier, and how features of the app are used. This includes how learning features are used and perform: lessons viewed, questions attempted, whether answers were correct, time taken, progress estimates, the study suggestions the app makes and what happens next, answer-builder activity, and interactions with the subscription screen. When you accept the first-run notice an acceptance record is always sent (consent identifier, time, app, platform, version, notice version and a hash of its text, the state of the usage setting, the random app identifier, country, locale and time zone). We do not collect your name, contacts, precise location, photographs, files, advertising identifiers or other text you enter in the app.

3. How we use information

To operate, maintain, secure and support the app; to diagnose problems; to provide and personalise the learning experience; to understand how the app is used; for research and development; for analytics and improvement of our apps and educational content; to read and act on feedback; and to comply with legal obligations.

4. Your choices

At first launch the app shows a notice and a separate usage switch. The switch is off by default in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and wherever your region cannot be determined. You can change it at any time under Me, Privacy and data. Diagnostics and feedback are sent only when you choose Send. To request deletion of your usage data use Delete my usage data in the app or write to diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

5. Storage and service providers

Information is transmitted over encrypted connections to a web application operated by EqualInformation, LLC and recorded in Google Workspace services (Google Sheets and Drive); diagnostic reports and acceptance notices are also delivered by email. Google acts as a service provider on our behalf. Subscription payments are handled by Apple or Google; we do not receive your payment details.

6. Retention

Usage, learning and diagnostic information is kept while it remains useful for the purposes above. Deletion requests are handled on request.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures, including encryption in transit. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. International users and legal bases

Where a legal basis is required we rely on your consent and on our legitimate interest in maintaining and improving the app. Information may be processed in the United States.

9. Children

We design our apps to collect only the information described in this policy. If you believe information has been provided to us that you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

10. Changes to this policy

If we make a material change, the version of the first-run notice changes and the notice is shown again.

11. Contact

EqualInformation, LLC. General questions: support@equalinformation.com. Data and deletion requests: diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

Examination board names and specification codes (AQA, Pearson Edexcel, OCR, WJEC Eduqas) are used only to describe course content. This independent app is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by any of them or by any religious organisation, and it does not predict or guarantee any grade.
