Civicwell: Citizenship Test, privacy policy

Publisher: Equal Information (equalinformation.com). Last updated 9 November 2026.

What stays on your device

Civicwell works fully offline. Your study progress, review schedule, streak, test date, interview history, the state and officeholder names you type into your profile, and your text-size choice are stored only on your device. We do not run servers for the app, we have no accounts, and we cannot see this data. Speech is produced on the device by the system voice; the app does not record or send your voice.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. After you answer Apple's tracking prompt and, where required, Google's consent form, AdMob may use an advertising identifier and device and usage data to show and measure ads, and to personalise them if you allow it. You can change your choice at any time in the iOS Settings app, and in the app under Settings, Privacy choices. The optional monthly ad-free subscription ($2.99 per month, billed by Apple) removes banner and interstitial ads. You can also earn ad-free time by watching optional rewarded videos.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple. We receive no payment details. Manage or cancel under Settings, Apple ID, Subscriptions.

Data we collect

We do not collect personal data ourselves. Google AdMob, acting for us, may collect device identifiers, coarse location from the network address, and ad interaction data as described in Google's advertising policy page.

Children

Civicwell is for adults preparing for the naturalization civics test and is not directed at children under 13.

Not legal advice

Civicwell is independent study software. It is not made by, affiliated with or endorsed by U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services or any government body, and it gives no legal or immigration advice. Always check uscis.gov for the rules that apply to you.
