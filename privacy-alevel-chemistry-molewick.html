A Level Chemistry: Molewick: Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Contact: support@equalinformation.com. Last updated 2026-11-27.

1. Introduction and scope

This Privacy Policy describes how EqualInformation, LLC ("we", "us") handles information in connection with A Level Chemistry: Molewick (the "app"), version 1.0 and later. It applies to the app only. The app has no account system.

2A. Information you choose to provide

Diagnostic reports: when you open Settings, Privacy and data, and choose Send diagnostics, the app shows you the exact content of the report, and it is sent only after you tap Send, together with any note you add. Feedback and suggestions: when you choose to send feedback, a suggestion or a problem report, we receive the category, your message, an optional role and level, an optional source link, an optional rating and, only if you tick the contact option, an email address for a reply.

2B. Diagnostic and app-usage information

With your agreement, or as permitted by applicable law, the app can send diagnostic and app-usage information while the optional usage setting is on: app version and build; device model; operating system and version; processor cores and architecture; graphics processor name; memory; screen and window size; language, region and time zone; text-size, display and accessibility settings; a random app identifier; performance and memory indicators; crash and error information; and how the app is used, including which screens, lessons, labs and builds are opened, which tests and quizzes are started and finished, the questions you attempt, whether your answers are correct, the time taken, topic-level progress estimates, the adaptive choices the app makes for you and how you respond to them, and interactions with the purchase screen. From version 1.1, these records also say where this copy of the app was installed from (for example the App Store, TestFlight, Google Play or another installer), whether it is a release or a test build, whether it is running on an emulator or simulator, the day the app was first opened and how many times it has been opened.

2C. Information we do not collect

The app does not collect your name, your contacts, your precise location, your photographs or files, an advertising identifier, or text you type into it, except what you choose to send as feedback or as a note with a diagnostic report. No question in the app asks for a typed answer. The search box on the Coverage screen filters the list on your device, and the words you type in it are not included in diagnostic or app-usage information. Your progress stays on your device unless the usage setting is on.

3. How we use information

To operate, maintain, secure and support the app; to diagnose problems; to provide and personalise the learning experience; to understand how the app is used; for research and development; for analytics and improvement of our apps and educational content; to read and act on feedback and suggestions; and to comply with legal obligations.

4. Your choices

When you first open the app, a notice explains these practices and offers a separate usage switch. The switch is off by default in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and where your region cannot be determined, until you turn it on, and is on elsewhere; you may change it at any time under Settings, Privacy and data. When it is off, the app sends only a minimal record that you accepted the notice. From version 1.1 that record also carries the install source, build type, emulator or simulator flag, first-launch day and launch count, alongside the app version, a random app identifier and your country, language region and time zone. Diagnostics and feedback are sent only when you choose to send them. To ask us to delete usage information associated with your app identifier, use Delete my usage data under Settings, Privacy and data, or email diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

5. Storage and service providers

Information is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections to a web application operated by EqualInformation, LLC that records it in our Google Workspace services (Google Sheets and Drive). Our server also writes to our own mailboxes about store-installed copies of Molewick, and these messages hold the records: consent to the notice, your lab and question activity in batches, crashes, diagnostics, feedback and deletion requests. Such mail may be forwarded to another of our mailboxes. Google acts as a service provider on our behalf. Subscription purchases are handled by Apple or Google; we learn only whether a subscription is active.

6. Retention

Usage, learning and diagnostic information is kept for as long as it is useful for the purposes described above. A deletion request from section 4 does not trigger any automatic erasure; we remove stored records manually. Messages already emailed to us are kept unless they are manually deleted too.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information, including encryption in transit. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. International users and legal bases

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we rely on your consent (the usage switch and the actions you take to send diagnostics or feedback) and on our legitimate interest in maintaining and improving the app. Information may be processed in the United States.

9. Children

We design our apps to collect only the information described in this policy. If you believe information has been provided to us that you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If a change is material, the version of the first-run notice changes and the notice is shown again.
