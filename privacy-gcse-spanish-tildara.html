GCSE Spanish Prep: Tildara: Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Contact: support@equalinformation.com. Last updated 2026-11-27.

1. Introduction and scope

This policy describes how EqualInformation, LLC (we, us) handles information in connection with GCSE Spanish Prep: Tildara, version 1.0 and later (the app). It applies to the app only. There is no account. Lessons, vocabulary, questions and your progress are stored on your own device, and the free part of the app works offline.

2. Information we collect

A. Information you choose to provide. Diagnostic reports: Settings, Privacy and data, Send diagnostics shows the complete report before anything leaves your device, and it is sent only when you tap Send. Feedback and suggestions: a category, your message (with email addresses, telephone numbers and links removed automatically), and, if you supply them, a role, a context, a source link and a rating. An email address is received only when you tick the option to be contacted, and is used only to reply.

B. Diagnostic and app-usage information, collected with your agreement or as permitted by applicable law while the optional usage setting is on: app version and build; device model; operating system and version; processor and memory details; screen size; language, region and time zone; text-size and display settings; network type; whether free or Plus is active; a randomly generated app identifier; session timing; crash and error information; performance indicators; the screens, lessons, quizzes, tests, mock papers, vocabulary reviews, verb drills, tasks, labs and builds you use; the questions you attempt, whether answers are correct and the time taken; progress estimates by topic; the choices the adaptive engine makes and what follows; and subscription-screen interactions. A single word typed in a verb drill may be included in shortened form with recognisable email addresses, telephone numbers and links removed.

When you accept the first-run notice, a short acceptance record is always sent: a consent identifier, the time, the app, platform, version and build, the notice version, the state of the usage setting, the random app identifier, and your country, locale and time zone.

C. Speaking practice. When you record yourself in the speaking studio, the audio is captured, played back and deleted on your device. It is not uploaded and is not collected by us. Text you write in writing and translation tasks also stays on your device.

D. We do not collect your name, contacts, precise location, photographs, files or advertising identifiers.

3. How we use information

To operate, maintain, secure and support the app; to diagnose problems; to provide and personalise the learning experience; to understand how the app is used; for research and development; for analytics and to improve our apps and educational content; to read and act on feedback; and to meet legal obligations.

4. Your choices

When you first open the app a notice explains these practices and offers a separate usage switch. It starts off in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and wherever your region cannot be determined, until you turn it on; elsewhere it starts on. You can change it at any time under More, Settings, Privacy and data. Diagnostics and feedback are sent only when you tap Send. The microphone permission is requested only when you first record, and you can withdraw it in your device settings. To request deletion of your usage data, use Delete my usage data in the app or email diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

5. Storage and service providers

Information is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections to a web application operated by EqualInformation, LLC that records it in our Google Workspace services (Google Sheets and Drive). For a Tildara installed from a store, emails to mailboxes under our control can reproduce what the app sent. Included are your notice acceptance, records of practice and app use, error crashes, feedback, diagnostic reports and deletion requests, and the mail may be relayed to another mailbox we control. Google acts for us as a service provider. Payments for Tildara Plus are processed by Apple or Google and we do not receive your payment details. Links to reference pages open public websites that follow their own privacy practices.

6. Retention

Usage, learning and diagnostic information is kept while it remains useful for the purposes above. To act on a request, someone here has to delete the records; the system will not do it unprompted. Email copies are untouched by that and remain unless separately removed by hand.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures, including encryption in transit. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. International users and legal bases

Where a legal basis is required, we rely on your agreement (the usage switch and your decision to send diagnostics or feedback) and on our legitimate interest in operating and improving the app. Information may be processed in the United States.

9. Children

We design our apps to collect only the information described in this policy. If you believe information has been provided to us that you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the version of the first-run notice changes and the notice is shown again.
