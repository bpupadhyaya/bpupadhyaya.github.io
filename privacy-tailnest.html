TAILNEST

Privacy policy

Updated 7 November 2026. Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC.

Your pet records stay on your device

Tailnest has no accounts and we run no server that receives your pets, entries, weights, meal plans, vet notes or photos. They are saved in the app's private storage on your iPhone or iPad. Deleting the app deletes them. We cannot see them and cannot recover them.

Photos

Photos you choose are copied into the app's private storage and are used only for the photo timeline, pet pictures and receipts. They are not uploaded by Tailnest.

Reminders and widgets

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. Widgets read a short list of upcoming items (pet name, title, date) that the app keeps in an iOS keychain item shared only between Tailnest and its own widget extension. It holds no notes or photos.

Sharing and exports

PDF records and household files are created only when you ask, and go only where you send them with the iOS share sheet. A household file contains pets, entries and meals without photos.

Advertising

The free app shows ads from Google AdMob. At first launch Tailnest asks for App Tracking Transparency permission and then, where the law requires, shows Google's consent form. If you decline tracking, ads are not personalised. AdMob may collect device identifiers, ad interactions and approximate location to serve and measure ads, under Google's privacy policy. Your pet records are never given to AdMob.

Subscription

The optional ad-free monthly subscription is handled by Apple. We receive no payment details. You can manage or cancel it in Settings, then your Apple ID, then Subscriptions.

Not veterinary advice

Tailnest is a record keeper. Vaccine intervals and weight ranges shown are general published guidance and are not veterinary advice. Ask your vet about your pet.

Children

Tailnest is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
