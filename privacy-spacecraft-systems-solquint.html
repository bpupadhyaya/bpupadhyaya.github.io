Spacecraft Systems: Solquint Privacy Policy

Spacecraft Systems: Solquint is published by EqualInformation, LLC. This policy was last updated on 2026-11-23.

Summary

This policy explains how Solquint, a Spacecraft Systems course app, handles information. In short: there is no sign-up, and we run no account system or user database.

Your data stays on your device

Your answers, mastery levels, review dates, chosen study path, rewarded-ad unlock times and settings are stored only on your device so the app works offline. They are not uploaded to us, and deleting the app deletes them.

What the app does not collect

The app never requests location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, health information or identity details. Personal data is not sold.

Advertising and rewarded videos

The free app shows Google AdMob advertising at natural breaks, never during a timed test or mock exam. You can also choose to watch an optional rewarded video to unlock Plus features for 24 hours. On iOS the App Tracking Transparency prompt comes first, then, where the law requires it, Google's consent message (UMP), and only then does the ad SDK start. If you allow it, Google receives an advertising identifier and ad-interaction data to show and measure ads; if you decline, ads are not personalised. More: how Google uses data from ads.

Age

This is an undergraduate-level study app for adults and older students, not for children under 13; we do not knowingly gather data from them.

Optional subscription

You may subscribe, monthly and auto-renewing, to remove ads and unlock every Plus feature. No trial period is offered. The store shows the price before purchase; the plan continues until you cancel in your App Store or Google Play account. Apple or Google processes the payment, so card details never reach us, and the app reads only whether the subscription is active. Our terms of use have more.

Choices, questions and requests

You can switch tracking off at any time under iOS Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking, and revisit the consent message from About when it is available. Questions, access requests and deletion requests go to bpupadhyaya@equalinformation.com; see also the support page.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
