Spacecraft Systems: Solquint Privacy Policy

Spacecraft Systems: Solquint is published by EqualInformation, LLC. This policy was last updated on 2026-11-23.

Summary

Solquint is a study app for a Spacecraft Systems course. This page says what the app stores, what it shares and with whom. There is no registration and no account.

What stays on the device

Your answers, mastery levels, review dates, chosen study path and settings are stored only on your device so the app works offline. They are not uploaded to us, and deleting the app deletes them.

What we never ask for

No location, contacts, photo library, camera, microphone or health data is requested, and nothing about you is sold.

Advertising and tracking

The app shows no advertising and collects no advertising data. It does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, does not use an advertising identifier and does not ask for tracking permission.

Age

This is an undergraduate-level study app for adults and older students, not for children under 13; we do not knowingly gather data from them.

Monthly subscription

Subscribing is optional: one auto-renewing monthly plan, Solquint Plus Monthly, unlocks all Plus features, with no free trial. The price is displayed by the store before you confirm, billing is done by Apple or Google, and cancelling is done in your store account. We receive only the active or inactive status. Read the terms of use.

Your choices and data requests

For privacy questions or requests to access or delete data we might hold about you, write to bpupadhyaya@equalinformation.com; the support page has more.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
