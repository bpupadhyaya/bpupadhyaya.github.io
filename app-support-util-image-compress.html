Slimwick: Image Compressor: Support

Slimwick compresses and resizes photos to a target file size or pixel size on your phone, and can remove location and camera details from copies.

Common questions

  • Are my photos uploaded? No. Compression happens on your device and the app works in airplane mode.
  • The limit could not be met. Some images cannot reach a very small size without becoming unreadable. Slimwick tells you plainly; try a larger size limit or smaller pixel dimensions.
  • What is removed when I strip details? Slimwick shows a list of what was removed (such as location, date and camera details) and what was kept.
  • Are the presets official? No. Presets are generic size targets. Check the current notice for your form before you upload.
  • How do I remove ads? Open the in-app purchase screen (Settings or More, or the ad-free prompt) and choose the ad-free subscription or the one-time purchase. You can also watch an optional short video where offered for temporary extras.
  • How do I restore a purchase? Open Settings (or the purchase screen) and tap Restore purchases, using the same Apple Account or Google account you bought with. This is needed after reinstalling or changing device.
  • How do I cancel the subscription? iPhone and iPad: Settings, your name, Subscriptions. Android: Google Play, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel it.
  • Can I change my ad choices? Yes. Use the privacy choices for ads in the app's Settings where shown, or your device's privacy settings.
