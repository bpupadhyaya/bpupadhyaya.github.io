A Level Accounting: Tickmarrow: Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 November 2026

This policy describes what the A Level Accounting: Tickmarrow app for iPhone, iPad and Android does with information. EqualInformation, LLC ("we") publishes the app, which is a revision aid for A-level Accounting.

Kept on your device

Your answers, learning record, study plan, practice paper answers and any text you have typed but not yet sent live in the app's own storage on your phone or tablet, and are deleted if you delete the app. There are no user accounts and the app never asks who you are.

The record made when you accept the notice

Accepting the first-run notice sends a single record, even if usage sharing is off. It holds: the time; the notice's version, its wording and a fingerprint of that wording; how long the notice was on screen and whether you scrolled to the end; the sharing choice you made; a random record number, a random app number and a count of visits; the app's identifier, version and build; the platform; where the app was installed from; whether this is a test or a store build and whether the device is an emulator; and the device's region, locale and time zone. With sharing on, the device model and system version are added.

Usage and learning information you can switch on

Nothing in this section leaves the device unless the sharing switch is on. The switch is set to off at first when the device reports a region in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, in certain other places with comparable rules, or when no region can be read; in every other place it is on at first, and you can turn it off before you accept. While it is on, the app sends:

  • learning activity: which items you tried, which choice you selected, whether you were right, how long you took, the type of exercise and the app's estimate of how secure each topic is;
  • each suggestion the app made, the reason it gave and whether you followed it;
  • how you use features: pages, client files, benches (with the slider settings), switches between views, text size, the study plan, cited pages, explanations and the subscription screen;
  • when visits start, how long they last, the screen layout and the number of visits;
  • fault records: the kind of fault and where in the program it happened, which for a crash reported by the phone can include the system's brief note on why the app stopped.

Every message also carries the technical items listed above: app identifier, platform, version, build, random app number, visit number, build type, install source and the emulator flag. The figures you type and the long answers you write are never included. Switching off ends optional sending and throws away anything still queued on the device.

What you decide to send

Diagnostics. The app shows you exactly what would be sent and sends it only when you press the button. That is: your optional note, the device model and system version, locale and time zone, which view, focus and text size you use, whether usage sharing is on, whether you have free or Plus access, a short log of app events and recent fault signatures, plus the technical items above.

Feedback. Your message, its category, any rating, any role, course, level or source link you add, the screen or item it concerns, and your email address only if you tick the box allowing us to reply. Tapping that an explanation helped or was unclear counts as feedback.

Deletion requests. The request itself and your random app number.

Because you ask for these to be sent, they go even when usage sharing is off. The app tries to strip contact details from notes, and our server removes some from feedback, but neither step is guaranteed, so please leave personal details out. A source link and a contact email go exactly as typed.

Our purposes

We use the information to run, secure and repair the app, to find faults, to tailor and study how learning in the app works, to improve our apps and teaching material, to reply to you, to prove the notice was accepted and to meet legal duties. We do not use it to follow you across other companies' apps or sites, and the app does not use an advertising identifier.

Where it is stored and who sees it

Messages are sent over an encrypted connection to a script that we run in our Google Workspace account. It writes them to our Google Sheets and Drive files, including an unaltered copy of accepted messages. Google is our service provider; as with any web service it sees connection details such as an IP address in order to deliver a request, but our script does not write that address into our records.

Copies by email. For store installs, acceptance records, diagnostics, deletion requests, fault records, feedback and usage and learning information may also be emailed to our company mailboxes, and mail arriving in one of them may be passed to another.

We may hand over information where the law demands it, or in a financing, merger or sale of our business, on the terms of this policy.

Keeping and deleting it

Our server does not remove anything on a timer and we have set no fixed retention period; records remain until a person deletes them.

To ask for deletion, choose Records, Privacy and data, Send deletion request in the app, or write to diagnostics@equalinformation.com with your app number if you have it. We carry out deletion by hand. Copies that were emailed to our mailboxes stay until removed by hand too, so tell us if you want those removed. Records with no identifier you can give us may be impossible to find.

What you can choose

You may keep usage sharing off, change it whenever you like, decline to send diagnostics or feedback, and ask us to delete your records. Depending on where you live you may also be entitled to see, correct, limit or object to our use of information about you, to withdraw consent and to complain to a data protection authority. Where a legal basis is needed we rely on your consent for optional usage sharing and for what you choose to send, and on our legitimate interest in keeping proof of acceptance and operating the app.

International processing

Our service providers may process information in the United States and elsewhere.

Subscriptions

Payment for a subscription is taken by Apple or Google under their own terms; we never see card details. The app asks the store on your device whether a subscription is active.

Other websites

Cited pages belong to other organisations and open inside the app. When you open one, that site receives an ordinary web request and its own privacy policy applies.

Children and guardians

A parent or guardian with a question, or a request to delete records, about a child's use of the app can write to support@equalinformation.com.

Safeguards

We restrict who can reach stored records. No method of storing or sending information is completely secure.

Updates and contact

We will change the date at the top when this policy changes. EqualInformation, LLC: support@equalinformation.com.
