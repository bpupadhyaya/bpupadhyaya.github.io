A Level Accounting: Tickmarrow: Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 November 2026

This policy explains what the A Level Accounting: Tickmarrow app for iPhone, iPad and Android handles. The app is published by EqualInformation, LLC ("we"). It is a study aid for A-level Accounting.

What stays on your device

Your answers, your learning record, your study plan, your practice paper answers and anything you have typed but not sent are kept in the app's storage on your device. Removing the app removes them. We do not run user accounts and the app does not ask for your name.

The acceptance record

When you accept the first-run notice, the app sends one record whether or not usage sharing is on. It contains the time, the notice version, its text and a digest of that text, how long the screen was open and whether you reached its end, your sharing choice, a random record number, a random app number and a visit number, the app's identifier, version and build, your platform, how the app was installed, whether it is a test or release build and whether the device is an emulator, and your device's region, locale and time zone. If sharing is on, it also contains your device model and system version.

Optional usage and learning information

This is sent only while the sharing switch is on. The switch starts off where the device's region is in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, in a few places that apply similar rules, and wherever the region is unknown. Elsewhere it starts on and you can turn it off before accepting. With it on, the app sends:

  • learning activity: the items attempted, the choice picked on choice items, whether an answer was right, the time taken, the kind of exercise, and estimates of how secure each topic is;
  • what the app suggested, the reason it gave and whether you took the suggestion up;
  • use of features: pages, client files, benches (including the slider values used), changes of view, text size and study plan, opening of cited pages and explanations, and what happens on the subscription screen;
  • the start and length of visits, screen layout and a visit count;
  • fault records: the type of fault and its position in the program code. A fault record from the phone's system may include the system's own short description of why the app stopped.

Each message carries the technical items named above: app identifier, platform, version, build, random app number, visit number, build type, install source and the emulator flag. Figures you type and long answers you write are not included. Turning the switch off stops further optional records and discards those still waiting on your device.

What you choose to send

Diagnostics. Nothing is sent until you press the send button, and the screen first shows what will go: your optional note, device model and system version, locale, time zone, the view, focus and text size you use, whether usage sharing is on, whether you have free or Plus access, a short log of app events and recent fault signatures, with the technical items above.

Feedback. Your message, category, rating, and any role, course, level or source link you add, the screen or item it concerns, and your email address only if you tick the box that allows us to contact you. A helped or unclear tap on an explanation is feedback too.

Deletion requests. The request and your random app number.

These are sent even when usage sharing is off, because you ask for them to be sent. The app tries to remove contact details from notes before sending, and our server removes some from feedback messages, but neither is certain. Please do not include personal information. A source link and a contact email are sent as you type them.

Why we use this information

To operate, protect and repair the app; to find and fix faults; to personalise and study how learning in the app works; to improve our apps and teaching material; to answer you; to keep a record that the notice was accepted; and to meet legal duties. We do not use it to track you across other companies' apps or websites, and the app has no advertising identifier.

Where it goes and who can see it

Messages travel over an encrypted connection to a script we run in our Google Workspace, which stores them in our Google Sheets and Google Drive files, including a raw copy of accepted messages. Google acts as our service provider and, like any web host, handles connection details such as an IP address to deliver a request; our script does not write the IP address into our records.

Email copies. Records from store installs of the app, namely acceptance records, diagnostics, deletion requests, fault records, feedback and usage and learning information, can also be emailed to our company mailboxes. Mail received in one of our mailboxes may be forwarded to another of our mailboxes.

We may disclose information where the law requires it, or as part of a financing, merger or transfer of our business, subject to this policy.

How long it is kept, and deletion

Our server does not delete anything automatically, and we have not set a fixed period. Records stay until someone removes them.

To ask for deletion, use Records, Privacy and data, Send deletion request in the app, or write to diagnostics@equalinformation.com and give your app number if you can. Deletion is carried out by hand. Copies that were emailed to our mailboxes remain unless they are also removed by hand, so ask us if you want that done. We may not be able to find records that carry no identifier you can give us.

Your choices and rights

You can leave usage sharing off, change it at any time, choose not to send diagnostics or feedback, and ask us for deletion. Depending on where you live you may also have rights to access, correct, restrict or object to our use of information about you, to withdraw consent and to complain to a data protection authority. Where the law asks for a legal basis, we rely on your consent for optional usage sharing and for what you choose to send, and on our legitimate interest in keeping a record of acceptance and in running the app.

Transfers

Our service providers may process information in the United States and other countries.

Purchases

Apple or Google handles any subscription payment under its own terms. We do not receive your card details. The app asks the store on your device whether a subscription is active.

Links to other sites

Cited pages are the websites of other organisations, shown inside the app. When you open one, that site receives an ordinary web request and applies its own privacy policy.

Children

A parent or guardian with a question or a deletion request about a child's use of the app can write to us at support@equalinformation.com.

Security

We limit who can reach the stored records. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Changes and contact

We will change the date above when this policy changes. EqualInformation, LLC: support@equalinformation.com.
