Amortlume: Mortgage Calculator 3D, Support

Amortlume turns a mortgage into a payment breakdown, a month-by-month schedule, a 3D stack of principal and interest by year, an extra-payment planner and a rent versus buy comparison. Everything works offline.

Common questions

My lender's payment is a little different. Lenders round differently, include escrow items you may not have entered, or use points and fees. Enter your exact rate and amounts; the payment formula is the standard one.

How do I make the text bigger? Use A+ at the top left of any screen, or iOS Settings, Display, Text Size.

How do I remove ads? More, Remove ads: subscribe monthly, or watch rewarded videos to earn ad-free time. Use Restore purchases on a new device.

I found a wrong number. Please tell us: use Report an error in More, Accuracy and sources.
