IB Global Politics: Sovrelle: Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Contact: support@equalinformation.com. Last updated 2026-11-27.

1. Introduction and scope

This Privacy Policy explains how EqualInformation, LLC ("we", "us") handles information in connection with IB Global Politics: Sovrelle (the "app"), version 1.0 and later, and applies to the app only. The app has no account system and works offline: lessons, case summaries, questions, simulations, your notes and your progress are stored on your device.

2A. Information you choose to provide

Diagnostic reports: if you open Settings, Privacy and data and choose Send diagnostics, the app shows you the full content of the report and it is sent only after you tap Send.

Feedback and suggestions: if you choose to send feedback, a suggestion or a problem report, we receive the category and your message (up to 2,000 characters, from which email addresses, telephone numbers and links are removed automatically) and, where you supply them, a role (for example IB student, teacher or tutor, parent or guardian, or other), a level or context, a source link and a rating from 1 to 5. We receive an email address only if you tick the option allowing us to reply, and we use it only for that reply.

2B. Diagnostic and app-usage information

With your agreement, or as permitted by applicable law, the app can send diagnostic and app-usage information while the optional usage setting is on. This comprises: app version and build; device model; operating system and version; processor details; graphics processor name; memory; screen size; language, region and time zone; text-size setting and a summary of accessibility and display settings; network type; a battery-level range; whether the free or Plus entitlement is active; a randomly generated app identifier that is not an advertising identifier; session timing; the screens you open and the time spent on them; lesson activity such as scroll depth and time; your quiz, practice, spaced-review and mock-paper attempts (the question, its topic and course component, whether the answer was correct, the time taken and the option chosen); progress estimates for each part of the course; the adaptive-learning decisions the app makes and their outcomes; your use of the Classical and Real-World views, study-plan pins, focus and exam-date choices; use of simulations and missions (control names and value ranges, never text you write); the sources and cross-references you open; interactions with the subscription screen; performance indicators such as render times and dropped-frame ranges; and crash and error information.

When you accept the first-run notice, a short acceptance record is always sent, whatever the setting: a consent identifier, the time, the app, platform, version and build, the notice version and a fingerprint of its text, whether the usage setting was on, the random app identifier, your country, locale region and time zone. If the usage setting is on, the device information listed above is added.

2C. Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, contacts, precise location, photographs, files, advertising identifiers or an account. The app has no field in which text you type is sent to us, other than the feedback message you choose to send. Notes you write for yourself stay on your device.

3. How we use information

We use the information to operate, maintain, secure and support the app; to diagnose problems; to provide and personalise the learning experience; to understand how the app is used; for research and development; for analytics and improvement of our apps and educational content; to read and act on feedback; and to comply with legal obligations.

4. Your choices

When you first open the app you see a notice and a separate usage-information switch. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and where your region cannot be determined, the switch starts off until you turn it on; elsewhere it starts on. You can change it at any time under Settings, Privacy and data. Diagnostics and feedback are sent only when you choose. To request deletion of usage information linked to your app identifier, use Delete my usage data in Settings, Privacy and data, or write to diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

5. Storage and service providers

Information is stored in our Google Workspace services (Google Sheets and Drive) and notification email, and is processed by service providers acting on our behalf. Sovrelle's collector may email us the body of records that come from store-installed copies: the notice acceptance, political studies learning batches, app activity, crashes, diagnostic reports, feedback and requests for deletion. Our own mailboxes receive these and may forward them to one another. It is transmitted over encrypted connections. Subscription payments are handled by the store through which you subscribe; we do not receive your payment details.

6. Retention

Usage and learning information is kept for as long as it is useful for the purposes in section 3. Deletion requests are handled when we receive them, by a person removing records. The collector itself deletes nothing, and emailed copies survive unless that person removes them too.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. International users and legal bases

Where the law requires a legal basis, we rely on your consent (the usage setting and the acts of sending diagnostics or feedback) or on our legitimate interests in operating and improving the app. Information may be processed in the United States.

9. Children

We design our apps to collect only the information described in this policy. If you believe information has been provided to us that you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

10. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes materially we update the date above and, where the first-run notice changes, its version, so that the notice is shown again.

11. Contact

EqualInformation, LLC, support@equalinformation.com. Deletion and data requests: diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
