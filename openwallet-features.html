Human Value Recognition (Articles I, III, IV)

Features that directly implement the core mission: recognizing and rewarding human contribution.

Multi-Channel OTK Tokens

Direct — Art I, III▼

OTK flows through 6 distinct value channels, each representing a dimension of human contribution:

  • nOTK (Nurture) — Parenting, caregiving, emotional support
  • eOTK (Education) — Teaching, mentoring, knowledge transfer
  • hOTK (Health) — Healthcare, wellness, nutrition
  • cOTK (Community) — Neighborhood service, volunteer work
  • xOTK (Economic) — Financial transactions, trade
  • gOTK (Governance) — Civic participation, voting, leadership

Implements: Article I (Foundational Principles), Article III Section 1 (The Nature of OTK)

Living Ledger

Direct — Art IV▼

A lifelong on-chain record of every value transfer — received, given, minted, burned — broken down by channel. Your Living Ledger tells the story of your contributions to humanity.

  • Channel breakdown (nOTK, eOTK, hOTK, cOTK, xOTK, gOTK)
  • Contribution score calculation
  • Ring attribution tracking (Self → Family → Mentors → Community → City → Country → Humanity)
  • Gratitude transaction history

Implements: Article IV (The Living Ledger) — "Every positive human contribution is recorded permanently."

Gratitude Transactions

Direct — Art III▼

Thank parents, teachers, and mentors with on-chain gratitude messages. Gratitude transactions are celebrated on Open Chain — a permanent record of appreciation. Four celebration tiers: Heartfelt, Great, Epic, Legendary.

Implements: Article III Section 4 — "A grown child sending nOTK to their parent's Universal ID is a Gratitude Transaction."

Milestone Oracle Network

Direct — Art III▼

Verifiers attest to human development milestones. When enough independent verifiers approve (minimum 2), OTK is minted for the achiever. Verifier accuracy is tracked; bad verifiers are suspended.

  • Register as a milestone verifier
  • Approve/reject pending milestones
  • Accuracy tracking with auto-suspension below 60%
  • Regional milestone definitions (35+ milestones across 5 regions)

Implements: Article III Section 2 (Milestone-Based Minting) — "OTK is minted when verified human development milestones are achieved."

Soulbound Achievement NFTs

Direct — Art I, IV▼

Non-transferable milestone badges auto-minted alongside OTK. Five levels: Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond. These cannot be bought, sold, or traded — they represent personal human development.

Implements: Article I Section 2 (The Right to Recognition) — "Every act of kindness, mentorship, and care deserves recognition."

Correction Mechanism (Negative OTK)

Direct — Art V▼

Negative value is visible, not ignored. -OTK requires community consensus (3+ independent verifiers). The accused can contest with counter-evidence. Wrong determinations can be reversed via governance.

  • Submit correction reports with evidence
  • 3+ verifiers required (higher threshold than positive milestones)
  • Right to contest and provide counter-evidence
  • Governance can reverse wrong determinations

Implements: Article V (Correction Mechanism) — "Making harm visible creates pressure for correction."

Appeal & Contest

Direct — Art V▼

Every person has the right to contest a correction report. Submit appeals with counter-evidence, witnesses, and detailed timelines. Appeals progress through stages: submitted → reviewed → hearing → resolved.

  • Evidence attachment (document hashes, chain records, video references)
  • Witness list with UID confirmation
  • Arbiter-mediated hearings
  • Resolution outcomes: upheld, reversed, modified

Implements: Article V Section 2 — "The right to contest is absolute."

Mediation & Conflict Resolution

Direct — Art V▼

Neutral third-party mediation for disputes between community members. Mediators are matched by expertise and community trust score. Resolution agreements are signed on-chain by both parties.

  • Dispute types: financial, community, identity, governance, family
  • Mediator matching with trust scores and expertise areas
  • Session notes and agreement drafting
  • On-chain resolution with dual-party signatures

Implements: Article V — Restorative justice through community-mediated resolution.

Correction Enforcement

Direct — Art V▼

When corrections are confirmed by community consensus, -OTK is automatically burned from the target's account. Governance can reverse wrong determinations, restoring the burned OTK. All enforcement is recorded in the Living Ledger.

Implements: Article V Section 1 — "Negative value is visible, not ignored."

Community Health Dashboard

Direct — Art V, X▼

Regional aggregate tracking: net OTK trends by region, channel health, alert zones for communities trending negative. This is the self-correcting feedback loop — resources and attention flow to where they're needed.

12 regions tracked: Global, South Asia, East Asia, Southeast Asia, East Africa, West Africa, Northern Europe, Southern Europe, North America, South America, Middle East, Oceania.

Implements: Article V Section 3 (Autonomous Correction) + Article X (The Vision) — "Nations measured not by GDP, but by aggregate human value."

Inter-Regional Cooperation

Direct — Art X▼

Track and promote partnerships between regions. Education exchanges, healthcare capacity building, cultural programs, and resource sharing. Each partnership has milestones, OTK exchanged, and beneficiary counts.

  • Partnership types: education, health, economic, cultural
  • Cultural exchanges: language learning, artisan skills, music, cuisine
  • Resource sharing: surplus food, medical supplies, educational materials
  • Propose new inter-regional cooperation initiatives

Implements: Article X — "World peace through cooperation, not competition."

Education Curriculum & Pathways

Direct — Art I (eOTK)▼

Visual skill tree with learning pathways across 6 categories: Technology, Science, Arts, Languages, Life Skills, Trade Skills. Each pathway contains milestones that earn eOTK when verified.

  • Pathway progress tracking (modules completed, eOTK earned)
  • Peer study groups (join or create)
  • Mentor assignment per pathway
  • Soulbound certificate on pathway completion

Implements: Article I Section 1 — Education as a dimension of human value.

Health Emergency Network (SOS)

Direct — Art I (hOTK)▼

Emergency SOS button broadcasts your location and ZK-encrypted medical profile to nearby UIDs. Emergency contacts, nearby health workers, first aid guides (offline-accessible), and emergency hOTK fund.

  • Medical profile (blood type, allergies, conditions) — ZK-encrypted, disclosed only in emergencies
  • Nearby community health responders with response times
  • Emergency history with outcomes
  • Regional emergency stats

Implements: Article I — Health as a fundamental value channel.

Community Projects

Direct — Art I (cOTK)▼

Community project management with milestones, funding, and team coordination. Categories: infrastructure, education, environment, health, culture, technology. Each project milestone earns cOTK for contributors.

  • Project lifecycle: planning → fundraising → in progress → completed
  • Team roles: lead, contributor, volunteer
  • Funding progress tracking
  • Impact metrics: beneficiaries, hours contributed, cOTK distributed

Implements: Article I — Community service as a valued contribution.

Time Bank

Direct — Art I▼

Time-based value exchange: 1 hour of teaching = 1 hour of gardening = 1 hour of cooking. All human time is valued equally. Offer and request services, track hours given vs. received.

  • Skill categories: tutoring, cooking, repair, gardening, tech help, and more
  • Community stats: total hours exchanged, most popular skills
  • Ratings and feedback per exchange

Implements: Article I Section 1 — "All dimensions of human contribution are recognized."

Mentor Matching

Direct — Art I (eOTK)▼

Smart matching algorithm connects mentors and mentees based on expertise overlap, availability, and language. Both parties earn eOTK for sessions. On-chain mentoring agreements with goals and milestones.

  • Match score (0-100%) with expertise and language overlap
  • Session logging with notes and feedback
  • Mutual ratings after each session
  • Become a mentor: register expertise, availability, languages

Implements: Article I — Mentoring recognized as a valued human contribution.

Identity & Privacy (Article II, VIII)

Privacy is not a feature — it is a fundamental human right embedded in the protocol.

Universal Identity (UID)

Direct — Art II▼

Privacy-preserving biometric proof: "this is a unique human being" without storing or transmitting biometric data. All processing on-device. No central identity registry exists or can exist.

  • Biometric proof generation (Face ID / fingerprint)
  • SHA-256 hash of proof stored on-chain (not biometric data)
  • Guardian support for minors
  • Self-sovereign upon reaching age of agency

Implements: Article II (Universal Identity) — "Every human being shall be entitled to a unique Universal Identity."

Selective Disclosure (Zero-Knowledge)

Direct — Art II▼

Prove claims without revealing identity: "I am a parent," "I am a certified teacher," "I reside in Region Y." The verifier learns only that the claim is true — nothing else.

Implements: Article II Section 2 (Selective Disclosure) — "Always voluntary. No institution may compel disclosure."

Cross-Chain Identity Linking

Direct — Art II▼

Link BTC, ETH, SOL, Cosmos addresses to your Universal ID with cryptographic proof. One identity across all blockchains. Reputation score (0-1000) based on activity: newcomer → active → trusted → elder → guardian.

Implements: Article II Section 1 — Universal identity that works across all chains.

Post-Quantum Cryptography

Direct — Art VIII▼

ML-DSA + Ed25519 hybrid signatures. If either quantum or classical computers are compromised, the other still protects. Migration path for existing keys. 13 tests passing.

Implements: Article VIII Section 3 (Security) — "Post-quantum cryptography ensures Open Chain remains secure against future threats."

Guardian Accounts

Direct — Art II▼

Parents and elders manage dependent accounts with spending limits, transaction approval, and emergency controls. Multi-guardian support (both parents). Graduation workflow transfers full ownership when a child comes of age.

  • Add dependents (children, elderly family members, wards)
  • Approve/reject transactions above threshold
  • Emergency account freeze
  • Graduation: transfer full control at age of agency

Implements: Article II Section 1 — "Minors receive Guardian UIDs managed by parents until age of agency."

Zero-Knowledge Proof Verification

Direct — Art VIII▼

Generate and verify ZK proofs for selective disclosure. Prove claims without revealing underlying data: age (>18 without birthdate), citizenship (country without passport), income bracket, education level, health status.

  • Proof gallery with active/expired status tracking
  • Generate new proofs via step-by-step wizard
  • Verify others' proofs by scanning or pasting
  • Privacy meter showing data protection level

Implements: Article VIII Section 2 — "Privacy by design. All identity claims use zero-knowledge proofs."

Data Sovereignty

Direct — Art VIII▼

Granular privacy controls for every data category: identity, financial, health, education, location, social, governance. Each toggleable between public/selective/private. Full access log showing who accessed your data and when.

  • 7 data categories with per-category privacy levels
  • Access log with entity names, actions, timestamps
  • Revoke access per entity
  • Export all on-chain data as JSON
  • Privacy score (0-100) based on protection level

Implements: Article VIII Section 1 — "Individuals own their data. No entity may access personal data without explicit, revocable consent."

PQC Key Management

Direct — Art VII, VIII▼

Post-quantum key management with migration wizard. View current key type (Ed25519, hybrid, or ML-DSA-65), PQC readiness score, and quantum threat level. Three-step migration from classical to quantum-safe hybrid signatures.

  • Key comparison: sizes, speeds, NIST levels across all algorithms
  • Migration wizard: generate → configure hybrid → verify → activate
  • Readiness score tracking (% of transactions using hybrid)

Implements: Article VII, VIII — Quantum-safe infrastructure and forward-looking security.

Governance (Article VI)

Democracy — not plutocracy, not technocracy, not oligarchy.

One-Human-One-Vote Governance

Direct — Art VI▼

Every Universal ID holder has exactly one vote. A person with 1 OTK and a person with 1,000,000 OTK each have one vote. Proposals, voting (yes/no/abstain), quorum tracking (>33% of registered UIDs), pass threshold (>50%).

Implements: Article VI Section 1 — "Every Universal ID holder has exactly one vote in Open Chain governance."

Constitutional Amendments

Direct — Art VI▼

The Human Constitution is a living document. Amendments require 90-day public deliberation + two-thirds supermajority. Article I (Foundational Principles) is immutable and cannot be amended.

Implements: Article VI Section 3 — "Amendments require a two-thirds supermajority of participating Universal ID voters."

DAOs (Decentralized Autonomous Organizations)

Indirect — Art VI▼

Create on-chain organizations with treasury, membership, and governance. Support both one-member-one-vote and token-weighted voting. DAOs enable community self-organization.

Community Treasury

Direct — Art VI▼

2% of all OTK minting goes to the community treasury. Distributed via governance proposals. Per-channel balance tracking. Funds development, grants, infrastructure, and community events.

Micro-Grants

Direct — Art VI▼

Apply for small grants funded by the community treasury and DAOs. Grant categories: education, health, infrastructure, environment, arts, technology, small business. Track impact after receiving funding.

  • Browse available grants with amounts, deadlines, applicant counts
  • Apply with milestones and fund usage plan
  • Post-funding milestone tracking and impact reporting

Implements: Article VI — Community-governed resource allocation.

Governance Proposal Execution

Direct — Art VI▼

When proposals pass, they execute automatically on-chain. Proposal types: parameter change, treasury spend, correction reversal, software upgrade, emergency override. DAO proposals auto-execute treasury spends.

Implements: Article VI Section 2 — "Governance decisions are binding and automatically enforced."

Technical Architecture (Article VII)

Mobile-first. Peer-to-peer. Every phone is the network.

100% Peer-to-Peer Network

Direct — Art VII▼

No central servers. TCP+JSON P2P networking with mDNS discovery (local WiFi), peer exchange (internet), block sync, mempool, and gomobile bindings. 10-node testnet generator included.

  • P2P node runner with CometBFT WebSocket client
  • QR-based peer sharing for easy phone-to-phone connection
  • Auto-switch between Server/P2P/Hybrid modes
  • Step-by-step 10-phone setup guide

Implements: Article VII Section 1 — "Phase 3: Full peer-to-peer — every phone is the network."

IBC (Inter-Blockchain Communication)

Direct — Art VII▼

Cross-chain OTK transfers via IBC protocol. Bridge screen with real CosmosSigner.sendIbcTransfer(). Connects Open Chain to 50+ Cosmos ecosystem chains.

Implements: Article VII Section 1 — "IBC enables interoperability with 50+ existing chains."

Inclusivity & Access (Article IX)

Designed for 8 billion humans — not just the technically literate.

Simple Mode

Direct — Art IX▼

Icon-driven interface accessible to anyone — no text needed to understand. Large buttons, very large balance font (56pt), color-coded actions. Designed for elderly users, low-literacy users, and anyone who finds crypto intimidating.

Implements: Article IX — "Open Wallet supports Simple Mode: Accessible to anyone — icon-driven, multilingual."

10 Languages

Direct — Art IX▼

English, Hindi, Spanish, Chinese, Vietnamese, Arabic, Portuguese, French, Japanese, Korean — covering approximately 75% of the world's population.

Implements: Article IX Section 1 — "Multiple languages, starting with those covering 75% of the world's population."

Low Bandwidth Mode

Direct — Art IX▼

Auto-detects 2G/3G/4G/WiFi. On slow connections: reduces refresh intervals, skips non-essential data, caches aggressively, limits concurrent requests. Works on basic smartphones.

Implements: Article IX Section 1 — "Works on basic smartphones and low-bandwidth connections."

Accessibility Settings

Direct — Art IX▼

Font size (S/M/L/XL), high contrast mode, reduce motion, screen reader hints, haptic feedback. No human shall be excluded due to disability.

Implements: Article IX Section 1 — "No human shall be excluded from participation due to... disability."

Regional Milestone Definitions

Direct — Art IX▼

35+ milestones across 5 regions: Global (14), South Asia (Annaprashan, Mundan, Upanayana...), East Africa (Naming Ceremony, Harambee...), Europe (Parental Leave, Gymnasium...), East Asia (Doljanchi, Shichi-Go-San...), Latin America (Quinceañera, Minga...).

Implements: Article IX Section 2 — "Human development milestones may vary across cultures. The system respects this diversity."

DeFi Features

Decentralized finance built into Open Chain.

Staking & Rewards

Indirect — Art VII▼

5% base APY + channel bonus (0.1% per verified milestone) + 1% gratitude bonus. Rewards distributed in channel-specific denoms. Delegation, redelegation, unbonding with 21-day period.

DEX (AMM + Order Book)

Neutral▼

On-chain AMM with constant product formula. Limit orders with atomic settlement. Liquidity pools, yield farming with LP token staking.

Lending & Borrowing

Neutral▼

Supply assets to earn interest, borrow against collateral. Health factor monitoring, liquidation protection, insurance fund (10% of interest). 5 markets: OTK, BTC, ETH, SOL, USDT.

Escrow & Dispute Resolution

Indirect — Art V▼

On-chain escrow for P2P trades. Arbiter-mediated dispute resolution. Create, fund, release, or dispute — all trustless.

Token Factory

Neutral▼

Create custom tokens on Open Chain. Set name, symbol, decimals, initial supply. Mint additional supply, burn tokens. Denom format: factory/{creator}/{symbol}.

Social Features

Building community on-chain.

Encrypted P2P Messaging

Indirect — Art VIII▼

End-to-end encrypted messaging between Universal IDs using X25519 + AES-256-GCM. Each UID registers an encryption public key. Sender generates ephemeral keypair, derives shared secret via ECDH. Messages stored as encrypted ciphertexts on-chain — only the recipient can decrypt. Future: ML-KEM-768 post-quantum encryption.

Community Feed & Profile

Indirect — Art I, X▼

Community activity feed showing gratitude transactions, milestone achievements, governance results, new verifiers, token launches. Public profile with UID, scores, badges, staking.

Security & Safety

Security is not optional — it's the foundation.

Paper Trading (Practice Mode)

Indirect — Art IX▼

Mandatory practice before real money on mainnet. Traffic light system: Red (0 trades, blocked), Orange (1-2, warning), Green (3+, cleared). Protects new users from costly mistakes.

Security Audit, Multi-Sig, Spending Limits

Neutral▼

8-point security health check (0-100 score). M-of-N multi-signature wallets. Daily/weekly spending limits per token. Emergency wipe with triple confirmation. Cloud backup (encrypted settings, NOT keys).

DApp Browser, NFT Gallery, Automation

Neutral▼

In-app WebView DApp browser with bookmarks. NFT gallery across Ethereum + Solana. Recurring payments (daily/weekly/monthly). Price-based automation rules.
