Axonlume Support

Neuroanatomy 3D: brain regions, cranial nerves, spinal tracts and study questions

Getting help

Email bpupadhyaya@equalinformation.com with your device model, iOS version and a short description of the problem. We reply within three working days.

Quick answers

  • How do I make the text bigger? Tap A+ at the top of any screen. It works together with the text size in iOS Settings.
  • How do I look inside the brain? On the Atlas tab, slide the transparency control or use Midline cut.
  • How do I see where a lesion blocks a pathway? Open Pathways, choose a tract, turn on Follow the signal, pick a level in the lesion explorer and press play.
  • How do I remove ads? More tab: subscribe to Ad-free (monthly), or watch a short video to switch ads off for 2 hours. Restore Purchases is on the same screen.
  • Does it work offline? Yes. Everything except ads and the subscription works without a connection.
