How Satwick works

Start with the diagnostic (a short two-stage mini test), then each day Satwick suggests a short plan (for example 10 minutes on your three weakest skills), brings back questions you are about to forget, and keeps every miss in a mistake notebook with its full solution. Module mocks and full mocks follow the digital SAT format: two timed Reading and Writing modules (27 questions, 32 minutes each) and two timed Math modules (22 questions, 35 minutes each); the second module of each section adapts to how you do in the first, and a graphing calculator (plot functions, tables, intercepts and intersections, sliders, zoom and pan) is available on every Math question. Interactive labs in the Topics tab let you explore ideas by moving controls and then answer a short check. A question leaves the notebook after you answer it correctly twice.

Credits

You start with 3 credits. The first four practice sets each day are free; after that a set costs 1 credit. A module mock costs 1 credit and a full mock 3 credits. Watching a short rewarded ad earns 1 credit (up to 12 a day); streak days (3, 7, 14, 30), your first full mock and watching three ads in a day also earn bonus credits. The diagnostic, the daily warm-up, the notebook, the first simulations in every area and all explanations are always free. If an ad cannot be shown, you get one free module at most every 24 hours. The ad-free monthly subscription removes ads and credit limits; it starts with a 7-day free trial and can be cancelled any time in your Apple ID or Google Play subscriptions. Use Restore purchases in Settings on a new device.

Your data

Progress is stored only on your device. To move it to another device use Settings, Export progress, then Import on the other device. Settings also has Erase all progress.

About the questions

All questions, passages and solutions are original practice material written for Satwick; they are not real SAT questions. The readiness range is a rough practice estimate from your recent answers and mock results, not a predicted score. SAT is a trademark of its owner and Satwick is not affiliated with it. If you find a mistake in a question, email us the question text and we will fix it.
