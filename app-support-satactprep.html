How Satwick works

Each day Satwick suggests a short plan (for example 10 minutes on your three weakest topics), brings back questions you are about to forget, and keeps every miss in a mistake notebook with its full solution. A question leaves the notebook after you answer it correctly twice.

Credits

You start with 2 credits. One credit opens one full practice session or timed drill (not each question). Watching a short rewarded ad earns 1 credit. The daily warm-up is free once a day, and the notebook and all explanations are always free. If an ad cannot be shown, you get one free session at most every 24 hours. The ad-free monthly subscription removes ads and credit limits; it starts with a 7-day free trial and can be cancelled any time in your Apple ID or Google Play subscriptions. Use Restore purchases in Settings on a new device.

Your data

Progress is stored only on your device. To move it to another device use Settings, Export progress, then Import on the other device. Settings also has Erase all progress.

About the questions

All questions, passages and solutions are original practice material written for Satwick; they are not real SAT or ACT questions. The readiness meter is a rough practice estimate from your recent answers, not a predicted score. SAT and ACT are trademarks of their respective owners and Satwick is not affiliated with them. If you find a mistake in a question, email us the question text and we will fix it.
