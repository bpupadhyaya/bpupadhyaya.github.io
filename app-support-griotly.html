Contact

Write to support@equalinformation.com. Please include your device model and system version. You can also send a diagnostic report or a suggestion from Settings, Privacy and data.

Subscriptions

To manage or cancel Griotly Plus, open the subscription settings of your Apple or Google account. To restore a subscription, open Griotly Plus in the app menu and choose Restore purchases.

If you find an error in a lesson or question, use the Report button under it. For exam rules, dates and required sources, rely on the current official College Board publications.
