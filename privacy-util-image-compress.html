Slimwick Privacy Policy

Slimwick: Image Compressor. Effective 13 November 2026. Publisher: Equal Information LLC.

Slimwick compresses and resizes photos to a target file size or pixel size on your phone, and can remove location and camera details from copies.

What stays on your device

Photos you choose are read and compressed on your device. They are not uploaded, copied to a server or shared with us or anyone else. Slimwick has no account and no analytics of its own. Compressed copies are saved or shared only where you tell the app to. The app stores your last settings, a daily export counter and total space saved on your device; uninstalling removes them.

Permissions

Choosing photos uses the system photo picker, which gives the app only the photos you select. On iPhone and iPad the app asks for add-only permission to save to your photo library when you tap Save to Photos; it does not read your library. On Android saving to the gallery needs no permission. The app does not use the camera, microphone, contacts or location.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob (a banner on some screens and an occasional full-screen ad after exports). Your photos are never used for ads or sent to AdMob. An optional short video can be watched for extra exports. On iPhone and iPad the system first asks whether the app may track you (App Tracking Transparency); in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some US states a Google consent message also appears (Google User Messaging Platform). On Android the Google consent message appears where the law requires it. You can decline and keep using every feature. With your permission, AdMob and its partners may use your device advertising identifier, approximate location derived from your IP address, and ad interaction and diagnostic data to choose, show and measure ads. See Google's advertising policies. You can change your choices later in the app under Settings (privacy choices for ads, where shown) and in your device settings. Ads are not served to people who have an ad-free purchase active.

Purchases

The optional ad-free monthly subscription and the one-time Unlock are handled by Apple or Google Play. We never see your payment details.

What is collected

Collected by us: nothing. Collected by Google AdMob in the free version: device identifiers (advertising identifier), approximate location from IP address, and ad interaction and diagnostic data, used for third-party advertising. Your photos are not collected.

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information, build profiles about you, run our own analytics, or ask you to create an account. No personal data is sent to us.

Children

Slimwick is a general utility and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and we do not collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new effective date.
