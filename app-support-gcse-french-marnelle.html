GCSE French Prep: Marnelle: Support

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Contact: support@equalinformation.com. Last updated 2026-11-25.

Help

Questions, content corrections and subscription help: support@equalinformation.com. Please include your device model, the app version and the screen or question you are on.

Send diagnostics

Open Me, Settings, Privacy and data and choose Send diagnostics. You see exactly what the report contains and nothing is sent until you tap Send. Reports arrive at diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

Feedback and suggestions

Use Feedback and suggestions in Settings, the thumbs and report controls under lessons and explanations, or Suggest content. Nothing is sent until you tap Send.

Speaking practice and the microphone

Recordings stay on your device. If recording does not start, check that microphone access is allowed for the app in your device settings.

Delete my usage data

Open Me, Settings, Privacy and data and choose Delete my usage data, or email diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

Subscription

Manage or cancel Marnelle Plus in your App Store or Google Play account settings. If a purchase does not appear, use Restore purchases on the Marnelle Plus screen.
