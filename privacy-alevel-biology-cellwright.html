A Level Biology: Cellwright - Privacy Policy

What the app stores

Your answers, learner model, practical logbook, test history, text size and study plan are saved only on your device. There is no account and no server of ours receives this data. Erase it in Me, or by deleting the app.

Tracking and third parties

The app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and does not collect personal data. Your study data never leaves your device.

Subscription

Cellwright Plus is an optional monthly subscription that unlocks all labs, builds, mock papers, stretch questions and practical cards. It is handled by Apple or Google; we never see payment details. Cancel any time in your store account settings: Plus stays active until the end of the period you paid for and you are not charged again.
