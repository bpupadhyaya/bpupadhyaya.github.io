A Level Biology: Cellwright - Privacy Policy

What the app stores

Your answers, learner model, practical logbook, test history, text size and 24-hour unlock times are saved only on your device. There is no account and no server of ours receives this data. Erase it in Me, or by deleting the app.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: an occasional full-screen ad after a finished quiz and optional rewarded videos that unlock features for 24 hours. AdMob may use your device advertising identifier and approximate device information. On iOS the app asks before tracking; where required a consent form is shown. If you say you are under 18, ads are non-personalised. Every study feature works if you decline.

Subscription

The optional monthly subscription removes ads and unlocks everything. It is handled by Apple or Google; we never see payment details. Cancel any time in your store account settings.
