Microeconomics AP: Pricevane: Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Contact: support@equalinformation.com. Last updated 2026-11-27.

1. Introduction and scope

EqualInformation, LLC ("we", "us") publishes Microeconomics AP: Pricevane, version 1.0.0 and later (the "app"). This policy explains how information is handled in connection with the app, and only the app. There is no account system. Lessons, questions, graph labs and practice papers are bundled with the app, your study progress is kept on your own device, and the free part of the app works without an internet connection.

2. Information we collect

A. Information you choose to give us. Diagnostic report: Settings, Privacy and data, Send diagnostics shows the whole report on screen first, and it is sent only when you tap Send. Feedback and suggestions: a category, your message (at most 2,000 characters; recognisable email addresses, telephone numbers and links are removed automatically) and, if you supply them, a role (AP student, teacher or tutor, parent or guardian, or other), the screen you were on, a source link and a rating. An email address reaches us only if you tick the reply option, and it is used only to reply.

B. Diagnostic and app-usage information, collected with your agreement or as permitted by applicable law while the optional usage setting is on: app version and build; device model; operating system and version; processor and memory details; display size; language, region and time zone; text-size, accessibility and display settings; network type; a battery range; whether Pricevane Plus is active; a randomly generated app identifier (not an advertising identifier); session timing; crash and error information; performance indicators; the screens, lessons, quizzes, graph labs, missions and mock exams you use; the questions you attempt, whether your answers are correct, the time taken and whether hints or explanations were opened; progress estimates by topic; the choices the adaptive-learning feature makes and what follows from them; and interactions with the subscription screen. For typed-number questions we record whether the answer was correct and how long it took, not what you typed.

When you accept the notice shown at first launch, a short acceptance record is always sent: a consent identifier, the time, the app, platform, version and build, the notice version and a fingerprint of its text, the state of the usage setting, the random app identifier, and your country, locale and time zone.

C. Information we do not collect: your name, contacts, precise location, photographs, files, advertising identifiers, or the text you type into the app, such as numbers in answer boxes, search words, plan names and free-response notes. What you write in Feedback and suggestions or in a note with Send diagnostics reaches us only when you choose to send it.

3. How we use information

To operate, maintain, secure and support the app; to diagnose problems; to provide and personalise the learning experience; to understand how the app is used; for research and development; for analytics and to improve our apps and educational content; to read and act on feedback; and to meet legal obligations.

4. Your choices

At first launch the app shows a notice describing these practices, with a separate usage switch. The switch starts off in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and wherever your region cannot be determined, until you turn it on; elsewhere it starts on. You can change it at any time under Settings, Privacy and data, where you can also read the notice again. Diagnostics and feedback are sent only when you choose to send them. To ask us to delete your usage data, use Delete my usage data in the app or write to diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

5. Storage and service providers

Information travels over encrypted (HTTPS) connections to a web application operated by EqualInformation, LLC, which records it in our Google Workspace services (Google Sheets and Google Drive). Where the app was installed from an app store, we may receive by email the full entry for an acceptance, a diagnostic report, feedback, a crash or a deletion request, plus batches of the usage and learning information described above. Mailboxes we own receive it and may forward it to each other. Google acts for us as a service provider. Apple or Google processes payments for Pricevane Plus under its own terms and we do not receive your payment details. Reference links open public web pages from their own publishers, which follow their own privacy practices.

6. Retention

Usage, learning and diagnostic information is kept for as long as it remains useful for the purposes in section 3. We take up deletion requests when you make them and carry them out manually. Nothing is deleted automatically; an email copy stays unless a person removes it.

7. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures, including encryption in transit. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. International users and legal bases

Where a legal basis is required we rely on your agreement (the usage switch and your decision to send diagnostics or feedback) and on our legitimate interest in running and improving the app. Information may be processed in the United States.

9. Children

We design our apps to collect only the information described in this policy. If you believe information has been provided to us that you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the version of the first-launch notice changes and the notice is shown again.

11. Contact

EqualInformation, LLC. Questions: support@equalinformation.com. Deletion and data requests: diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
