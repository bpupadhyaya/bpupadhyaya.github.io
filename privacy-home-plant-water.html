Leafwell privacy policy

Leafwell: Plant Watering Planner is made by Equal Information. Last updated 9 November 2026.

What stays on your device

Your plants, nicknames, watering and feeding history, settings and text-size choice are stored only on your device. We have no accounts and no servers that receive this data. Watering reminders are local notifications created on your device.

Ads and tracking

Leafwell is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. If you allow it in the system prompt, Google may use your advertising identifier to show more relevant ads. In regions where the law requires it, Google's consent form is shown first. AdMob may collect coarse location, the device identifier, product interaction and advertising data for advertising purposes. You can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking. Subscribers and people who earn ad-free time do not see ads.

Purchases

The optional ad-free monthly subscription is handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Children

Leafwell is a general-audience app and is not directed to children under 13.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: bpupadhyaya@equalinformation.com. Deleting the app removes all data it stored.
