Support

Italian Language AP: Favellume · EqualInformation, LLC

Contact

Write to support@equalinformation.com. In the app you can also use More, then Send diagnostics or Feedback and suggestions.

No sound on a listening item?

Listening items are read by your device's Italian voice. Check the volume, then make sure an Italian text-to-speech voice is installed in your device's accessibility or speech settings. You can also use the transcript.

Recording does not start

Allow microphone access for the app in your device settings. Recordings stay on the device.

Subscription

Use Restore purchases under More after reinstalling or changing devices with the same store account. To cancel, open Manage subscription under More, or your Apple or Google account's subscription page. Cancelling takes effect at the end of the month already paid for.

Your data

See the Privacy Policy. To have usage information deleted, use More, Privacy and data choices, or write to diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
