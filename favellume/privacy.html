Privacy Policy

Italian Language AP: Favellume · EqualInformation, LLC

Scope

This policy covers the Favellume app for iPhone, iPad and Android, an Italian practice companion for the AP Italian Language and Culture course, published by EqualInformation, LLC.

What stays on your device

Your practice recordings, essays, mission notes, speaking notes, study plan, exam date and progress are saved on your device. The app does not upload them. You can delete recordings in the app, and removing the app removes its saved data. If you use Copy or Share, the text goes where you send it.

Your agreement record

When you accept the first-run notice, the app sends a short record: a record number, the time, the notice version and a fingerprint of its text, the way you accepted, the position of the sharing switch, a random installation number, the platform, app version and build, how the app was installed (for example from an app store or as a test copy, and whether it runs on an emulator), and your device's country or region setting, locale and time zone. If sharing is off, this record contains no other device details.

Learning, usage and device information

If the sharing switch is on, the app sends:

  • learning activity: which practice questions you attempt, the option you choose, whether it is correct, response time, hints and replays, attempt number, topic-level progress estimates, quiz, mock and mission progress, self-check scores, and the adaptive guide's decisions;
  • how features are used: screens and lessons opened, time spent, studio and map use, view switching, text size, plan changes, thumbs ratings, and whether the membership screen was shown, closed or completed (no payment details);
  • technical information: device model, system and app version, language, region, time zone, processor, installed memory, graphics, screen and window size, your device’s text-size setting, session counts and times, membership state, install source, crash signatures and slow-screen signals, and, with your first consent record, a copy of the notice, reading time and whether you reached its end.

These items are linked to the random installation number, not to your name. The app does not collect your name, contacts, precise location, photos or advertising identifier, and nothing you type or record while practising is included.

What also reaches our email

Our spreadsheets are not the only destination. Once Favellume is released, our collection service also emails records from copies installed through the App Store or Google Play to a mailbox that belongs to EqualInformation and is run on Google Workspace.

  • While sharing is on: the learning activity, feature use and technical entries listed above, grouped into messages that carry the installation number and app and device details, and any crash report.
  • With sharing on or off: your agreement record (with the notice text and device details if sharing was on when it was made), a diagnostics report or feedback that you send, including a contact address if you gave one, and a deletion request.

Not every entry is certain to be mailed: when the day's sending allowance runs low, some learning and usage groups stay out of email, and a very long message is cut short. Our mail system may hold more than one copy of a message, and may forward a copy to another Google mail account that we control.

Information you choose to send

Diagnostics. If you press Send on the diagnostics screen, we receive the report shown in the preview: app and device details, settings, membership state, a short log of app actions, crash signatures and any note you add. Sending works even when sharing is off.

Feedback. If you send feedback we receive your message and anything you select or add: category, role, rating, a source link, the question it concerns, and an email address only if you ask to be contacted. We use that address only to reply.

Why we use it

To run, secure and support the app; to personalise and improve the learning experience; to find and fix problems; to research and develop our apps and educational content; to answer you; and to meet legal obligations. Information may be transferred as part of a merger, acquisition, financing or similar business transaction, subject to this policy.

Storage and service providers

Information travels over encrypted connections and is stored in our Google Workspace services (spreadsheets, files and email), with Google processing it on our behalf. Apple or Google processes subscriptions under its own terms; we do not receive your card details.

Your choices

You can leave sharing off on the first screen or change it later under More, Privacy and data choices. It starts switched off for devices set to a European Economic Area country, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or an unknown region, and switched on elsewhere. Turning it off stops this sharing and discards waiting items of that kind, including any crash report that has not yet been sent. Ask for Deletion on the same screen, or write to diagnostics@equalinformation.com with your installation number, to ask for stored usage information to be removed; the retention section below explains how far that reaches. Depending on where you live you may also have rights of access, correction, objection, portability or complaint to a regulator. Where the law asks for a legal basis, we rely on your consent for optional sharing and on our legitimate interests or legal duties for keeping the agreement record and handling what you send us.

Retention, security and transfers

We keep information for as long as it serves the purposes above or the law requires. The deletion button does not erase anything by itself: it records your request and passes it to us, and for a valid request a person removes the matching stored records. Emailed copies have no set expiry, and neither that button nor the collection service deletes them. A message already in our mail, or forwarded from it, can therefore remain after other records have gone, unless someone deletes it there by hand. We restrict access to it, although no system can be guaranteed secure. It may be processed in the United States and other countries.

Children

The app asks for no names or accounts. A parent, guardian or school with a question about a child's information, or wishing to have it removed, can write to us and we will help.

Changes and contact

We will post changes here with a new effective date, and the app will ask you to read a materially changed notice again. Contact EqualInformation, LLC at support@equalinformation.com.
