Privacy: Math Grade 5: Brisklet

Applies to the Brisklet app for iPhone, iPad and Android. Written 27 November 2026.

The short version

Brisklet does not collect anything about you or your child. Nothing the app learns is sent to us or to anyone else. There are no accounts, no adverts, no analytics, no crash reports and no tracking.

What stays on the device

To remember where your child is, the app keeps a small file on the device: answers given and whether they were right, which parts are finished, settings such as text size, and a note of which plan the store last said the household owns. This file is not sent anywhere. On Android it is not included in device backups. On iPhone and iPad, the device's own backup may copy it, as it does for most apps. Deleting the app deletes the file, and a grown-up can also erase progress under “For grown-ups” then Settings.

What the app does not use

Brisklet does not ask for the microphone, camera, location, contacts, photos or notifications. Numbers a child types are used only to mark that answer.

Reading aloud

The speaker buttons use the speech engine built into the device. We send no text anywhere. Some phones use their own speech service; that is governed by the phone's settings and the maker of that service.

Plans and payments

A grown-up can buy a monthly plan for the Survey Log or the Expedition. The purchase, the payment and the cancellation are handled entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see card details or any account details. The app asks the store, on the device, which plans the account owns. The store may give us anonymous sales totals; these do not identify anyone.

Children

Brisklet is made for children of about ten and eleven to use with a grown-up nearby. Because we collect no personal information, there is none to access, correct or delete. The grown-up check in the app keeps settings and plans away from young players. It is a deterrent, not a way of confirming age or identity, and it is not parental consent.

Web pages

The app opens a web page only when a grown-up taps a link to this privacy page, the terms or the support page. Your browser then handles that visit.

Changes and questions

If this ever changes, we will update this page and the app's store listing before a new version is released. Questions: support@equalinformation.com.
