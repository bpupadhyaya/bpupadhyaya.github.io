Privacy: Math Grade 5: Brisklet

Applies to the Brisklet app on phones and tablets. Written 27 November 2026.

The short version

Brisklet sends nothing by itself. Nothing the app learns about your child is sent to us or to anyone else. There are no accounts, no analytics, no automatic crash reports and no tracking. We receive something only when a grown-up chooses to email us.

If a grown-up emails us

In the grown-ups’ area, under Settings, you can send diagnostics or feedback. The app shows you the whole message, then opens your own mail app; nothing is sent unless you send it. A diagnostics message holds the app version, the device’s system version and model, its language and region, and anything you add. It holds no answers, progress, identifiers or child’s name. We receive your email address and what you sent, use them only to deal with your message and improve the app, and keep them in our email service. Write to diagnostics@equalinformation.com to ask us to delete them.

What stays on the device

To remember where your child is, the app keeps a small file on the device: answers given and whether they were right, which parts are finished, settings such as text size, and a note of which plan the store last said the household owns. The app does not send this file anywhere. Depending on the device and its settings, the device’s own backup may copy it. Deleting the app deletes the file, and a grown-up can also erase progress under “For grown-ups” then Settings.

What the app does not use

Brisklet does not ask for the microphone, camera, location, contacts, photos or notifications. Numbers a child types are used only to mark that answer.

Reading aloud

The speaker buttons use the speech engine built into the device. We send no text anywhere. Some devices use their own speech service; that is governed by the device's settings and the maker of that service.

Plans and payments

A grown-up can buy a monthly plan for the Survey Log or the Expedition. The purchase, the payment and the cancellation are handled entirely by the app store on your device. We never see card details or any account details. The app asks the store, on the device, which plans the account owns. The store may give us anonymous sales totals; these do not identify anyone.

Children

Brisklet is made for Grade 5 learners to use with a grown-up nearby. The app collects no information from a child. A grown-up who has emailed us can ask us to show, correct or delete that message. The grown-up check in the app keeps settings and plans away from young players. It is a deterrent, not a way of confirming who someone is, and it is not parental consent.

Web pages

The app reaches a web page only from the grown-ups’ area, after the grown-up question.

Cited sources. When a grown-up opens a source, the app shows that one page in a popup, loaded from its publisher: the California Department of Education (its printing of the Common Core standards), GOV.UK, the World Health Organization or the American Academy of Pediatrics. As with any web visit, the publisher’s server receives the device’s internet address and basic technical details of the request, under that publisher’s own privacy policy. We do not receive them. The app blocks the page’s scripts and outside resources, refuses cookies, keeps nothing after the popup is closed, does not open other pages from it, and sends nothing about your child.

This privacy page, the terms and support. These open in your browser, which then handles the visit.

Changes and questions

If this ever changes, we will update this page and the app's store listing before a new version is released. Questions: support@equalinformation.com.
