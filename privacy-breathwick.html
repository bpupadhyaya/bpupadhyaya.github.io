Breathwick Privacy Policy

Equal Information LLC. Effective 8 November 2026.

Breathwick: Breathing Trainer is a guided breathing app for relaxation and general wellness. This page explains what the app stores and what advertising partners may collect.

What stays on your device

Your sessions (date, rhythm, length), streaks, settings and reminder time are stored only on your device. We do not run accounts, we do not receive your sessions and we do not upload them. The app works fully offline. The app does not read any health sensor, does not use HealthKit and does not use the camera or microphone. Erasing your history is available in Settings.

Advertising

Breathwick is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. With your permission (the iOS tracking prompt and, where required, the Google consent form), AdMob and its partners may use your device advertising identifier and collect coarse location, ad interactions and device information to show and measure ads. You can decline the prompt, change the choice in iOS Settings (Privacy and Security, Tracking), or use Ad privacy choices in the app's Settings when it is shown. The monthly ad-free subscription, or ad-free time earned by watching rewarded videos, turns ads off.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. Manage or cancel in your Apple Account settings.

Notifications

If you switch on the daily reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device. No notification data is sent to us.

Children

Breathwick is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Health notice

Breathwick is not a medical device and does not give medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.
