Subscription help

To restore an existing subscription, open Settings and choose Restore purchases. To change or cancel it, choose Manage subscription, or open the subscription settings of your Apple Account or Google Play account. Cancelling stops the next renewal; access continues until the end of the paid month.

Common questions

Does it work offline? The course does. Reference links, purchases and sending feedback need a connection.

How do I change text size? Use A- and A+ at the top of the screen, from 50 to 300 percent.

What are the two views? Syllabus follows course order. Real-World organises the same history into commissions where you make something. They share your progress.

I think a question or fact is wrong. Use Report a problem or Feedback and suggestions and include a source link if you can.
