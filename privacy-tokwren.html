Generative AI Skills: Tokwren - Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Last updated 2026-11-20.

Tokwren does not ask for your name, e-mail or birth date, and it has no account and no server. Your progress, text size and answers stay on your device. Anything you type into a lab (for example a practice prompt) is processed on the device and is not sent to us.

Age answer. On first launch the app asks whether you are under 13, 13 to 17, or 18 or older. The answer is stored only on your device. Under 13 is not supported and no advertising is started.

Advertising. The free version shows ads through Google AdMob. For adults, and with permission where the law or the platform requires it, Google may collect device identifiers, approximate location derived from the IP address and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. If you say you are 13 to 17, Tokwren requests only non-personalised ads, limits ad content to a teen rating, does not show the consent form and does not ask for tracking permission. Tokwren is not directed to children under 13.

Purchases. The optional monthly Ad-Free subscription is handled by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.

Sources. When you open a cited source, the page loads inside the app from the publisher's website, and that site may collect data as any website does. Cookies are not kept after the sheet closes.

You can change your age answer in the menu. Questions: support@equalinformation.com.
