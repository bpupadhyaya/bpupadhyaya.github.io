GRE Math Subject Test: Calcarc Privacy Policy

GRE Math Subject Test practice. Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Effective 15 November 2026.

What the app stores

Your practice history, levels, review schedule, mistake notebook, streak days, credits and settings are saved only on your device. There is no account, no sign-up and no server of ours that receives this data. You can export your progress to a file you control, import it again, or reset it in More. Deleting the app deletes the data.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on some screens, an occasional full-screen ad after a finished practice block, and optional rewarded videos that earn credits. There are no ads in mock tests, simulations or while an answer is being explained. AdMob may use your device advertising identifier, approximate location and device information to choose and measure ads. On iOS the app asks permission before tracking across other companies’ apps and websites, and where the law requires it, shows a consent form from Google’s User Messaging Platform. You can refuse and keep using the app. The first session has no ads. See policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Purchases

The optional ad-free plan is an auto-renewing monthly subscription handled by Apple or Google. We receive no payment details. Manage or cancel it in your store account settings. Cancel any time in your App Store or Google Play account settings. If you cancel, you keep ad-free until the end of the period you already paid for, and you will not be charged again.

Web references

When you open a reference inside the app and you are online, the page of the cited publisher loads in a restricted in-app viewer. That site may see your device’s network address like any website visit. Offline, only the bundled citation text is shown.

Practice content

GRE is a registered trademark of ETS. Calcarc is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by ETS. The name is used only to describe the kind of exam the practice suits. All questions and explanations are original practice material stored inside the app. The readiness range and the rough scaled-score range are estimates from your own answers, not predictions of any exam score.

Children

The app is an education tool for graduate-school applicants and is not directed to children under 13.
