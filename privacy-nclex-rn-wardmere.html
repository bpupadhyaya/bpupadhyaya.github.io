Privacy policy

NCLEX-RN Prep: Wardmere

Effective 27 November 2026. This policy describes how EqualInformation, LLC ("we", "us") handles information in the app NCLEX-RN Prep: Wardmere ("the app").

1. Information we collect

Information you choose to provide. Diagnostic reports you send and any note you add; feedback and suggestions you submit (category, message, optional role, source link, rating) and, only if you tick the contact option, an email address for a reply.

Diagnostic and app-usage information collected with your agreement or as permitted by applicable law. Device and operating-system information (model, system version, processor, memory, display size, language, region and time zone, accessibility and display settings), app version, performance and memory indicators, crash and error information, a random app identifier, and how features of the app are used: lessons viewed, questions attempted, whether answers are correct, time taken, progress estimates, study-plan decisions and their outcomes, and activity in labs and mock exams.

Information we do not collect. Your name, contacts, precise location, photographs, files, advertising identifiers, or text you enter elsewhere in the app.

2. How we use information

To operate, maintain, secure and support the app; to diagnose problems; to personalise your study plan; to understand how the app is used; for research and development; for analytics and improvement of our apps and educational content; to read and act on feedback; and to comply with legal obligations.

3. Your choices

When you first open the app a notice is shown. The usage-information switch under Settings, Privacy and data is off by default in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland until you turn it on, and you can change it at any time. Diagnostics and feedback are sent only when you choose to send them. To request deletion use "Delete my usage data" in Settings, Privacy and data, or email diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

4. Storage and service providers

Information is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored in our Google Workspace services (Google Sheets and Drive) and notification email, and processed by service providers acting on our behalf. Email notifications from store copies of Wardmere may contain nursing practice and usage batches as well as the acceptance record, crash information, diagnostic reports, feedback and deletion requests. All go to mailboxes we operate, with forwarding to another of ours possible. Information may be processed in the United States. The optional monthly subscription, Wardmere Plus, is handled by Apple or Google; we do not receive your payment details.

5. Retention and security

Usage information is kept while it is useful for the purposes above; deletion requests are handled by hand, since our system does not purge records. If a record was emailed to us, that email is kept unless it is deleted by hand too. We use reasonable measures to protect information, although no method is completely secure.

6. Children

We design our apps to collect only the information described in this policy. If you believe information has been provided to us that you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

7. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, the notice shown when you first open the app will carry a new version and be shown again.

8. Contact

EqualInformation, LLC, support@equalinformation.com. Deletion and data requests: diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
