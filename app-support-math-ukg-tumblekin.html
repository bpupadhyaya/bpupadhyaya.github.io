Support: Math UKG: Tumblekin

Math UKG: Tumblekin is a children's maths practice app published by EqualInformation LLC. Last updated 27 November 2026.

Common questions

There is no sound. Check the volume and the silent switch. In the grown-up area, About, make sure Spoken voice is on.

The words are too small or too large. Use A− and A+ on any screen: they go from 50% to 300%.

How do I get to the grown-up area? Tap the lock icon on the first screen and answer the two written questions.

I bought a plan but it is locked. In the grown-up area open Plans and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same store account that bought it.

How do I cancel? Open Plans and tap Manage or cancel, or follow the steps on the Terms page.

How do I erase my child's progress? Grown-up area, About, Erase progress. Removing the app also removes it.

Getting in touch

Email support@equalinformation.com. Please say which device and system version you use. The grown-up area can prepare a short report (counts only, no name) that you may paste into your message; the app never sends it for you.
