IB Biology Prep: Genemere: Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Contact: support@equalinformation.com. Last updated 2026-11-24.

1. Introduction and scope

This Privacy Policy describes how EqualInformation, LLC ("we", "us") handles information in connection with IB Biology Prep: Genemere (the "app"), version 1.0 and later. It applies to the app only. The app has no account system and works offline; lessons, questions, labs and your progress are stored on your device.

2A. Information you choose to provide

Diagnostic reports: when you open Settings, Privacy and data, and choose Send diagnostics, the app shows you the exact content of the report, and it is sent only after you tap Send.

Feedback and suggestions: when you choose to send feedback, a suggestion or a problem report, we receive the category, your message (up to 2,000 characters, from which email addresses, telephone numbers and links are removed automatically), and, if you provide them, a role (for example IB student, teacher or tutor, parent or guardian, or other), a level or context, a source link and a rating from 1 to 5. We receive an email address only if you tick the option allowing us to contact you about the feedback, and we use it only for that reply.

2B. Diagnostic and app-usage information

With your agreement, or as permitted by applicable law, the app can send diagnostic and app-usage information while the optional usage setting is on: app version and build; device model; operating system and version; processor cores and architecture; graphics processor name; memory; screen size; language, region and time zone; text-size setting and a summary of accessibility and display settings; network type; a battery-level range; whether the free or Plus entitlement is active; a randomly generated app identifier; session timing; the screens you open and how long you spend in them; lesson activity such as scroll depth and time; your quiz, practice and mock-paper attempts (the question, its topic and concept, whether the answer was correct, the time taken, the option chosen and hints used); the answers you type into question fields, shortened and automatically cleaned of recognisable email addresses, telephone numbers, links and card or identity numbers; topic-level progress estimates; the adaptive-learning decisions the app makes and their outcomes; use of labs and guided builds (control names and value ranges, steps completed); interactions with the subscription screen; performance indicators such as render times and dropped-frame ranges; and crash and error information.

When you accept the first-run notice, a short acceptance record is always sent, whatever the setting: a consent identifier, the time, the app, platform, version and build, the notice version and a hash of its text, the state of the usage setting, the random app identifier, your country, locale and time zone. A notification email about that record is sent to EqualInformation.

2C. Information we do not collect

The app does not collect your name, your contacts, your precise location, your photographs or files, or other text you type into the app, such as notes and internal-assessment drafts. Those stay on your device.

3. How we use information

To operate, maintain, secure and support the app; to diagnose problems; to personalise your revision and the adaptive-learning features; to understand how the app is used; for research and development; for analytics and improvement of our apps and educational content; to read and act on feedback and suggestions; and to comply with legal obligations.

4. Your choices

When you first open the app, a notice explains these practices and offers a separate usage switch. The switch is off by default in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and where your region cannot be determined, until you turn it on, and is on elsewhere; you may change it at any time under Settings, Privacy and data. If you state that you are under 13, the app sends and stores nothing. Diagnostics and feedback are sent only when you tap Send. You can view the first-run notice again from the same screen.

To request deletion of your usage data, use Delete my usage data in the app or email diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

5. Storage and service providers

Information is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections to a web application operated by EqualInformation, LLC that records it in our Google Workspace services (Google Sheets and Drive); diagnostic reports and acceptance notifications are also delivered by email. Google acts as a service provider on our behalf. Apple or Google processes subscription payments for Genemere Plus and we do not receive your payment details. Source and reference links open public web pages inside the app; those sites apply their own privacy practices.

6. Retention

Usage, learning and diagnostic information is kept for as long as it is useful for the purposes described above. Deletion requests are handled on request, as described in section 4.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information, including encryption in transit. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. International users and legal bases

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we rely on your agreement (the usage switch and the actions you take to send diagnostics or feedback) and on our legitimate interest in maintaining and improving the app. Information may be processed in the United States.

9. Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. A user who states that they are under 13 sends and stores nothing.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If a change is material, the version of the first-run notice changes and the notice is shown again.

11. Contact

EqualInformation, LLC. Questions: support@equalinformation.com. Deletion and data requests: diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
