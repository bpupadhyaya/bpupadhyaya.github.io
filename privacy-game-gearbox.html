Cogridge: Gear Train Puzzle

Privacy Policy. Effective 9 November 2026. Publisher: Equal Information LLC.

The short version

Cogridge has no account, no sign-in and no analytics SDK. Your puzzle progress, stars, streaks and settings stay on your device. The app works fully offline.

What stays on your device

Levels solved, stars, hints used, the daily puzzle streak, text size and ad-free time earned are stored locally (UserDefaults). We never receive this data and it is removed when you delete the app.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use the device advertising identifier and coarse device and ad-interaction data to select and measure ads. On first launch the app asks for App Tracking Transparency permission, and, where required by law, shows Google's consent message (EEA, UK and some US states) so you can accept or refuse personalised ads. If you refuse, ads are non-personalised. You can change the tracking choice at any time in iOS Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking. Google's practices are described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

The optional monthly ad-free subscription is processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. Subscriptions renew monthly until cancelled in your Apple ID settings. You can also earn ad-free time by watching rewarded videos.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect names, emails, contacts, photos, location or health data.
  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not use artificial intelligence services or send puzzle data to any server.

Children

Cogridge is a general puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help.
