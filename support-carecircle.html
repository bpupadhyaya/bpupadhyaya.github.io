Coordinate care for your loved ones — tasks, medications, vitals, schedules, and guides. All offline, all private.

CareCircle is a caregiver coordination app that helps families manage care for elderly parents, disabled loved ones, or patients recovering at home. It is built and maintained by a single developer who has seen firsthand how overwhelming caregiving can be without the right tools.

Every feature is designed with privacy at its core — all data stays on your device with no accounts, no cloud sync, and no tracking. CareCircle is available on iOS and Android. Core caregiving features are free, with advanced features available through a one-time in-app purchase. Your support helps us continue improving the app and building more tools for caregivers.

Why CareCircle Exists

Over 53 million Americans serve as unpaid family caregivers. Many juggle caregiving with full-time jobs, managing medications, coordinating with siblings, and tracking symptoms — often with nothing more than sticky notes and group texts.

Caregiver burnout affects 40-70% of family caregivers. When multiple family members share the caregiving load, coordination becomes the hardest part — who gave the medication, when was the last vital reading, what did the doctor say?

There is no dominant caregiver app on either app store. Existing solutions are either too complex, too expensive, or require cloud accounts that many elderly care receivers cannot manage. CareCircle is designed to be simple, private, and offline-first.

CareCircle is not a medical device. It is a coordination tool that helps families organize care activities, track health trends, and communicate with each other — so that the people who matter most get the best care possible.

Disclaimer: CareCircle is for care coordination and informational purposes only. It is not intended to replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult qualified healthcare professionals for medical decisions.
