Privacy Policy

IB Maths Prep: Integrex

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Contact: support@equalinformation.com. Last updated 2026-11-24.

1. Introduction and scope

This Privacy Policy describes how EqualInformation, LLC ("we", "us") handles information in connection with IB Maths Prep: Integrex (the "app"). It applies to the app only. The app has no account system and works offline.

2A. Information you choose to provide

Diagnostic reports: when you open Settings, Privacy and data, and choose Send diagnostics, the app shows you the exact content of the report, and it is sent only after you tap Send. Feedback and suggestions: when you choose to send feedback, a suggestion or a problem report, we receive the category, your message (up to 2,000 characters, from which email addresses, telephone numbers and links are removed automatically), and, if you provide them, a role (for example school student, teacher, parent, undergraduate or other), a level or context, a source link and a rating from 1 to 5. We receive an email address only if you tick the option allowing us to contact you about the feedback.

2B. Diagnostic and app-usage information

With your agreement, or as permitted by applicable law, the app can send diagnostic and app-usage information while the optional usage setting is on: app version and build; device model; operating system and version; processor cores and architecture; graphics processor name; memory; screen size; language, region and time zone; text-size setting and a summary of accessibility and display settings; network type; a battery-level range; whether the free or Plus entitlement is active; a randomly generated app identifier (not an advertising identifier); session timing; the screens, labs and builds you open and how long you spend in them; lesson activity such as scroll depth and time; quiz, test and mock-paper attempts (the question, its topic, whether the answer was correct, the time taken, whether a hint was used and the option chosen); topic-level progress estimates; the adaptive-learning decisions the app makes and their outcomes; use of the labs (control names and value ranges); interactions with the subscription screen; performance indicators such as render times and dropped-frame ranges; and crash and error information. When you accept the first-run notice, a short acceptance record is always sent, whatever the setting: a consent identifier, the time, the app, platform, version and build, the notice version and a hash of its text, the state of the usage setting, the random app identifier, your country, locale and time zone. A notification email about that record is sent to EqualInformation.

2C. Information we do not collect

The app does not collect your name, your contacts, your precise location, your photographs or files, an advertising identifier, or the text you type into it (such as Exploration planning notes), except the feedback you choose to send. Your progress, plans and entries stay on your device.

3. How we use information

To operate, maintain, secure and support the app; to diagnose problems; to provide and personalise the learning experience; to understand how the app is used; for research and development; for analytics and improvement of our apps and educational content; to read and act on feedback and suggestions; and to comply with legal obligations.

4. Your choices

When you first open the app, a notice explains these practices and offers a separate usage switch. The switch is off by default in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and where your region cannot be determined, until you turn it on, and is on elsewhere; you may change it at any time under More, Settings, Privacy and data. Diagnostics and feedback are sent only when you tap Send. You can view the first-run notice again from the same screen. To request deletion of your usage data, use Delete my usage data in the app or email diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

5. Storage and service providers

Information is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections to a web application operated by EqualInformation, LLC that records it in our Google Workspace services (Google Sheets and Drive); diagnostic reports and acceptance notifications are also delivered by email. Google acts as a service provider on our behalf. Apple or Google processes subscription payments and we do not receive your payment details. Source links open public web pages in a sheet inside the app; those sites apply their own privacy practices.

6. Retention

Usage, learning and diagnostic information is kept for as long as it is useful for the purposes described above. Deletion requests are handled on request, as described in section 4.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information, including encryption in transit. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. International users and legal bases

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we rely on your consent (the usage switch and the actions you take to send diagnostics or feedback) and on our legitimate interest in maintaining and improving the app. Information may be processed in the United States.

9. Children

We design our apps to collect only the information described in this policy. If you believe information has been provided to us that you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If a change is material, the version of the first-run notice changes and the notice is shown again.

11. Contact

EqualInformation, LLC. Questions: support@equalinformation.com. Deletion and data requests: diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
