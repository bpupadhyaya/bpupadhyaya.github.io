Quoinstake: support

Help for "Math Grade 7: Quoinstake".

Common questions

  • Text is too small or too large. Use A− and A+ at the top of any screen.
  • A subscription is missing. Open Grown-ups > Plans > Restore purchases, using the same store account that bought it.
  • How do I cancel? Grown-ups > Plans > Manage subscriptions, or your store account settings.
  • Does it work offline? The course does. Buying, and opening a cited web page, need the internet.
  • How do I start over? Grown-ups > Settings > Erase all progress on this device.
