Dimeglow privacy policy

Last updated 7 November 2026. Publisher: Equal Information.

What Dimeglow keeps

Dimeglow is a spending log. The purchases you type, the limits you set, the exchange rates you enter and any bank export you import are saved in one file inside the app on your own device. There is no account. There is no Dimeglow server, so we never receive, see or store any of it. Deleting the app, or using Settings, Erase all purchases, removes it.

Bank statements

When you import a CSV file, the app reads it on your device and keeps only the purchases you choose to import. Dimeglow never asks for a bank login or card number and never connects to a bank.

Widgets

The Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets show totals the app has computed. The app shares those totals with its widget through a protected storage item on the same device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Ads

Dimeglow is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Before ads load, iOS asks whether you allow tracking, and where the law requires it Google's consent form asks for your choice. Google may receive your device's advertising identifier, coarse location and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads, as described in Google's advertising policy. Your purchase log is never shared with advertisers. You can say no to tracking and the app works the same, and you can reopen your privacy choices in Settings when your region offers them.

A monthly subscription removes ads. Payments are handled entirely by Apple. We receive no payment details.

Children

Dimeglow is a general-audience budgeting tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Not financial advice

Dimeglow is a personal record. Its totals and estimates come only from what you enter, and it does not give financial, tax or investment advice.
