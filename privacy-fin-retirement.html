Compoundhill Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026.

What Compoundhill stores

Compoundhill is a retirement growth calculator. The numbers you enter, your saved what-ifs and your text-size choice stay on your device. There are no accounts and we cannot see them. The app works fully offline.

Ads

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier and approximate location to choose and measure ads. On iOS the app first asks whether you allow tracking, and where required (for example the EEA, UK and some US states) shows a consent form. You can say no and still use every calculator. A paid or earned ad-free period removes the ads.

Subscription

The optional monthly Ad-free subscription is processed by Apple. We receive no payment details. Manage or cancel it in your Apple ID subscription settings. Terms of Use: Apple standard licensed application end user licence agreement.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal data. Compoundhill does not collect names, email addresses, contacts, financial account details, or photos, and does not ask for camera, microphone, health or location permissions.

Children

Compoundhill is for adults and is not directed to children under 13.

Not financial advice

Compoundhill is an illustration tool. It does not give financial, tax or investment advice. Real returns, fees, inflation and taxes will differ.
