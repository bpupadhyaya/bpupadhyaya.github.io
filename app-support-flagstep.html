Citizenship test practice for iPhone and iPad

Flagstep is an independent study app. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) or any government agency, and it does not give legal advice.

Where the questions come from

The questions and answers are the free public lists published by USCIS: the 128-question 2025 civics test (publication M-1778, 09/25) and the 100-question 2008 civics test (rev. 01/19). Official page: uscis.gov study materials.

Which test do I take?

USCIS uses the 2025 test for applications (Form N-400) filed on or after October 20, 2025, and the 2008 test for earlier filings. Check your receipt or ask your attorney or accredited representative. Flagstep includes both; choose it on the Today screen.

Answers that change

Some answers depend on elections, appointments or where you live. Flagstep ships a dated list of national officeholders (checked against uscis.gov/citizenship/testupdates), refreshes it from a small update file and in each app update, and lets you type your own state officials under Settings, My answers. Always confirm the current names before your interview.

Ad-free options

Settings and the sparkle button offer a monthly ad-free subscription, Restore purchases, and a way to earn ad-free time by watching videos. Manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple ID settings.
