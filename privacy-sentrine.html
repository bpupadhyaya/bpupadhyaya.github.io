Privacy Policy

Cybersecurity AP: Sentrine · EqualInformation, LLC · Effective 27 November 2026

1. Scope

This policy explains how EqualInformation, LLC handles information in the Cybersecurity AP: Sentrine app on phones and tablets. The app is a study aid for defensive cybersecurity. It has no account and no sign-in.

2. What stays on your device

Your study progress, mastery estimates, study plan, exam date, text size, written practice answers, the made-up passwords and firewall rules you type in simulations, and the documents you build are stored on your device. The simulations use invented data and do not scan or connect to other systems. Removing the app or choosing Reset my progress removes this study record from the app.

3. Information you choose to send

Diagnostics. If you open Send diagnostics, you can preview the report, add a note and send it. Feedback. If you send feedback, a suggestion, a rating or an error report, we receive your message, the category, any optional details you pick (such as a role or a source link) and the screen or item it concerns. We receive an email address only if you type one and ask for a reply; we use it to reply.

4. Diagnostic and app-usage information

When the usage switch is on, and as permitted by applicable law, the app collects diagnostic and app-usage information:

  • device and system details such as model, operating system version, processor, memory, display size, language, region, time zone and accessibility or display settings;
  • app version, performance and memory indicators, and crash and error information;
  • a random identifier created by the app;
  • how features are used, such as screens and lessons opened, questions attempted, whether answers were correct, response times, progress estimates for course objectives, simulation and mission activity, next-step suggestions and what followed them, and subscription-screen interactions;
  • words you type into the objective search box, shortened and automatically cleaned of recognisable email addresses, telephone numbers, links and card or identity numbers.

A record that you accepted the first-run notice (a random identifier, time, app version, notice version, the position of the usage switch, and your country, locale and time zone) is sent when you accept it. If the switch is off, that record is limited to these items and no usage information follows.

5. What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, contacts, precise location, photographs, files or advertising identifier, and we do not receive your written practice answers, simulation passwords, firewall rules or study plan. The app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

6. How we use information

We use it to run, maintain, secure and support the app; to find and fix faults; to provide and personalise study features; to research, develop and improve our apps, teaching content and adaptive features; to read and act on feedback; and to meet legal obligations.

7. Your choices

Before first use you see a notice with a usage switch. It starts off in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and where the region cannot be determined. You can change it at any time under Me, Settings, Privacy and data. Diagnostics and feedback are sent only when you tap Send. To ask us to delete usage data, use Delete my usage data in the same place or write to diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

8. Storage, providers and transfers

Information is sent over encrypted connections and stored in our Google Workspace services (Sheets, Drive and notification email). For installs from a store, our server may email our own mailboxes the records Sentrine sends, contents and all: acceptance, security lab and question batches, usage events, crashes, diagnostics, feedback and deletion requests. They may be forwarded on to another mailbox of ours. Providers acting for us process it for those purposes. It may be processed in the United States and other countries. Apple or Google handles subscription payment and account details under its own policy; we do not receive your payment card details.

Where the law requires a legal basis, we rely on your consent for optional usage sharing, and on your request or our legitimate interest in operating and securing the app for what you choose to send. If our business is reorganised or transferred, information may pass to the successor, which remains bound by this policy.

9. Retention and security

We keep usage, learning and diagnostic information while it is useful for the purposes above, and delete or de-identify it on a valid deletion request, unless the law requires us to keep it. This work is manual. The button sends a request without removing anything automatically, and changes to stored records do not alter emailed copies, which stay unless removed by hand. Feedback is kept while it is useful for improving the app. We take reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

10. Cited web pages

Source links open in a reading panel inside the app. Those websites apply their own policies.

11. Children

We design our apps to collect only the information described in this policy. If you believe information has been provided to us that you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

12. Rights, changes and contact

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to processing of your information, and to complain to a data protection authority. If this policy changes materially we will update this page and, where appropriate, show the notice in the app again. Contact EqualInformation, LLC at support@equalinformation.com; deletion and data requests: diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
