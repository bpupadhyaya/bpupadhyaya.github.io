Teeloft: Privacy Policy

Effective 7 November 2026. Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC.

Teeloft is a golf scorecard and handicap-estimate app for iPhone and iPad. This page explains what the app does with your information, in plain words.

What stays on your phone

Your courses, rounds, scores, notes and preferences are stored on your device only. We do not run a server for them, there is no account or sign-in, and we cannot see them. The sentences you type or dictate in the “Say it” box are interpreted on the device and are not sent anywhere. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it. You can export it first from More.

What we collect

Nothing about you directly. Teeloft has no analytics service of our own and no advertising profile of our own.

Advertising

Teeloft is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve them, Google and its ad partners may collect your device’s advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking), IP address, approximate location, and how you interact with ads. This is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy. Your scores and courses are never shared with advertisers.

  • On first launch iOS asks whether Teeloft may track you across other companies’ apps. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, only non-personalised ads are shown.
  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or a US state with a privacy law, a consent or opt-out message from Google’s User Messaging Platform is shown. You can change your choice later in More, Ad privacy choices, when your region requires it.
  • You can switch ads off by subscribing, or by watching rewarded videos (ad-free time for the rest of the month).

Purchases

The optional ad-free monthly subscription is handled by Apple. We receive no payment details. Apple tells the app whether the subscription is active. Manage or cancel it in your Apple Account settings.

Children

Teeloft is a general-audience golf tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices and rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete on our side. For data held by advertising partners you can use the iOS tracking switch, the in-app privacy options, and Google’s tools at adssettings.google.com. Residents of the EEA, UK and some US states have rights of access, deletion and opt-out; contact us and we will help route a request.

Handicap disclaimer

Teeloft estimates a handicap index with published World Handicap System formulas for your own tracking. It is not an official Handicap Index and is not issued, endorsed or verified by any golf association.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: support@equalinformation.com.
