Support: Math Grade 5: Brisklet

Help for the Brisklet app on iPhone, iPad and Android.

I bought a plan but it is not open

Open “For grown-ups” (it asks a short question first), choose Settings, then “Restore plans bought with this store account”. Make sure the device is signed in to the same store account that paid. If it still does not open, write to us with the date of purchase.

How do I cancel or change a plan?

In the grown-ups’ area open Settings, choose the plan, then “Manage subscriptions in the store”. Cancelling stops the next renewal; the plan stays open until the end of the paid period.

How do I start again or clear my child’s progress?

Grown-ups’ area, then Settings, then “Erase all learning progress”. It asks twice. Progress is kept only on the device, so deleting the app also clears it.

The grown-up question keeps refusing me

After two wrong answers there is a short pause that grows if guessing continues. Wait for it to end and read the new question carefully. Closing the app does not skip the pause.

Text is too small or too large

Use A− and A+ in the bar at the top of the app. The setting is remembered.

Something looks wrong or a question seems incorrect

Please tell us which part and, if you can, what the question said. Screenshots help.
