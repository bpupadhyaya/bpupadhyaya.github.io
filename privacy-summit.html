Top-Tier Admission: Summit: Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Contact: support@equalinformation.com. Last updated 2026-11-23.

What stays on your device

Your progress, quiz answers, the scores and grades you type into the planning tools, your school list, timeline and checklist ticks, your age band and your settings are stored only on your device. There is no account system and no server of ours that receives this data. Deleting the app erases it. We do not collect your name, email address or contact details.

What we do not collect

Summit collects no personal data, uses no analytics and has no third-party trackers. It does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Subscription

Summit Plus is an optional monthly subscription. Apple or Google processes the payment and we never see your payment details. You can cancel at any time in your App Store or Google Play account settings; cancelling takes effect from the next billing period and you keep Plus until the period already paid for ends.

Sources you open

Source links open public web pages in a sheet inside the app. Those sites apply their own privacy practices.

Audience

The app is designed for students and families aged 13 and over, and for parents.
