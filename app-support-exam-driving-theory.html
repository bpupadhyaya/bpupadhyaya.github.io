Signaro: Driving Test Prep: Support

Signaro helps learner drivers prepare for the written driving knowledge test with original practice questions, road-sign flashcards and 3D right-of-way scenes. It is an independent study aid and is not affiliated with any government agency.

Common questions

  • Found a mistake in a question? Email us with the question text and your pack (for example California or United Kingdom). We will review it.
  • Is this the official test? No. Questions are original and written for this app from public handbooks. The real test can differ; always check your local handbook for the current rules.
  • Does it work offline? Yes. Everything except ads and store prices works without a connection.
  • Change the text size? Use A- and A+ at the top right of reading screens.
  • How do I remove ads? Open the in-app purchase screen (Settings or More, or the ad-free prompt) and choose the ad-free subscription or the one-time purchase. You can also watch an optional short video where offered for temporary extras.
  • How do I restore a purchase? Open Settings (or the purchase screen) and tap Restore purchases, using the same Apple Account or Google account you bought with. This is needed after reinstalling or changing device.
  • How do I cancel the subscription? iPhone and iPad: Settings, your name, Subscriptions. Android: Google Play, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel it.
  • Can I change my ad choices? Yes. Use the privacy choices for ads in the app's Settings where shown, or your device's privacy settings.
