Support RepairMate

Help us make home repair accessible to every homeowner.

Fix it yourself — 20 step-by-step repair guides, cost estimator, tool tracker, and more. All offline, all private.

RepairMate is a DIY home repair companion that gives homeowners and renters the confidence to tackle common repairs themselves. It is built and maintained by a single developer who believes everyone deserves access to clear, practical repair guidance without paying hundreds for a professional visit.

Every feature is designed with privacy at its core — all data stays on your device with no accounts, no cloud sync, and no tracking. RepairMate is available on iOS and Android. Core repair guides and reference tools are free, with advanced features available through a one-time in-app purchase. Your support helps us continue adding more guides and building better tools.

Why RepairMate Exists

The average homeowner spends $3,000–$5,000 per year on home maintenance and repairs. Many of these repairs are simple enough to DIY — fixing a running toilet, patching drywall, or unclogging a drain — but people lack the confidence to try because they don't know where to start.

Professional service calls often start at $75–$150 just for showing up, before any work is done. For a simple repair that takes 15 minutes and $5 in materials, that's a significant overpayment.

Existing DIY resources are scattered across YouTube videos, forums, and blogs — often with conflicting advice, no safety warnings, and no way to track your progress. RepairMate brings it all together in one offline-first app with step-by-step guides, safety checklists, tool tracking, and cost comparisons.

RepairMate is not a substitute for licensed professionals. For electrical work involving your panel, gas line repairs, or structural modifications, always hire a qualified contractor. RepairMate helps you know the difference.

Disclaimer: RepairMate provides home repair guides for informational purposes only. Always follow local building codes and regulations. When in doubt, consult a licensed professional. The app is not responsible for any damage or injury.
