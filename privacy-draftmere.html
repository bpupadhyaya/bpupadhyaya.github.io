College Essays: Draftmere: Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Contact: support@equalinformation.com. Last updated 2026-11-21.

What stays on your device

Your drafts, exercise answers, progress, quiz history, streak, pledge and settings are stored only on your device. The app has no account system and no server that receives this data. Deleting the app erases it. Lessons and data are bundled in the app and it works offline. We do not collect your name, email address or contact details.

No AI, no uploads

Draftmere has no AI feature and does not send your writing anywhere. Your essays are never read by us.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on browse screens and an occasional full-screen ad after a finished quiz, never while you write. AdMob may use your advertising identifier, approximate location and device and usage data to select and measure ads, under Google's own privacy policy. Adults on iOS are asked for permission before tracking, and where the law requires it Google shows a consent form. Declining gives non-personalised ads.

Teens and children

The app is for ages 13 and up. It asks your age once, on the device. If you are 13 to 17 the app shows only non-personalised ads, limits ad content to teen ratings, and never shows the tracking prompt. If you say you are under 13 the app stops: no ads are loaded and nothing is collected. The app is not directed to children.

Subscription

The optional monthly plan only removes ads. Purchases are processed by Apple or Google; we never see your payment details. Cancel any time in your App Store or Google Play account settings.

Sources you open

Source links open public web pages inside the app. Those sites apply their own privacy practices.
