Backgammon Table - Support

EqualInformation

Backgammon Table is a 3D backgammon game with a neural-network opponent, position studies and match review. This page answers the common questions.

How do I play?

Tap Play a match, choose an opponent and match length, then tap Roll. Tap a checker to see where it can go, tap the highlighted point to move it. The About page has the full rules and a how-to.

What do the opponent levels mean?

Rookie and Casual make human-like slips. Club always plays the move its network rates best. Expert, Master and Champion also look one reply ahead at their four, eight and twelve best candidates.

How do I remove ads?

Open About, then use Earn ad-free (watch rewarded videos for ad-free time) or Monthly pass. Restore is on the same page if you already own the one-time ad-free purchase or a subscription.

Cancel or manage a subscription

iOS Settings, tap your name, Subscriptions, Backgammon Table.

Ad privacy choices

In the app, Settings, Privacy choices for ads (shown where consent is required). Tracking can also be changed in iOS Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking.
