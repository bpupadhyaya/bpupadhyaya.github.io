Terms of use: Math UKG: Tumblekin

Math UKG: Tumblekin is a children's maths practice app published by EqualInformation LLC. Last updated 27 November 2026.

What the app is

Math UKG: Tumblekin is a practice garden for early maths, for children of about five and six to use with a grown-up. Treat it as extra practice. It does not replace a teacher, it is not a test, it cannot show how a child is developing, and we promise no result.

Free and paid

About half of each way of playing is free and stays free. Two optional monthly plans open the rest: Classical opens the whole Trail, and Real-World opens all the Helpers projects. The price is shown, in your own currency, on the Plans page in the grown-up area before you buy. There is no free trial.

How the monthly plans work

You are charged when you confirm the purchase, and the plan renews every month at the price shown until you cancel. You can cancel at any time. On Apple devices, cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current month, in your device settings under your name, then Subscriptions. On Android, cancel in Google Play under Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions. After you cancel, no further month is charged and the plan stays open until the month already running is over. Refund requests are decided by Apple or Google, not by us. If you reinstall or change device, use Restore purchases on the Plans page.

Using it safely

Please play nearby and stop when your child has had enough. Some tasks suggest using real objects: take care with small parts.
