Zymewell Privacy Policy

Zymewell (Enzyme Tours) is published by Equal Information and is available on iPhone, iPad and Android. Last updated 10 November 2026.

What stays on your device

Your study record (enzymes opened, quiz answers, streak, points, text size, ad-free progress) is stored only on your device. Zymewell has no accounts, no sign-in and no server that receives this record. All 3D structures, questions and the kinetics lab are bundled in the app and work offline.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device advertising identifier, approximate location and ad interaction data to choose and measure ads. On iPhone and iPad the app first asks for permission to track across other companies' apps and websites. On Android the app uses the Google advertising ID, which you can reset or delete in your Android settings. Where the law requires it, a Google User Messaging Platform form lets you choose how ad data is used, and you can reopen it from More. If you decline, ads are still shown but are less relevant. The Zymewell Plus subscription turns ads off.

Subscription

The optional ad-free monthly subscription is processed entirely by Apple (iPhone and iPad) or Google Play (Android). We never receive your payment details. Manage or cancel it in your Apple ID subscription settings or in Google Play under Payments and subscriptions.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, health data, microphone or camera input, and we do not sell personal data.

Children

Zymewell is an education app for students and is not directed to children under 13.

Education only

Zymewell is a study aid. It is not medical advice and must not be used to diagnose, treat or make decisions about any condition or medicine.

Data credits

Protein structures come from the RCSB Protein Data Bank (wwPDB), whose archive data are released under CC0 1.0. Credits to the depositors are shown in the app under More, Data sources.
