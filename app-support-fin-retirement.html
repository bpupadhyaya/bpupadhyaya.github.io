Common questions

Does it need internet? No. Everything works offline. Only ads and the subscription need a connection.

How do I make text bigger? Tap A+ at the top of any screen, or change your system text size.

How do I remove ads? More, then Go ad-free: subscribe monthly, or watch 100 short videos in a month (up to 40 a day) to remove ads for the rest of that month. Use Restore purchases on a new device.

Where do the formulas come from? More, then Accuracy and sources lists each formula, a worked reference value and two named sources.

I found a wrong number. Use Report an error in Accuracy and sources, or email us below.
