Privacy Policy

A Level English Language: Lectmere. Effective 27 November 2026.

1. Introduction and scope

EqualInformation, LLC publishes the app A Level English Language: Lectmere. This policy explains what information is handled when you use the app, from version 1.0.0. It applies to the app only.

2. Information we collect

Information you choose to provide: diagnostic reports you decide to send, with any note you add; feedback and suggestions you submit (category, message, optional role and rating) and, only if you tick the contact option, an email address so we can reply. Diagnostic and app-usage information collected with your agreement or where applicable law permits: device and operating-system details (model, system version, processor and memory, display size, language, region and time zone, accessibility and display settings), app version, performance and memory indicators, crash and error information, a random identifier created by the app, and how features are used. This includes which lessons, labs and screens you open, practice questions attempted, whether answers are correct, time taken, hints used, topic progress estimates, study suggestions made and their outcomes, and activity in labs. Typed answers to questions or search terms may be included after personal details are removed. We do not collect your name, contacts, precise location, photographs, files, an advertising identifier, or the notes, essay plans and other writing you enter in the app, which stay on your device.

3. How we use information

To operate, maintain, secure and support the app; to diagnose problems; to provide and personalise your study path; to understand how the app is used; for research, development and improvement of our apps and educational content; to read and act on feedback; and to comply with legal obligations.

4. Your choices

When you first open the app a notice explains these practices and offers a separate usage switch. The switch is off by default in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and wherever your region cannot be determined. You can change it at any time under Me, Settings, Privacy and data, where you can also request deletion of your usage data. Diagnostics and feedback are sent only when you choose to send them.

5. Storage and service providers

Information is sent over encrypted connections to a web application operated by EqualInformation, LLC and recorded in Google Workspace services (Google Sheets and Drive) and notification email, and handled by service providers acting on our behalf. A copy of Lectmere from a store can have its records written out in email to us: acceptance, language analysis practice batches, usage events, crashes, diagnostic reports, feedback and deletion requests. This mail is ours alone to receive and may be forwarded to another of our mailboxes. Purchases are processed by Apple or Google; we do not receive your payment details.

6. Retention and security

Usage, learning and diagnostic information is kept for as long as it remains useful for the purposes above; a deletion request is carried through manually, there being no automatic removal. Records already sent to us by email remain there unless manually deleted. We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. International users and legal bases

Where a legal basis is required we rely on your consent (the usage switch and your choice to send diagnostics or feedback) and on our legitimate interest in maintaining and improving the app. Information may be processed in the United States.

8. Children

We design our apps to collect only the information described in this policy. If you believe information has been provided to us that you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

9. Changes to this policy

If we make a material change, the version of the first-run notice changes and the notice is shown again.

10. Contact

EqualInformation, LLC. General questions: support@equalinformation.com. Deletion and data requests: diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
