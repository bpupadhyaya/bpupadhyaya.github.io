Reaching us

Email support@equalinformation.com and tell us which device you use and what you saw. Inside the app, More then Feedback or Send diagnostics also reaches us.

Subscription help

On a new device, open More, then Wiremoot Plus, then Restore purchases, signed in to the store account that paid. To stop renewal, use your Apple or Google subscription settings.

Common questions

  • Where is my progress? Only on your device; it does not move between devices.
  • I found an error. Send it from More, then Feedback, quoting the topic code.
  • Is this official? No. It is independent of the IB.
