Lexiesta: Spanish Flashcards: Support

Lexiesta teaches Spanish to English speakers with original flashcards, example sentences, quizzes and offline pronunciation.

Common questions

  • No sound? The app uses the Spanish voices on your device. iPhone: Settings, Accessibility, Spoken Content, Voices, Spanish. Android: Settings, System, Languages, Text-to-speech output, then install Spanish voice data. Check that the ringer or media volume is up.
  • Text too small? Use the A- and A+ buttons at the top of any screen, or change your device text size.
  • Lost progress? Progress lives on your device only. Deleting the app or resetting its data removes it, and we cannot recover it.
  • Does it work offline? Yes. Cards, quizzes and pronunciation work without a connection once the voice data is installed.
  • How do I remove ads? Open the in-app purchase screen (Settings or More, or the ad-free prompt) and choose the ad-free subscription or the one-time purchase. You can also watch an optional short video where offered for temporary extras.
  • How do I restore a purchase? Open Settings (or the purchase screen) and tap Restore purchases, using the same Apple Account or Google account you bought with. This is needed after reinstalling or changing device.
  • How do I cancel the subscription? iPhone and iPad: Settings, your name, Subscriptions. Android: Google Play, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel it.
  • Can I change my ad choices? Yes. Use the privacy choices for ads in the app's Settings where shown, or your device's privacy settings.
