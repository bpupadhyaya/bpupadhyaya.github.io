Slatewise support

Help for Slatewise: Time Block Planner on iPhone and iPad.

Common questions

How do I add a block?
Type into the bar on the Today screen, for example "gym 6pm for 45 min". Leave out the time and Slatewise suggests slots based on when you usually do that kind of thing. You can also double-tap an empty spot on the timeline.
How do I move or resize a block?
Press and hold a block, then slide it. Drag its bottom edge to change the length. Edges snap to your chosen step and to neighbouring blocks.
What happens to things I did not finish?
They are kept. A banner on Today and the Inbox offer to roll them into open time, push them to tomorrow, mark them done or drop them.
Widgets and Live Activity do not update.
Open Slatewise once, then add the widget again. Live Activities must be allowed in iOS Settings, Slatewise.
How do I remove ads?
Settings, Go ad-free: subscribe monthly or earn the month by watching short videos. "Restore purchases" is on the same screen.
Where is my data?
Only on your phone. See the privacy policy. Settings, Delete all my data erases it.
