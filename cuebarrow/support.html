Support

Help with GCSE Media Studies: Cuebarrow. Email support@equalinformation.com and tell us your device and what you were doing.

Where do I report a fault?

In the app, open More, then Send a snag report. You see everything before you press Send.

How do I restore or cancel Plus?

Restore: More, Settings, Restore purchases. Cancel: Manage subscription on the same screen, or your App Store or Google Play subscriptions. Cancelling takes effect at the end of the paid month.

How do I change text size?

Use A minus and A plus at the top of any screen, from 50 to 300 percent.

Does it include the Close Study Products?

No. AQA publishes that list to schools and changes it. Use the framework practice here with the products your teacher gives you.

How do I ask for my records to be deleted?

More, then Privacy and data, or email diagnostics@equalinformation.com. Deletion is manual, and copies already emailed to our mailboxes can remain.
