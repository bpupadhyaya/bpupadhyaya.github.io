Support

Help with GCSE Media Studies: Cuebarrow. Email support@equalinformation.com with your device and what you were doing when the problem appeared.

Reporting a fault

Open More, then Send a snag report. The whole report is shown before you decide to send it.

Restoring or cancelling Plus

To restore, go to More, Settings, Restore purchases. To cancel, use Manage subscription on that screen or the subscriptions section of your App Store or Google Play account. A cancellation applies when the paid month ends.

Changing text size

The A minus and A plus buttons at the top of each screen scale text from 50 to 300 percent.

Close Study Products

They are not included. AQA releases and revises that list for schools, so use the framework practice here alongside the products your teacher assigns.

Deleting your records

Go to More, then Privacy and data, or write to diagnostics@equalinformation.com. We delete by hand, and copies already emailed to our mailboxes may stay.
