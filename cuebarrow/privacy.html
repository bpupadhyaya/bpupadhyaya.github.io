Privacy policy

This page describes the information GCSE Media Studies: Cuebarrow (published by EqualInformation, LLC for iPhone, iPad and Android) sends away from your device, where it ends up, and the controls you have.

Kept on your device only

Your progress, revision schedule, pinned items, study plan, exam date, lab settings, Statement of Intent drafts, practice paper answers and searches never leave the device by themselves. The one exception is the short set of summary counts you can read in a snag report preview before choosing to send it.

The acceptance receipt

Pressing the start button on the opening notice always sends one receipt, even with sharing off. It holds a random app number generated on your device, a receipt number, the time, the notice version with its full text and a fingerprint of it, how long the notice stayed open, whether you scrolled to its end, where the sharing switch was set, your device region setting, the app version, build and platform, a label saying whether this looks like a store install, a test build or an emulator, and, only if sharing is on, the device model.

Usage and learning records

Sharing is off when you first install. You can turn it on at the opening notice or later in More, then Privacy and data.

With it on, the app reports which screens, lessons, briefs, labs, questions, practice papers, planning tools, source links and subscription screens you open; whether answers and choices were correct; the option you picked; how long you took; progress estimates; which suggested next step you followed; session length, text size and screen layout; and notes about crashes or script errors. Each record carries your random app number and the device and build details above, but not your name. Whether you have bought or hold a subscription is not part of any record we send.

Messages you send on purpose

  • Snag report: sent only when you press Send, after the full contents are shown to you. It has the app, platform, build and device model, your note, a few settings and progress counts, a short trail of recent screens and answer results, and any crash signature.
  • Notes to the team, thumbs and problem reports: whatever you write or tap, plus the screen or question involved and any role, course, rating or source link you add. An email address travels only if you tick the contact box and type one.
  • Deletion request: your random app number and the request itself.

Please do not type names or other personal details into these boxes.

Not collected

There is no sign-in. The app does not ask for your name, contacts, precise location, photos, camera, microphone or files, has no advertising identifier and does not track you across other apps or sites. Like any internet request, your network address is visible to the servers that receive it.

What we use it for

We use the records to prove the notice was accepted, to operate, protect and support the app, to track down faults, to see which material helps and where learners get stuck, to improve questions, explanations and learning features, to reply to you, and to comply with the law.

Where it goes

Records travel over an encrypted connection to a service we operate on Google Apps Script and are stored in Google Sheets and Drive files in our Google Workspace account. Google handles them on our behalf.

For copies installed from the App Store or Google Play, records, including usage records, may also be emailed to company mailboxes, which can forward them to another mailbox we control.

Purchases are processed by Apple or Google under their own terms and we never see card details. Web pages opened from source links belong to other organisations and follow their own policies.

Retention and deletion

We do not delete anything on a schedule. To have your records removed, choose Delete my usage data in More, Privacy and data, or write to diagnostics@equalinformation.com quoting the random app number shown on that screen. We carry this out by hand. Emails already sent, including forwarded ones, may remain in mailboxes. Uninstalling the app clears what is on the device but not what was sent earlier.

Your choices and rights

The app works fully with sharing off. Depending on where you live you may be entitled to see, correct, erase, restrict or object to the use of information about you, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority. Optional records rest on your choice of the switch; the receipt, support and security uses rest on our legitimate interests. Information may be handled in the United States.

Young people and parents

Cuebarrow is made for GCSE students, most of whom are 14 to 16, so every optional record is off until the learner switches it on. No account is needed. A parent, carer or teacher who wants information removed can contact us and we will help.

Updates and contact

If we change these practices we will revise this page and, for significant changes, show a new opening notice. Questions go to support@equalinformation.com.
