Privacy policy

This policy explains what GCSE Media Studies: Cuebarrow, an app by EqualInformation, LLC for iPhone, iPad and Android, sends from your device, where it goes and what you can do about it.

What stays on your device

Your progress, review schedule, pins, plan, exam date, lab choices, practice Statement of Intent drafts, practice paper answers and search words are saved on your device. They are not included in the records described below, except for summary counts shown to you in a snag report preview.

The receipt sent when you accept the opening notice

When you press the start button, the app sends one receipt whether or not sharing is on. It contains a random app number created on your device, a receipt number, the time, the notice version, its text and a fingerprint of that text, how long the notice was open and whether you reached the end, the position of the sharing switch, your device region setting, the app version and build, the platform, whether the copy appears to be a store install, a test build or an emulator, and the device model. With sharing off in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or an unknown region, the device model is left out.

Usage and learning records, only while sharing is on

The switch starts off in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and where the region is unknown, and starts on elsewhere. You can change it on the opening screen or later under More, then Privacy and data.

While it is on, the app sends which screens, lessons, briefs, labs, questions, practice papers, plan tools, source links and subscription screens you use; whether answers or choices were right; the option picked; time taken; progress estimates; which suggested next step you followed; session length, text size and screen layout; purchase status without any payment details; and notes about crashes or script errors. These records carry the random app number and the device and build details listed above, not your name.

Things you choose to send

  • A snag report: sent only when you press Send, after you have seen its full contents. It holds app, platform, build and device model details, your note, plan status, a short list of recent screens and answer results, a few settings and progress counts, and any crash signature.
  • Notes to the team, thumbs up or down and problem reports: what you write or tap, with the screen or question concerned, and any role, course, rating or source link you add. An email address is included only if you tick the contact box and type one.
  • A deletion request: your random app number and the request.

Please leave names and other personal details out of anything you type.

What we do not collect

The app does not ask for your name, account, contacts, precise location, photographs, camera, microphone or files, and does not use advertising identifiers or follow you across other apps and websites. As with any internet request, your network address reaches the servers that receive it.

Why we use the records

To keep a record of notice acceptance; to run, secure and support the app; to find and fix faults; to understand which parts help and where learners struggle; to improve the questions, explanations and learning features; to answer you when you ask; and to meet legal obligations.

Where records go

Records are sent over an encrypted connection to a service we run on Google Apps Script and are kept in Google Sheets and Google Drive files in our Google Workspace account.

Records from a copy installed from the App Store or Google Play, including usage records, can also be emailed to our own mailboxes. Those mailboxes may forward the email to another mailbox that we control. Google processes the information as our service provider.

Apple or Google handles purchases under their own terms. We do not receive your card details. Pages you open from source links belong to other organisations and follow their own policies.

How long records are kept and how deletion works

Nothing is deleted automatically. We keep records until we delete them. To ask for deletion, use Delete my usage data under More, then Privacy and data, or email diagnostics@equalinformation.com and include the random app number shown on that screen. Deletion is done by hand. Copies that have already been emailed, including forwarded copies, can remain in those mailboxes. Removing the app deletes what is stored on your device but not records already sent.

Your choices and rights

You can keep sharing off and still use the app. Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the use of information, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority. Where the law requires a basis, optional records rely on your choice of the switch, and the receipt, support and security uses rely on our legitimate interests. Information may be processed in the United States.

Children

The app is designed to send only what this policy describes and needs no account. A parent, carer or teacher who believes information should be removed can contact us.

Changes and contact

If the practices change we will update this page and, where the change is significant, show a new opening notice. Contact EqualInformation, LLC at support@equalinformation.com.
