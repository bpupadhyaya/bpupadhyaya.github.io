Osteowell: Bone Anatomy 3D

A 3D skeleton you can rotate, peel apart and quiz yourself on: 198 separately selectable bones, landmarks, joint range of motion and fracture patterns. Works offline.

Help

  • Rotate and zoom: drag to orbit, pinch to zoom, two fingers to move, double-tap to reset the view.
  • Bigger text: use the A- and A+ buttons on any reading screen; the app also follows your system text size.
  • Remove ads: Me tab, Ad-free. Choose the monthly subscription, or earn ad-free time by watching rewarded videos. Restore Purchases is on the same sheet.
  • Cancel the subscription: Settings on your iPhone or iPad, your name, Subscriptions.
