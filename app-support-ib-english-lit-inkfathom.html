Help for IB English Lit Prep: Inkfathom

Answers and a way to reach us. In force from 27 November 2026.

Write to us

support@equalinformation.com. It helps to name your device, the app version shown under More, and what you were doing.

Send a problem report from the app

Open More and scroll to Privacy and data; the problem report button is there. Check what it lists, write a line about the fault if that helps, then tap Send report.

Plus did not appear after reinstalling

Sign in to the store with the account that bought it, then choose Restore purchases under More or on the Plus screen.

Stopping Plus

Tap Manage subscription on the Plus sheet, or use the subscription list kept by your store. Access lasts to the next billing date.

Removing usage records

Inside Privacy and data under More there is a deletion button. Writing to diagnostics@equalinformation.com works too.

A course fact looks wrong

Go by your school and the current guide, and tell us with 'Report an error here' under the lesson or explanation.
