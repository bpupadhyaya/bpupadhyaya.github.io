Support

Math LKG: Pipperoo, by EqualInformation LLC

Help for grown-ups using Math LKG: Pipperoo. Write to support@equalinformation.com and say which device and app version you have. Please do not send your child’s name or photographs.

Opening the grown-up area

On the first screen, press and hold “Grown-ups” for about a second, then answer the written multiplication.

A plan I paid for is not showing

Check that the device is online and signed in with the same Apple Account, then press “Get my plans back on this device” in the grown-up area under Plans.

Changing or cancelling a plan

Press “Change or cancel in the store” in the same place, or open your device Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions.

No sound

Check the device volume, and that “Sound and voice” is on in the grown-up settings.

Text size

Use A− and A+ on any screen with words: 50 to 300 per cent.

Diagnostics and feedback

In the grown-up area, “Send diagnostics” shows what it will write and opens a draft in your own mail app; “Send feedback” opens an empty draft. Nothing is sent until you send it. If no mail app opens, write to the address shown.

Starting again

“Erase all progress” in the grown-up settings clears the record on this device.
