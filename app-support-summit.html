Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Contact: support@equalinformation.com. Last updated 2026-11-24.

Download Top-Tier Admission: Summit on the App Store

Support

Questions, content corrections and subscription help: support@equalinformation.com. Include your device model and app version.

Send diagnostics

Open More, Settings, Privacy and data and choose Send diagnostics. The app shows you exactly what the report contains; nothing is sent until you tap Send. The report is delivered to diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

Feedback and suggestions

Use Feedback and suggestions under Settings, the thumbs and Report a problem controls under answers, lessons and tools, or Suggest content. Nothing is sent until you tap Send. An email address is included only if you tick that we may contact you about it.

Delete my usage data

In the app, open More, Settings, Privacy and data and choose Delete my usage data, or email diagnostics@equalinformation.com with your request.

Subscription

Manage or cancel the plan in your App Store or Google Play account settings. Use Restore purchases on the Summit Plus screen if a purchase does not appear.

Found an error?

Send the unit or question and the official page that disagrees. Each cited source opens inside the app.
