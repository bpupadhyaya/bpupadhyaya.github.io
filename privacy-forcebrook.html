Forcebrook: AP Physics 1 - Privacy Policy

Forcebrook stores your study progress only on your device. We do not run accounts and we do not receive your answers, plans or scores.

Ads: the free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Where required you are first asked for consent (App Tracking Transparency on iPhone and iPad, and Google's consent form in regions that need it). Google may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve and measure ads, as described in its own policies. Subscribers see no ads.

Purchases are handled by Apple and Google. We receive no payment details.

Reference pages open inside the app only when you ask for them and only when you are online.

Children: the app is a study tool for general audiences and is not directed to children under 13.
