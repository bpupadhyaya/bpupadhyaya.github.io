Get help

E-mail support@equalinformation.com and say which device you use and what happened. In the app, More then Privacy and data lets you read a fault report and then send it, and More then Feedback lets you tell us about a content problem. The "Aa" button changes the reading size.

Subscriptions

To get Plus back on a device, open More, then Saucequill Plus, and choose "Restore my Plus purchase" while signed in to the account that bought it. To stop it, use "Change or cancel in the store" on the same screen, or your App Store or Google Play account settings. Cancelling applies from the next billing period.

Common questions

Does it work offline? Lessons, questions, jobs, labs and papers are inside the app. Buying, restoring, opening web pages and sending records need a connection.

Where is my progress? On the device you study on. There is no account, so it does not move between devices.

Is it for my exam board? It follows the Department for Education subject content that England's GCSE specifications share, with section headings for AQA, Eduqas and OCR. Check your own specification.

I am worried about an allergy or illness. The app cannot advise you. Speak to a doctor; for a severe reaction call your emergency number at once.
