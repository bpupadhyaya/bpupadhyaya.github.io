1. Scope

This policy explains how EqualInformation, LLC ("we") handles information in the GCSE Food: Saucequill app for iPhone, iPad and Android. Saucequill has no user accounts.

2. Information kept on your device

Your progress, study plan, notes, written practice answers, job documents and anything else you type while studying are stored in the app on your device. They are not included in usage records. You can remove them with "Erase progress" under More, then Privacy and data, or by deleting the app.

3. Information we receive

A. Record of agreement. When you accept the first-run study notice, the app sends a short record: a random identifier created for this installation, a record identifier, the time, the version and fingerprint of the notice, the way you agreed, the position of the usage switch, your device's region, locale and time zone settings, the platform, app version and build, and how this copy was installed (store, testing or other), whether it is a test build and whether it is running on an emulator. Records of the kinds described below carry the same installation facts.

B. Usage, learning and diagnostic information, only while the usage switch is on.

  • With the agreement record: the notice text shown, how long it was open and whether it was scrolled to the end, installation and session dates, first launch time and launch counts, and device information.
  • Device information: device model, operating system and version, app version, languages, region and time zone, processor, graphics hardware name, memory, screen and window size and the system text-size setting.
  • App use: sessions and study time, screens opened, view and text-size changes, plan changes, subscription screen interactions, and ratings of explanations.
  • Learning activity: the identifiers of questions attempted, whether each answer was correct, which option position was chosen, response time, hint use, attempt number, topic progress estimates, the app's decisions about what to suggest next and whether you followed them, job steps started and completed, and lab use, including control names and the numerical values set.
  • Performance and faults: memory use, a short signature of a script error or a previous crash.

These records never include text or numbers that you type into the app. We do not collect your name, contacts, photographs, files, precise location or advertising identifier.

C. Diagnostic reports you send. If you choose "Send diagnostics", you first see the report. It contains the device information above, storage capacity and use, your subscription state and settings, a list of recent app events, the last crash signature and any note you add. It is sent only when you press Send, whether or not the usage switch is on. A request to delete usage data is sent in the same way.

D. Feedback you send. If you send feedback, we receive your message, the category you pick, and, if you give them, your role, a source link and the question concerned. The app removes text that looks like an e-mail address, web link or long number from the message. We receive an e-mail address only if you tick the box allowing us to contact you and enter one. Please do not put personal details in notes or messages.

4. How we use information

We use it to operate, maintain and secure the app; to diagnose problems; to provide and personalise the learning experience; to understand how learning features are used; for research and development; to analyse and improve our apps and educational content; to answer requests; and to meet legal obligations. Information may be transferred as part of a financing, merger, acquisition or similar business transaction, subject to the commitments in this policy.

5. Your choices

  • The usage switch starts off if your device region is in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, or cannot be read. Elsewhere it starts on. You can change it on the first screen and at any time under More, then Privacy and data. When you turn it off, usage records not yet sent are discarded.
  • Diagnostic reports and feedback are sent only when you choose to send them.
  • To ask for access to or deletion of information, use the button under Privacy and data or write to diagnostics@equalinformation.com. Please quote the installation identifier shown in the diagnostic report preview.

6. Storage and service providers

Records are sent over encrypted connections and stored using Google Workspace services, including spreadsheets, stored files and e-mail, by service providers acting for us. Records from copies installed from a store, including agreement records, reports and batches of usage and learning records, may also be copied by e-mail to our own mailboxes, and may be forwarded from there to another mailbox of ours. As with any internet service, the providers that carry a record can see technical connection information such as an internet address. Apple or Google processes subscription payments under its own terms; we do not receive your payment card details. Web pages you open from the app are run by their own publishers.

7. Retention and security

We keep information for as long as it is useful for the purposes above or as the law requires, and delete it on a valid request. We delete by hand: the button under Privacy and data only sends the request, nothing is erased automatically, and e-mailed copies stay unless they are also removed by hand. We use reasonable measures to protect it, although no system can be guaranteed to be secure.

8. International users and legal bases

Information may be processed in the United States and other countries. Where a legal basis is required, we rely on your consent for optional usage sharing and for what you choose to send, and on our legitimate interests in keeping a record of agreement and running the app. You may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object, and to complain to your data protection authority.

9. Children

The app is a study aid that asks for no name or account. A parent, carer or school who believes that a child has sent personal information in a note or message can contact us to have it deleted.

10. Changes and contact

We will post changes here and update the date above. Contact EqualInformation, LLC at support@equalinformation.com.
