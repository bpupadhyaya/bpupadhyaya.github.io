Support

Getting help

Write to support@equalinformation.com. Tell us the device, its system version and what happened. Please do not include your child’s name.

Common questions

How do I reach the plans?

Open Grown-ups on the home screen, pass the adult check, then choose Plans.

How do I restore a plan on a new device?

In Plans, choose Restore purchases while signed in to the same app store account.

How do I stop a plan?

In Plans choose Manage or cancel, or go to the subscriptions page of your app store account. Stopping applies from the next renewal.

Can I make the text bigger?

Yes: A+ at the top of every screen, from 50 to 300 percent.

How do I erase my child’s progress?

Grown-up area, Settings, Erase all progress. It is only stored on the device.

How do I report a problem?

Open Grown-ups, pass the adult check, then choose Help and reports. You can read the whole report there. It opens as a draft in your own mail app, and nothing is sent until you press send. You can also write to diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

Does the app need the internet?

No. Buying or restoring a plan talks to the store, an email you choose to send goes through your mail app, and showing a cited official page in the grown-up area needs the internet (the app keeps its own summary for use without it).
