App Support — PlainScam

Get help with PlainScam — an offline, plain-language scam-education app.

  • Home
  • /
  • App Support — PlainScam

PlainScam is a plain-language, pattern-recognition scam-education app for iOS and Android. It packs over 1,100 free and Pro guides across 35 modules — phone/robocall, SMS, email/phishing, tech-support, government/tax impersonation, delivery, romance, crypto, investment, and elder/family-targeted scams among them — plus real, interactive tools: a decision tree for live pressure moments, a message/call pattern-checker, a numeracy gut-check tool for "is this return realistic," quizzes, checklists, red-flag scorers, a personal log, a shareable card generator, and reminders. It's 100% on-device, works fully offline, and never monitors your real accounts, calls, or messages.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is PlainScam available yet?

Not yet — PlainScam is currently in development. Both the iOS and Android versions build and run cleanly, and the app has been extensively tested; this page will be updated with real download links as soon as it's submitted and approved on the App Store and Google Play.

Does PlainScam work without internet?

Yes — completely. Every guide, decision tree, quiz, checklist, scorer, and tool works fully offline. The only thing that ever talks to the network is an optional Background Sync (or manual "Sync Now") that downloads a static, read-only content file when a newer version is available — entirely optional, and off doesn't affect anything else.

Is my data private?

Yes. PlainScam has no account, no login, and no company-run server. Bookmarks, personal logs, checklist progress, and reminder preferences are stored only in a local database on your device and are never uploaded anywhere. Settings > Delete All My Data clears all of it, in one step, at any time. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Can PlainScam detect fraud on my actual accounts, calls, or texts?

No, and it's not designed to. PlainScam is an educational, pattern-recognition tool — it never has access to your real accounts, calls, or messages. Its checker tool works by you pasting text or describing what you heard/read, entirely on-device; it never monitors anything automatically.

What's the difference between the free and Pro tiers?

The core protection modules (phone/robocall, SMS, email/phishing, tech-support, government impersonation, delivery scams, the universal pressure-tactics module, and the real-time decision-support tools) are free, permanently. PlainScam Pro is an optional subscription that unlocks specialized-depth modules — crypto, investment, banking, romance, and business-fraud scams among them.

What is the numeracy gut-check tool?

A lightweight, scam-specific slider tool: enter a promised annual return or interest rate, and it compares it against plausible real-world ranges (savings/bonds, stock-market averages, and clearly-unrealistic territory), flagging the "guaranteed + high return" combination specifically since that pairing alone is a strong fraud signal. It's a gut check, not investment advice.

How does the pattern-checker tool work?

Paste a suspicious text message or email, or describe what a caller said, and it's checked entirely on-device against PlainScam's bundled scam-pattern library — nothing you paste is ever sent anywhere.

Can I reorder the modules or manage my reminders in one place?

Yes. More > Reorder Modules lets you move your most-relevant modules to the top of Home (with a one-tap reset back to the curated default order), and Settings > Notification Preferences lists every reminder the app can send, each toggleable from a single screen.

What accessibility features does PlainScam have?

Full VoiceOver/TalkBack support on every interactive tool, Dynamic Type plus an independent in-app Text Size control, Dyslexia-Friendly Text, Reduce Motion, a Color-Blind-Safe Palette, a High Contrast Mode, adjustable read-aloud speaking rate, and Dark/Light/System appearance. See the in-app Accessibility Statement (Settings) for the full, honest current status.

Does PlainScam cost anything?

The core protection modules are free, with no ads. PlainScam Pro is an optional subscription for specialized-depth modules — see Settings in the app for current pricing.

The app is crashing or not loading. What should I do?

Try these steps:
1. Force-close and reopen the app.
2. Restart your device.
3. Make sure you are running the latest version once PlainScam is available on the App Store or Google Play.
4. If the issue persists, use the "Report an issue with this guide" link on the relevant guide, or contact us below with your device model, OS version, and a description of the problem.
