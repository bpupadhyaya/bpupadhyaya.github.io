Contact

Write to support@equalinformation.com. Please include your device model and the app version shown at the bottom of Settings.

No sound in a listening task

Wortmoor reads German with the speech engine on your device. Check the volume and silent mode, and install or download a German (Germany) voice in your system's speech or accessibility settings.

Recording does not start

Allow microphone access for Wortmoor in your system settings. Recordings stay on your device.

Subscription

Open Settings in the app to restore purchases or to manage your subscription. Cancel any time in your app store account; cancellation takes effect from the next billing period.

Text size

Use A- and A+ at the top of the screen (50% to 300%).
