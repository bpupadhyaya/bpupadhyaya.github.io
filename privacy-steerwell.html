What we collect

We do not run a server or accounts for this app. Your progress, credits and text-size setting stay on your device.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. With your consent (Google consent form and, on iPhone, the tracking prompt), advertising partners may use your device advertising identifier to personalise ads. You can decline and keep using every lesson. Subscribers see no ads.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple or Google. We receive no payment details.

Your age answer

Your age answer, asked on first launch and kept only on this device, decides how ads behave: under 13 sees no ads and no advertising code runs; 13 to 17 sees only non-personalised ads with a teen rating and is never asked about tracking; 18 and over sees ads according to consent choices. You can change it in the app.

Children

General-audience learning tool, not directed to children under 13.
