Jigsaw Atelier support

Jigsaw Atelier is an iPhone and iPad jigsaw by EqualInformation with 120 painted pictures, 12 to 204 pieces and a daily canvas. It works fully offline.

Common questions

My table disappeared. Tap Back on the puzzle screen and choose Save and leave. The Wall then shows a Pick up where you left off card.

How do I turn ads off? Open About and choose Earn ad-free (watch videos) or Monthly pass. Restore purchases is on the same page and in Settings.

I cannot reach a piece. Pinch to zoom, drag an empty spot to pan, or tap Tidy to lay every loose piece out in rows.

How do I cancel the monthly pass? Settings, your name, Subscriptions, Jigsaw Atelier.
