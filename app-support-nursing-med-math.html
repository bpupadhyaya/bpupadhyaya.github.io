Download Dosage Calculation: Dosewick on the App Store

Dosewick is an offline dosage-calculation practice app: build each dose from unit fractions, watch the units cancel, check rounding, take a timed exam and review a log of your slips. Education only, invented examples.

Report an error

Found a wrong value, label or shape? Email support@equalinformation.com. Every topic lists its sources on the Accuracy and sources page.

Ad-free, trial and restore

Open the Ad-free button, then choose the monthly Dosewick Ad-free subscription ($2.99 per month, 7-day free trial for new subscribers, renews until cancelled in your Apple Account settings) or the one-time Remove Ads purchase. Use Restore purchases on a new device. You can also earn ad-free time by watching an optional video.

Text size

Use A- and A+ in the app. Dosewick also follows your system text size.
