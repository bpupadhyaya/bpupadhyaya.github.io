What we collect

We do not run a server or accounts for this app. Your progress, credits and text-size setting stay on your device.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. With your consent (shown by the Google consent form and, on iPhone, the tracking prompt), advertising partners may use your device advertising identifier to personalise ads. You can decline and keep using every lesson. Subscribers see no ads.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple or Google. We receive no payment details.

Children

The app is a general-audience learning tool and is not directed to children under 13. On first launch the app asks whether you are 18 or older. If you answer that you are under 18, ads are non-personalised, limited to teen-suitable content, tagged for the under-age-of-consent setting, and the iPhone tracking prompt is not shown.
