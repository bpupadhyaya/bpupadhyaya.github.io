Pockline support

Pickleball scorekeeper, event generator and open-play queue for iPhone and iPad.

Quick answers

  • Who serves? The scoreboard shows the score call, the server and the court side. It follows the USA Pickleball rulebook; tap Rules inside a match to see each rule and its number.
  • A tap was wrong. Press Undo. Every rally is a log entry, so undo is exact, back to the first serve.
  • Rally scoring. Choose it when you start a match; every rally scores and one player serves per turn.
  • Fairness numbers. An event's Fairness tab lists measured partner repeats, games per player and sit-outs for that exact schedule.
  • Ads. Settings shows the earn-ad-free counter, the monthly subscription, Restore purchases and, where required, Ad privacy choices.
  • Cancel the subscription. Settings on your iPhone, your name, Subscriptions, Pockline.
