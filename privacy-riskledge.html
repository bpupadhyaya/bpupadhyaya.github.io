AI Risk Management: Riskledge - Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Last updated 2026-11-20.

Riskledge teaches AI governance and risk work with fictional organisations. It has no account and no server of ours. Progress (stars, text size, credits) stays on your device.

Ads

The free version shows ads from a third-party ad network. Where required you are asked for consent; with your permission the network may use your device advertising identifier to show and measure ads. You can decline and still use every lesson, and change your choice under More, Privacy options, or in device settings.

Purchases

An optional monthly subscription removes ads. Payment is handled by your app store; we do not see payment details.

Sources

When you open a cited source, the page loads from its publisher and is subject to that publisher's policy.

Children

The app is for adults (18+).
