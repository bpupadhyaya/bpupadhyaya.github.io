Privacy Policy — PlainScam

Effective Date: September 4, 2026

  • Home
  • /
  • Privacy Policy
  • /
  • PlainScam

1 Who We Are

PlainScam is provided by EqualInformation, LLC. PlainScam is a plain-language, pattern-recognition and education app that helps you recognize common scam tactics — over 1,100 free and Pro guides, real-time decision-support tools, and a fully offline, on-device library.

If you have privacy questions or requests, you may contact us at:
Email: privacy@equalinformation.com
Contact page: equalinformation.weebly.com/contact.html | bpupadhyaya.github.io#contact

2 Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the PlainScam app (iOS bundle identifier: com.equalinformation.plainscam.app; Android package name: com.equalinformation.plainscam.app), available on the App Store and Google Play.

3 What We Collect

Nothing. PlainScam does not require an account and does not collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information. It does not use analytics or advertising SDKs of any kind.

4 Information PlainScam May Store — On Your Device Only

Depending on how you use the app, PlainScam may store the following locally, never transmitting it:

  • Bookmarked guides, your recently-viewed guide list, and your custom module order (if you reorder them)
  • Personal scam-pattern logs and checklist/scorer progress you create in the app
  • Card drafts (e.g. from the shareable red-flags card generator)
  • Reminder preferences (which local notifications you've turned on, and how often)
  • Appearance settings — color scheme, text size, dyslexia-friendly text, reduce motion, color-blind-safe palette, high contrast mode, and read-aloud speaking rate

All of it lives only in a local database on your device. It is never uploaded anywhere, and we have no way to see it. Settings > Delete All My Data clears all of it, in one step, at any time.

5 The One Thing That Talks to the Internet

If you turn on Automatic Background Sync in Settings (or tap Sync Now), the app periodically checks a public, read-only file for updated guide content and downloads it if newer. This is a plain file download — no account and no personal data of any kind is sent with the request, only a request for a static file. You can turn this off at any time in Settings, and the app works fully offline either way.

6 Reminders and Notifications

If you set up a reminder (from a guide, or from the unified Notification Preferences screen), it is scheduled and delivered entirely by your device's own operating system. No reminder content ever leaves your device.

7 Local-First, Offline Design

PlainScam's core library, decision trees, quizzes, checklists, scorers, and tools (including its pattern-checker and numeracy gut-check tools) all run entirely on-device. There is no account, no company-run server, and no user tracking.

8 No Analytics or Tracking

PlainScam does not use any analytics SDKs, crash reporters, advertising SDKs, or tracking tools. No data is sent to third-party analytics services.

9 Educational Content and Disclaimer

PlainScam is an educational, pattern-recognition tool that helps you learn to recognize common scam tactics. It is not a substitute for professional legal, financial, or law-enforcement advice, and it cannot detect fraud on your actual accounts, calls, or messages — it never has access to them. It never monitors your real accounts, calls, or messages, and never stores or transmits your personal financial data.

10 Permissions

PlainScam does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, or photos. On Android, it requests only Internet and Network State (for the optional content sync above) and Notifications (for reminders you turn on yourself).

11 Subscriptions

PlainScam's core guides and tools are free. PlainScam Pro is an optional subscription that unlocks specialized-depth modules (crypto, investment, banking, romance, and business fraud, among others). If you subscribe, your payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google. We never see or store your payment details — we only receive confirmation from the App Store or Google Play that an active subscription exists.

12 Sharing and Sale of Data

EqualInformation, LLC does not sell personal or sensitive user data. We do not share users' data with data brokers or for advertising purposes. The company does not intentionally transmit personal or sensitive PlainScam user data to its own servers or unrelated third parties.

13 Data Retention and Deletion

Settings > Delete All My Data removes your bookmarks, personal logs, checklist progress, saved cards, reminders, and recently-viewed list — complete and immediate, since all of it lives only on your device. Uninstalling PlainScam removes all locally stored data. Neither action affects the bundled guide library itself, which simply reinstalls with the app.

14 Children's Privacy

PlainScam is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

15 Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be included in the app and posted here, with the Effective Date above revised.
