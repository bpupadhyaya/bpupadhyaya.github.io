Unitwick Privacy Policy

Unitwick: Unit Converter, published by Equal Information. Effective 9 November 2026.

What stays on your device

Unitwick works fully offline. Your conversions, history, favourites and text size are stored only on your device. We do not run accounts, servers or analytics that receive them, and we cannot see them.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show ads, Google and its partners may collect your device's advertising identifier, coarse location derived from your IP address, and information about how you interact with ads. On iOS the app first asks, through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, whether it may use the advertising identifier for personalised ads; if you decline, you still see ads but they are not personalised. Where required (EEA, UK and some US states) a consent message from Google's User Messaging Platform lets you choose and later change your choices. Learn more at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Subscriptions and purchases

The optional ad-free subscription is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details or Apple Account. The app checks your subscription status with StoreKit on your device. Rewarded videos that earn ad-free time are provided by AdMob; the earned time and counters are kept on your device.

Data we do not collect

Unitwick does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location, and does not use artificial intelligence services.

Children

Unitwick is a general-audience utility and is not directed to children under 13.

Your choices

You can reset the advertising identifier or turn tracking off in iOS Settings, remove the app to delete all local data, or cancel the subscription in Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions.
