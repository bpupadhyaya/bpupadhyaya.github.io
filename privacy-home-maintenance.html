Fixwick Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 November 2026. Fixwick (Home Maintenance) is published by Equal Information.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter, including your home profile, job history, appliance details, warranty dates and expenses, is stored only on your device. We have no account system and no server that receives this data. Local reminders are scheduled on your device by iOS.

Advertising

Fixwick is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob unless you choose to go ad-free. On first launch iOS may ask whether the app can track you across other companies' apps and websites, and, where the law requires it, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform appears. If you allow tracking or consent, Google may use your device's advertising identifier and ad interaction data to personalise ads. If you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalised. You can change this in iOS Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking. Google's practices are described at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Subscription

The optional ad-free subscription is handled entirely by Apple. We do not see your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether a subscription is active.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal data, we do not build profiles of you, we do not use analytics SDKs, and we do not ask for your location, contacts, photos or microphone.

Children

Fixwick is meant for adults who look after a home and is not directed to children.

Deleting your data

Use Settings, Erase all Fixwick data inside the app, or delete the app. Both remove everything Fixwick stored.
