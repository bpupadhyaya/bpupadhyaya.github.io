Pillridge Privacy Policy

Effective 8 November 2026. Publisher: Equal Information LLC.

Your study data stays on your device

Pillridge: Drug Class Cards is a study app for pharmacy, nursing and allied-health students. Your review history, streak, text size and settings are saved only on your device. There are no accounts, and we cannot see your study data. All lessons and 3D structures are bundled in the app and work offline.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. With your permission, AdMob and its partners may use your device advertising identifier and approximate location to show ads and measure them. On iPhone and iPad, the system asks first (App Tracking Transparency); in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some US states a consent message also appears. You can decline and keep using every feature. You can change choices in the app under More, then Privacy choices for ads (where shown), and in iOS Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking.

Subscription

The optional ad-free subscription is handled by Apple through your Apple Account. We receive confirmation that a subscription is active; we do not receive your payment details.

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information, build profiles from your study activity, or collect health information. Pillridge is educational and does not ask for medical details.

Children

Pillridge is intended for students aged 13 and over.
