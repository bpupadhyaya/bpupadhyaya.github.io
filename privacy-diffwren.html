AI Assisted Coding: Diffwren: Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Contact: support@equalinformation.com. Last updated 2026-11-27.

1. Introduction and scope

This Privacy Policy describes how EqualInformation, LLC ("we", "us") handles information in connection with the app AI Assisted Coding: Diffwren ("Diffwren", the "app"). It applies to the app on iPhone, iPad and Android devices.

2. Information we collect

A. Information you choose to provide. If you choose to send a diagnostic report, we receive the report and any note you add. If you submit feedback or a suggestion, we receive its category, your message, and any optional role, level, source link and rating you provide; if, and only if, you select the contact option, we also receive the email address you enter so that we can reply.

B. Diagnostic and app-usage information collected with your agreement or as permitted by applicable law. When the usage-information setting is on, the app collects: device and operating-system information (model, system version, processor, memory, display size, language, region and time zone, and accessibility and display settings); the app version; performance and memory indicators; crash and error information; a randomly generated app identifier; and information about how features of the app are used, including how learning features are used and perform: screens and tickets viewed, questions attempted, whether answers are correct, time taken, whether explanations were opened, progress estimates for topics, the adaptive-learning decisions the app makes and their outcomes, activity in labs and builds (which controls were changed and over what ranges), and interactions with the subscription screen. When you accept the first-run notice we also keep a record of that acceptance (the notice version and its time, and your region and language).

C. Information we do not collect. We do not collect your name, contacts, precise location, photographs, files, advertising identifiers, or the text you type into the labs.

3. How we use information

We use information to operate, maintain, secure and support the app; to diagnose problems; to provide and personalise the learning experience; to understand how the app is used; for research and development; for analytics and improvement of our apps and educational content; to read and act on feedback; and to comply with legal obligations.

4. Your choices

At first launch the app shows a notice and a separate switch for usage information. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and where the region cannot be determined, the switch is off until you turn it on; elsewhere it is on by default and clearly visible. You can change it at any time under Settings, Privacy and data. Diagnostic reports and feedback are sent only when you choose to send them. To ask us to delete usage information associated with your installation, use "Delete my usage data" in Settings or email diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

5. Storage and service providers

Information is stored in our Google Workspace services (Google Sheets and Drive) and in notification email, and is processed by service providers acting on our behalf. Where Diffwren was installed from a store, the notifications can carry data: batches of how you worked and used the app, the acceptance record, crash records, diagnostics, feedback and requests to delete. Our mailboxes receive them, and one may forward to another of ours. It is transmitted over encrypted connections. The subscription is processed by the App Store or Google Play; we do not receive your payment details.

6. Retention

Usage and learning information is kept for as long as it is useful for the purposes in section 3. To fulfil a deletion request we remove records manually. They are not deleted automatically, and what was emailed to us is left in place unless manually deleted too.

7. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. International users and legal bases

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we rely on your consent (the first-run notice and the usage switch) and on our legitimate interests in operating and improving the app. Information may be processed in the United States.

9. Requests to remove information

We design our apps to collect only the information described in this policy. If you believe information has been provided to us that you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the first-run notice version changes and the notice is shown again.

11. Contact

EqualInformation, LLC, support@equalinformation.com. Requests about data and deletion: diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
