AI Assisted Coding: Diffwren - Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC.

Last updated: 2026-11-20.

Diffwren teaches how developers use AI coding tools at work, using fictional companies and code. It has no account and no server of ours. Your progress (pages read, stamps, unlock passes, text size) stays on your device, and everything you type or tap in a lab is processed on the device and never sent anywhere.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob at natural breaks and offers optional rewarded videos. Where required you are asked for your choice (consent message; on iPhone and iPad also the tracking permission). With your permission the ad network may use your device advertising identifier to show and measure ads. You can decline and still use every lesson, and change your choice under More, Privacy options, or in your device settings.

Purchases

An optional monthly subscription removes ads and unlocks every premium feature. Payment is handled by your app store; we do not see payment details.

Cited sources

When you open a cited source inside the app, the page loads from its publisher and is subject to that publisher's own policy. The app does not keep cookies from those pages.

Children

The app is for adults (18+).
