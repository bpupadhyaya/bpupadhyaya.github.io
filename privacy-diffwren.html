AI Assisted Coding: Diffwren: Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Contact: support@equalinformation.com. Last updated 2026-11-24.

What stays on your device

Your progress, stamps, quiz answers, learner model and text size are stored only on your device. There is no account and no server of ours. Everything you type or tap in a lab is processed on the device and never sent anywhere. Deleting the app erases it.

What we do not collect

Diffwren collects no personal data, uses no analytics and has no third-party trackers. It does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Subscription

Diffwren Plus is an optional monthly subscription. Apple or Google processes the payment and we never see your payment details. You can cancel at any time in your App Store or Google Play account settings; cancelling takes effect from the next billing period and you keep Plus until the period already paid for ends.

Sources you open

Cited sources open public web pages in a sheet inside the app. Those sites apply their own privacy practices; the app keeps no cookies from them.

Audience

The app is for adults (18 and over).
