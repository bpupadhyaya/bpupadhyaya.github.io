PRISMWELL

Privacy policy, effective 9 November 2026. Publisher: Equal Information LLC.

What stays on your device

Your puzzle stars, daily streak, saved light-lab table, text size and settings are stored only on your device. We have no accounts and we do not collect your name, email or location.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device advertising identifier and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. On iOS the app first asks whether it may track you across other companies' apps and sites; you can say no and still play everything. In the EEA, UK and some US states a consent form is shown first, and you can reopen it from Settings, Ad privacy choices. See Google's policy at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Subscription

The optional ad-free subscription is handled by Apple. We receive no payment details. We only learn, through StoreKit on your device, whether a subscription is active.

Children

Prismwell is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

Change tracking permission in iOS Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking. Erase your saved progress in the app under Settings. Delete the app to remove all local data.
