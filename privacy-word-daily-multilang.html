Lexidial Privacy Policy

Lexidial: Word Puzzle Daily. Effective 13 November 2026. Publisher: Equal Information LLC.

Lexidial is a daily five-letter word puzzle in English, Portuguese and Spanish, played with a brass dial.

What stays on your device

Your language choice, text size, colour mode, streak, statistics and daily results are saved only on your device. There are no accounts and no servers of ours. Daily puzzles are worked out on your device from the calendar date, so the app plays fully offline. The app does not use the camera, microphone, contacts, photos, health data or location.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob: a banner on some screens, an occasional full-screen ad after you finish a puzzle (never during one) and an optional video you can watch for an extra hint. On iPhone and iPad the system first asks whether the app may track you (App Tracking Transparency); in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some US states a Google consent message also appears (Google User Messaging Platform). On Android the Google consent message appears where the law requires it. You can decline and keep using every feature. With your permission, AdMob and its partners may use your device advertising identifier, approximate location derived from your IP address, and ad interaction and diagnostic data to choose, show and measure ads. See Google's advertising policies. You can change your choices later in the app under Settings (privacy choices for ads, where shown) and in your device settings. Ads are not served to people who have an ad-free purchase active.

Purchases

Lexidial Plus (monthly subscription, 7-day free trial where offered) and Remove Ads (one-time purchase) are handled by Apple or Google Play. We never see your payment details.

What is collected

Collected by us: nothing. Collected by Google AdMob in the free version: device identifiers (advertising identifier), approximate location from IP address, and ad interaction and diagnostic data, used for third-party advertising only.

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information, build profiles about you, run our own analytics, or ask you to create an account. No personal data is sent to us.

Children

Lexidial is a general-audience word game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and we do not collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new effective date.
