Privacy Policy: GCSE PE: Sinewren

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Contact: support@equalinformation.com. Last updated 2026-11-26.

1. Introduction and scope

EqualInformation, LLC ("we", "us") publishes GCSE PE: Sinewren (the "app"). This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the app on iOS and Android.

2. Information we collect

A. Information you choose to provide. Diagnostic reports you send from the app and any note you add; feedback and suggestions you submit (category, message, optional role, optional level, optional source link, optional rating) and, only if you tick the contact option, an email address so that we can reply.

B. Diagnostic and app-usage information collected with your agreement or as permitted by applicable law. Device and operating-system information (model, operating-system version, processor, memory, display size, language, region and time zone, accessibility and display settings), app version, performance and memory indicators, crash and error information, a random app identifier, and how features of the app are used. This includes how learning features are used and perform: lessons viewed, questions attempted and whether answers were correct, time taken, hints and explanations opened, topic-level progress estimates, study sessions, lab use, purchase-screen interactions and the choices made by the adaptive features. The answers you type into questions and any words you search for are not included in this information: what you type stays on your device. Text leaves the app only when you choose to send it as feedback, a suggestion, a problem report or a note with a diagnostic report (section A).

C. Information we do not collect. Your name, contacts, precise location, photographs, files, advertising identifiers, or, in usage information, the answers you type, search words or other text you enter such as notes.

3. How we use information

To operate, maintain, secure and support the app; to diagnose problems; to provide and personalise the learning experience; to understand how the app is used; for research and development; for analytics and improvement of our apps and educational content; to read and act on feedback; and to comply with legal obligations.

4. Your choices

The first-run notice explains the optional usage-information switch. You can change it at any time under More, Privacy and data. It is off by default in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and wherever the region cannot be determined, until you turn it on. Diagnostics and feedback are sent only when you choose to send them. To request deletion, use Delete my usage data in the app or email diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

5. Storage and service providers

Information is stored in our Google Workspace services (Google Sheets and Drive) and notification email, and processed by service providers acting on our behalf. It is transmitted over encrypted connections. Purchases are processed by Apple or Google and we do not receive your payment details.

6. Retention

Usage and learning information is kept while it remains useful for the purposes above. Deletion requests are handled on request.

7. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. International users and legal bases

Where applicable we rely on your consent or on our legitimate interests in improving the app. Information may be processed in the United States.

9. Children

We design our apps to collect only the information described in this policy. If you believe information has been provided to us that you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

10. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, the notice shown when the app is first opened changes version and is shown again.

11. Contact

EqualInformation, LLC, support@equalinformation.com. Deletion and data requests: diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
