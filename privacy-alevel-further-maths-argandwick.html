Further Maths Prep: Argandwick: Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Last updated 2026-11-25.

1. Introduction and scope

This policy explains how EqualInformation, LLC (we, us) handles information in connection with Further Maths Prep: Argandwick, version 1.0 and later (the app). It applies to the app only. There is no account: lessons, questions, labs and your progress are kept on your device.

2. Information we collect

A. Information you choose to provide. From Settings, under Privacy and data, Send diagnostics shows the whole report before anything leaves your device, and it is sent only when you tap Send. Feedback and suggestions (category, message, optional role, rating) are sent only when you tap Send; an email address is used only if you tick the option to receive a reply.

B. Diagnostic and app-usage information, collected with your agreement or as permitted by applicable law while the optional usage setting is on: app version and build; device model; operating system and version; processor, memory and display size; language, region and time zone; accessibility and display settings including text size; performance and memory indicators; crash and error information; a random app identifier; and how the app is used. For learning this includes topics, lessons, quizzes, mock papers, labs and builds opened, questions attempted, whether answers were correct, time taken, estimates of your mastery, and the app's adaptive decisions and their outcomes. Answers you type into question fields are kept in shortened form with email addresses, telephone numbers, links and card or identity numbers removed.

When you accept the first-run notice a short acceptance record is always sent: a consent identifier, the time, the app, platform, version and build, the notice version, a fingerprint of its text and the state of the usage setting.

C. We do not collect your name, contacts, precise location, photographs, files, advertising identifiers, or other text you write in the app.

3. How we use information

To operate, maintain, secure and support the app; to diagnose problems; to provide and personalise the learning experience; to understand how the app is used; for research and development; for analytics and improvement of our apps and educational content; to read and act on feedback; and to comply with legal obligations.

4. Your choices

On first opening, a notice explains these practices and offers a separate usage switch. The switch starts off in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and wherever your region is unknown. You can change it at any time under Settings, Privacy and data. You can ask us to delete usage information with Delete my usage data in the same place, or by email to diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

5. Storage and service providers

Information is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections to a web application operated by EqualInformation, LLC, which records it in our Google Workspace services (Google Sheets and Drive) and notifies us by email. Service providers act on our behalf. Purchases are handled by Apple or Google; we do not receive your payment details.

6. Retention

Usage, learning and diagnostic information is kept for as long as it is useful for the purposes above. Deletion requests are handled on request.

7. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures, including encryption in transit. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. International users and legal bases

Where a legal basis is required we rely on your agreement (the usage setting and your decision to send diagnostics or feedback) and on our legitimate interest in running and improving the app. Information may be processed in the United States.

9. Children

We design our apps to collect only the information described in this policy. If you believe information has been provided to us that you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the version of the first-run notice changes and the notice is shown again.

11. Contact

EqualInformation, LLC. Questions: support@equalinformation.com. Deletion and data requests: diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
