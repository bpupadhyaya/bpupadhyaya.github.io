What stays on your device

Your progress, text-size choice and credits are stored only on your device. There is no account, no sign-in and no analytics SDK. We do not receive your answers.

On iOS you are asked for tracking permission; where required you are asked for consent through Google User Messaging Platform. You can change these choices in your device settings or in More. The Ad-Free Monthly subscription removes ads.

Subscriptions

Payments are handled by Apple or Google. We do not see your payment details.

Web pages you open

Source links open public web pages (books, papers, course pages) inside the app. Those sites have their own privacy practices; the app keeps no cookies after you close the sheet.
