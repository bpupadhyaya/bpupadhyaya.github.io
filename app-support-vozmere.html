Support: Spanish Language AP: Vozmere

EqualInformation, LLC

Get help

Email support@equalinformation.com with your device model and the app version shown under More, About. You can also use More, Privacy and data, Send diagnostics, or Feedback.

No sound in listening

The app reads listening texts with your device's Spanish voice. If you hear nothing, check the volume and install a Spanish (Spain or Mexico) voice: on iPhone or iPad under Settings, Accessibility, Spoken Content, Voices; on Android under the text-to-speech settings.

Recording

The microphone is requested only when you tap Record. If you refused it, allow it for the app in your device settings. Recordings stay on your device and can be deleted in the app.

Vozmere Plus

Cancel or change the subscription in your Apple or Google account. To move it to a new device, use Restore purchases on the Plus screen with the same store account.
