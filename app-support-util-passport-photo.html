Portlume: Passport Photo: Support

Portlume turns a portrait into a correctly sized passport, visa or ID photo, or a print sheet, entirely on your phone.

Common questions

  • Are my photos uploaded anywhere? No. All processing is on your phone and the app works offline.
  • Will my photo be accepted? Photo rules differ by country and change over time. Portlume shows each rule with the date it was reviewed and a link to the official page, but we cannot guarantee that any authority will accept a photo. Always read the official requirements before you submit or print.
  • My country is not listed. Create a custom size (millimetres, inches or pixels, head range, eye line, background colour and upload limit) and save it.
  • Which print sizes are supported? 4x6 in, 5x7 in, 10x15 cm, A4 and US Letter sheets as JPEG or PDF, plus a digital upload JPEG at an exact pixel size under a file-size limit.
  • Text too small? Use A- and A+ on every screen, or change the system text size.
  • How do I remove ads? Open the in-app purchase screen (Settings or More, or the ad-free prompt) and choose the ad-free subscription or the one-time purchase. You can also watch an optional short video where offered for temporary extras.
  • How do I restore a purchase? Open Settings (or the purchase screen) and tap Restore purchases, using the same Apple Account or Google account you bought with. This is needed after reinstalling or changing device.
  • How do I cancel the subscription? iPhone and iPad: Settings, your name, Subscriptions. Android: Google Play, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel it.
  • Can I change my ad choices? Yes. Use the privacy choices for ads in the app's Settings where shown, or your device's privacy settings.
