Privacy Policy: GCSE Business Prep: Bizmere

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Contact: support@equalinformation.com. Last updated 2026-11-24.

1. Introduction and scope

EqualInformation, LLC ("we", "us") publishes GCSE Business Prep: Bizmere (the "app"). This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the app on iOS and Android.

2. Information we collect

A. Information you choose to provide. Diagnostic reports you send from the app and any note you add; feedback and suggestions you submit (category, message, optional role, level, optional source link, optional rating) and, only if you tick the contact option, an email address so that we can reply.

B. Diagnostic and app-usage information collected with your agreement or as permitted by applicable law. Device and operating-system information (model, operating-system version, processor, memory, display size, language, region and time zone, accessibility and display settings), app version, performance and memory indicators, crash and error information, a randomly generated app identifier, and how the app is used and how its learning features perform: lessons viewed, questions attempted, whether answers are correct, time taken, explanations opened, progress estimates, the app's adaptive decisions and their outcomes, and activity in labs, simulations, builds and mock papers.

C. Information we do not collect. Your name, contacts, precise location, photographs, files, advertising identifiers, or other text you enter. Answers you write in Write-it practice stay on your device.

3. How we use information

To operate, maintain, secure and support the app; to diagnose problems; to provide and personalise the learning experience; to understand how the app is used; for research and development; for analytics and improvement of our apps and educational content; to read and act on feedback; and to comply with legal obligations.

4. Your choices

When you first open the app it shows a notice and a separate switch for usage information. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and where your region cannot be determined, the switch is off until you turn it on; elsewhere it is on and you can switch it off. You can change it at any time under More, Settings, Privacy and data. Diagnostics and feedback are sent only when you tap Send. To request deletion of usage information, use Delete my usage data in the same place, or write to diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

5. Storage and service providers

Information is stored in our Google Workspace services (Google Sheets and Drive) and notification email, and is processed by service providers acting on our behalf. It is transmitted over encrypted connections. Your learning progress is kept on your device. Purchases are processed by Apple or Google; we do not receive your payment details.

6. Retention

Usage and learning information is kept while it remains useful for the purposes above. Deletion requests are handled on request.

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. International users and legal bases

Where applicable, we rely on your consent or on our legitimate interests in improving the app. Information may be processed in the United States.

9. Children

We design our apps to collect only the information described in this policy. If you believe information has been provided to us that you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

10. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, the notice shown when you first open the app changes version and is shown again.

11. Contact

EqualInformation, LLC, support@equalinformation.com. Deletion and data requests: diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
