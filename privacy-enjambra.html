Privacy Policy

English Lit AP: Enjambra · EqualInformation, LLC · Effective 26 November 2026

1. Scope

This policy explains how EqualInformation, LLC handles information in the app English Lit AP: Enjambra (“Enjambra”) on Apple and Google devices. Enjambra has no user accounts.

2. Information you choose to give us

If you tap Send, we receive the diagnostic report shown to you in the preview, together with any note you add. If you submit feedback or a suggestion, we receive its category and message and any optional details you include, such as your role, level, a source link or a rating. An email address is collected only if you tick the option to be contacted about that submission.

3. Diagnostic and app-usage information

When the usage setting is on, or as otherwise permitted by applicable law, the app collects diagnostic and app-usage information. This includes device and operating system details (such as model, system version, processor, memory, screen size, language, region, time zone, text size and accessibility display settings), app version, performance indicators, crash and error information, a random identifier created for this installation, and how features are used. For learning features it includes the lessons, passage sets, quizzes, mock papers, labs and builds you open, the questions you attempt, whether answers are correct, time taken, estimates of progress on course points, study sessions, the recommendations the app makes and what follows them. The app has no field that sends your essays, drafts, planning notes or build writing; these stay on your device.

When you first accept the in-app notice, a record of that acceptance is sent (a consent identifier, date and time, app and notice version, the setting you chose, the installation identifier, platform, country or region code, locale and time zone) even if the usage setting is off.

4. What we do not collect

The app does not collect your name, contacts, precise location, photographs, files or advertising identifiers.

5. How we use information

We use it to operate, secure, maintain and support the app; to find and fix problems; to provide and personalise the learning experience; to understand how features are used and how well they work; for research and development and to improve our apps and educational content; to read and act on feedback; and to meet legal obligations.

6. Your choices

The first screen of the app presents a notice and a usage-information switch. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, and where your region cannot be determined, the switch is off until you turn it on. You can change it at any time under More, Settings, Privacy and data. Diagnostics and feedback are sent only when you choose to send them. You can ask for deletion with “Delete my usage data” in the app or by writing to diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

7. Storage, service providers and purchases

Information is sent over encrypted connections and stored in our Google Workspace services (including Google Sheets, Drive and email), which act as service providers to us. Subscriptions are processed by Apple or Google under their own terms; we do not receive your payment card details. Source links open third-party websites, which have their own privacy practices.

8. Retention and security

We keep usage, learning, diagnostic and feedback information for as long as it is useful for the purposes above, unless a longer or shorter period is required by law, and we act on valid deletion requests. We use reasonable safeguards, although no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

9. International users and legal bases

Information may be processed in the United States. Where a legal basis is required, we rely on your consent or on our legitimate interests in operating and improving the app, as applicable. You may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your information; contact us to use them.

10. Children

We design our apps to collect only the information described in this policy. If you believe information has been provided to us that you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

11. Changes and contact

If this policy changes materially, the in-app notice version changes and the notice is shown again. EqualInformation, LLC, support@equalinformation.com. Deletion and data requests: diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
