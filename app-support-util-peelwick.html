Background Remover: Peelwick: Support

Peelwick is a background remover and sticker maker app for iPhone, iPad and Android. Peelwick removes the background from a photo, lets you refine it with brushes, adds a backdrop and makes sticker packs, entirely on your phone. No account is needed.

Common questions

  • Are my photos uploaded anywhere? No. The cut-out model runs on your phone and the app works offline.
  • The cut-out missed a part. What now? Use the Erase and Restore brushes (with undo, redo and zoom) and the edge softness slider to correct it.
  • Which files can I export? PNG or JPG at Standard (1080) or HD (2048) size, and sticker packs of 512 x 512 WebP stickers.
  • How do I make a sticker pack? Finish a cut-out, choose Add to pack, then share the pack to a messaging app that accepts sticker packs.
  • What are credits? You start with 2 credits. A cut-out uses 1 credit the first time you export, share or add it to a pack. Watch a short ad to earn another, or go ad-free.
  • How do I remove ads? Open the purchase screen in Peelwick (Settings or More, or the ad-free prompt) and choose the ad-free monthly subscription or the one-time Unlock. Both remove ads and credit limits.
  • How do I restore a purchase? Open Settings (or the purchase screen) and tap Restore purchases, using the same Apple Account or Google account you bought with. This is needed after reinstalling or changing device.
  • How do I cancel the subscription? iPhone and iPad: Settings, your name, Subscriptions. Android: Google Play, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel it.
  • Text too small? Use A- and A+ on the screens, or change the system text size.
  • Can I change my ad choices? Yes. Use the privacy choices for ads in the app where shown, or your device's privacy settings.
