App Support — RepairMate

Get help with RepairMate — your DIY home repair companion.

  • Home
  • /
  • App Support — RepairMate

RepairMate is a DIY home repair companion available on iOS and Android. It provides 20 step-by-step repair guides across 7 categories, 8 emergency procedures, a cost estimator, DIY advisor, tool tracker, shopping list generator, seasonal maintenance calendar, and more. All guides work offline — perfect for garages and basements with no signal. No accounts, no data collection.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data private?

Yes. RepairMate stores all data locally on your device. There are no accounts, no cloud sync, and no analytics. We never collect any personal information. See our Privacy Policy for details.

What features are free?

The free tier includes: all 20 bundled repair guides (plumbing, walls, doors, electrical, kitchen, bathroom, general), 8 emergency quick reference procedures (gas leak, burst pipe, etc.), full-text search with difficulty filter, category and room browsers, before-you-start safety checklist, cost estimator (DIY vs Pro comparison), DIY advisor, tool box tracker, shopping list generator, repair history log, stats dashboard, home profile, seasonal maintenance (23 tasks), quick tips (25 tips across 7 categories), repair glossary (27 terms), and skill assessment quiz.

What does Pro include?

Pro ($3.99 one-time purchase) unlocks advanced features including an expanded guide library, enhanced tools checklist, and additional reference content as it becomes available.

Does the app require internet?

No. RepairMate is 100% offline. All 20 repair guides, emergency procedures, and reference content are bundled as JSON with the app. This is by design — you can use it in your garage, basement, or anywhere without signal.

When should I NOT attempt a DIY repair?

RepairMate clearly flags when to call a professional: electrical work involving your panel, gas line repairs, structural modifications, or any repair where safety is at risk. The DIY Advisor feature provides recommendations on whether to attempt a repair yourself or hire a pro based on the task difficulty and your skill level.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

iOS: Open RepairMate → Settings → Restore Purchases. Your purchase is linked to your Apple ID.
Android: Open RepairMate → Settings → Restore Purchases. Your purchase is linked to your Google account.

The app is crashing or not loading. What should I do?

Try these steps:
1. Force-close and reopen the app.
2. Restart your device.
3. Make sure you are running the latest version from the App Store or Google Play.
4. If the issue persists, contact us (see below) with your device model, OS version, and a description of the problem.
