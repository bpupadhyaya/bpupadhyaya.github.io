AI Ethics: Candorline - Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC.

Effective 2026-11-17. Candorline works offline with no account. Your progress, text size and credits are stored on your device only.

Advertising

The free version shows ads at natural breaks using Google AdMob. Where required, a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) and the Apple tracking prompt ask for your choice. Google may collect device identifiers and approximate usage data for ads per its policy. A subscription removes ads.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple or Google; we receive no payment details.

Cited sources

When you open a cited source, the page loads from its own website in a window without cookies being kept after you close it; that website may see your request.

Children

The app is for learners aged 13 and over; non-personalised ads are used where the age setting requires.
