AI Ethics: Candorline: Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Contact: support@equalinformation.com. Last updated 2026-11-20.

What stays on your device

Candorline works offline with no account and no server of ours. Your progress, text size and 24-hour unlock passes are stored only on your device. Deleting the app erases them.

Your age answer

On first launch the app asks whether you are 18 or older; the answer stays on your device. Adults on iPhone see the Apple tracking prompt and, where required, Google's consent form. If you answer under 18, there is no tracking prompt, ads are non-personalised and limited to a teen content rating.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob at natural breaks, never during a lab or a question. With your permission AdMob may use your advertising identifier and device data to show and measure ads under Google's own policy. An optional monthly subscription removes ads and opens every premium lab and the mock test; without it, they open for 24 hours after an optional rewarded video you choose to watch.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple or Google; we receive no payment details.

Cited sources

When you open a cited source, the page loads from its own website inside the app without keeping cookies after you close it; that website may see your request and applies its own policy.
