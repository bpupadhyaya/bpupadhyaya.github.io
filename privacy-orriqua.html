1 The short version

Math Grade 6: Orriqua, from EqualInformation, LLC, is a Grade 6 math practice app for learners and the adults who help them. It keeps what it needs on the device and sends nothing by itself: there are no automatic usage figures or crash reports. We receive information only when an adult chooses to email us.

2 What the app writes on the device

Progress by skill, recent answers and how long they took, design choices in Real-World jobs, text size and other settings, and a four-digit code that an adult chooses for the grown-ups area.

This stays in the app's own storage. It is left out of device cloud backup.

3 What you choose to email us

Behind the grown-ups check an adult can start an email to us: "Send feedback" (to support@equalinformation.com) or "Send diagnostics" (to diagnostics@equalinformation.com). The app only prepares a draft in the adult's own mail app; nothing is sent unless the adult presses Send.

We then receive the email address it comes from, whatever is written, and for diagnostics the details shown on screen first: app version, device model and system version, language, region and time zone, screen and text size, whether a plan is active, how many skills and builds are done, and recent program error messages. It holds no names, answers or codes added by the app.

We use these emails only to answer and to fix problems, and do not use them for advertising or tracking. Ask us at privacy@equalinformation.com and we will delete them.

4 Network and tracking

The app has no sign-in, analytics, device identifier collection or social features. Behind the grown-ups check an adult can open this policy, the terms and the support page in the device browser, and can see the pages the app cites (cde.ca.gov, gov.uk, who.int, nhs.uk, healthychildren.org) in a window inside the app. Showing a cited page fetches it from its publisher, whose website receives the request, including the device's internet address and browser details, under its own privacy policy; a web page is shown with page scripts off and images and other parts from other sites held back, a PDF file is held in memory only and opened at the cited page; the window keeps no cookies or history and does not follow links. Our legal pages open in the browser, and the app itself downloads nothing. Inside the app each cited source is a summary stored with the app.

5 Buying a monthly plan

An adult may buy either of two monthly plans. Apple takes the payment and holds the account and payment details under its own privacy policy. The app is told only which plan is active and the price to show. We do not receive card details. Apple gives developers sales reports that do not come from inside the app.

6 Permissions

No camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos. Read aloud uses a voice already on the device.

7 Children

The app is intended for use by children with the knowledge of a parent, guardian or teacher. It asks a child for no personal details. Purchases and links out of the app sit behind a grown-up check.

8 Removing information

An adult can erase all progress in the grown-ups area. Deleting the app removes its stored data. We hold none of that stored data. Emails you chose to send us are kept only as long as needed to answer and to fix problems; ask and we will delete them.

9 Changes and contact

If the app starts handling information differently, this page will say so before that version is offered. Questions about privacy: privacy@equalinformation.com.
