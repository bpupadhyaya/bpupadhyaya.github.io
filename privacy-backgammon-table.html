Backgammon Table - Privacy Policy

Effective 7 November 2026

Backgammon Table is a backgammon app published by EqualInformation. It has no accounts and never asks for your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.

What stays on your device

Match history, your play ratings, study stars, daily streak, settings and the ad-free time you earn are kept in the app's own storage on your device. The opponent is a neural network that runs on the device, so your games are never sent to us.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may use device identifiers (including the advertising identifier if you allow tracking), an approximate location derived from your IP address, and ad interaction and diagnostic data, to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. On first launch iOS asks whether the app may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you decline, you still get the whole game and see ads that are not personalised. Where the law requires it, Google's User Messaging Platform shows a consent form, and you can reopen it at any time from Settings. Google explains its use of data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Ad-free options and purchases

You can earn ad-free time by watching rewarded videos, or subscribe to the monthly ad-free pass. A one-time ad-free purchase made in earlier versions is still honoured. Payments are handled entirely by Apple, and we never see your card or Apple ID details; the app only learns on your device whether an entitlement is active.

Children

Backgammon Table is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

  • Tracking: iOS Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking.
  • Ad consent: Settings inside the app, Privacy choices for ads.
  • Subscription: iOS Settings, your name, Subscriptions.
  • Erase local data: Settings inside the app, Erase my progress, or delete the app.
