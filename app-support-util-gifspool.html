Video to GIF Maker: Gifspool: Support

Gifspool is a video to GIF maker app for iPhone, iPad and Android. Gifspool turns a video or a set of photos into a GIF (or an MP4) on your phone: trim, speed, crop, captions, stickers and a target file size. No account is needed.

Common questions

  • Are my videos uploaded anywhere? No. All processing is on your phone and the app works offline.
  • How do I make a GIF fit a size limit? In the Size tab pick a target (for example 1 MB, 8 MB or 25 MB). Gifspool lowers frame rate, width and colours step by step and tells you if the target cannot be reached.
  • How long can the video be? Up to 30 seconds of video, or 2 to 60 photos. Long clips are sampled to keep GIFs small.
  • Which formats can I export? GIF and MP4 (no audio) on iPhone and iPad; GIF, animated WebP and MP4 on Android.
  • What are credits? You start with 2 credits. Each finished export uses 1 credit. Watch a short ad to earn another, or go ad-free.
  • How do I remove ads? Open the purchase screen in Gifspool (Settings or More, or the ad-free prompt) and choose the ad-free monthly subscription or the one-time Unlock. Both remove ads and credit limits.
  • How do I restore a purchase? Open Settings (or the purchase screen) and tap Restore purchases, using the same Apple Account or Google account you bought with. This is needed after reinstalling or changing device.
  • How do I cancel the subscription? iPhone and iPad: Settings, your name, Subscriptions. Android: Google Play, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel it.
  • Text too small? Use A- and A+ on the screens, or change the system text size.
  • Can I change my ad choices? Yes. Use the privacy choices for ads in the app where shown, or your device's privacy settings.
