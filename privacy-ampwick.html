Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Contact: support@equalinformation.com. Last updated 2026-11-22.

Short version

Joulz is a study app for adults. It has no accounts and no servers of ours. Your study data stays on your phone.

Data kept on your device

Answers, review schedule, flags, mock results, credits, streak, text size and your confirmation that you are 18 or older are saved only on your device. Deleting the app or choosing Reset in the app erases them. We never receive your name, email address or contact details.

Adults only

The app is for people aged 18 and over. At first launch you confirm your age; if you say you are under 18 the app does not open its content and shows no ads.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob between study sessions, and optional rewarded videos that earn credits. No ads appear during a timed mock. AdMob may use your device advertising identifier, approximate location and usage data to choose and measure ads, under Google's own privacy policy. On iPhone and iPad the app asks permission before tracking across other companies' apps. Where the law requires, a Google consent form asks about personalised ads first. You can change the choice later in the app's More tab (Privacy choices) and in your device settings.

Subscription

The optional monthly ad-free subscription is handled entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We do not see your payment details. Manage or cancel it in your store account settings.

Sources and links

Cited sources open in an in-app web sheet. Opening one contacts that site, which may collect data under its own policy. Nothing else in the app uses the network except ads and the store.

Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Your rights and contact

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete. For questions write to support@equalinformation.com.
