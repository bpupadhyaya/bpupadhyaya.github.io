Quoinstake: privacy policy

For the app "Math Grade 7: Quoinstake" on phones and tablets, from EqualInformation. Effective 27 November 2026.

In short: the app sends us nothing about the learner. What it learns about your child stays on your device.

What the app keeps on your device

Answers and which were right, the app's estimate of progress in each topic, expedition maps, test results, pinned items, settings, and a short list of any program errors. This is used to run the course and to choose what to show next. It is not sent to us.

Your phone or tablet may include app data in its own backup, under the settings of your device account.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting software, and no advertising identifier.
  • No microphone, camera, location, contacts or photos.
  • No chat, sharing between users or public content.

When something does leave your device

  • Email you choose to send. This is the only route by which anything reaches us, and it is optional. Behind the adult check, a grown-up can open a diagnostics report or an empty feedback draft in their own mail app. The report lists the app version, the app shell, the browser engine description (which includes the system version and may include the device model), window size, text size, whether 3D drawing works, and recent program error lines. It holds no names, answers or progress. Nothing is sent unless you press send. If you send it, we receive your email address and what you wrote. We use it only to answer and to fix problems, and delete it on request.
  • Purchases. The app store you bought from runs the checkout and keeps your payment details under its own privacy policy. All the app hears back is which plans are switched on.
  • Outside web pages. A grown-up can open a cited source, this policy or the terms in a window inside the app. That website receives the usual connection information, such as your IP address.

App stores may give us their own summary figures and crash reports about apps, according to the choices in your device settings.

Children

The course is written for learners of about 12 to 13. We designed it so that it does not collect personal information from them. Purchases, outside links and email drafts are placed behind an adult check, which is not a way of verifying age or consent. Please do not put a child's name or other details in emails to us.

Deleting data

Use Grown-ups > Settings > "Erase all progress on this device", or delete the app. For an email you sent us, write to the address below and ask us to delete it.

Changes and contact

If a later version collects anything, this page and the store privacy information will say so before it does. Questions: diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
