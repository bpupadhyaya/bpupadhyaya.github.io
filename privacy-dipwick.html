Dipwick: Privacy Policy

Last updated November 6, 2026. Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC.

What stays on your device

Dipwick has no account and no server of its own. The vehicles, logbook entries, costs, odometer readings, service plan and receipt photos you add are stored only on your iPhone or iPad. The text you type or dictate into the logbook, and the text found on a receipt photo, is read by software on the device. None of it is uploaded to us.

Camera and photos

The camera and photo library are used only when you tap to add a receipt. Receipt photos are saved in the app's own storage on the device. You can delete an entry, or a vehicle, and its photos are removed with it.

Reminders

If you turn on service reminders, Dipwick schedules local notifications on the device. No notification content is sent anywhere.

Ads (free version)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Where the law requires it, a Google consent form is shown first, and on iOS Dipwick asks for App Tracking Transparency permission before ads load. If you allow it, Google and its ad partners may use your advertising identifier, device and ad-interaction information, and approximate location to choose and measure ads. If you decline, ads are not personalised. This data is handled by Google under its own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). You can use Dipwick without ads by subscribing, or by watching rewarded videos for ad-free time.

Subscription

The optional monthly ad-free subscription is processed by Apple. We receive no payment details. Apple tells the app only whether a subscription is active.

Exports

CSV and PDF exports are created on the device and go only where you choose to share them.

Children

Dipwick is a general audience app for vehicle owners and is not directed to children.

Your choices

You can change tracking permission in iOS Settings, reopen the privacy choices form from Dipwick's Settings screen where it is offered, and delete all data by deleting the app.

Contact

Questions: support@equalinformation.com. Support page: app-support-dipwick.html.

Service intervals shown in Dipwick are typical ranges, not manufacturer instructions. Always check your owner's manual.
