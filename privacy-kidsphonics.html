Phonics for Kids: Soundwick Privacy Policy

Effective 14 November 2026. Publisher: Equal Information LLC.

Short version

Soundwick is a reading-practice app for children aged 4 to 7. It has no accounts, no sign-up, and it collects no personal information from children. Your child's progress stays on your device.

What the app stores on the device

The learner's nickname (you choose it, a nickname is fine), a chosen cartoon avatar, which sounds and words have been practised, results, play days, credits and settings. This is saved only in the app's private storage on your device. We cannot see it. You can export it to a file you choose, import it again, reset it, or delete it by removing the app.

What children see

The screens children use contain no ads, no purchase buttons and no links. Settings, credits, purchases, links and the progress file are behind a grown-up gate (a spelled-out arithmetic question).

Ads in the grown-ups area

A grown-up can choose to watch a short rewarded ad to earn a play credit. These ads come from Google AdMob and are requested with child-directed treatment turned on, the under-age-of-consent tag, a maximum content rating of G (all ages) and non-personalised settings. The app does not track you, does not ask for tracking permission, and does not use the advertising identifier for personalised ads. Google may process technical information such as IP address and device details to deliver and measure an ad, as described in Google's own privacy policy. Ads are shown only in the grown-ups area.

Purchases

The optional ad-free monthly subscription is handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We receive no payment details and no name or email address.

Third parties

Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google User Messaging Platform (ads and consent), Apple StoreKit or Google Play Billing (subscription). Nothing else. No analytics, no crash-reporting service, no social features.

Children's privacy

The app is designed for use by children together with a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the app has no feature that asks a child for a name, email address, photo, voice recording, location or contact details. The only text a child may enter is an optional nickname, which stays on the device. If you believe a child has given us personal information, or you have a question about this notice, email support@equalinformation.com and we will help. Parents can review or delete everything the app holds by using Export or Reset in the grown-ups area, or by deleting the app.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the effective date.
