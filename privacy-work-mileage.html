Waywick privacy policy

App: Waywick: Mileage Log. Last updated 9 November 2026.

Waywick keeps your mileage log on your own device. We run no account system and no servers for your trips, so we cannot see them.

What stays on your device

Trips, vehicles, reasons, odometer readings, notes, settings and your ad-free progress are stored locally in the app. Exports (CSV and PDF) are created on your device and shared only when you choose where to send them.

Location

If you use Drive to record a trip, Waywick asks for location access While Using the App. The location is used only to add up the distance of that trip. Waywick does not track you in the background and does not upload your location. You can decline and add trips by hand.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Before ads start, the app asks for tracking permission (Apple App Tracking Transparency) and, where required, shows Google's consent form. Google and its partners may collect device identifiers, coarse location, ad interaction and similar data to show and measure ads, according to your choices. You can say no to tracking and still use every feature. See how Google uses information from partner apps. You can remove ads with the monthly ad-free subscription or by earning ad-free time in the app.

Subscription

The optional ad-free subscription is handled by Apple. We receive no payment details. Manage or cancel it in your Apple ID settings.

Children

Waywick is a work and tax tool for adults and is not directed to children.

Your choices

Delete a trip in the app, or delete the app to remove all its data. Change the tracking choice any time in iOS Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking.

Not tax advice

Waywick shows estimates based on published mileage rates. It is not tax, legal or financial advice.

Contact

Questions or a rate error to report: support@equalinformation.com. Terms of Use: Apple Standard EULA.
