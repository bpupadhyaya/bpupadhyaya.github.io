Help

Questions, content corrections and subscription help: support@equalinformation.com. Please include your device model, the app version and the screen or question number.

Send diagnostics

Open Settings, Privacy and data and choose Send diagnostics. You see exactly what the report contains and nothing is sent until you tap Send. Reports arrive at diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

Feedback and suggestions

Use Feedback and suggestions in Settings, the thumbs and Report a problem controls under answers and lessons, or Suggest content. Nothing is sent until you tap Send.

Delete my usage data

Open Settings, Privacy and data and choose Delete my usage data, or email diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

Subscription

Manage or cancel Reagmere Plus in your App Store or Google Play account settings. If a purchase does not appear, use Restore purchases on the Reagmere Plus screen.
