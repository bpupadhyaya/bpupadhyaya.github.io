How the gears work

Gears mesh when their pitch circles touch. A driven gear turns the opposite way to the gear driving it, and its speed is the driver's speed times driver teeth divided by driven teeth. Idler gears change direction but not speed. Two gears on one shaft turn together at the same speed, which is how compound trains gain big ratios.
