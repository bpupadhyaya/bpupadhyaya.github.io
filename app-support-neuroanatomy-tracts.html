Neuroanatomy: Axonlume Support

EqualInformation, LLC

Axonlume is an offline 3D neuroanatomy lesion clinic: place a lesion in a brain, brainstem and spinal cord model and see which pathways fail, or localise a lesion from findings. It works offline and is for education only.

Report an error

Found a wrong value, label or shape? Email support@equalinformation.com. Every claim in the app lists its sources under the Sources page.

Ad-free, trial and restore

Open More (or the Ad-free button), then choose the monthly Axonlume Ad-free subscription ($2.99 per month, 7-day free trial for new subscribers, renews until cancelled in your Apple Account settings) or the one-time Remove Ads purchase. Use Restore purchases on a new device. You can also earn ad-free time by watching an optional video.

Text size

Use A- and A+ in the app. Axonlume also follows your system text size.
