Common questions

A diagram looks wrong for my tuning. Open More, then Instrument, and check the tuning. Shapes are calculated from the open-string notes you choose.

My songs disappeared. Songs live only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them; use the share button in a song to keep a copy.

How do I remove ads? More, then Go ad-free: subscribe monthly, or earn ad-free time for the month by watching rewarded videos. Restore purchases is on the same sheet.

Cancel the subscription. Settings, your Apple ID, Subscriptions, Fretnook.

Listen check says the wrong chord. It is a beta that works best on plain major, minor and suspended chords in a quiet room.
