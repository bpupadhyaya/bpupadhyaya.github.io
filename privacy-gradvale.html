Optimization for ML: Gradvale - Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Last updated: 2026-11-20.

What we collect

We run no server and no accounts for this app. Your progress, credits and text-size setting stay on your device. We do not ask for your birth date; the app asks once whether you are 18 or older and stores only that yes/no answer on the device.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob at natural breaks. For adults, with your consent (Google consent form and, on iPhone, the tracking prompt), advertising partners may use your device advertising identifier to personalise ads; you can decline and still use every lesson. If you say you are under 18, only non-personalised ads with a teen content rating are requested. Subscribers see no ads.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple or Google. We receive no payment details.

Sources

Tapping a source opens the cited web page inside the app. That page's own site may set cookies for the session; the app does not keep them after you close it.

Children

This is a university-level learning tool and is not directed to children under 13.

Questions: support@equalinformation.com.
