Dayspire support

Dayspire counts down to the days that matter, with scenes that change by event type, Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, and a Live Activity for the day itself.

How do I add a countdown quickly?
Tap the plus button and type it as you would say it, for example "Lisbon trip June 14" or "retire in 18 months". Dayspire reads the date and picks a scene. You can change every field afterwards.
How do I add a widget?
Touch and hold an empty spot on the Home Screen (or the Lock Screen), tap Edit or the plus button, search for Dayspire, and pick a size. Touch and hold the widget and choose Edit Widget to select which countdown it shows. If a widget says to add a countdown, open Dayspire once so it can share your list with the widget.
The Lock Screen tracker did not start.
It starts for events that are today or tomorrow when you open the app, or when you tap "Day-of tracker" on the event. Check that Live Activities are allowed for Dayspire in iOS Settings.
How do I import from my calendar?
Tap the calendar button at the top, allow access, then tick the events you want to count down to.
How do I remove ads?
Open Settings (the sliders button). Choose the monthly ad-free subscription, or watch a short video to earn 30 minutes without ads. "Restore purchase" brings back a subscription on a new device. You can cancel any time in your Apple ID subscriptions.
Where is my data?
On your device only. See the privacy policy.
