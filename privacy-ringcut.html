Ringtone Maker: Snipwren: Privacy Policy

Snipwren: ringtone maker and audio cutter. Effective 14 November 2026. Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC.

Snipwren cuts and trims audio, makes ringtones, notification and alarm tones, and converts audio, entirely on your phone.

What stays on your device

Your audio and video files never leave your device. Snipwren decodes, trims, fades, joins and exports locally with the phone's own audio tools; there is no server and no account. A finished file leaves your device only when you tap Share or Save, or set it as a tone, and we never receive it. Your edits, credits and settings are stored on your device.

Permissions

You choose the files to open with the system file picker (and, to take the sound from a video, the system photo picker). On Android, saving a tone writes it to the device's Ringtones, Notifications or Alarms folder, and setting it as the default tone uses the system "modify system settings" permission, which you grant on a system page and can withdraw at any time. On iPhone and iPad, iOS does not let apps set ringtones, so the app saves or shares the file and explains how to install it. The app does not use the microphone, camera, contacts or location.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob (a banner on the home screen and a short full-screen ad after an export). Your audio is never used for ads or sent to AdMob. An optional short video can be watched to earn one more credit. On iPhone and iPad the system first asks whether the app may track you (App Tracking Transparency); in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some US states a Google consent message also appears (Google User Messaging Platform). On Android the Google consent message appears where the law requires it. You can decline and keep using every feature. With your permission, AdMob and its partners may use your device advertising identifier, approximate location derived from your IP address, and ad interaction and diagnostic data to choose, show and measure ads. See Google's advertising policies. You can change your choices later in the app (privacy choices for ads, where shown) and in your device settings. Ads are not served to people who have an ad-free purchase active.

Purchases

The optional ad-free monthly subscription and the one-time Unlock are handled by Apple or Google Play. We never see your payment details. Credits, purchase status and your settings are stored on your device.

What is collected

Collected by us: nothing, and your audio files are never collected. Collected by Google AdMob in the free version: device identifiers (advertising identifier), approximate location from IP address, and ad interaction and diagnostic data, used for third-party advertising and measuring ads.

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information, build profiles about you, run our own analytics, or ask you to create an account. No personal data is sent to us.

Children

Snipwren is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Nothing is sent to us in any case.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new effective date.
