IB Global Politics: Sovrelle: Support

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Contact: support@equalinformation.com. Last updated 2026-11-25.

Help

IB Global Politics: Sovrelle works offline. If something does not work, open Settings, Privacy and data and choose Send diagnostics, which shows you the report before anything is sent, or write to support@equalinformation.com.

Subscription

Sovrelle Plus is an optional monthly subscription. Manage or cancel it in your store account settings; use Restore on the subscription screen if you reinstall the app or change device.

Your data

To request deletion of usage information, use Delete my usage data in Settings, Privacy and data, or write to diagnostics@equalinformation.com. See the Privacy Policy for detail.

Feedback

Settings includes a feedback and suggestions form. We read what arrives; not every suggestion can be adopted.
