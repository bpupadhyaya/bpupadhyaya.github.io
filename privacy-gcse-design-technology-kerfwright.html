Privacy Policy

GCSE Design Tech: Kerfwright, published by EqualInformation, LLC. Effective 26 November 2026.

1. What this policy covers

This policy explains what information the GCSE Design Tech: Kerfwright app (the app) handles, why, and the choices open to you. The app works without an account.

2. Information that stays on your device

Your study progress, plan, answers you type into question boxes, written practice answers, design notes and search words are kept on your device. They are not included in usage information. You can erase study progress in the app's settings, and removing the app removes this stored data.

3. Information you choose to send

If you use Send diagnostics, the app sends a report about the app and device after showing you a preview, together with any note you write. If you use feedback or suggestions, the app sends the category, your message, any optional role, course or source link and rating you add, and, only if you tick the contact option, your email address for a reply. Please leave other personal details out of anything you write.

4. Usage and diagnostic information

When the optional usage switch is on, as permitted by applicable law, the app collects diagnostic and app-usage information: device and operating system details (model, version, processor, memory, screen size, language, region and time zone, accessibility and display settings), app version, performance, crash and error information, a randomly generated app identifier, and how learning features are used. The last of these includes lessons opened, questions attempted and whether they were correct, which option position was chosen, time taken, topic-level progress estimates, next-step decisions made by the app and their results, activity in benches, builds and practice papers, study sessions and interactions with the subscription screen. It is linked to the random identifier, not to your name or contact details.

When you accept the first-run notice, the app records that acceptance (the notice version, time, a random reference, app and device platform, language region, country code and time zone, and the position of the switch) even if the switch is off.

5. Information the app does not collect

The app does not ask for or collect your name, contacts, precise location, photographs, files or an advertising identifier. It has no account or sign-in. Payments are handled by Apple or Google; the app learns only whether a subscription is active and its price.

6. How information is used

To run, secure and support the app; to find and fix faults; to provide and personalise learning; to understand how features are used; to research, develop and improve our apps and educational content; to read and act on feedback; and to meet legal obligations.

7. Your choices and deletion

A notice with a separate usage switch appears when you first open the app. The switch is off until you turn it on in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and where the region is unknown. You can change it at any time under More, Settings, Privacy and data. There you can also send a request to delete your usage data, or you can email diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

8. Storage and service providers

Information we receive is stored in our Google Workspace services (Google Sheets, Drive and email) and handled by providers acting on our behalf. It is sent over encrypted connections. It may be processed in the United States or other countries where those providers operate.

9. Retention and security

Usage and learning information is kept while it remains useful for the purposes above. Deletion requests are acted on when received. We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of sending or storing it is completely secure.

10. Legal bases

Where the law requires a basis, we rely on your consent for usage information and on our legitimate interests in running, securing and improving the app, as applicable.

11. Children

We design our apps to collect only the information described in this policy. If you believe information has been provided to us that you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

12. Changes and contact

If we make a material change, the first-run notice receives a new version and is shown again. Contact: EqualInformation, LLC, support@equalinformation.com. Deletion and data requests: diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

This app is independent. It is not affiliated with, approved by or endorsed by the Department for Education, Ofqual, AQA, Pearson, OCR or WJEC Eduqas. Names of boards and qualifications are used only to say which published course the material relates to.
