Jorrery support

Math Grade 11: Jorrery, published by EqualInformation, LLC. Last updated 27 November 2026.

Get help

Write to support@equalinformation.com from your own email app. Say what you were doing and what happened. In the app, the grown-up area under "Settings and privacy" shows device facts you can copy into the message if you wish.

Restore or manage a subscription

In the app open Grown-ups, then Subscriptions, then "Restore purchases" or "Manage subscription". Use the same store account that made the purchase.

Erase your study record

Open Grown-ups, then Settings and privacy, then "Erase progress".
