Support PlainDental

Help us make dental health education accessible to everyone.

Dental health explained in plain language — so everyone can understand their teeth, conditions, and treatment options.

PlainDental is a dental health education app that explains conditions, procedures, emergencies, and oral hygiene in plain, jargon-free language. It is built and maintained by a single developer who believes that understanding your dental health should not require a medical degree.

Every feature is designed with privacy at its core — all content is bundled offline with no data collection, no accounts, and no tracking. PlainDental is available on iOS and Android. Core features are free, with optional premium content available through a one-time in-app purchase. Your support helps us continue improving the app and building more educational tools.

Why PlainDental Exists

Dental anxiety affects 36% of adults. Much of that fear comes from not understanding what is happening in your mouth, what a diagnosis means, or what a procedure involves. When people understand their dental health in plain language, fear decreases and better decisions follow.

Dental visits can cost $150 to $3,000 or more. People deserve to understand what they are paying for, what their options are, and what questions to ask before sitting in the chair. PlainDental provides that understanding — for free.

Oral health is directly linked to heart disease, diabetes, and pregnancy complications. By helping people understand and care for their teeth, we contribute to overall health outcomes far beyond the mouth.

PlainDental is not a substitute for professional dental advice. It is an educational companion that helps you have more informed conversations with your dentist and make better decisions about your oral health.

Disclaimer: PlainDental is for educational purposes only. The information provided is not intended to replace professional dental advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always seek the advice of a qualified dental professional with any questions regarding a dental condition.
