RTO LL Test India: Roadlark: Privacy Policy

Roadlark: India learner's licence test practice. Effective 14 November 2026. Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC.

Roadlark helps you practise for the learner's licence (LL) test in India with road signs, traffic rules and timed mock tests for cars and two-wheelers, in English and Hindi.

What stays on your device

Roadlark works offline and has no account. Your answers, mistakes, progress, credits and settings are stored on your device only and are never sent to us. You can remove them by clearing the app's data or deleting the app.

Permissions

Roadlark does not use the camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location, and asks for no storage permission.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob (a banner on the Home, Practice, Signs and Progress screens and a short full-screen ad after a finished practice, mock test or flashcard session; no ads during a quiz or mock test). Ads and the consent message start after you accept the first-launch notice and are never shown in a quiz or mock test. An optional short video can be watched to earn one more mock test. On iPhone and iPad the system first asks whether the app may track you (App Tracking Transparency); in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some US states a Google consent message also appears (Google User Messaging Platform). On Android the Google consent message appears where the law requires it. You can decline and keep using every feature. With your permission, AdMob and its partners may use your device advertising identifier, approximate location derived from your IP address, and ad interaction and diagnostic data to choose, show and measure ads. See Google's advertising policies. You can change your choices later in the app (privacy choices for ads, where shown) and in your device settings. Ads are not served to people who have an ad-free purchase active.

Purchases

The optional ad-free monthly subscription and the one-time Unlock are handled by Apple or Google Play. We never see your payment details. Credits, purchase status and your settings are stored on your device.

What is collected

Collected by us: nothing, and your answers and progress are never collected. Collected by Google AdMob in the free version: device identifiers (advertising identifier), approximate location from IP address, and ad interaction and diagnostic data, used for third-party advertising and measuring ads.

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information, build profiles about you, run our own analytics, or ask you to create an account. No personal data is sent to us.

Children

Roadlark is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Nothing is sent to us in any case.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new effective date.
