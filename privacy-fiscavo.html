Macroeconomics AP: Fiscavo: Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Contact: support@equalinformation.com. Last updated 2026-11-27.

1. Introduction and scope

This policy describes how EqualInformation, LLC ("we", "us") handles information in connection with Macroeconomics AP: Fiscavo, version 1.0.0 and later (the "app"). It applies to the app only. The app has no account system. Its lessons, questions, labs and practice papers are bundled with it, your study progress is stored on your own device, and the free part of the app works without an internet connection.

2. Information we collect

A. Information you choose to give us. Diagnostic report: under Settings, Privacy and data, Send diagnostics displays the complete report before anything leaves your device, and the report is sent only when you tap Send. Feedback and suggestions: a category, your message (limited to 2,000 characters, with recognisable email addresses, telephone numbers and links removed automatically) and, if you provide them, a role (AP student, teacher or tutor, parent or guardian, or other), the screen you were on, a source link and a rating. An email address reaches us only if you tick the option to receive a reply, and it is used only to reply.

B. Diagnostic and app-usage information, collected with your agreement or as permitted by applicable law while the optional usage setting is on: app version and build; device model; operating system and version; processor and memory details; display size; language, region and time zone; text-size, accessibility and display settings; network type; a battery range; whether Fiscavo Plus is active; a randomly generated app identifier (not an advertising identifier); session timing; crash and error information; performance indicators; the screens, lessons, quizzes, labs, missions and mock exams you use; the questions you attempt, whether your answers are correct, the time taken and hints used; progress estimates by topic; the choices the adaptive-learning feature makes and what follows from them; and interactions with the subscription screen. Short answers typed into question boxes are included in shortened form after recognisable email addresses, telephone numbers, links and card or identity numbers have been removed.

When you accept the notice shown at first launch, a short acceptance record is always sent: a consent identifier, the time, the app, platform, version and build, the notice version and a fingerprint of its text, the state of the usage setting, the random app identifier, and your country, locale and time zone.

C. Information we do not collect: your name, contacts, precise location, photographs, files, advertising identifiers, or other text you write in the app, such as notes.

3. How we use information

To operate, maintain, secure and support the app; to diagnose problems; to provide and personalise the learning experience; to understand how the app is used; for research and development; for analytics and to improve our apps and educational content; to read and act on feedback; and to meet legal obligations.

4. Your choices

At first launch the app shows a notice that explains these practices and offers a separate usage switch. The switch starts off in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and wherever your region cannot be determined, until you turn it on; elsewhere it starts on. You can change it at any time under Settings, Privacy and data, where you can also read the notice again. Diagnostics and feedback are sent only when you choose to send them. To ask us to delete your usage data, use Delete my usage data in the app or write to diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

5. Storage and service providers

Information travels over encrypted (HTTPS) connections to a web application operated by EqualInformation, LLC, which records it in our Google Workspace services (Google Sheets and Google Drive). Besides storing them, the web application can email us the records from store-installed copies of Fiscavo. That applies to acceptance records and diagnostic reports, and equally to feedback, crash data, deletion requests and the grouped records of how you study and use the app. The recipients are our own mailboxes, with possible forwarding to one more of ours. Google acts for us as a service provider. Apple or Google processes payments for Fiscavo Plus under its own terms, and we do not receive your payment details. Reference links open public web pages from their own publishers, which follow their own privacy practices.

6. Retention

Usage, learning and diagnostic information is kept for as long as it remains useful for the purposes in section 3. Deletion requests are dealt with by hand when you make them; pressing the in-app button only files and emails the request. Emailed material remains until a person deletes it.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures, including encryption in transit. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. International users and legal bases

Where a legal basis is required we rely on your agreement (the usage switch and your decision to send diagnostics or feedback) and on our legitimate interest in running and improving the app. Information may be processed in the United States.

9. Children

We design our apps to collect only the information described in this policy. If you believe information has been provided to us that you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the version of the first-launch notice changes and the notice is shown again.

11. Contact

EqualInformation, LLC. Questions: support@equalinformation.com. Deletion and data requests: diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
