Lexidial: Word Puzzle Daily: Support

Lexidial is a daily five-letter word puzzle in English, Portuguese and Spanish, played with a brass dial.

Common questions

  • How do I play? A secret five-letter word is locked in a dial. Swipe each drum to any letter, then press Try. Each drum shows a check mark when right, or chevrons telling you whether the secret letter is later (up) or earlier (down) in the alphabet, and how far. A theme clue is shown from the start and you have seven tries.
  • Is the daily puzzle the same for everyone? Yes. It is calculated on your device from the calendar date, in each of the three languages, so no connection is needed.
  • How do I change language, colours or text size? Use the language chip on the home screen or Settings. Settings also has three colour modes (Classic, High contrast, Colour-safe) and the A- and A+ text size control. The Portuguese and Spanish screens are machine drafts under native review; please write to us with corrections.
  • How do I remove ads? Open the in-app purchase screen (Settings or More, or the ad-free prompt) and choose the ad-free subscription or the one-time purchase. You can also watch an optional short video where offered for temporary extras.
  • How do I restore a purchase? Open Settings (or the purchase screen) and tap Restore purchases, using the same Apple Account or Google account you bought with. This is needed after reinstalling or changing device.
  • How do I cancel the subscription? iPhone and iPad: Settings, your name, Subscriptions. Android: Google Play, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel it.
  • Can I change my ad choices? Yes. Use the privacy choices for ads in the app's Settings where shown, or your device's privacy settings.
