Tallybell support

Help for the subscription tracker

Reach us

Email support@equalinformation.com and mention Tallybell and your iOS version. We reply within a few days.

Common questions

Where is my data? Only on your phone. There is no account and no cloud copy, so back it up with Settings, Share the CSV export.

I did not get a reminder. Check that reminders are on in Tallybell Settings, that notifications are allowed for Tallybell in iOS Settings, and that the item is not marked Cut. Focus modes can also silence them.

How do I move my list from a spreadsheet? Settings, Import from a CSV file, or Paste CSV text. Columns named name, price, currency, cycle (monthly, yearly, weekly) and next_charge are recognised.

Can Tallybell cancel a subscription for me? No. It keeps a checklist; the cancellation itself is done in the store account or service where you signed up.

How do I remove ads? Tap No ads on the Bells screen: subscribe monthly, or earn about seven ad-free hours per short clip. Use Restore purchases after reinstalling or on a new device.

Cancel the ad-free subscription. iOS Settings, your name, Subscriptions, Tallybell.
