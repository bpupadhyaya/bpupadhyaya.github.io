Invoicelume Support

Invoicelume: Invoice Maker creates invoices and estimates as PDFs, keeps a client list, tracks payments, and works offline.

Common questions

Where are my invoices? On your device only. Back up the device as usual; there is no cloud copy.

How do I send an invoice? Open it, tap Share PDF, and pick Mail, Messages, Files or any app.

How do I make text bigger? Use the A- and A+ buttons in the app, or the system Larger Text setting.

How do I remove ads? Open Settings in the app, then Ad-free: subscribe monthly or earn ad-free time by watching videos. Restore Purchases is on the same sheet.

Is the tax figure correct for my country? The app multiplies by the rate you enter and rounds to the currency's smallest unit. Rules differ by place; check with an adviser.
