GRE Physics Test: Fluxarc support

Help for GRE Physics Test: Fluxarc

Physics practice app with simulations, topic drills and full mock tests by EqualInformation, LLC.

Contact

Write to support@equalinformation.com. Please include your device model, system version and the app version shown at the bottom of the Me tab.

Restore a subscription

Open Me, then Earn credits or go ad-free, then Restore purchase. Subscriptions are managed in your Apple ID or Google Play account settings, where you can also cancel.

Move your progress

Me has Export progress and Import progress. The file holds your learning data only, not credits or purchases.

Report a content error

Open Full solution and send the question number or a screenshot to support@equalinformation.com. Questions are original practice content that has not been reviewed by a physics expert, so reports are welcome.
