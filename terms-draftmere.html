Using the app

Draftmere teaches college essay writing as general education. It gives no guarantee of admission or of any result, and application rules change; always confirm current requirements with each college or application service.

Your work, your words

You must write your own application. Do not copy the invented examples or any essay written by someone else. The app does not write or rewrite essays for you.

Ads and subscription

The app is free with ads. An optional auto-renewing monthly subscription (7-day free trial where offered) removes ads. Payment is charged to your Apple ID or Google Play account and renews until cancelled; manage or cancel it in your store account settings.

Age

You must be at least 13 to use the app.

Content and sources

Lesson text and questions are original. Cited sources belong to their publishers and open from their own sites.
