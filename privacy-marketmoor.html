Privacy Policy: A Level Economics: Marketmoor

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Contact: support@equalinformation.com. Last updated 2026-11-24.

1. Introduction and scope

This Privacy Policy explains how EqualInformation, LLC ("we", "us") handles information in connection with A Level Economics: Marketmoor (the "app"), version 1.0.0 and later. It applies to the app only. The app has no account system.

2A. Information you choose to provide

Diagnostic reports: in Settings, Privacy and data, you may choose Send diagnostics. The app shows you the exact content first and sends it only after you tap Send. Feedback and suggestions: if you choose to send feedback, a suggestion or a problem report, we receive its category, your message (up to 2,000 characters, with email addresses, telephone numbers and links removed automatically) and, if you provide them, a role, a level or context, a source link and a rating from 1 to 5. We receive an email address only if you tick the option allowing us to contact you about your feedback.

2B. Diagnostic and app-usage information

While the optional usage setting is on (or where the law permits), the app can send: app version and build; device model; operating system and version; processor cores and architecture; graphics processor name; memory; screen size; language, region and time zone; text-size setting and a summary of accessibility and display settings; network type; a battery-level range; whether the free or Plus entitlement is active; a randomly generated app identifier (not an advertising identifier); session timing; the screens and simulations you open and for how long; lesson activity such as scroll depth and time; practice-question and mock-paper attempts (the question, its topic, whether the answer was correct, the time taken and the option chosen); topic-level progress estimates; the study suggestions the app makes and what follows them; use of simulations (control names and value ranges, presets and goals reached); interactions with the subscription screen; performance indicators; and crash and error information. When you accept the first-run notice, a short acceptance record is always sent, whatever the setting: a consent identifier, the time, the app, platform, version and build, the notice version and a hash of its text, the state of the usage setting, the random app identifier, your country, locale and time zone.

2C. Information we do not collect

The app does not collect your name, contacts, precise location, photographs or files, an advertising identifier, or the text you write in it (essay plans, notes or policy briefs), except the feedback you choose to send. Your progress and writing stay on your device.

3. How we use information

To operate, maintain, secure and support the app; to diagnose problems; to personalise the learning experience; to understand how the app is used; for research and development and to improve our apps and educational content; to read and act on feedback; and to comply with legal obligations.

4. Your choices

When you first open the app, a notice explains these practices and offers a separate usage switch. It is off by default in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and where your region cannot be determined, until you turn it on, and on elsewhere. You can change it at any time under More, Settings, Privacy and data. To request deletion of your usage data, use Delete my usage data in the app or email diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

5. Storage and service providers

Information is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections to a web application operated by EqualInformation, LLC that records it in Google Workspace services (Google Sheets and Drive). Diagnostic reports and acceptance notifications are also delivered by email. Google acts as a service provider on our behalf. Apple or Google processes subscription payments and we do not receive your payment details.

6. Retention and security

Usage, learning and diagnostic information is kept for as long as it is useful for the purposes above. Deletion requests are handled on request. We use reasonable technical and organisational measures, including encryption in transit; no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. International users and legal bases

Where the law requires a legal basis we rely on your consent (the usage switch and the actions you take to send diagnostics or feedback) and our legitimate interest in maintaining and improving the app. Information may be processed in the United States.

8. Information you want removed

We design our apps to collect only the information described in this policy. If you believe information has been provided to us that you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

9. Changes and contact

If a change to this policy is material, the version of the first-run notice changes and the notice is shown again. Questions: support@equalinformation.com. Deletion and data requests: diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

Exam board names and specification codes (AQA, Pearson Edexcel, OCR, WJEC Eduqas) and the Department for Education are used only to describe course content. This independent app is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by any of them. It does not predict or guarantee any grade.
