CPA Exam Prep: Ledgerwick: Support

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Contact: support@equalinformation.com. Last updated 2026-11-24.

Get help

Email support@equalinformation.com with your device model, app version and a short description of the problem. You can also use More, Privacy and data, Send diagnostics, which shows exactly what will be sent before you tap Send.

Manage or restore Ledgerwick Plus

In the app open More, then See Plus: use Restore purchases or Manage subscription. You can also cancel in your App Store or Google Play account settings.

Your data

Under More, Privacy and data you can switch usage sharing off, send feedback or a suggestion, view the first-run notice, and request deletion of usage information associated with your installation. You can also email diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

Found a content error?

Use the Report a problem button under an explanation or lesson, or tell us the section, the question text and what you think is wrong, with a source if you have one.
