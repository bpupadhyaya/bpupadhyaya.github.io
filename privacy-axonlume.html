Axonlume Privacy Policy

Effective 8 November 2026. Publisher: Equal Information LLC.

Your study record

Axonlume is a neuroanatomy study app. Quiz answers, streaks and mastery are kept in the app's storage on your device. We run no accounts and have no server that receives this record.

Advertising

The free app shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier, approximate location and ad interactions to choose and measure ads. On iOS the app asks for your permission to track before ads are requested; you can say no and still use everything, with non-personalised ads. In some regions a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform also appears, and you can reopen it from the More tab.

Subscription

The optional monthly ad-free subscription is processed by Apple. We receive no card or payment details. You can manage or cancel it in your Apple ID subscription settings.

What we never do

We do not sell personal data, collect names, emails or contacts, or ask for camera, microphone, photo, health or location permissions.

Children

Axonlume is a study tool for students and adults and is not directed to children under 13.

Study use only

The 3D atlas is a simplified schematic made by the app. Axonlume does not diagnose, treat or advise. Speak to a qualified clinician about health concerns.
