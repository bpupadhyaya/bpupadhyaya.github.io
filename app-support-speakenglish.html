Common questions

How do credits work? You start with 2 credits. 1 credit starts 1 new lesson, and that lesson then stays open. Watch a short ad to earn 1 credit. Reviews, the mistake notebook, today's plan and replaying lessons are always free. If no ad can be shown you get 1 free credit once every 24 hours.

How do I go ad-free? In the app open Credits and ad-free and start the monthly plan (7-day free trial, then $2.99 per month in the US). Cancel any time in your Apple ID or Google Play subscription settings. Use Restore purchases on a new device.

Speaking practice does not work. Allow the microphone and speech recognition in your device settings, and make sure English dictation is installed. If your device cannot recognise speech offline the app uses self-check instead.

Is the Hindi correct? All Hindi text is a machine draft awaiting a native speaker's review. Please report mistakes to us.

Move my progress. Settings, Export progress file, then Import on the other device.

Speaking feedback and the readiness number are rough practice aids, not tests or predictions.
