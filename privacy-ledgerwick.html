CPA Exam Prep: Ledgerwick: Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Contact: support@equalinformation.com. Last updated 2026-11-26.

Introduction and scope

This Privacy Policy describes how EqualInformation, LLC ("we", "us") handles information in the application CPA Exam Prep: Ledgerwick (the "app"). It applies to the app on iPhone and iPad and to the Android version. It was last updated on 2026-11-26.

Information we collect

Information you choose to provide. If you send a diagnostic report, the report and any note you add. If you send feedback or a suggestion, its category, your message, and optionally your role, level, a source link, a rating and, only if you tick the contact option, an email address for a reply.

Diagnostic and app-usage information, collected with your agreement or as permitted by applicable law: device and operating-system information (model, operating-system version, processor, memory, display size, language, region and time zone, accessibility and display settings such as text size), app version, performance and memory indicators, crash and error information, a randomly generated app identifier, and how features of the app are used. This includes how you use its learning features: lessons viewed, questions attempted, whether answers are correct, time taken, topic-level progress estimates, study sessions, simulations and guided builds, mock examinations, the adaptive decisions the app makes and their outcomes, and purchase-screen interactions. The answers you type and the words you search for are not included in this information: what you type stays on your device. Text leaves the app only when you choose to send it as feedback, a suggestion, a problem report or a note with a diagnostic report.

Information we do not collect: your name, contacts, precise location, photographs, files, cross-app device identifiers, or, in usage information, the answers, search words or other text you enter in the app. Your practice history, review schedule, mock results, guided builds and settings are stored on your device.

How we use information

To operate, maintain, secure and support the app; to diagnose problems; to provide and personalise your study path; to understand how the app is used; for research and development; for analytics and improvement of our apps and educational content; to read and act on feedback; and to comply with legal obligations.

Your choices

When you first open the app a notice is shown that you must accept to continue. It includes a usage-information switch. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and where your region cannot be determined, the switch is off until you turn it on; elsewhere it is on by default and you may turn it off. You may change it at any time under More, Privacy and data.

Diagnostic reports and feedback are sent only when you choose to send them.

To request deletion of usage information associated with your installation, use Delete my usage data under More, Privacy and data, or email diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

Storage and service providers

Information is stored in our Google Workspace services (Google Sheets and Drive) and notification email, and processed by service providers acting on our behalf. It is transmitted over encrypted connections. Subscription purchases are processed by Apple or Google; we do not receive your payment details. Source and reference links open public web pages inside the app; those sites apply their own privacy practices.

Retention

Usage and learning information is kept for as long as it is useful for the purposes described above. Deletion requests are handled on request.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

International users and legal bases

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we rely on your consent, which you may withdraw at any time using the switch, and on our legitimate interests in operating and improving the app. Information may be processed in the United States.

Children

We design our apps to collect only the information described in this policy. If you believe information has been provided to us that you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update this page and the version of the first-run notice, which is then shown again.
