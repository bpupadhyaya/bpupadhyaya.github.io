What stays on your device

Your answers, review schedule, progress, mock results, guided builds and settings are stored only on your device. Deleting the app erases them.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account system, no analytics and no tracking, and we do not collect your name, email address, identifiers or usage data.

Subscription

Ledgerwick Plus purchases are processed by Apple or Google; we never see your payment details.

Sources you open

Source and reference links open public web pages inside the app. Those sites load from their publishers and apply their own privacy practices.

Audience

The app is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed to children.
