Support Open Wallet

Help us build financial infrastructure for all of humanity.

Every parent's sacrifice should be seen. Every teacher's impact should be measured. Every human should have equal access to the global economy. Help us make it real.

Open Wallet is not just another crypto wallet — it is the front door to a new kind of economy. One where human value is recognized, where contributions to raising children and educating communities are quantified and rewarded, and where financial tools are available to every person on Earth at near-zero cost.

Behind Open Wallet are two deeper projects: Open Chain — a mobile-first blockchain where every phone is a validator, powered by Proof of Humanity and Proof of Contribution; and Open Token (OTK) — a multi-dimensional token that represents not just money, but nurture, education, health, community, and governance.

The Vision: World Peace Through Better-Raised Humans

Wars come from greed. Greed comes from unmet needs. Unmet needs come from people who were never given the tools to identify and utilize the resources around them. When children are raised well, they grow into adults who can serve themselves and others — creating harmony, not conflict.

Yet the invisible labor of parents, teachers, caregivers, and communities has never been formally recognized or rewarded by any economic system in history. A farmer in India spending sleepless nights raising her children deserves the same recognition as any professional in any city.

Open Chain changes this. Through the Living Ledger, every human gets a lifelong record of value received and given — across six dimensions: nurture, education, health, community, economic, and governance. Through Contribution Rings, when a child thrives, the parents, teachers, community, city, and country that helped raise them all receive recognition.

This entire ecosystem is 100% open source, with zero pre-mine, zero VC allocation, and zero founder tokens. Every OTK in existence is earned through real human contribution. We wrote these principles into our founding document — the Human Constitution.

What We're Building

Open Wallet

Universal crypto wallet — one app for every token, every chain. Multi-chain swaps, fiat on/off ramps, hardware wallet support, WalletConnect, and post-quantum encryption. Designed for a farmer in India and a Wall Street analyst alike.

Open Chain

Mobile-first blockchain where every phone is a validator. Proof of Humanity + Proof of Contribution consensus — one human, one vote. Universal ID for privacy-preserving identity. Near-zero fixed fees.

Open Token (OTK)

Multi-dimensional token representing human value across six channels: nurture, education, health, community, economic, and governance. No pre-mine. 100% earned. A universal basic participation income.

Living Ledger

Every human gets a lifelong on-chain record of value received and given. Contribution Rings attribute value to parents, teachers, and communities. Milestone minting rewards human development.
