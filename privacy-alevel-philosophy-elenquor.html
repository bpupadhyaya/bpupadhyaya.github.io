Privacy Policy

A Level Philosophy: Elenquor · EqualInformation, LLC · Effective 27 November 2026

About this policy

EqualInformation, LLC ("we") publishes Elenquor, a study app for A-level Philosophy, for Apple and Android devices. This page explains which information the app and this app's support pages involve, what we do with it and the choices open to you.

What stays on your device

Your study record, plan, pins, dispute rulings, practice essays and notes are kept in the app on your device. Words you type into answers, notes, rulings and searches are not sent to us. The only typed words that leave the device are those you choose to send as feedback (including any course context, source link and contact address you add) or as a note with a diagnostic report. Deleting the app removes the copy on the device.

When you use the share button on a dispute paper, your device passes the text to whichever app or person you pick. We do not receive it.

Information sent when you agree to the first-run notice

Tapping Agree sends a short record of that agreement, whether or not you share usage records. It holds a random identifier made for this app, a record number, the time, the notice version and its text with a fingerprint of that text, how long the notice was open and whether it was scrolled to the end, your choice about sharing, the app name, version and build, the platform, the country setting, language region, time zone and app language, and technical details used to tell a store copy from a test copy: device model, system version, build type, installer or store channel and whether the device is an emulator.

Usage and learning records (your choice)

A switch controls these. It starts off where your device's country setting is in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or certain associated territories, and also where the country cannot be told. Elsewhere it starts on, and you can turn it off on the first screen or at any later time in Settings.

While it is on, we receive records tied to the random identifier and a session number, with the technical details listed above:

  • app openings and pauses, time in use, screens opened, window layout, text size and whether you use the free course or Plus
  • which questions and written-practice points you attempt, the option chosen, whether it was sound, time taken, attempt number and study mode
  • estimates of your progress on each study point
  • the study suggestions the app makes, why in brief codes, and whether you follow them
  • lessons, explanations, step-by-step reasoning and cited sources you open, plan and view changes, use of the other-courses map, disputes steps and logic benches
  • events about the subscription screen and purchase outcome, without any payment details
  • reports of crashes and program errors (kind, a short signature, message and technical stack)

Turning the switch off stops these records and discards any still waiting on the device.

Things you send on purpose

Diagnostic reports are sent only when you press Send after seeing a preview. They hold the technical details above, region and time zone, access level, a summary of settings, a list of recent app events made of program codes, signatures of recent crashes and your optional note.

Feedback holds your message, the kind of feedback, and anything optional that you add: your role, course context, a source link, a rating and an email address if you ask to be contacted. A thumbs rating is sent when you tap it while sharing is on. A deletion request holds your random identifier and the type of request.

Before a message or note is sent, the app hides text that looks like an email address, a web link or a long number. This is a safeguard and may miss things, so please leave personal details out. A contact address you type into its own box is sent as typed.

What we do not collect

The app does not ask for your name, and does not collect contacts, photos, files or precise location.

Why we use the information

We use it to run, secure, repair and support the app; to record agreement to the notice; to understand how the learning features perform; to improve how study is adapted to learners; to improve our apps and educational material; to carry out research and development for those ends; to answer you; and to meet legal duties. Where the law requires a legal basis, we rely on your consent for optional usage and learning records and for what you choose to send, and on our legitimate interest in keeping a record of agreement and keeping the service working.

Where it goes and how long it stays

Records travel over encrypted connections to our Google Workspace services (spreadsheets, files and mail) and are handled by Google as our service provider. For copies installed from an app store, these records (agreement, diagnostic reports, deletion requests, crash reports, feedback, and usage and learning records) can also be emailed to our own mailboxes, and those messages may be forwarded to another of our mailboxes. They may be processed in countries other than yours.

Our server does not delete records automatically. We keep them until we remove them. Information may pass to a successor if our business or this app is merged, financed, reorganised or passes to a new owner, and remains subject to this policy. We may also disclose information where the law requires it.

Deleting your records

In the app, open Settings, then Privacy and data, and choose Ask us to delete my records, or write to diagnostics@equalinformation.com and quote the install number shown on that screen. We act on valid requests by hand. Copies that were emailed remain unless someone removes them by hand. Erasing your study record on the device, or removing the app, does not by itself delete records already sent.

Your other rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to see, correct, delete, restrict or object to the use of information about you, to withdraw consent and to complain to a data protection authority. Because records carry a random identifier and not your name, we may need that identifier to find them.

Store purchases

Apple or Google takes payment for Elenquor Plus. We do not receive your card details. The app asks the store whether a subscription is active.

Cited websites

Links to cited pages open other organisations' websites inside the app. Their own privacy practices apply.

Children

The app is built to collect only what this policy describes. If you believe information has reached us that should be removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will update this page and its date, and where the first-run notice changes it will be shown again. EqualInformation, LLC: support@equalinformation.com. Data and deletion requests: diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
