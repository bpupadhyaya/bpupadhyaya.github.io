Support

Support Zoel AI

Your private AI — running entirely on your device.

Zoel AI is built by a small, independent team and is funded by the people who use it — not by ads, data sales, or tracking. The Health module is free forever for everyone, and every conversation runs on your own device. Your support is what keeps it that way, and what funds new on-device models, knowledge, and features.

Support the development

The most direct way to support Zoel is to upgrade your membership. Every dollar goes straight into development — better on-device models, the knowledge domains, and ongoing research. Nothing is ever spent on ad networks, and nothing about you is ever sold.

  • Zoel Select — $4.99 one-time. Health plus any 4 knowledge modules you choose, unlocked forever.
  • Zoel Unlimited — $4.99/month or $49.99/year. Every module, current and future. Cancel anytime.

Your subscription funds the models and the research — not ad networks. Nothing about you is ever sold.

Other ways to help

Support doesn't have to be financial. Each of these genuinely helps a privacy-first, independent app:

  • Rate & review Zoel on the App Store and Google Play — honest reviews are how privacy tools get discovered.
  • Tell a friend. Word of mouth is how an app with no ad budget grows.
  • Share feedback. Feature ideas, missing topics, and bug reports directly shape what gets built next.

Direct support & partnerships

If you’d like to support the project directly — sponsorship, partnership, or another arrangement — we’d love to hear from you.

  • Email: bpupadhyaya@gmail.com
  • Website: equalinformation.com

Where your support goes

  • Optimizing on-device AI models so they run fast and privately on more phones
  • Expanding and refreshing the knowledge domains with cited, trustworthy content
  • Improving multilingual voice and on-device speech
  • Keeping the Health module free, forever, for everyone
