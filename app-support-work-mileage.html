Waywick support

Mileage Log for work, medical and charity driving.

Quick answers

Where do my trips live? Only on your device. Back up your phone as usual; use Reports, then Export, to keep a CSV or PDF copy.

Which rates does Waywick use? Official published rates: IRS standard mileage rates (United States, including the July 2026 change), HMRC approved mileage rates (United Kingdom), CRA per-kilometre rates (Canada) and the ATO cents-per-kilometre method (Australia). Settings, then Accuracy and sources, lists each rate period and its source. Rows marked as one source should be checked on the official page.

Distance from Drive looks off. GPS can differ from your odometer. Open the trip and correct the distance or enter odometer readings.

Drive stops when I lock the phone. Recording runs while Waywick is open. For other trips, add them by hand or with odometer readings.

Ad-free and restore. Settings, then Go ad-free. Restore purchases is on the same screen.

Report a rate or total error

Email support@equalinformation.com with the region, the date and what looks wrong. Fixes ship in the next update.
