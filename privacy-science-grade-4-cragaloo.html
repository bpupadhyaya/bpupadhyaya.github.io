Privacy Policy

Science Grade 4: Cragaloo. Last updated 28 November 2026.

This app is a science study aid published by EqualInformation. It has no accounts, no tracking and no third-party software that collects information.

What stays on the device

Learning progress, settings and a short list of recent actions are stored only on the device. "Erase progress" in the grown-ups area removes progress. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

What is sent, and only if a grown-up sends it

The app sends nothing by itself. Behind a grown-up check, a diagnostics message can be previewed and sent from the user's own mail app to diagnostics@equalinformation.com. It lists app version, device model, system version, language and region, time zone, text size, the view in use, plan status, a count of answers and recent actions. A feedback message can be sent the same way to support@equalinformation.com. We then receive the sender's email address and what the message contains. We use these only to fix problems and reply. Please leave out a child's name and other personal details. To ask for a message to be deleted, write to support@equalinformation.com.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We do not receive payment card details.

Outside web pages

Cited sources open only after a grown-up check. The outside site receives the usual connection details and has its own privacy practices. The window does not keep cookies or other site data after it closes.

Children

The app is used by children and is designed so that it does not collect information from them. Sending a message is for grown-ups.

Changes and contact

Changes will appear on this page. Contact: support@equalinformation.com.
