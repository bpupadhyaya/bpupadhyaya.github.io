Data Science Basics: Datawren - Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC.

Last updated: 2026-11-17.

What stays on your device

The course runs offline. There is no account and no server of ours. Your answers, credits, progress and text-size choice are stored only on your device.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob at natural breaks. If you consent (Google consent form, and on iPhone the tracking prompt) advertising partners may use your device advertising identifier to personalise ads. You may decline and still use every lesson. Subscribers see no ads.

Sources window

When you open a cited source, the in-app window loads that public web page. Cookies are not kept after the window closes, and the page cannot talk to the app.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple or Google. We never receive payment details.

Children

A general-audience learning app, not directed to children under 13.
