GCSE German Prep: Lernmoor: Support

Published by EqualInformation, LLC. Last updated 2026-11-25.

Help

Questions, content corrections and subscription help: support@equalinformation.com. Please include your device model, the app version and the screen or question you are on.

Send diagnostics

Open More, Settings, Privacy and data and choose Send diagnostics. You see exactly what the report contains and nothing is sent until you press Send.

Feedback and suggestions

Use Feedback and suggestions in Settings, the thumbs and report controls under lessons and explanations, or Suggest content. Nothing is sent until you press Send.

Speaking practice and the microphone

Recordings stay on your device and are deleted after playback. If recording does not start, check that microphone access is allowed for the app in your device settings.

German audio

If you hear no German voice, open your device settings, install a German voice for text to speech and restart the app.

Delete my usage data

Open More, Settings, Privacy and data and choose Delete my usage data, or email diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

Subscription

Manage or cancel Lernmoor Plus in your App Store or Google Play account settings. If a purchase does not appear, use Restore purchases on the Lernmoor Plus screen.

Contact

support@equalinformation.com. Privacy and deletion requests: diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
