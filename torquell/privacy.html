This policy explains what the A Level PE: Torquell app for phones and tablets sends off your device, why, and what you can do about it. EqualInformation, LLC ("we") is responsible for this information. There is no account to create and the app never asks who you are.

What stays on your device

Your progress, study plan, exam date, pins, build notes, written practice answers and searches are kept in the app's storage on your device. Written answers, build notes and search words are not sent to us. Removing the app removes them.

What is sent, and when

WhenWhat it contains
Once, when you press the button on the first-run briefing (and again only if that text changes). The random number is made by this app and is not shared between apps. As with any internet connection, the computers that carry a message receive your network address; we do not add it to the records described above. Please do not put names or other personal details in feedback or report notes.

Why we use it

To run and secure the app, to answer you, to keep evidence of what was shown at first run, to personalise and improve how the app suggests next steps, to find faults and weak or wrong teaching material, for analysis, research and development of our apps and educational content, and to meet legal obligations.

Your choices

  • The sharing switch starts off if your device's country setting is in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, Gibraltar, Jersey, Guernsey or the Isle of Man, or cannot be read. Elsewhere it starts on. It is shown before you enter and can be changed at any time under You, then Privacy and data. Every part of the course works with it off. Turning it off stops further usage records and clears those still waiting.
  • Under Privacy and data you can send us a request to delete your shared records; the button also turns sharing off. The button only sends the request. It does not erase anything by itself. We remove records by hand, and copies that reached our mailboxes by email remain unless we also remove them by hand. You can also email diagnostics@equalinformation.com and quote the app number shown there.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to ask for access, correction, deletion or restriction, to object, and to complain to your data protection authority.

Legal bases

Where the law of the United Kingdom or the European Economic Area applies: your consent for the optional usage and learning records; and our legitimate interests in keeping evidence of the first-run notice, answering the reports and feedback you send, and keeping the service secure.

Storage, providers and transfers

Messages travel over encrypted connections to a script we run on an outside provider's cloud services. They are stored in spreadsheets and files that the provider hosts for us. The provider processes them for us and may do so in countries other than yours, including the United States. Access is limited to people who need it. Information may also pass to a successor in a merger, reorganisation or sale of the business, subject to this policy.

Copies by email

For copies of the app installed from an app store, the following can also be emailed to our own company mailboxes: first-run acceptance records, fault reports (diagnostics), deletion requests, crash records, feedback, and usage and learning records. Such mail may be forwarded to another mailbox of ours. An emailed copy is separate from the copy in our spreadsheets and files: removing one does not remove the other.

Other services you reach from the app

Cited pages open inside the app from their publishers' websites, which apply their own privacy policies. Plus is sold and charged through the checkout of the app store you use, so your card or payment details never reach us.

Retention and security

Our system does not delete records, or remove the app number from them, automatically. Records in our spreadsheets, files and mailboxes stay until someone on our side removes them by hand. We have not set a fixed deletion period. To have records removed, send the request described under Your choices; we may keep what the law requires us to keep. No system is completely secure, and we limit what is collected for that reason.

Permission under local law

If you believe someone who needed a parent or guardian's permission under local law has sent us information without it, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when the app's practice changes and show the first-run briefing again if its wording changes. Contact: EqualInformation, LLC, support@equalinformation.com.
