Privacy Policy

This policy explains what the A Level PE: Torquell app for iPhone, iPad and Android sends off your device, why, and what you can do about it. EqualInformation, LLC ("we") is responsible for this information. The app has no account and does not ask for your name.

What stays on your device

Your progress, study plan, exam date, pins, build notes, written practice answers and searches are kept in the app's storage on your device. Written answers, build notes and search words are not sent to us. Removing the app removes them.

What is sent, and when

WhenWhat it contains
Once, when you press the button on the first-run briefing (and again only if that text changes). This is sent whichever way the sharing switch is set.A record of what you were shown: time, text version, a copy and short fingerprint of the text, the button used, seconds on the screen, whether you reached the end, the sharing switch position, a random number made by this app, a record number, your device's country setting, language region and time zone, the app's name, version and platform, and technical facts about the install (release or test build, store or other source, whether it is an emulator). If sharing is on, it also contains device model and system name and version.
While the optional sharing switch is onUsage and learning records: question identifiers, right or wrong, option picked, time taken, topic grip estimates, next-step suggestions the app showed or you followed, view switches, build step progress, lab use (which lab, duration, number of adjustments, whether the goal was reached), screens and lessons opened with time on them, cited sources and sheets opened, one-tap helpful or not-yet votes, sitting length, reading size, layout shape, free or Plus status, and the random app and sitting numbers with app and content versions. If the app hits an error or crash: its type, a short description and a technical trace.
Only when you press "Send this report"A fault report. You choose which parts to include and see them first: device model, system version, language region and time zone; settings and subscription status; up to 200 short lines of recent app activity; recent crash notes; and your optional note.
Only when you press "Send feedback"Your message, its kind, and any role, rating, source link or item number you add. Your email address is included only if you tick that we may contact you.
Only when you ask for deletion in the appThe random app number and the request.

These records are not combined with advertising identifiers, contacts, photos or precise location, and the app asks for none of them. The random number is made by this app and is not shared between apps. As with any internet connection, the computers that carry a message receive your network address; we do not add it to the records described above. Please do not put names or other personal details in feedback or report notes.

Why we use it

To run and secure the app, to answer you, to keep evidence of what was shown at first run, to personalise and improve how the app suggests next steps, to find faults and weak or wrong teaching material, for analysis, research and development of our apps and educational content, and to meet legal obligations.

Your choices

  • The sharing switch starts off if your device's country setting is in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, Gibraltar, Jersey, Guernsey or the Isle of Man, or cannot be read. Elsewhere it starts on. It is shown before you enter and can be changed at any time under You, then Privacy and data. Every part of the course works with it off. Turning it off stops further usage records and clears those still waiting.
  • Under Privacy and data you can ask for your shared records to be deleted. You can also email diagnostics@equalinformation.com and quote the app number shown there. We handle requests by hand.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to ask for access, correction, deletion or restriction, to object, and to complain to your data protection authority.

Legal bases

Where the law of the United Kingdom or the European Economic Area applies: your consent for the optional usage and learning records; and our legitimate interests in keeping evidence of the first-run notice, answering the reports and feedback you send, and keeping the service secure.

Storage, providers and transfers

Messages travel over encrypted connections to a script we run on Google's services. They are stored in our Google Workspace spreadsheets and files, and some are also copied to our company mailboxes. Google processes them for us and may do so in countries other than yours, including the United States. Access is limited to people who need it. Information may also pass to a successor in a merger, reorganisation or sale of the business, subject to this policy.

Other services you reach from the app

Cited pages open inside the app from their publishers' websites, which apply their own privacy policies. Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple or Google; we do not receive your payment card details.

Retention and security

We keep records for as long as they serve the purposes above, then delete them or remove the app number from them; deletion requests are acted on unless the law requires us to keep something. No system is completely secure, and we limit what is collected for that reason.

Permission under local law

If you believe someone who needed a parent or guardian's permission under local law has sent us information without it, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when the app's practice changes and show the first-run briefing again if its wording changes. Contact: EqualInformation, LLC, support@equalinformation.com.
