PrivacyTermsSupport

Email support@equalinformation.com. Tell us your device and what you were doing. Please do not send personal details you would rather we did not have.

In the app

  • Report a wrong or unclear question: press "Report a problem" under its explanation.
  • Suggest something: You, then Feedback and suggestions.
  • Send a fault report: You, then Send diagnostics. You see it before it goes.
  • Text size: the "Aa size" button at the top changes it from 50% to 300%.

Torquell Plus

  • New phone or reinstall: sign in to the same store account, open Torquell Plus and press Restore purchases.
  • Cancel: press Manage subscription, or open subscriptions in your Apple or Google account. You keep access until the paid month ends.
  • Refunds: request them from Apple or Google.

Offline use

Lessons, questions, builds, labs and papers work without a connection. Cited web pages and the store need one.
