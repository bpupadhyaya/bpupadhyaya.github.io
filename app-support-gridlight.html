Gridlight: Picture Logic - Support

Need help or found a problem? Email bpupadhyaya@equalinformation.com with your device model and iOS version and we will reply as soon as we can.

Common questions

How do I play? Open the About page from the menu: it has the full rules, tips and a glossary.

Can I get stuck? No. Every picture is checked by a line solver and can be finished by logic alone. The Light button explains the next certain step.

How do I remove ads? Watch rewarded videos for ad-free time, or take the monthly pass from the About page or the Ad-free chip on the menu. Use Restore on those screens if you already subscribed on this Apple ID.

How do I cancel the monthly pass? iOS Settings, your name, Subscriptions, Gridlight.
