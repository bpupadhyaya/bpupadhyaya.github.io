Calculus I Course: Epsilume Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-11-16. Calculus I Course: Epsilume (a first-semester calculus course (limits, derivatives and integrals)) is published by EqualInformation, LLC.

Summary

Epsilume has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of ours. What you learn and how you do stays on your device. The only outside company involved is Google, through the advertising that keeps the app free.

Stored on your device only

Your practice progress, mastery estimates, review schedule, settings and any ad-free time you have earned live in the app's private storage and work offline. We cannot see, copy or restore them, and deleting the app deletes them.

Data the app does not collect

No name, email address, location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, health or fitness data. We do not sell personal data and we do not build profiles of you.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. On iOS the app first shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, and where required Google's consent message appears (Google User Messaging Platform) so you can allow or limit personalised ads. When you allow it, Google may use your advertising identifier, IP address and ad-interaction data to deliver and measure ads. If you decline, ads are not personalised. Ads never appear during timed tests. See how Google uses data from ads.

Optional subscription

An optional auto-renewing monthly subscription removes ads. The price is shown by the App Store or Google Play before you buy, a free trial is included where offered, and the subscription renews until you cancel it. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google; we never see your card details and the app only reads whether your subscription is active. Manage or cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account settings. See the terms of use.

Children

Epsilume is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices and data requests

Change tracking permission any time in iOS Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking, and reopen the ad consent message from the app's About screen where available. To ask a privacy question or to request access to, or deletion of, any data we might hold about you, write to bpupadhyaya@equalinformation.com; the support page has more help.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it.
