Tallybell

Privacy policy, updated 6 November 2026

In one breath: your subscription list lives only on your phone. Tallybell has no sign-in, never connects to a bank and has no server that receives your entries. The free app shows ads through Google AdMob, and you decide what ad tracking is allowed.

Kept on your device

Names, prices, billing dates, notes, verdicts, checklists, settings and your reminder preferences are saved in the app's private storage. We cannot read them. Deleting the app, or using Settings, Delete everything, erases them. CSV files you export are created only when you ask and are shared only where you send them.

Reminders

Renewal and trial reminders are local notifications scheduled by iOS on your device. Nothing about them is sent to us.

Who we are and what we collect

The publisher is EqualInformation, LLC. We do not collect personal information through Tallybell, we do not run analytics of our own, and we never sell data.

Advertising

The free version shows banner, full-screen and optional rewarded ads from Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google and its partners may process your advertising identifier, approximate location from your IP address, ad interactions and basic device information. On iOS the app asks for tracking permission first; if you say no, ads are not personalised with that identifier. Where required (for example the EEA, UK and some US states) a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform appears, and you can reopen it from Settings, Privacy choices. See how Google uses data from partner apps.

Optional ad-free subscription

An optional monthly subscription removes ads. Payment and renewal are handled entirely by Apple; we never see your card or Apple ID. Manage or cancel it in your Apple account settings. Time earned by watching rewarded clips is counted on your device.

Children

Tallybell is a tool for adults managing household spending and is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

  • Turn off tracking for Tallybell in iOS Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking.
  • Reset your advertising identifier in iOS Settings.
  • Delete all in-app data from Settings, Delete everything.

Changes and contact
