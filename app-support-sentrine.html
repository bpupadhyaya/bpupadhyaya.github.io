Support

Cybersecurity AP: Sentrine · EqualInformation, LLC · Effective 26 November 2026

Get help

Email support@equalinformation.com. Tell us your device model and what you were doing. Inside the app you can also use Me, Settings, Privacy and data to send diagnostics (with a preview) or feedback.

Restore or cancel Sentrine Plus

Restore: Me, Settings, Restore purchases, using the same Apple or Google account you bought with. Cancel: Me, Settings, Manage subscription, or your store account's subscription page. After cancelling you keep Plus until the paid month ends.

Report a content error

Under an explanation or lesson choose Report a problem, or email us with the objective code (for example 3.4.B) and what looks wrong.

Text size and layout

Use A- and A+ at the top of the screen to size text from 50 to 300 percent. Tablets show extra panes in wide layouts.

About the course

Check the current exam date and course documents on the College Board's AP Central website.
