Debtfall: Debt Payoff Planner. Privacy Policy

Effective 9 November 2026. Publisher: Equal Information LLC.

Your debts stay on your device

The balances, interest rates, minimum payments and extra-payment amounts you type into Debtfall are stored only on your device. There is no account, no sign-in, no server we run for your data and no bank linking. We cannot see or recover your figures.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device advertising identifier, product interaction data and approximate location to select and measure ads. On iOS the app first asks whether you allow tracking across other companies' apps and websites; where required by law, a Google consent form follows. You can say no and still use every planning feature.

Subscription and rewards

The optional monthly ad-free subscription is handled by Apple; we receive no card or payment details. Manage or cancel it in your Apple ID settings. Terms of Use: Apple standard license agreement. You can also watch rewarded videos to earn ad-free time.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal data, ask for your name, email or contacts, or request camera, microphone, photo, health or precise-location access.

Not financial advice

Debtfall is an educational calculator. Results are estimates from the numbers you enter and may differ from your lender's statements. Confirm payoff amounts with your lender.

Children

Debtfall is not directed to children under 13.
