LifeOS is live on Google Play. This page documents each production release with what changed, what's new, and what to expect.

These release notes are maintained by AI agents. While we strive for accuracy, limitations may apply.

v1.0.0 Stable Current May 26, 2026

LifeOS is live on Google Play — your all-in-one life management system powered by Zoe, your AI life guide. Eight core life domains in one app, 100% offline-first, no account required.

What's included

  • Health — steps, weight, heart rate, water intake tracking
  • Finance — budget, expenses, and financial goals
  • Mental Health — mood tracking, journaling, stress monitoring
  • Fitness — workouts, progress tracking, and plans
  • Nutrition — food log, barcode scanner, macro tracking (Premium)
  • Family — shared schedule and contacts (Premium)
  • Travel — trips and itinerary planner (Premium)
  • Emergency — SOS, location sharing, emergency contacts
  • Zoe AI — voice-powered AI life guide with configurable API key; keyword-mode banner shown when no AI is configured
  • Three-tier IAP: Free + Premium ($4.99 one-time) + Professional ($9.99/mo or $79.99/yr)
  • Institutional licensing available via equalinformation.com
  • 100% offline-first — all data on-device, no account required, no tracking
  • Android (Jetpack Compose), versionCode 5, v1.0.0
  • Package ID: com.equalinformation.lifeos.app
Android
v0.9.0 Beta May 2026

Internal beta — all 44 modules wired and building on iOS (SwiftUI) and Android (Jetpack Compose). Deployed to internal test devices.

What's included

  • 44 full-featured modules across Health, Finance, Fitness, Nutrition, Family, Travel, Emergency, and Professional tiers
  • 100% offline-first, all data on-device SQLite — no cloud required
  • Optional external AI via user-provided API keys (OpenAI, Anthropic, etc.)
  • Voice input (offline STT) and TTS as first-class features on every module
  • iOS (SwiftUI) and Android (Jetpack Compose) build successfully with zero errors
