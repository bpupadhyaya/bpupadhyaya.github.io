Dentiwell Privacy Policy

Effective 8 November 2026. Publisher: Equal Information LLC.

What Dentiwell stores

Dentiwell is a tooth anatomy study app. Your quiz results, streak and text-size choice are stored only on your device. There are no accounts and we cannot see this information. The 3D teeth, charts and lessons are built into the app and work offline.

Ads

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier and approximate location to choose and measure ads. On iOS the app first asks whether apps may track you across other companies' apps and websites, and where the law requires it shows a Google consent form so you can choose how ad data is used. You can decline and still use everything; ads are then non-personalised. See policies.google.com/technologies/ads. You can remove ads with the monthly Ad-free subscription or by earning ad-free time.

Subscription

The optional monthly Ad-free subscription is processed by Apple. We receive no payment details. Manage or cancel it in your Apple ID subscription settings. Terms of Use: the Apple standard licensed application end user agreement.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal data, and Dentiwell does not collect names, email addresses or contacts, and does not ask for camera, microphone, photo, health or location permission.

Children

Dentiwell is an education app for students and adults and is not directed to children under 13.

Educational use only

Dentiwell is a study aid. Its 3D teeth are illustrative models with typical proportions, not patient scans. It does not diagnose, treat or advise. See a dentist for any concern about your own teeth.
