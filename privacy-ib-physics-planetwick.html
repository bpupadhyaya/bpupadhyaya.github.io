Privacy policy

IB Physics Prep: Planetwick

Last updated 23 November 2026.

The app works offline and has no account. Your study progress, settings and notes are stored only on your device and are not sent to us.

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob. Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent in a consent message, and on iOS the app asks permission before tracking. AdMob may collect device identifiers and ad interaction data under Google's own policy. You can refuse tracking or change your consent and the app keeps working. The app does not knowingly show personalised ads to children under 13 and is for learners aged 13 and over.

Purchases (the optional monthly ads-removal subscription) are handled by Apple or Google; we do not receive your payment details. We do not sell personal data.

Deleting the app deletes the data stored on your device. Ask us any privacy question by email.

Planetwick is not affiliated with or endorsed by the International Baccalaureate Organization. IB is named in plain text only to describe the course the app helps with.
