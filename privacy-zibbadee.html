Math Grade 1: Zibbadee

Privacy

Last updated 27 November 2026

Math Grade 1: Zibbadee is first-grade maths practice for a child, to be used with an adult nearby.

What leaves the device

Nothing. This version of the app has no way to send information anywhere: no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, no crash reporting, no microphone, no camera, no contacts and no location. We never receive anything about you or your child. The only code from outside is the purchase library of the store itself: StoreKit on Apple devices and Google Play Billing on Android. There is no analytics, advertising or tracking code.

What stays on the device

To choose what to offer next and to show progress to a grown-up, the app remembers on the device: which skills were tried, answers given, kinds of slip, how long answers took, the real-object checks a grown-up recorded, text size and sound settings. Erase all progress in the grown-up area, or delete the app, and it is gone. Your phone or tablet may include app data in its own backup, depending on your device settings.

Speech

Read aloud uses the voice built into the device. How the device produces that voice is up to its maker.

Purchases

The app has two optional monthly plans. Payment, renewal and cancellation are handled entirely by Apple or Google under your own account with them. The app only asks the store whether a plan is active; we do not see your payment details or your name.

Grown-up area

Everything about plans, progress and settings sits behind an adult check. Passing it is not proof of age or consent.

Children

Because nothing is collected or sent, there is nothing about a child for us to hold, sell or share. If we ever change this we will publish a new version of this page first.
