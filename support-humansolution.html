About Human Solution

Human Solution is a personal assistant super app that brings together multiple specialized modules in one place. Instead of installing separate apps for health, legal, auto, and home needs, Human Solution lets you access everything from a single app — and install only the modules you need.

Included Modules

  • PlainHealth — Health education in plain language (labs, meds, conditions)
  • CareCircle — Caregiver coordination for families
  • ChronoTrack — Chronic condition tracking with pattern detection
  • Kindly — AI mental wellness companion (mood, journal, breathing)
  • MediSight — Medicine identifier and drug reference
  • PlainDental — Dental health education
  • PlainLaw — Legal rights and terms in plain language
  • PlainAuto — Vehicle maintenance for owners and mechanics
  • RepairMate — Step-by-step home repair guides

More modules will be added over time.

How It Works

  • Android: Only the shell app downloads initially (~3MB). Each module downloads on demand when you tap "Get." You can remove modules to free space.
  • iOS: The app includes all module code. Module content downloads on first use and can be freed up later.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it require internet?
Only for the initial module download. After that, everything works 100% offline.

Is my data private?
Yes. All data stays on your device. No accounts, no cloud sync, no tracking. See our Privacy Policy.

Can I use individual modules separately?
Yes. Each module (PlainHealth, CareCircle, etc.) is also available as a standalone app. Human Solution simply bundles them together for convenience.

How much does it cost?
Core features in every module are free. Two premium tiers are available:
• Personal Premium — $14.99 one-time: Unlock advanced features across all 9 modules. No subscription.
• Professional Premium — $29.99/month or $199.99/year: Full access including clinical tools, professional legal references, and institutional features.
