Quarterwick Privacy Policy

Effective 8 November 2026. Publisher: Equal Information LLC.

Your income and expense log

Quarterwick: Freelance Tax is an estimated-tax planner. Everything you enter (income, expenses, miles, jar deposits, payments, tax profile) is stored only on your device. There is no account, no server we operate, and no bank or payroll linking. We cannot see your entries. Exporting a CSV is your choice and goes only where you send it.

Ads

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier, product interaction data and approximate location to choose and measure ads. On iOS the app first asks permission before tracking across other companies' apps and websites. Where the law requires it, a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) lets you choose how ad data is used; you can reopen it from More. If you decline, ads are non-personalised and the app works the same. Google explains its practices at policies.google.com/technologies/ads. Your income log is never sent to advertisers.

Subscription

The optional monthly ad-free subscription is processed by Apple. We receive no card or payment details. Manage or cancel in your Apple ID subscription settings. Terms of Use: Apple standard license agreement.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal data, collect names, emails or contacts, and do not request camera, microphone, photo, health or precise location permissions.

Not tax advice

Quarterwick gives estimates from published IRS tables and your own numbers. It is not tax, legal or accounting advice. Confirm amounts with the IRS or a tax professional.

Children

Quarterwick is not directed to children under 13.
