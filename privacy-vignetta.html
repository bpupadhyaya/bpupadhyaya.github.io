USMLE Step 2 CK Prep: Vignetta: Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Effective 2026-11-27.

1. Introduction and scope

This Privacy Policy explains how EqualInformation, LLC ("we", "us") handles information in connection with the USMLE Step 2 CK Prep: Vignetta app. It applies to the app and to the first-run notice shown when you first open it.

2. Information we collect

A. Information you choose to provide. Diagnostic reports that you choose to send, with any note you add. Feedback and suggestions that you submit, with their category, message, optional role, level, source link and rating, and an email address only if you choose to give one for a reply.

B. Diagnostic and app-usage information, collected with your agreement or as permitted by applicable law. Device and operating-system information (model, system version, processor, memory, display size, language, region and time zone, accessibility and display settings), app version, performance and memory indicators, crash and error information, a randomly generated app identifier, and how features of the app are used. Because this is a learning app, this includes how learning features are used and perform: lessons viewed, questions attempted, whether answers are correct, time taken, hints used, progress estimates by topic, study sessions, adaptive-learning decisions and their outcomes, and activity in labs and simulations. That a search was made, how many results it showed and which result you opened are included. From version 1.0.1 the search words are not (the earlier version is described below under Typed answers and search words).

C. Information we do not collect. Your name, contacts, precise location, photographs, files, advertising identifiers, or, from version 1.0.1, text that you type in the app, such as search words and notes, apart from the feedback and diagnostic notes you choose to send.

Your study progress, answers and settings are also stored on your device so that the app works offline.

Typed answers and search words: current and earlier versions

Version 1.0.1 and later. The words you type into Study search stay on your device and are not part of the usage information. While the optional usage setting is on, the app records only that a search was made, how many results it showed and which result you opened. Whatever you write in a feedback message or a diagnostic note is different: it is sent only when you choose to send it.

Earlier version still installed. Version 1.0 behaves differently until you update it. While the optional usage setting is on, it sends the words you search for in Study (on Apple devices when you submit the search; on Android also after a short pause once three characters are typed), cut to 200 characters, with anything recognised as an email address, web link, telephone number or card or identity number removed. The text under the usage switch in that version names only answers to questions and does not mention search words, although its first-run notice does. To stop this in version 1.0, turn the usage setting off under More, Privacy and data.

3. How we use information

To operate, maintain, secure and support the app; to diagnose problems; to provide and personalise the learning experience; to understand how the app is used; for research and development; for analytics and improvement of our apps and educational content; to read and act on feedback; and to comply with legal obligations.

4. Your choices

When you first open the app a notice is shown. The optional usage-information switch is under More, Privacy and data; it is off by default in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland until you turn it on. "Delete my usage data", in the same place, is a request rather than an erase button: it stops usage sharing and asks us to delete your usage information. You can also ask by writing to diagnostics@equalinformation.com. Diagnostic reports and feedback are sent only when you choose to send them.

5. Storage and service providers

Information is stored in our Google Workspace services (Google Sheets and Drive) and notification email, and processed by service providers acting on our behalf. Email is more than an alert here. For store-installed copies, our server can send the content of incoming records to mailboxes we operate (possibly forwarded on to a second one of ours): acceptance of the notice, reports you send, feedback, requests, crashes, and the usage and study records collected while sharing is on. Information is transmitted over encrypted connections. Subscription payments are processed by Apple or Google; we do not see payment details.

6. Retention

Usage and learning information is kept for as long as it is useful for the purposes above. No automatic process deletes these records. After a request reaches us, removal depends on a person doing it, and an emailed copy survives that unless it is also removed by hand.

7. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. International users and legal bases

Where applicable we rely on your consent or on our legitimate interests. Information may be processed in the United States.

9. Children

We design our apps to collect only the information described in this policy. If you believe information has been provided to us that you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

10. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes the first-run notice version changes and the notice is shown again.

11. Contact

EqualInformation, LLC, support@equalinformation.com. Deletion and data requests: diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
