Support

Questions, content corrections and subscription help: support@equalinformation.com. Include your device model and app version. Inside the app you can also use Settings, Feedback and suggestions, or Send diagnostics.

Subscription

Cancel or manage Mbavane Plus in your App Store or Google Play account settings. Use Restore purchases in the app if a purchase does not show.

Privacy requests

To request deletion of usage information associated with your installation, use Delete my usage data under Settings, Privacy and data, or write to diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

Found an error?

Send the question code shown in its explanation and the source that disagrees. Each cited source opens inside the app.
