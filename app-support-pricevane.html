Microeconomics AP: Pricevane: Support

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Contact: support@equalinformation.com. Last updated 2026-11-25.

Contact

Write to support@equalinformation.com and include your device model, operating system version and the app version (More, About).

Subscription help

Manage or cancel Pricevane Plus in the subscription settings of your App Store or Google Play account. If a purchase does not appear, open the Plus screen in the app and tap Restore purchases.

Reporting a mistake in a question

Send the question number shown in its explanation and what you think is wrong. You can also use Settings, Feedback and suggestions inside the app.

Your data

To delete usage data, use Settings, Privacy and data, Delete my usage data, or write to diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
