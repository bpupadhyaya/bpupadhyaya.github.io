Clockridge Privacy Policy

Effective 9 November 2026. Publisher: Equal Information LLC.

Your hours stay on your phone

Clockridge: Timesheet and Overtime keeps your shifts, breaks, jobs, pay rates and settings only on your device. There is no account, and we cannot see your timesheet. Exports (CSV or a printable summary) are created on your device and go only where you choose to send them with the iOS share sheet.

Ads

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier and approximate location to choose and measure ads. The app first asks whether it may track you across other companies' apps and websites and, where the law requires, shows a Google consent form. You can decline and still use every timesheet feature; ads are then non-personalised. See policies.google.com/technologies/ads. You can remove ads with the monthly Ad-free subscription or by earning ad-free time.

Subscription

The optional monthly Ad-free subscription is handled by Apple; we receive no payment details. Manage or cancel it in your Apple ID subscription settings. Terms of Use: Apple standard licensed application end user agreement.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal data. Clockridge does not collect names, email addresses or contacts and does not request location, camera, microphone, photo or health permission.

Not legal or payroll advice

Overtime rules and earnings shown in the app are estimates to help you keep your own records. They are not legal, tax or payroll advice and do not replace your employer's pay records. Check your pay with your employer or your labour agency.

Children

Clockridge is for working adults and is not directed to children under 13.
