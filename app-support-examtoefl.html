Getting started

Take the short starting check, then follow the daily plan on the Today tab. Practice sets, interactive labs and full mock tests cover Reading, Listening, Writing and Speaking in the 2026 format.

Credits and the ad-free plan

You start with 2 credits. Watch a short video to earn 1 more. Credits open mocks 4 to 6 and advanced labs; practice, reviews, the mistake notebook and every full solution are always free. The ad-free monthly plan includes a 7-day free trial and can be cancelled in your Apple ID or Google Play subscription settings. Use Restore purchases on the plan screen after reinstalling.

Speaking does not work?

Allow the microphone and speech recognition in your phone settings. If the offline voice pack is missing, install the English dictation pack in your keyboard or voice settings, or use the self-check option.
