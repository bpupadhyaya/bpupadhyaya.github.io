Spoken English Hindi: Bolwren privacy policy

Updated 14 November 2026. Publisher: Equal Information LLC. Contact: support@equalinformation.com

Bolwren helps Hindi speakers practise spoken English. It works without an account. Your lessons, answers, mistakes and settings are stored only on your device, in a file you can export or reset in Settings.

What stays on your device

Progress, scores, mistakes, practice days, settings and credits. We do not run a server for the app and we cannot see them.

Microphone and speech recognition

When you choose to practise speaking, Bolwren asks for microphone and speech recognition permission. By default speech is recognised only on your device. On iPhone and iPad Bolwren uses on-device recognition; on Android Bolwren uses the on-device recogniser and can offer to download the offline speech pack. If your device cannot recognise English offline, speaking practice offers self-check (you say the sentence and rate yourself) and no audio is sent.

Online recognition is optional and off by default. Only if you explicitly choose "Use online recognition" for a practice session, the speech you record in that session is sent to the speech service of your phone's maker (Google's speech service on Android, Apple's speech service on iPhone and iPad) so it can be turned into text. Bolwren shows a notice before this happens, never switches to online silently, and returns to offline-only afterwards. Bolwren does not store your audio or the text it returns beyond the current practice step. The speech service providers process that audio under their own privacy policies. On Android versions older than 13, the system speech service is asked to prefer offline recognition, and some phones may still process audio through Google as set in your phone's speech settings. You can use the whole app without ever allowing the microphone.

Advertising

Bolwren is free to use with rewarded ads (watch an ad to earn a credit) and with banner and occasional full-screen ads, or ad-free with a monthly subscription. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect the advertising identifier, device and usage data to serve and measure ads, and, where you consent (or where the law allows), to personalise them. On iPhone and iPad you are asked for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency); in the UK, EEA and some US states you see a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform, which you can reopen in Settings. Your lessons, voice and answers are never given to advertisers. See Google's advertising policy.

Purchases

The monthly ad-free subscription is handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We receive no payment details. Restore purchases is available in the app.

Children

Bolwren is a general-audience learning app and is not directed to children under 13.

Your choices

Export or reset your progress in Settings, or delete the app to remove all data. To ask a question or report a problem write to support@equalinformation.com.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date above.
