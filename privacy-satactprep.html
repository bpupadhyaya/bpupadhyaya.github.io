SAT ACT Math Prep: Satwick: Privacy Policy

Satwick: original SAT-style and ACT-style practice. Effective 14 November 2026. Publisher: Equal Information LLC.

Satwick helps students practise for the SAT and ACT with original SAT-style and ACT-style math, reading and writing questions, step-by-step solutions, timed drills and a mistake notebook. SAT and ACT are trademarks of their respective owners; Satwick is not affiliated with them.

What stays on your device

Satwick works offline and has no account or sign-up. Your answers, mistakes, review schedule, streak, credits and settings are stored on your device only and are never sent to us. You can export your progress to a file you choose, import it again, or erase everything in Settings. Deleting the app removes it too.

Permissions

Satwick does not use the camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location, and asks for no storage permission. Export and import use the system file picker, which only gives Satwick the one file you choose.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob: a banner on the Today, Practice, Topics and Notebook screens, a short full-screen ad after some finished sessions, and optional rewarded videos you can watch to earn practice credits. No ads are shown during a question, a drill or the review of a solution, and none in your first session. On iPhone and iPad the system first asks whether the app may track you (App Tracking Transparency); in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some US states a Google consent message also appears (Google User Messaging Platform). On Android the Google consent message appears where the law requires it. You can decline and keep using Satwick. With your permission, AdMob and its partners may use your device advertising identifier, approximate location derived from your IP address, and ad interaction and diagnostic data to choose, show and measure ads. See Google's advertising policies. Ads are not shown to people with an active ad-free subscription.

Credits and subscription

Satwick is free to use with credits: new users start with 2 credits, a watched rewarded ad earns 1, and one credit opens one full practice session. If no ad can be shown, one free session is granted at most once every 24 hours. The optional ad-free monthly subscription (7-day free trial, then the monthly price shown in the app and store) is handled by Apple or Google Play; we never see your payment details. Credits, purchase status and your settings are stored on your device.

What is collected

Collected by us: nothing. Your answers and progress are never collected. Collected by Google AdMob in the free version: device identifiers (advertising identifier), approximate location from IP address, and ad interaction and diagnostic data, used for third-party advertising and measuring ads.

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information, build profiles about you, run our own analytics, or ask you to create an account. No personal data is sent to us.

Children

Satwick is intended for high-school students and adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and nothing is sent to us in any case.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new effective date.
