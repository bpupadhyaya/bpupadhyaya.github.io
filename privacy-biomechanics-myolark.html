Biomechanics: Myolark Privacy Policy

Biomechanics: Myolark is published by EqualInformation, LLC. This policy was last updated on 2026-11-23.

Summary

Myolark is a study app for a Biomechanics course. This page says what the app stores, what it shares and with whom. There is no registration and no account.

What stays on the device

Your answers, mastery levels, review dates, chosen study path, rewarded-ad unlock times and settings are stored only on your device so the app works offline. They are not uploaded to us, and deleting the app deletes them.

What we never ask for

No location, contacts, photo library, camera, microphone or health data is requested, and nothing about you is sold.

Advertising and rewarded videos

To stay free, the app uses Google AdMob: short ads at natural breaks (never in a timed test), plus optional rewarded videos that unlock Plus features for 24 hours. The order on iOS is the tracking prompt, then Google's consent message where required, then the ad SDK. With your consent Google may use an advertising identifier and ad-interaction details to deliver and measure ads; without it the ads are generic. See Google's advertising data page.

Age

This is an undergraduate-level study app for adults and older students, not for children under 13; we do not knowingly gather data from them.

Monthly subscription

Subscribing is optional: one auto-renewing monthly plan removes ads and unlocks all Plus features, with no free trial. The price is displayed by the store before you confirm, billing is done by Apple or Google, and cancelling is done in your store account. We receive only the active or inactive status. Read the terms of use.

Your choices and data requests

Change tracking permission any time in iOS Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking, and reopen the ad consent message from the app's About screen where available. For privacy questions or requests to access or delete data we might hold about you, write to bpupadhyaya@equalinformation.com; the support page has more help.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
