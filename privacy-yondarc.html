Math Grade 12: Yondarc / Privacy

What Yondarc does with your information

In one paragraph

Yondarc does not ask you to sign in, does not measure how you use it, shows no advertising, and does not send your study records anywhere on its own.

What is saved on the device

Answers, estimated progress, the builds you chose, where you were on screen and your text size are saved so you can pick up later. The erase button in the adult area clears them, and so does removing the app. On iPhone and iPad, a device backup can include the app’s saved data. On Android the app is set to stay out of backups.

A report you decide to send

The adult area can build a short text report: app version, screen and text size, a description of the device and how many questions were answered. It leaves the device only if you share it, and then only to the place you pick. If you send it to us, we read it to find the fault and delete it if you ask.

Payments

Apple or Google take payment under their own privacy terms. The app only asks the store whether a pass is active and never sees card details. On Android, Google Play’s billing component runs inside the app and Google’s terms cover what it handles.

Pages opened from the app

Source pages and these pages load from the web when an adult asks for them. Those sites see an ordinary visit and follow their own policies.

Personal details

The app never asks for a name, birth date, email address or location.

If this changes

Any later version that gathered more would say so here, and in the store listing, before it was released.
