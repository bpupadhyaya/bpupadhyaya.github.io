Yondarc: privacy

The app “Math Grade 12: Yondarc” is published by EqualInformation LLC. This text dates from 27 November 2026.

The short version

Yondarc has no sign-in and no analytics, shows no advertising, and does not send your study records to us.

Kept on your device

Your answers, estimated progress, build choices, screen position and text size are saved on the phone or tablet so you can carry on later. The erase button in the adult area, or deleting the app, removes them. Your device backup settings may copy app data to your own cloud account.

If you send a report

The adult area can show a short diagnostic text: app version, screen and text size, the device description and how many questions were answered. It reaches us only if you share it to us yourself. We use what you send to fix the problem and delete it when you ask.

Buying

Apple or Google handle payment under their own privacy terms. Yondarc asks the store whether a subscription is active and receives no card details. On Android, Google Play’s billing component runs inside the app and Google’s terms cover what it processes.

Web pages you open

Cited sources and these legal pages load from the internet when an adult asks for them. Those websites see an ordinary visit and follow their own policies.

Young people

The app is written for students of about 17 and 18. It never asks for a name, birth date, email address or location.

If this changes

A version that gathered more would be described here, and in the store declarations, before its release.
