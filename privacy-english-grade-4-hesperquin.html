Privacy Policy: English Grade 4: Hesperquin

Effective 28 November 2026. Published by EqualInformation.

This policy covers the iPhone and iPad app English Grade 4: Hesperquin.

What stays on the device

Answers, progress, pinned lessons, settings and anything typed in writing tasks are stored only on the device. We cannot see them. Deleting the app removes them.

What is sent, and only by choice

The app sends nothing automatically. It has no accounts, no analytics and no third-party code, and does not use the camera, microphone, contacts or location.

In the Parent area, behind a grown-up check, an adult may send diagnostics or feedback. The whole message is shown first and is sent from the adult's own mail app. Diagnostics contain the app version, device model, system version, language and region, time zone, screen size, app settings, counts of work done, which subscriptions are active and recent error messages. Feedback contains the topic, the words the adult writes and, if reporting a question, its identifier. Neither includes what the learner typed. We receive the sender's email address with the message.

We use these messages only to fix problems, answer and improve the app. We do not sell them, use them for marketing or use them to follow anyone across apps or websites. They are held in our email system, which a third-party service provider runs for us.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. We receive no payment card details.

Web pages

Buttons for sources and legal pages open the named website inside the app. That website has its own privacy practices and receives the usual connection information, such as an IP address.

Keeping and deleting

We keep messages only as long as needed to deal with them. To ask for a message to be deleted, or for a copy of it, write to support@equalinformation.com from the same address.

Children

We do not ask children for personal information. Messages are meant to be sent by a parent, guardian or teacher.

Changes and contact

Changes appear on this page with a new date. Questions: support@equalinformation.com.
