Support

A Level Philosophy: Elenquor · EqualInformation, LLC · Effective 27 November 2026

Get help

Write to support@equalinformation.com and say which device you use and what happened. In the app, Settings has Send diagnostics (with a preview before anything goes) and Feedback and suggestions.

Common questions

Where is my progress? It is kept on your device. It does not move between devices, and removing the app removes it.

How do the two views differ? Classical follows the order of the AQA subject content; Real-World organises the same study points as disputes you rule on. Both use one record of your progress.

I missed a question. What now? The app links the lesson and lets you retry that question. This repair path is free.

How do I make text larger? Use A- and A+ at the top of the screen (50% to 300%).

Do cited pages work offline? No. They are other websites and need a connection. Lessons and questions work offline.

Subscription

Elenquor Plus is a monthly subscription with no free trial. Cancel any time in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings; Plus stays open until the paid month ends. If you reinstall or change device, use Restore purchases with the same store account.

Your data

To ask for deletion use Settings, Privacy and data, or write to diagnostics@equalinformation.com with the install number shown there.
