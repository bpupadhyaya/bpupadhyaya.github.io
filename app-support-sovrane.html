Help with Comparative Gov AP: Sovrane.

Contact

Email support@equalinformation.com. Tell us your device, system version and what you were doing. Inside the app, More, Privacy and data also has Send diagnostics and Feedback.

Switching views

Use the Syllabus / Real world switch at the top of the main tabs. Both views share your progress, pins and plan.

Text size

Use A- and A+ at the top of any screen, or choose a size under More.

Subscription

To restore a purchase, open More and tap Restore purchases. To cancel, open More and tap Manage subscription, or use your Apple Account or Google Play subscription settings. You keep Plus until the end of the period you paid for.

A source page will not open

Source pages are on other websites and need a connection. The citation stays visible in the app.

Delete my usage data

Open More, Privacy and data and choose Delete my usage data, or email diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
