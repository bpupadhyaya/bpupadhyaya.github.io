Gridlight: Picture Logic - Privacy Policy

Effective 7 November 2026

Gridlight is a puzzle game made by EqualInformation. It has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or location.

What stays on your device

Your progress, stars, settings, daily streak and skill estimate are stored locally on your device. We do not receive them.

Ads

Gridlight is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier if you allow tracking), approximate location derived from your IP address, and ad interaction and diagnostic data, to show and measure ads and prevent fraud. On first launch iOS asks whether the app may track you; you can say no and still play everything, and you will then see non-personalised ads. In the EEA, UK and some US states a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform lets you choose, and you can reopen it any time from Settings, Ad privacy choices. See how Google uses data.

Removing ads

You can watch rewarded videos to earn ad-free time, or subscribe to the monthly ad-free pass. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details. We learn only whether an entitlement is active, on your device, through StoreKit.

Children

Gridlight is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

You can change tracking permission in iOS Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking. You can cancel a subscription in Settings, your name, Subscriptions. Deleting the app removes all local data.
