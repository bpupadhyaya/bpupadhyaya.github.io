Privacy Policy

Math LKG: Pipperoo, by EqualInformation LLC

Last updated 28 November 2026. This page is for the grown-up who looks after the child using Math LKG: Pipperoo.

What the app sends by itself

Nothing. The app sends no information about you, your child or your device on its own. It has no account, no analytics, no tracking, no microphone, no camera, no location and no typing by the child. The only text box is the search on the grown-up Coverage screen; what is typed there is not saved or sent.

Diagnostics and feedback you choose to send

In the grown-up area, “Send diagnostics” and “Send feedback” open a draft in your own mail app. Nothing is sent unless you send it there, and you can read and change it first. If you send it, we receive your email address, which identifies you; for diagnostics, the app name and version, device model, system version, languages, time zone, screen and text size, the app’s settings, whether each plan is held, whether the store answered, and error messages from the current run; for feedback, what you write, with the app name and version. The draft holds nothing your child did. We use it only to answer you, fix problems and keep the app working. It is not used for tracking and is not sold. It is kept in our email system (Google Workspace) for as long as we need it for those purposes. Ask us to delete it at any time.

What stays on your device

The app keeps a record of your child’s activities and answers, your notes in the grown-up area and your settings in one file on the device. It uses them to choose the next activity. The file is left out of device backups. You can erase it with “Erase all progress” in the grown-up area, or by removing the app.

Plans

If a grown-up starts, restores or manages a monthly plan, the App Store handles it under Apple’s own privacy policy. The app is told by the App Store whether a plan is held. We do not receive your name or payment details through the app. Apple gives developers sales reports and figures that do not tell us who your child is.

Links to outside pages

The grown-up area links to this policy, our terms, our support page and nine public documents we cite or map. A page opens only when a grown-up presses its link, in a window inside the app with Back and Close. That window moves only within the page shown and between the pages named here and the Apple standard licence agreement that our terms link to. The app keeps no cookies from them and does not run their scripts. As with any website, the server that delivers a page receives an ordinary web request, which includes your internet address. Our pages are hosted by GitHub Pages; the cited documents are on their publishers’ own sites, under their own policies.

The voice

Spoken instructions use the voice already on your device. Nothing is recorded.

If you write to us

If you email us in any other way, we receive your email address and what you write, and use them only to answer you. Please do not send your child’s name or photographs.

Changes and contact

If a later version collects anything more, this page and the App Store privacy details will be changed first. Questions: support@equalinformation.com.
