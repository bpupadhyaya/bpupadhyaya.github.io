Support PlainRescue

Help us put calm, cited, life-saving first-aid guidance in everyone's pocket — offline and free.

No signal, no panic — PlainRescue gives calm, step-by-step, cited first-aid guidance for the moments that matter most, and it works entirely offline.

PlainRescue is an offline-first first-aid and emergency-response app for iOS and Android, built and maintained by EqualInformation, LLC. It guides you through dozens of life-threatening situations — CPR, choking, severe bleeding, anaphylaxis, stroke, heart attack, and many more — one calm step at a time, with a CPR metronome, recheck timers, and read-aloud voice guidance so you can keep your hands on the person.

Every feature is designed with privacy at its core — PlainRescue is 100% on-device with no accounts, no servers, no cloud sync, and no tracking, and the whole app works fully offline. PlainRescue is coming soon to the App Store and Google Play, and it is free for everyone. Your support helps us keep it free, expand and translate the protocols, and keep every step backed by an authoritative source.

Why PlainRescue Exists

In an emergency, the first few minutes decide the outcome — and they often happen where there is no signal, no time to search, and too much panic to think. PlainRescue is built for exactly that moment: fast, calm, one-handed, dark-readable, and voice-guided.

Trust matters most when a life is on the line. Every step in every protocol is backed by at least one citation from an authoritative body — the American Heart Association, American Red Cross, European Resuscitation Council, WHO, ILCOR, CDC, St John Ambulance, and the NHS — and you can tap any citation to read the source.

PlainRescue is guidance, not diagnosis. Every flow reinforces calling your local emergency services — the goal is to empower you to act and to escalate, never to replace professional help.

Information shared is power shared. Life-saving knowledge should reach everyone, in their own language, for free — that is why PlainRescue is part of the EqualInformation portfolio.

Disclaimer: PlainRescue provides first-aid information for educational purposes only and is guidance, not diagnosis. It is not intended to replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. If you think you may have a medical emergency, contact your local emergency services immediately.
