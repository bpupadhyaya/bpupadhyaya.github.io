Enamelume: Preventive Dentistry - Privacy Policy

Equal Information LLC. Last updated 9 November 2026.

What stays on your device

Enamelume is an offline education app. Your quiz scores, daily routine ticks and text-size choice are saved only on your device. We do not run accounts and we cannot see this data. The caries-risk checklist is never stored or sent.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. With your permission (Apple's tracking prompt and, where required, Google's consent form) AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier and ad interaction data to show relevant ads and measure them. If you decline, you still see ads, but they are not personalised. The monthly ad-free subscription removes ads.

Purchases

The ad-free subscription is handled by Apple. We receive no payment details.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email, location, health information, contacts, photos or microphone data. Enamelume does not use or ask for any of these.

Children

Enamelume is for adults, students and parents or carers. It is not directed to children under 13. The "Child" mouth view shows a baby-teeth model for parents and carers to learn from.

Contact

Questions or error reports: support@equalinformation.com. See also the support page.

Education only. Enamelume is not dental or medical advice and does not diagnose or treat anything. See a dentist for your own care.
