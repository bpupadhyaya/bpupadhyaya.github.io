A Level Maths: Surdmere privacy policy

Surdmere has no account. Your progress, settings and unlocks are stored only on your device. We do not collect your answers.

The app does not track you, does not collect your data and contains no third-party tracking code.

Subscriptions are handled by Apple or Google; we only learn whether a subscription is active. Emails you send to support are used only to answer you.
