Surdmere privacy policy

Surdmere has no account. Your progress, settings and unlocks are stored only on your device. We do not collect your answers.

The free app shows ads from Google AdMob. Before ads, the app asks for consent where required (App Tracking Transparency on iOS and a consent form in regions that need one). Ads for users under 18 are non-personalised. AdMob may process device identifiers and usage data under Google's policy.

Subscriptions are handled by Apple or Google; we only learn whether a subscription is active. Emails you send to support are used only to answer you.
