GCSE German Prep: Lernmoor: Privacy Policy

Published by EqualInformation, LLC. Last updated 2026-11-27.

1. Introduction and scope

This Privacy Policy explains how EqualInformation, LLC (we, us) handles information in connection with GCSE German Prep: Lernmoor, version 1.0 and later (the app). It applies to the app only. The app has no account, no sign-in and no advertising.

2. Information we collect

A. Information you choose to provide. A diagnostic report that you send from Settings, Privacy and data, Send diagnostics: the complete report is displayed before anything leaves your device and is sent only when you press Send. Feedback and suggestions that you submit (category, message, optional role, level, source link and rating) and, only if you tick the contact option, an email address for a reply.

B. Diagnostic and app-usage information, collected with your agreement or as permitted by applicable law while the optional usage setting is on: app version and build; device model; operating system and version; processor, memory and display size; language, region and time zone; accessibility and display settings; performance and memory indicators; crash and error information; a randomly generated app identifier; and how the features of the app are used. For learning features this includes lessons viewed, questions attempted, whether answers are correct, time taken, hints and explanations opened, topic-level progress estimates, study sessions, practice, listening, dictation, translation, writing and speaking-practice activity (but never the recording itself), mock-test activity, subscription-screen interactions and the adaptive-learning decisions made by the app and their outcomes. Short answers that you type into answer fields are included, shortened and automatically cleaned of recognisable personal identifiers.

C. Speaking practice. When you record yourself in the speaking studio, the audio is captured, played back and deleted on your device. It is not uploaded and is not collected by us. The app does not perform speech recognition on a server.

D. Information we do not collect: your name, contacts, precise location, photographs, files, advertising identifiers, longer text you write in writing tasks or notes, or your recordings.

3. How we use information

To operate, maintain, secure and support the app; to diagnose problems; to provide and personalise the learning experience; to understand how the app is used; for research and development; for analytics and improvement of our apps and educational content; to read and act on feedback; and to comply with legal obligations.

4. Your choices

When you first open the app a notice explains these practices and offers a separate usage switch. The switch is off by default in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and wherever your region cannot be determined, and you may change it at any time under Settings, Privacy and data. Diagnostics and feedback are sent only when you press Send. To ask for deletion, use Delete my usage data in Settings, Privacy and data, or email diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

5. Storage and service providers

Information is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections to a web application operated by EqualInformation, LLC that records it in our Google Workspace services (Google Sheets and Drive) and notification email. For a copy installed from the App Store or Google Play, we can be emailed the details of German practice and usage batches, the acceptance, diagnostics, crash data, feedback and deletion requests. Delivery is to our mailboxes and may continue by forwarding to another of them. Purchases are processed by Apple or Google; we do not receive your payment details.

6. Retention

Usage, learning and diagnostic information is kept while it remains useful for the purposes above. Our handling of a deletion request is manual, with no automatic erasing at the server. A mailed copy of a record remains until it is separately deleted by hand.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures, including encryption in transit. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. International users and legal bases

Where a legal basis is required we rely on your agreement (the usage switch and your decision to send diagnostics or feedback) and on our legitimate interest in operating and improving the app. Information may be processed in the United States.

9. Children

We design our apps to collect only the information described in this policy. If you believe information has been provided to us that you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the version of the first-run notice changes and the notice is shown again.

11. Contact

EqualInformation, LLC. Questions: support@equalinformation.com. Deletion and data requests: diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
