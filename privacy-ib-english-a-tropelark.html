IB English A Prep: Tropelark: Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Contact: support@equalinformation.com. Last updated 2026-11-25.

1. Introduction and scope

This policy explains how EqualInformation, LLC (we, us) handles information in connection with IB English A Prep: Tropelark, version 1.0 and later (the app). It covers the app only. There is no account. The lessons, questions, passages and studios are stored in the app and work without a network connection.

2. Information we collect

A. Information you choose to provide. Diagnostic reports: under Settings, Privacy and data, Send diagnostics shows the full content of the report before anything is sent, and it is sent only when you tap Send. Feedback and suggestions: a category, your message, an optional role and rating, and, only if you choose to give one, an email address so we can reply.

B. Diagnostic and app-usage information, collected with your agreement or as permitted by applicable law while the optional usage setting is on: app version and build; device model; operating system and version; processor and memory details; screen size; language, region and time zone; accessibility and display settings; performance, memory and crash information; a random app identifier; and how the learning features are used, namely lessons viewed, questions attempted, whether answers are correct, time taken, progress estimates, the decisions the app makes about what to study next and their outcomes, activity in the studios and timed practice, and use of the purchase screen. Search terms you type in the glossary are included, shortened and automatically cleaned of recognisable email addresses, telephone numbers, links and card or identity numbers.

When you accept the first-run notice, a short acceptance record is always sent: a consent identifier, the time, the app, platform, version and build, the notice version and a hash of its text, the state of the usage setting, the random app identifier, and your country, language region and time zone. Where the usage setting is off, nothing else is sent unless you choose to send diagnostics or feedback.

C. We do not collect your name, contacts, precise or approximate location, photographs, files, advertising identifiers, or the text you write in the app, such as essay drafts, oral outlines, annotations and notes, which stay on your device.

3. How we use information

To operate, maintain, secure and support the app; to diagnose problems; to provide and personalise the learning experience; to understand how the app is used; for research and development; for analytics and to improve our apps and educational content; to read and act on feedback; and to comply with legal obligations.

4. Your choices

On first opening, a notice explains these practices and offers a separate usage switch. It starts off in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and wherever your region is unknown, until you turn it on; elsewhere it starts on. You can change it at any time under Settings, Privacy and data. Diagnostics and feedback are sent only when you tap Send. To request deletion of usage data use Delete my usage data in the same place, or email diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

5. Storage and service providers

Information travels over encrypted (HTTPS) connections to a web application run by EqualInformation, LLC that records it in our Google Workspace services (Google Sheets and Drive); diagnostic reports and acceptance notices are also sent to us by email. Purchases are processed by Apple or Google under their own terms; we do not receive your payment details.

6. Retention

Usage, learning and diagnostic information is kept for as long as it remains useful for the purposes above; deletion requests are handled on request.

7. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures, including encryption in transit. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. International users and legal bases

Where a legal basis is required we rely on your agreement (the usage switch, and your decision to send diagnostics or feedback) and on our legitimate interest in operating and improving the app. Information may be processed in the United States.

9. Children

We design our apps to collect only the information described in this policy. If you believe information has been provided to us that you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the version of the first-run notice changes and the notice is shown again.

EqualInformation, LLC. Questions: support@equalinformation.com. Deletion and data requests: diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
