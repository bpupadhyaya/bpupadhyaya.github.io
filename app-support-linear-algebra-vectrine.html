Linear Algebra: Vectrine Support

Linear Algebra: Vectrine is an undergraduate linear algebra course. Published by EqualInformation, LLC.

Restore a purchase

Reinstalled the app or changed device? Sign in to the same Apple Account (or Google account on Android), open About and tap Restore Purchases. No new charge is made.

Manage or cancel the subscription

The optional monthly ad-free subscription is managed by the store, not by us. On iOS: Settings, your name, Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play, Profile, Payments and subscriptions. Cancel at least a day before renewal to avoid the next charge. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google.

Get help

Email bpupadhyaya@equalinformation.com with your device model, system version and a short description of the problem. We usually reply within a few days. Privacy questions and data requests go to the same address.

Report a content error

Found a wrong answer, a typo or an unclear explanation? Email bpupadhyaya@equalinformation.com with the topic and question text (a screenshot helps) and, if you can, the source you checked. We review every report and fix confirmed mistakes in an update.

Common questions

How does the adaptive path work? On the Personalised path, Vectrine tracks your mastery topic by topic, notices the pattern behind a wrong answer and chooses your next question and review from that, all on your device. Prefer a fixed order? Switch to the Traditional syllabus path at any time without losing progress.

What do the interactive labs do? They let you change parameters, predict what will happen and then see the result. Vectrine includes the grid morpher, eigenvector explorer, row-reduction stepper and least-squares labs.

Does Vectrine need an internet connection? No. Lessons, questions, worked solutions, mock exams and the grid morpher, eigenvector explorer, row-reduction stepper and least-squares labs all run offline. A connection is only needed to show ads and to buy or restore the subscription.

Are ads shown during exams? No. Ads appear only at natural breaks, never during timed tests or mock exams. Worked solutions are free and never hidden behind ads.

Where do the further-reading links lead? To the original sources (for example MIT OpenCourseWare or OpenStax), which are cited and linked, not copied. Their licences remain with their owners.

Is this a replacement for my course? No. Vectrine is a practice and study aid that goes alongside a course, textbook or instructor; it does not replace them.
