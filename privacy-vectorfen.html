Vectorfen: AP Physics C Mech - Privacy Policy

Short version: this app has no account and no servers of ours. Your study progress stays on your device.

What we store: your answers, plan, notebook, credits and settings, in a file in the app's private storage. Delete the app to erase it.

Ads: the free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers and approximate usage data to serve and measure ads. Where required, you will see Google's consent form and can reopen it from Settings. Subscribers see no ads.

On iPhone and iPad we ask for tracking permission before any ad request; you can say no and every study feature still works. Purchases are handled by Apple or Google.

We never see your payment details.

Children: this app is meant for students aged 13 and over.

Web pages: when you tap a reference, the page opens from its publisher and is governed by that publisher's policy.

Questions: see the Support page on our website.
