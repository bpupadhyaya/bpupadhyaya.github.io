Mechanical Behavior: Yieldora Support

Mechanical Behavior: Yieldora is a Mechanical Behavior course study app. Published by EqualInformation, LLC.

Restore a purchase

Reinstalled the app or changed device? Sign in with the same Apple Account (or Google account on Android), open About and tap Restore Purchases. No new charge is made.

Manage or cancel the subscription

The optional monthly subscription is managed by the store, not by us. On iOS: Settings, your name, Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play, Profile, Payments and subscriptions. Cancel at least a day before renewal to avoid the next charge. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google.

Get help

Email bpupadhyaya@equalinformation.com with your device model, system version and a short description of the problem. We usually reply within a few days. Privacy questions and data requests go to the same address.

Report a content error

Found a wrong answer, a typo or an unclear explanation? Email bpupadhyaya@equalinformation.com with the topic and question text (a screenshot helps) and, if you can, the source you checked. Confirmed mistakes are fixed in an update.

Common questions

How does the adaptive path work? The adaptive path tracks what you know topic by topic and chooses what to practise or review next from your mistakes, using only data on your device. You can move to the course-order path at any time and keep your progress.

Can I choose how long a quiz is and how hard? You set the quiz length and the level yourself. Timed tests and mock exams never show ads while they run.

What are Plus features and how do I unlock them? Plus features are the extra labs, levels, modes or tools that are marked with a lock in the app; the free core course stays free. You can unlock them for 24 hours by watching an optional rewarded video, or remove ads and unlock all of them with the monthly subscription. Watching ads is never required to study the free core course.

Does the app need an internet connection? Studying works offline. You only need to be online for ads and rewarded videos, and to buy or restore the subscription.

Are ads shown during exams? Never during a timed test or mock exam. Ads come at natural breaks only, and worked solutions are free of ads.

Where do the further-reading links lead? To the original sources, which are cited and linked, not copied. Their licences stay with their owners.

Is this a replacement for my course? No, Yieldora is a companion for study and practice, not a substitute for a class or instructor.
