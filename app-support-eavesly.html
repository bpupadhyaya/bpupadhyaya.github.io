Get help

Email support@equalinformation.com with your iOS version and what you were doing. We reply within a few days.

Common questions

Where is my data? Only on your device. Eavesly has no account. Back up your device to keep your records safe when you change phones.

Why this schedule? Each job names its public source (for example the U.S. Department of Energy, EPA, NFPA, CSIA). Your equipment's manual always takes priority over a general interval.

Scans are not reading correctly. Use good light, flatten the paper, and fill the frame. You can correct the model, serial and warranty before saving.

Restore or cancel the ad-free subscription. Settings, Remove ads, Restore purchase. To cancel, open iOS Settings, your name, Subscriptions, Eavesly.

Reminders do not appear. Turn on Settings, Reminders in Eavesly and allow notifications for Eavesly in iOS Settings.
