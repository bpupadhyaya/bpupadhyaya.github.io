Contact us

Write to support@equalinformation.com. Grown-ups, please leave out the learner’s name and school.

What is in the app

The 33 grade 8 standards of the US Common Core, each at its smallest published part, shown as a drawing register and as a works yard. The England and IB tags are for comparison only; the IB ones are unchecked.

Report a mistake

Send the drawing number (such as B-02) or the name of the works order, and say what looks wrong.

Getting into the grown-up side

Tap Grown-ups and type the five numbers shown, last one first. This stops stray taps; it does not check age.

Plans and billing

Classical Plus and Real-World Plus are billed monthly and separately by your app store. Stop either one in your store account; the paid month still runs. On a new device, use Restore purchases in the Grown-ups area. Ask that store about money back.

Where progress lives

Only on the device. Erasing it, or deleting the app, removes it for good, and we have no copy to restore.
