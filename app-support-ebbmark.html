Ebbmark support

Ebbmark is a study app for iPhone and iPad that schedules reviews of your own notes. Write to support@equalinformation.com and include your device and iOS version.

How do I make slips from my notes?

Open Make, choose Notes to slips, paste text and tap Find slips. Untick any you do not want, pick a shelf and tap Add.

How do I import from another study app?

Export your material as CSV or plain text (tab separated) from the other app, then use Make, Import a file. Header rows, tag and shelf columns and fill-the-gap markers such as {{c1::word}} are recognised. Review history is not carried over; imported slips start as new.

How do I export my slips?

More, Your data, then Export as CSV or Export as plain-text notes.

Why does the app say it is tuning my gaps?

After about 60 reviews spread over several days, Ebbmark compares how often you actually remembered with what its model predicted and stretches or shortens future gaps to fit you. You can also change your target recall in Outlook.

Ads, ad-free and restoring

Free use is supported by ads. In More, tap Go ad-free to subscribe monthly, or earn ad-free time by watching rewarded videos. Subscribed on another device or reinstalled? Open Go ad-free and tap Restore purchases. Cancel any time in iOS Settings, your name, Subscriptions.

Where is my data?

Only on your device. There is no account, so deleting the app deletes the data; use an export first if you want a copy. Read the privacy policy.
