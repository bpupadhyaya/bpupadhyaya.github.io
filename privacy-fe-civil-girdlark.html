Privacy policy

FE Civil Prep: Girdlark

Last updated 23 November 2026.

Girdlark works fully offline and needs no account. What you study, your answers, scores, settings and text size are kept only on your device. We do not receive them, and the app does not collect personal information, track you, or share data with other companies.

If you buy Girdlark Plus, the purchase is handled by Apple or Google. We see only whether a subscription is active on your device, never your payment details. Opening a source link in the reader sheet loads that website, which has its own privacy policy.

Deleting the app deletes your study data from your device. Questions about privacy: support@equalinformation.com.
