Privacy Policy

FE Civil Prep: Girdlark

Effective 27 November 2026. EqualInformation, LLC (“we”) publishes FE Civil Prep: Girdlark (the “app”). This policy explains the information the app handles, how it is used and the choices available to you. The app works offline and does not require an account.

1. Information we collect

Information you choose to provide. Diagnostic reports you send and any note you add; feedback and suggestions you submit (category, message, optional role, level context, source link and rating) and, only if you tick the contact option, an email address for a reply.

Diagnostic and app-usage information collected with your agreement or as permitted by applicable law. Device and operating-system information (model, operating-system version, processor, memory, display size, language, region and time zone, accessibility and display settings), app version, performance and memory indicators, crash and error information, and a randomly generated app identifier. How the app's features are used and perform: lessons viewed, practice and mock-exam questions attempted, whether answers are correct, time taken, topic-level progress estimates, the adaptive decisions the app makes and their outcomes, activity in labs and builds, text-size and view settings, and purchase-screen interactions. Typed answers and search terms are not collected.

Information we do not collect. Your name, contacts, precise location, photographs, files, advertising identifiers, or other text you enter in the app such as notes.

2. How we use information

To operate, maintain, secure and support the app; to diagnose problems; to provide and personalise your study path; to understand how the app is used; for research and development; for analytics and improvement of our apps and educational content; to read and act on feedback; and to comply with legal obligations.

3. Your choices

The first-run notice explains the optional usage-information setting, which you can change at any time under Me, Settings, Privacy and data. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and where the region cannot be determined, the setting is off until you turn it on. Diagnostics and feedback are sent only when you choose to send them. To request deletion of your usage information, use “Delete my usage data” in the app or write to diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

4. Storage and service providers

Information is stored in our Google Workspace services (Google Sheets and Drive) and notification email, and is processed by service providers acting on our behalf. From App Store and Google Play installs, civil engineering practice batches, usage records, acceptance of the notice, crash and diagnostic reports, feedback and deletion requests can be emailed with their contents to mailboxes belonging to us, including any forwarding address of our own. It is transmitted over encrypted connections. Subscriptions are processed by Apple or Google; we do not receive your payment details. Opening a cited source link in the reader sheet loads that website, which has its own privacy policy.

5. Retention

Usage and learning information is kept while it is useful for the purposes above; deletion requests are dealt with manually. Pressing the control in the app does not erase records, nothing expires by itself, and copies in our mail stay unless removed by hand.

6. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. International users and legal bases

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we rely on your consent or on our legitimate interests in operating and improving the app. Information may be processed in the United States.

8. Children

We design our apps to collect only the information described in this policy. If you believe information has been provided to us that you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

9. Changes to this policy

If we make a material change, the notice shown when you first open the app changes version and is shown again.

10. Contact

EqualInformation, LLC. General: support@equalinformation.com. Deletion and data requests: diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
