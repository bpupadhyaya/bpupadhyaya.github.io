Chemical Process Design: Vexal Support

Chemical Process Design: Vexal is a Chemical Process Design course study app. Published by EqualInformation, LLC.

Restore a purchase

After a reinstall or a new device, use the same Apple Account (or Google account on Android), open About and choose Restore Purchases. You are not charged again.

Manage or cancel the subscription

The optional monthly subscription is managed by the store, not by us. On iOS: Settings, your name, Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play, Profile, Payments and subscriptions. Cancel at least a day before renewal to avoid the next charge. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google.

Get help

Email bpupadhyaya@equalinformation.com with your device model, system version and a short description of the problem. We usually reply within a few days. Privacy questions and data requests go to the same address.

Report a content error

Found a wrong answer, a typo or an unclear explanation? Email bpupadhyaya@equalinformation.com with the topic and question text (a screenshot helps) and, if you can, the source you checked. Confirmed mistakes are fixed in an update.

Common questions

How does the adaptive path work? On the personalised path Vexal follows your mastery topic by topic, notices the pattern behind wrong answers and picks your next question and review from that, all on your device. Prefer a fixed order? Switch to the traditional syllabus path any time without losing progress.

Can I choose how long a quiz is and how hard? Yes, you set the quiz length and the level yourself.

What are Plus features and how do I unlock them? Plus features are the extra labs, levels, modes or tools that are marked with a lock in the app; the free core course stays free. The monthly subscription unlocks all of them.

Does the app need an internet connection? Studying works offline.

Where do the further-reading links lead? To the sources the lesson cites. We link to them rather than copy them, so their licences remain with their owners.

Is this a replacement for my course? No. Vexal goes alongside a course, textbook or instructor; it does not replace them.
