Privacy Policy — PlainHealth

Effective Date: April 24, 2026

  • Home
  • /
  • Privacy Policy
  • /
  • PlainHealth

1 Who We Are

PlainHealth is provided by EqualInformation, LLC.

If you have privacy questions or requests, you may contact us at:
Email: bpupadhyaya@equalinformation.com
Contact page: equalinformation.weebly.com/contact.html | bpupadhyaya.github.io#contact

2 Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the PlainHealth app (iOS bundle identifier: com.equalinformation.plainhealth.app; Android package name: com.plainhealth.app). PlainHealth is available on iOS (App Store) and Android (Google Play).

3 Information PlainHealth May Access or Store

PlainHealth is a health education app. Depending on how you use the app, PlainHealth may store information on your device, including:

  • Bookmarked lab tests, medications, and conditions
  • Health tracker entries (vitals, weight, blood sugar, etc.)
  • My Medications personal list
  • Family health history entries
  • In-app purchase status (premium unlock state)
  • App settings and preferences (onboarding completion)

PlainHealth does not collect or transmit any of this data. All information remains on your device.

4 How We Use Information

PlainHealth uses locally stored information to:

  • Display your bookmarked health topics
  • Show your health tracking history
  • Maintain your personal medication list
  • Display appropriate content based on purchase status (free vs premium)

5 Local-First Design

PlainHealth does not collect, transmit, or store any user data on servers. All health content is bundled within the app. There is no cloud sync, no accounts, and no user tracking. The app operates 100% offline.

6 No Analytics or Tracking

PlainHealth does not use any analytics SDKs, crash reporters, advertising SDKs, or tracking tools. No data is sent to third-party analytics services.

7 App Store and Google Play Billing

PlainHealth may use Apple App Store In-App Purchase (on iOS) or Google Play Billing (on Android) for eligible in-app purchase functionality. Apple or Google may process payment and transaction information under their own respective policies. PlainHealth may receive limited purchase-status or product information needed to unlock purchased content on your device. EqualInformation, LLC does not receive your full payment card number through PlainHealth.

8 Health Content

All health content in PlainHealth is bundled locally within the app. No external API calls are made for content delivery. The content provided is for educational purposes only and is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always seek the advice of your physician or other qualified health provider with any questions regarding a medical condition.

9 Permissions

PlainHealth may request the following device permissions:

  • Billing (Android): Used only for in-app purchase verification

PlainHealth does not require internet, camera, microphone, location, contacts, or other sensitive permissions for its core functionality.

10 Sharing and Sale of Data

EqualInformation, LLC does not sell personal or sensitive user data. We do not share users' data with data brokers or for advertising purposes. The company does not intentionally transmit personal or sensitive PlainHealth user data to its own servers or unrelated third parties.

11 Data Retention and Deletion

Purchase-related unlock state may be restorable through App Store or Google Play for eligible purchases. Uninstalling PlainHealth removes all locally stored data from your device, including bookmarks, health tracker entries, medication lists, and family history.

12 Security

PlainHealth relies on iOS and Android platform protections and app sandboxing as part of its local-first design. However, no method of storage or electronic transmission is completely secure, and the company cannot guarantee absolute security.

13 Your Choices

You may choose to purchase premium content or not. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data from your device.

14 Children's Privacy

PlainHealth is designed for adults seeking health education. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe information has been provided inappropriately, please contact the company.

15 Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the updated version on this page and revise the Effective Date above.
