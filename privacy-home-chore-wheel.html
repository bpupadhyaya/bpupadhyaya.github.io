Chorewick Privacy Policy

App: Chorewick: Family Chore Wheel (iPhone and iPad). Publisher: Equal Information LLC. Last updated: 9 November 2026.

Short version: your family list, chores, points and history stay on your device. We run no accounts and no servers for the app. The only data leaving your device is what Google AdMob needs to show ads, and you control that.

What stays on your device

Names you type for family members, chores, prizes, points, completed jobs and your text-size choice are stored in the app's private storage on your device. We cannot see them, and they are not uploaded. Deleting the app deletes them.

Ads

Chorewick is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. The app is meant to be operated by parents and carers. To show ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device and app information and ad interactions. Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some US states), a Google consent message asks for your choice first. On iPhone and iPad the system asks whether the app may track you; if you choose Ask App Not to Track, ads can still appear but are not personalised across other apps and sites. Learn more in how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Ad-free subscription

The optional monthly subscription is handled entirely by Apple through your Apple Account. We receive no payment details and no personal information. Apple's own terms and privacy policy apply to purchases. Watching rewarded videos to earn ad-free time keeps only a small counter on your device.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no sign-up.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting service of our own.
  • No sale of personal data by us.
  • No contact lists, photos, location or microphone access.

Children

Chorewick is for parents and carers. It is not directed to children under 13 and children are not meant to use the app on their own. Parents choose what names and chores are stored.

Your choices

  • Change tracking permission in Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking.
  • Reset the family data in the app (More, Reset) or delete the app.
  • Cancel the subscription in your Apple Account settings.

Contact

Questions: Chorewick support. We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
