Privacy Policy

French Language AP: Causelin · EqualInformation, LLC · 26 November 2026

1. Introduction and scope

EqualInformation, LLC (“we”, “us”) publishes French Language AP: Causelin (“the app”) for iPhone, iPad and Android. This policy, effective 26 November 2026, explains what information the app handles. The app has no account and no sign-in. The course is stored in the app, and your progress, essays, notes, plans and recordings are stored on your device.

2. Information you choose to give us

Diagnostic report. Under More, Privacy and data, Send diagnostics shows you the complete report before anything leaves your device. It is sent only when you press Send, together with any note you add.

Feedback and suggestions. If you send them, we receive the category, your message (automatically cleaned of recognisable email addresses, telephone numbers and links) and anything optional you add, such as a role, a source link or a rating. We receive an email address only if you tick the option to be contacted, and use it only to reply.

3. Diagnostic and app-usage information

While the optional usage setting is on, the app collects diagnostic and app-usage information that is permissible under applicable law: app version and build; device model, operating system, processor, memory and display characteristics; language, region and time zone; text-size and accessibility settings; network type; whether the free course or Causelin Plus is in use; a randomly generated app identifier; session timing; crash, error and performance information; the screens, lessons, question sets, mock papers, labs and journeys you use; the questions you attempt, whether your answers are correct and how long you take; the app’s progress estimate for each objective; the suggestions the adaptive engine makes and what follows them; and interactions with the subscription screen.

When you accept the notice shown at first launch, a short acceptance record is sent: a consent identifier, the time, the app, platform, version and build, the notice version, the state of the usage setting, the random app identifier, and your country, locale and time zone. With the usage setting on, this record also includes the device information described above.

4. Speaking, writing and listening practice

Your voice recordings are made, played back and deleted on your device. They are not uploaded and we do not receive them. A recording is removed when you leave the task or make a new one. The text of your essays, journey drafts, presentation outlines and searches inside the app is not collected.

Listening scripts are read aloud by the speech service installed on your device, which is provided by the device or software maker and follows that provider’s own privacy practices.

5. What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, contacts, precise location, photographs, files or advertising identifiers.

6. How we use information

To operate, maintain, secure and support the app; to diagnose problems; to provide and personalise the learning experience; to understand how the app is used; for research and development; for analytics and to improve our apps and educational content; to read and act on feedback; and to comply with legal obligations.

7. Your choices

The first-launch notice offers a separate usage switch. It starts off in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and where your region cannot be determined; elsewhere it starts on. You can change it at any time under More, Privacy and data. Diagnostics and feedback are sent only when you press Send. The microphone permission is asked for only when you first record and can be withdrawn in your device settings. To ask for deletion of your usage data, use Delete my usage data in the app or write to diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

8. Storage and service providers

Information travels over encrypted (HTTPS) connections to a web application we operate, which records it in our Google Workspace services (Google Sheets and Drive). Diagnostic reports and acceptance notices are also delivered to us by email. Google acts as our service provider. Subscription payments are handled by Apple or Google; we do not receive your payment details. Pages you open from a citation or link belong to their publishers and follow their own privacy practices.

9. Retention and security

We keep usage, learning and diagnostic information for as long as it remains useful for the purposes above and handle deletion requests when you make them. We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, including encryption in transit; no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

10. International users and legal bases

Where a legal basis is required, we rely on your agreement (the usage switch, and your decision to send diagnostics or feedback) and on our legitimate interest in running and improving the app. Information may be processed in the United States.

11. Children

We design our apps to collect only the information described in this policy. If you believe information has been provided to us that you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

12. Changes and contact

If this policy changes materially, the version of the first-launch notice changes and the notice is shown again. Questions: support@equalinformation.com. Deletion and data requests: diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
