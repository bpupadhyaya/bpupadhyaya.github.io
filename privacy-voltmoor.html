Voltmoor: AP Physics 2 - Privacy Policy

Voltmoor has no account and does not collect your name, email or contact details. Your study record (answers, plan, notebook, credits) is stored only on your device.

Advertising: the free version shows ads supplied by Google AdMob. On iOS the app asks for tracking permission; where required it shows Google's consent form before ads load. You can decline and still study. The ad-free subscription removes ads.

Purchases are handled by the App Store or Google Play; we receive no payment details.

The app works offline. It uses the network only for ads, subscription checks and when you open a reference page.
