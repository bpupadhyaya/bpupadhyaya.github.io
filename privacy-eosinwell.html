Eosinwell Privacy Policy

Effective 7 November 2026. Publisher: Equal Information LLC.

What Eosinwell stores

Eosinwell is a histology study app. Your study record (slides viewed, practical results, streak, mastery) is stored only on your device. We do not run accounts and we cannot see this record.

Ads

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier and approximate location to select and measure ads. On iOS the app asks permission before tracking across other companies' apps and websites, and, where required by law, shows a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) so you can choose how ad data is used. You may decline and still use the app; ads will then be non-personalised. Google describes its practices at policies.google.com/technologies/ads. You can remove ads with the Ad-Free subscription or by earning ad-free time.

Subscription

Payments for the optional monthly Ad-Free subscription are handled entirely by Apple. We receive no payment details. Manage or cancel in your Apple ID subscription settings.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal data, do not collect names, emails or contacts, and do not request camera, microphone, photo, health or location permissions.

Children

Eosinwell is a general education app and is not directed to children under 13.

Educational use only

Eosinwell is a study aid. Its images are simulated teaching renderings, not patient material. It is not for diagnosis, treatment or clinical decisions.
