Privacy policy

Bytelet has no account and no sign-in. Your study progress, scores and settings are stored only on your device.

Ads. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Where required, you are asked for consent before ads are requested. Google may collect device identifiers and advertising data to serve and measure ads, as described in Google's privacy policy. You can change your choice at any time in More, under privacy choices, or in your device settings. Subscribers do not see interstitial ads.

Purchases. Subscriptions are handled by Apple or Google. We do not receive your payment details.

Children. The app is aimed at high school students and older. It is not directed to children under 13.
