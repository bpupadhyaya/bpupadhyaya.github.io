RTO LL Test India: Roadlark: Support

Roadlark is a learner's licence test practice app for iPhone, iPad and Android. Roadlark helps you practise for the learner's licence (LL) test in India with road signs, traffic rules and timed mock tests for cars and two-wheelers, in English and Hindi. No account is needed.

Common questions

  • Do I need an account or internet? No. Roadlark works offline and has no account.
  • Is this the official test? No. Roadlark is an independent practice app. The real test is taken through your state transport department's process.
  • Is my state's test format included? No. The app has one clearly labelled general practice format (20 questions, 12 to pass, 20 minutes). State formats are not verified and are not included.
  • Which languages are available? English and Hindi. The Hindi text is machine-drafted and may contain mistakes.
  • What are credits? You start with 2 credits. A mock test uses 1 credit; practice, topic quizzes and sign flashcards are free. Watch a short ad to earn another, or go ad-free.
  • How do I remove ads? Open the purchase screen in Roadlark (Settings or More, or the ad-free prompt) and choose the ad-free monthly subscription or the one-time Unlock. Both remove ads and credit limits.
  • How do I restore a purchase? Open Settings (or the purchase screen) and tap Restore purchases, using the same Apple Account or Google account you bought with. This is needed after reinstalling or changing device.
  • How do I cancel the subscription? iPhone and iPad: Settings, your name, Subscriptions. Android: Google Play, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel it.
  • Text too small? Use A- and A+ on the screens, or change the system text size.
  • Can I change my ad choices? Yes. Use the privacy choices for ads in the app where shown, or your device's privacy settings.
