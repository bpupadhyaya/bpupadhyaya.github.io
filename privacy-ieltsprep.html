IELTS Practice Test: Bandholm privacy policy

Bandholm is an independent IELTS practice app (Academic and General Training) by EqualInformation, LLC. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the IELTS owners.

What stays on your device

Your answers, scores, notebook, plan, credits and settings are stored only on your device. There is no account and no server of ours. You can export or erase your data in Settings.

Microphone and speech

Speaking practice uses the microphone only while you record. Recognition runs on your device. Only if this device has no offline language pack and you choose "use online recognition for this session", Apple or Google may process that recording to return text; this applies to that session only. Recordings are not saved by the app.

Ads and purchases

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob and may use the advertising identifier if you allow tracking; a consent form appears where required. Rewarded videos give practice credits only when you watch them. An optional monthly subscription removes ads; payments are handled by Apple or Google, never by us.

Children

The app is for learners aged 13 and over.
