Help

Email support@equalinformation.com with your device and app version (More, About). You can also use More, Settings, Privacy and data, Send diagnostics, which shows exactly what will be sent before you confirm.

Subscriptions

Manage or cancel Quartwren Plus in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings. Use Restore purchases in More, Settings if Plus does not appear after reinstalling.

Your data

Learning progress is stored on your device; deleting the app deletes it. To request deletion of usage information, use Delete my usage data under More, Settings, Privacy and data, or email diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
