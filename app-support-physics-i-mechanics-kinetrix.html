Report a content error

Found a wrong answer, a typo or an unclear explanation? Email bpupadhyaya@equalinformation.com with the topic and question text and, if you can, the source you checked. Confirmed mistakes are fixed in an update.

Get help

Email the same address with your device model, system version and a short description. We usually reply within a few days.

Manage or cancel Plus

On iOS open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. On Android open Google Play, then Payments and subscriptions. Cancelling keeps Plus until the end of the month you paid for. Refund requests go through Apple or Google.

Restore a purchase

To restore, or to delete your usage data or send diagnostics and feedback, open More in the app. For Plus, choose Restore purchases while signed in with the account that bought Plus.

Common questions

Does it need internet? No. Lessons, questions, worked solutions, mock exams and the 3D labs run offline; a connection is only needed to buy or restore Plus.

Is it a replacement for my course? No. It is a study aid that goes alongside a course, textbook or instructor.

How does the adaptive path work? Kinetrix tracks your mastery topic by topic, notices the pattern behind a wrong answer and chooses your next question and review from that, on your device. Prefer a fixed order? Switch to the Syllabus view at any time without losing progress.

Where do the further-reading links lead? To the original sources, which are cited and linked, not copied.
