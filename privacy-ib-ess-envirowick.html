Envirowick privacy notice

EqualInformation, LLC. Effective 26 November 2026. For IB ESS Prep: Envirowick.

Envirowick (listed as IB ESS Prep: Envirowick) asks for no sign-up. Course material, your answers' history and your study plan live on your phone or tablet, and revision works with no connection. This notice sets out the limited cases in which something leaves the device and what EqualInformation, LLC does with it. It covers this app only, from version 1.0.

What stays with you

Whatever you type into essay practice or mission notes is stored on the device and is never uploaded. The app does not ask for or read your name, address book, exact whereabouts, camera roll or documents. It has no search box, and it reads no identifier that follows you between apps.

Why we use it

To run and protect the app and help people who use it; to find and repair faults; to shape revision around each learner; to learn which parts of the course and which tools are working; for research, development and analysis that improve our apps and teaching material; to consider and answer what you send us; and to meet legal duties.

Your controls

  • Sharing switch. It appears on the opening notice and afterwards under Settings, Privacy and data. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and whenever the region is unknown, it starts switched off. Elsewhere it starts switched on. Change it whenever you like.
  • Deletion. Delete my usage data, in the same place, turns sharing off and sends us a request to remove the usage information linked to your installation. You can also write to diagnostics@equalinformation.com. We act on requests as we receive them.
  • Sending. Reports and messages go only when you choose to send them. If there is no connection at that moment, the app keeps them and tries again later.

Where it goes and how long we keep it

Transfers are encrypted with HTTPS. A web service run by EqualInformation records them in our Google Workspace account (Sheets and Drive); acceptance receipts, diagnostics reports and deletion requests can also reach us as e-mail. Google processes this for us. Purchases of Envirowick Plus are handled entirely by Apple or Google, and card details never reach us. Web sources you open from a lesson are run by other organisations under their own privacy terms.

We hold usage, study and diagnostic records for as long as they help with the purposes listed above, and nothing erases them on a fixed timetable. Removal happens when you ask, as described under Your controls.

Safeguards, legal grounds and location

We apply sensible organisational and technical protections, though no system or network can be guaranteed secure. Where the law asks us to name a ground, it is your consent (for the sharing switch and for anything you send) or our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and making it better. Records may be handled in the United States.

Children

The app is built to gather no more than this notice lists. If you think something has reached us that should not be with us, tell us; after a valid request we will erase it.

Updates and contact

If we change these practices in an important way, we issue a new version of the opening notice and the app shows it to you again. General questions: support@equalinformation.com. Data and deletion requests: diagnostics@equalinformation.com. Responsible company: EqualInformation, LLC.
