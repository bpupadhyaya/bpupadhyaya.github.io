Turnwick: Encounter Tracker. Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 November 2026. Publisher: Equal Information.

What stays on your device

Everything you create in Turnwick (encounters, combatants, hit points, conditions, dice rolls, saved rolls, the journal) is stored only on your own device. We run no account system and no server that receives your tabletop data. We cannot see it and we do not sell it.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. The ad software may collect your device advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking when iOS asks), coarse location derived from your IP address, and information about how you interact with ads, in order to show and measure ads. On first launch iOS asks whether Turnwick may track you; where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and some US states) a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform follows. If you decline, you still get every feature and see non-personalised ads. You can change your choice later in iOS Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking, and in the privacy options row of the More tab where it appears.

Google's handling of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Optional subscription and earning ad-free time

A monthly ad-free pass is sold through the App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never see your card or Apple ID. The pass renews monthly until cancelled in your Apple account settings. You can also earn ad-free time by watching rewarded videos; the remaining time is counted on your device.

What we do not do

We do not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, microphone or precise location. We do not build profiles ourselves, and the app has no sign-in.

Children

Turnwick is a general-audience tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all data stored on your device. Ad-related identifiers can be reset in iOS Settings.
