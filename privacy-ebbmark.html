Ebbmark privacy policy

Updated 7 November 2026 · Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC

Your study material stays with you

Ebbmark has no sign-in and no server of ours. Your shelves, slips, pictures, review history, streaks and settings live in the app's private storage on your iPhone or iPad. Turning pasted notes into slips, finding text in a picture and working out your review schedule all happen on the device. We cannot see any of it.

Files you import or export

When you import a CSV or text file, or share an export, the file goes only where you choose through the system file and share sheets.

Pictures

Ebbmark reads a picture only after you pick it in the system photo picker. A reduced copy is stored inside the app so the picture slip can be shown later. Deleting the slip removes that copy.

Reminders

If you switch on a daily reminder, the notification is scheduled locally by iOS. Nothing about it is sent to us.

Advertising

The free version of Ebbmark shows ads served by Google AdMob. On iOS the app first asks for App Tracking Transparency permission, then, where the law requires it, shows Google's consent form, and only then loads ads. If you allow tracking and consent, Google and its partners may process your advertising identifier, coarse location, and information about how you interact with ads to show and measure ads. If you decline, ads are not personalised. You can change the tracking choice in iOS Settings and, in some regions, reopen the consent form from More inside the app. See how Google uses data from ads.

Ad-free subscription

The optional monthly subscription is handled entirely by Apple through your Apple Account. We receive no payment details or personal data.

Children

Ebbmark is a general-audience study tool and is not directed to children under 13.

Contact

Questions: support@equalinformation.com. We update this page when the app's data practices change.
