Bindwell support

Bindwell is a receptor and kinetics lab for pharmacology students. Published by EqualInformation, LLC.

Get help

Email bpupadhyaya@equalinformation.com with your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version. We reply within a few days.

Common questions

Does it need internet? No. Every lab, class and drill works offline. Only ads and the subscription need a connection.

How do I remove ads? Open About and choose the monthly ad-free subscription, or watch optional rewarded videos to earn ad-free time. Use Restore Purchases after reinstalling.

Cancel the subscription? iOS Settings, your name, Subscriptions, Bindwell.

Is this medical advice? No. Bindwell is for education. It gives no doses, no patient calculators and no interaction checks. The model drugs in the kinetics lab are fictional. Sources with dates are listed in each class.

Found an error in a class sheet? Tell us the class and the source you checked and we will review it.
