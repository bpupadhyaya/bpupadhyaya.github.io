Email support@equalinformation.com. Please mention your device, the app version (Settings, About) and what you were doing. We reply within a few days.

Common questions

Where is my progress stored? Only on your device. Use Settings, Export progress to save a file, and Import progress to restore it on a new device. Deleting the app deletes the progress.

How do credits work? You start with 2. Watch a short optional video to earn 1 (up to 12 a day), or go ad-free with the monthly plan for unlimited use. The diagnostic, daily plan, warm-up, mistake notebook, concept cards and every full solution are always free.

How do I restore my subscription? Open Settings, Ad-free plan, then Restore purchases. On Android, make sure you are signed in to the same Google account in Play Store; on iPhone, the same Apple ID.

How do I cancel? Subscriptions are managed in your Apple ID subscriptions or in Google Play, Payments and subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Is the practice score a prediction? No. It is a rough practice range from our own practice questions, which are original and not calibrated by the test maker.
