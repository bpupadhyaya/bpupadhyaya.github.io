Privacy Policy — CareCircle

Effective Date: April 24, 2026

  • Home
  • /
  • Privacy Policy
  • /
  • CareCircle

1 Who We Are

CareCircle is provided by EqualInformation, LLC.

If you have privacy questions or requests, you may contact us at:
Email: privacy@equalinformation.com
Contact page: equalinformation.weebly.com/contact.html | bpupadhyaya.github.io#contact

2 Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the CareCircle app (iOS bundle identifier: com.equalinformation.carecircle.app; Android package name: com.carecircle.app). CareCircle is available on iOS (App Store) and Android (Google Play).

3 Information CareCircle May Access or Store

CareCircle is a caregiver coordination app. Depending on how you use the app, CareCircle may store information on your device, including:

  • Caregiver names, roles, and contact information (phone, email)
  • Care receiver names and relationship information
  • Care tasks, medications, vital readings, and symptom logs
  • Visit schedules, appointments, and care notes
  • Expense records and meal plans
  • Emergency contact information
  • Document metadata (names, categories — not file contents)
  • Wellbeing check-in data (mood, energy, sleep, pain)
  • In-app purchase status (premium unlock state)
  • App settings and preferences

Important: All of this information is stored exclusively on your device. CareCircle does not transmit, upload, or sync any of this data to external servers.

4 How We Use Information

CareCircle uses information to:

  • Help you coordinate care tasks, medications, and schedules
  • Track health metrics (vitals, symptoms) for the care receiver
  • Provide a simplified Companion Mode interface for care receivers
  • Display appropriate content based on purchase status (free vs premium)
  • Maintain app preferences locally

5 Local-First Design

CareCircle does not collect, transmit, or store any user data on servers. All data is stored locally on your device using SwiftData (iOS) or Room (Android). There is no cloud sync, no user accounts, and no user tracking. CareCircle does not require an internet connection to function (except for in-app purchase verification).

6 No Analytics or Tracking

CareCircle does not use any analytics SDKs, crash reporters, or tracking tools. No data is sent to third-party analytics services. No advertising identifiers are collected.

7 App Store and Google Play Billing

CareCircle may use Apple App Store In-App Purchase (on iOS) or Google Play Billing (on Android) for eligible in-app purchase functionality. Apple or Google may process payment and transaction information under their own respective policies. CareCircle may receive limited purchase-status or product information needed to unlock purchased content on your device. EqualInformation, LLC does not receive your full payment card number through CareCircle.

8 Health and Care Information

CareCircle allows you to record health-related data such as vital signs, symptoms, and medications. This information is stored exclusively on your device and is never transmitted to external servers. CareCircle is not a medical device and is not intended to replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

9 Permissions

CareCircle may request the following device permissions:

  • Billing (Android): Used only for in-app purchase verification

CareCircle does not require camera, microphone, location, contacts, or other sensitive permissions.

10 Sharing and Sale of Data

EqualInformation, LLC does not sell personal or sensitive user data. We do not share users' data with data brokers or for advertising purposes. The company does not intentionally transmit personal or sensitive CareCircle user data to its own servers or unrelated third parties.

11 Data Retention and Deletion

All data in CareCircle is stored locally on your device. Purchase-related unlock state may be restorable through App Store or Google Play for eligible purchases. Uninstalling CareCircle removes all locally stored data from your device, including all care records, contacts, and preferences.

12 Security

CareCircle relies on iOS and Android platform protections and app sandboxing as part of its local-first design. Care data is stored within the app's private sandbox and is not accessible to other apps. However, no method of storage or electronic transmission is completely secure, and the company cannot guarantee absolute security.

13 Your Choices

You may choose to purchase premium content or not. You control what information you enter into the app. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data from your device.

14 Children's Privacy

CareCircle is intended for adult caregivers. It is not designed for or directed at children under 13. CareCircle does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe information has been provided inappropriately, please contact the company.

15 Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the updated version on this page and revise the Effective Date above.
