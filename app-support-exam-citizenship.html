Common questions

Which test applies to me? USCIS decides by the date you filed Form N-400: before 20 October 2025 you take the 2008 test (100 questions); on or after that date you take the 2025 test (128 questions). In the app open Profile, then Which test applies to me, and always confirm on uscis.gov.

Why does the app not tell me who the President is? Several answers change with elections and appointments, and USCIS wants the official serving on the day of your interview. Enter your own officeholders in Profile and check uscis.gov/citizenship/testupdates.

How do I remove ads? Open Settings, then Remove ads, for the $2.99 monthly subscription or earn ad-free time with optional videos. Restore Purchases is on the same sheet.

I found a wrong answer. In the app use Accuracy and sources, then Report an error, or write to the address above with the question number and version.
