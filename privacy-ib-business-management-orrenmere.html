IB Business Mgmt: Orrenmere: Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Contact: support@equalinformation.com. Last updated 2026-11-25.

1. Introduction and scope

This policy describes how EqualInformation, LLC (we, us) handles information in connection with IB Business Mgmt: Orrenmere, version 1.0 and later (the app), a study aid for the IB Diploma Programme Business Management course. It applies to the app only. The app has no account or sign-in, and its lessons, questions, labs, builds, notes and progress are kept on your device.

2. Information we collect

A. Information you choose to provide. Diagnostic reports: under Settings, Privacy and data, Send diagnostics shows the complete report before anything is sent, and it is sent only when you tap Send. Feedback and suggestions: category, message, an optional role, level, source link and rating, and an email address only if you tick the option to be contacted.

B. Diagnostic and app-usage information, collected with your agreement or as permitted by applicable law while the optional usage setting is on: app version and build; device model; operating system and version; processor and memory details; display size; language, region and time zone; accessibility and display settings; performance and memory indicators; crash and error information; a random app identifier; how features are used; and how learning features are used and perform: lessons viewed, questions attempted, whether answers were correct, time taken, progress estimates, adaptive-learning decisions and their outcomes, and activity in labs and builds. Answers typed into question fields are included in shortened form with recognisable personal identifiers removed automatically.

When you accept the first-run notice a short acceptance record is always sent: a consent identifier, the time, the app, platform, version and build, the notice version and a hash of its text, the state of the usage setting, the random app identifier, and your country, region setting and time zone.

C. We do not collect your name, contacts, precise location, photographs, files, advertising identifiers, or other text you write in the app such as research project notes and drafts.

3. How we use information

To operate, maintain, secure and support the app; to diagnose problems; to provide and personalise the learning experience; to understand how the app is used; for research and development; for analytics and to improve our apps and educational content; to read and act on feedback; and to comply with legal obligations.

4. Your choices

On first opening, a notice explains these practices and offers a separate usage switch. The switch starts off in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and wherever your region is unknown until you turn it on; elsewhere it starts on. You can change it at any time under Settings, Privacy and data. Diagnostics and feedback are sent only when you tap Send. To delete usage information, use Delete my usage data in the same place or write to diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

5. Storage and service providers

Information travels over encrypted (HTTPS) connections to a web application operated by EqualInformation, LLC that records it in our Google Workspace services (Google Sheets and Drive) and sends notification email; service providers act on our behalf. Subscription payments are processed by Apple or Google; we do not receive your payment details.

6. Retention

Usage, learning and diagnostic information is kept while it remains useful for the purposes above. Deletion requests are handled on request.

7. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures, including encryption in transit. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. International users and legal bases

Where a legal basis is required we rely on your agreement (the usage switch and your decision to send diagnostics or feedback) and on our legitimate interest in operating and improving the app. Information may be processed in the United States.

9. Children

We design our apps to collect only the information described in this policy. If you believe information has been provided to us that you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

10. Changes

If this policy changes materially, the version of the first-run notice changes and the notice is shown again.

11. Contact

EqualInformation, LLC. Questions: support@equalinformation.com. Deletion and data requests: diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
