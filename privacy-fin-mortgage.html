Amortlume: Mortgage Calculator 3D, Privacy Policy

Effective 9 November 2026. Publisher: Equal Information LLC.

What stays on your device

Every loan, rent and scenario figure you type, your saved scenarios and your text-size choice are stored only on your device. The app has no account, no sign-in and no server of ours that receives your numbers. The calculators work fully offline.

Advertising

Amortlume is free and shows ads from Google AdMob unless you subscribe or earn ad-free time. If you allow tracking, AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier to show more relevant ads and to measure them. You can decline the tracking prompt, change the choice later in iOS Settings, and use the privacy choices option inside the app's More screen where your region requires a consent form. AdMob may collect device identifiers, advertising data, coarse location derived from your IP address and interaction data, as described in Google's advertising policies.

Subscription

The optional monthly ad-free subscription is handled entirely by Apple. We do not receive your payment details. Manage or cancel it in your Apple Account settings.

Children

Amortlume is for adults who plan household finances and is not directed to children.

Your choices and contact

Delete the app to remove all data stored on your device. Questions or requests: support@equalinformation.com.

Not financial advice

Amortlume gives estimates for planning. It is not a loan offer, tax advice or financial advice.
