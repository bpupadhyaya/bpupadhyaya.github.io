College Costs: Netwren: Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Contact: support@equalinformation.com. Last updated 2026-11-21.

What stays on your device

Your age answer, progress, answers, badges, tool inputs and settings are stored only on your device. The app has no account system and no server that receives this data. Deleting the app or using Reset in the app erases it. We do not collect your name, email address or contact details.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on some screens, an occasional full-screen ad at the end of a quiz, and an optional rewarded video. No ads appear while you answer a question. AdMob may use your advertising identifier, approximate location and device and usage data to select and measure ads under Google's own privacy policy. For adults on iOS the app asks permission before tracking across other companies' apps, and where the law requires it Google shows a consent form. Declining gives non-personalised ads.

Ages 13 to 17

The app is for ages 13 and over. People under 13 are blocked at the first screen. For users who say they are 13 to 17 the app shows no tracking prompt, requests only non-personalised ads, caps ad content at a teen-appropriate rating, and does not use any AI feature.

Subscription

The optional monthly plan only removes ads. Purchases are processed by Apple or Google; we never see your payment details. You can cancel any time in your App Store or Google Play account settings and keep the benefit until the paid period ends.

Sources you open

Source links open public web pages inside the app. Those sites load from their publishers and apply their own privacy practices. College data inside the app is bundled and works offline.
