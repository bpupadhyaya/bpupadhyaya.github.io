Live by design, not by default — Sristi plans, executes, and evaluates the essentials of a healthy life so you can act on what matters most each day.

Sristi is an AI-first personal life optimizer. It plans, executes, and evaluates the essentials of a healthy life across 13 cited life domains: Sleep, Hydration, Nutrition, Movement, Mental & Emotional, Social & Relationships, Focus & Productivity, Environment & Light, Health Monitoring, Financial Wellness, Spiritual & Purpose, Life Transitions, and Safety & Emergency. It is voice-first (voice, tap, or text) and built and maintained by EqualInformation, LLC.

Every feature is designed with privacy at its core — Sristi is 100% on-device with no accounts, no servers, no cloud sync, and no tracking, and the core works fully offline. Sristi is available now on Google Play, and in review on the Apple App Store. The core is free for everyone, with optional life-optimization modules available through a one-time in-app purchase. Your support helps us continue improving the app and building more tools that help people live well.

Why Sristi Exists

Most of us drift through the day by default — reacting instead of acting on what matters. Sristi generates a daily plan that adapts to your logged energy, so the right next step is always in front of you.

Good guidance should be trustworthy. Sristi is evidence-based with tappable citations (WHO, IOM/NASEM, EFSA, ACSM, NSF, AASM, ACC/AHA, APA, CFPB, CDC, NHTSA, and peer-reviewed studies), so you can see the source behind every recommendation.

Sristi logs hydration, sleep, movement, energy, mood, and meals, then gives you an evening recap and weekly/monthly reflection — turning small daily actions into a picture of how you are really doing.

Sristi is not a substitute for professional medical advice. It is an educational companion that helps you build healthier routines and make better decisions across the essentials of daily life.

Disclaimer: Sristi is for educational purposes only. The information provided is not intended to replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always seek the advice of a qualified health professional with any questions regarding a medical condition. If you think you may have a medical emergency, contact your local emergency services immediately.
