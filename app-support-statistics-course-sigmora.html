Support

Questions, corrections and subscription help: support@equalinformation.com. Include your device model and the app version.

Subscription

Sigmora Plus is a monthly subscription. To cancel or manage it, use Manage subscription in the app (More, then Sigmora Plus) or open your App Store or Google Play account settings. If you cancel, Plus stays active until the end of the month you paid for. Use Restore purchases in the app if a purchase does not show.

Your data

Your study progress is stored on your device. The app works offline; opening a cited source, sending feedback or diagnostics, and optional usage sharing need a connection. You can control usage sharing and send diagnostics or feedback under More, Privacy and data. Deletion requests: diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
