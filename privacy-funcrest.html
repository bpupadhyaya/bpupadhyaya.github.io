Funcrest: AP Precalculus - Privacy Policy

This policy explains what the Funcrest AP Precalculus exam prep app does with information. Short version: your study data stays on your device.

What stays on your device

Your answers, study plan, notebook, streak, settings and credits are stored only on your device. There is no account, sign-up or server holding your study data. You can export or delete your progress from Settings, and deleting the app removes it.

Ads

Unless you subscribe, the app shows ads through Google AdMob and offers optional rewarded videos. Google and its partners may process your advertising identifier, approximate location and device information to serve ads. Where required you are asked for consent first (App Tracking Transparency on iOS and Google's user messaging consent form on both platforms). If you decline, ads are not personalised or are limited. Ads are never shown during timed tests, mock exams, simulations or answer timers.

Students and age

The app is meant for students aged 13 and over. It is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Ads are set to a general-audience content rating.

Subscriptions and purchases

The monthly ad-free subscription is handled by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details. You can cancel in your App Store or Google Play account settings.

Reference links

Reference buttons show a stored citation. Links to the College Board open in your own browser only when you tap them. Pages from open textbooks may open inside the app when you tap them; those sites have their own policies.
