Eavesly Privacy Policy

Effective 7 November 2026. Publisher: Equal Information LLC.

Your home records stay on your device

Eavesly keeps your home profile, schedule, equipment register, job history, contractor notes, scanned receipts, manuals and photos only in the app's storage on your iPhone or iPad. There is no Eavesly account and no Eavesly server that receives them. Reading text from scans and understanding the sentences you type both happen on the device with Apple frameworks.

Ads and tracking

Eavesly is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. With your permission (the App Tracking Transparency prompt, and where required a Google consent form), AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier and coarse location to choose and measure ads. The data AdMob collects is described in Google's policy at policies.google.com/privacy. If you decline, you still get ads, but they are not personalised. In regions where a consent choice applies you can reopen it from Settings, Privacy choices for ads.

Removing ads

You can subscribe monthly for no ads, or watch short rewarded videos to earn ad-free time. Purchases are handled by Apple; we never see your payment details.

Notifications, camera and photos

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device, only if you turn them on. The camera is used only while you scan a document, and the photo picker only for the images you choose. None of this leaves your device.

Sharing the binder

The home binder PDF is created on your device. It goes wherever you send it with the iOS share sheet; Eavesly does not upload it.

Children

Eavesly is a household tool for adults and is not directed to children under 13.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove all records it holds. Ad identifiers can be reset or limited in iOS Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking.

Not advice

Maintenance schedules and cost ranges are general public guidance, not professional advice. Follow the manufacturer's manual for your equipment and use licensed professionals for gas, electrical, roof and structural work.
