A Level Psych Prep: Mindvale - Privacy Policy

Effective 22 November 2026.

Mindvale works offline. Your study progress, settings and unlock times are stored only on your device and are not sent to us. We run no accounts and no analytics.

Ads

The free app shows optional ads through Google AdMob. On iOS we ask for tracking permission (adults only) and use Google's consent form where required; Google may collect the device advertising identifier and ad interaction data under its own policy (policies.google.com/privacy). If you say you are under 18, only non-personalised ads are requested. Subscribers see no ads.

Purchases

The monthly subscription is handled by Apple or Google Play; we do not receive payment details.

Your choices

Change tracking in device settings, reset progress in Settings, or delete the app to remove all data. Contact: support@equalinformation.com.
