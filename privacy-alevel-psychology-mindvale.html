A Level Psych Prep: Mindvale - Privacy Policy

Effective 23 November 2026.

Mindvale works offline. Your study progress, settings and plan state are stored only on your device and are not sent to us. We run no accounts, no analytics and no tracking, and we do not collect personal data.

Purchases

The optional Mindvale Plus subscription is handled by Apple or Google Play; we do not receive payment details. You can cancel any time in your account settings and keep Plus until the end of the month you have paid for.

Your choices

Reset progress in Settings or delete the app to remove all data. Contact: support@equalinformation.com.
