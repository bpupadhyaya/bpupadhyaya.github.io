Lexiridge Privacy Policy

Lexiridge: IELTS TOEFL Vocab. Effective 13 November 2026. Publisher: Equal Information LLC.

Lexiridge helps learners study academic English vocabulary with flashcards, quizzes and a 3D word sky. It is an independent study aid and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the owners of the IELTS or TOEFL tests.

What stays on your device

Your study progress, settings, streak and explanation-language choice are stored only on your device and are removed when you delete the app. There are no accounts and no servers of ours. Word pronunciation uses your device's built-in offline text-to-speech; no audio is recorded. The app does not use the camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. Ads are never shown in your first session and are removed when you subscribe or buy Remove Ads. An optional short video can be watched to unlock Ridge 8 for 24 hours or to get extra new words and hints. On iPhone and iPad the system first asks whether the app may track you (App Tracking Transparency); in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some US states a Google consent message also appears (Google User Messaging Platform). On Android the Google consent message appears where the law requires it. You can decline and keep using every feature. With your permission, AdMob and its partners may use your device advertising identifier, approximate location derived from your IP address, and ad interaction and diagnostic data to choose, show and measure ads. See Google's advertising policies. You can change your choices later in the app under Settings (privacy choices for ads, where shown) and in your device settings. Ads are not served to people who have an ad-free purchase active.

Purchases

Lexiridge Premium (monthly subscription, 7-day free trial where offered) and Remove Ads (one-time purchase) are handled by Apple or Google Play. We never see your payment details.

What is collected

Collected by us: nothing. Collected by Google AdMob in the free version: device identifiers (advertising identifier), approximate location from IP address, and ad interaction and diagnostic data, used for third-party advertising and measuring ads.

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information, build profiles about you, run our own analytics, or ask you to create an account. No personal data is sent to us.

Children

Lexiridge is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and we do not collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new effective date.
