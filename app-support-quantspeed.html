How credits work

You start with 2 credits. One credit starts one full session: Today's plan, a topic drill, a speed sprint or a mini mock test. Watching a short ad adds 1 credit. Reviewing due items, the mistake notebook, shortcut cards and every explanation are always free. If no ad can be shown, you receive one free credit at most once every 24 hours. The monthly ad-free plan removes ads and the need for credits.

Subscription

The ad-free plan is $2.99 per month with a 7-day free trial, renewing monthly until cancelled. Cancel or manage it in your Apple ID or Google Play subscription settings. Use Restore purchases in the app if you reinstall.

Back up your progress

Settings, then Export progress saves a file you can import on another device (iPhone and Android files are compatible). Credits and purchases are not part of the file.

Questions or problems

Email support@equalinformation.com with your device model and app version (Settings, About).

About the questions

All questions are original practice material. If you find a mistake, tell us the question text and we will correct it.
