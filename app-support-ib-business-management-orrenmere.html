Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Contact: support@equalinformation.com. Last updated 2026-11-25.

Help

Questions, content corrections and subscription help: support@equalinformation.com. Please include your device model, the app version and the screen or question concerned.

Send diagnostics

Open Settings, Privacy and data and choose Send diagnostics. You see exactly what the report contains and nothing is sent until you tap Send. Reports arrive at diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

Feedback and suggestions

Use Feedback and suggestions in Settings, the helpful and Report controls under answers, lessons and labs, or Suggest content. Nothing is sent until you tap Send.

Delete my usage data

Open Settings, Privacy and data and choose Delete my usage data, or email diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

Subscription

Manage or cancel Orrenmere Plus in your store account settings. If a purchase does not appear, use Restore purchases on the Orrenmere Plus screen.

Contact

support@equalinformation.com. Privacy and deletion requests: diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
