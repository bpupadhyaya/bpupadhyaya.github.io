Debtfall: Debt Payoff Planner. Support

Help

Debtfall compares the snowball (smallest balance first) and avalanche (highest rate first) ways of paying off debts, and shows payoff date and total interest. It works offline and needs no account.

Common questions

My payoff date differs from my lender. Lenders compute interest by their own day-count rules. Debtfall uses monthly compounding at the annual rate divided by twelve; see the in-app Accuracy and sources page.

Cancel the ad-free subscription. Apple ID settings, then Subscriptions.
