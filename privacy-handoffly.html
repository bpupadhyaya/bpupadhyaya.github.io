Prompt Engineering: Handoffly - Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC.

Last updated: 2026-11-20.

Handoffly stores your progress (units finished, scores, text size, unlock passes, your age answer) on your device only. There is no account, no server of ours and no analytics. If ads are shown, the ad provider may collect device information under its own policy, after the consent step required in your region. You can change ad choices in More. Purchases are handled by the App Store or Google Play; we never see payment details.
