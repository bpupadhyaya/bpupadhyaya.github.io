Sristi is under active development. This page documents each production release with what changed, what's new, and what to expect.

These release notes are maintained by AI agents. While we strive for accuracy, limitations may apply.

v1.0.0 Submitted Current June 2026

Initial release of Sristi — your personal life optimizer, "live by design, not by default." Submitted to the Apple App Store and awaiting review (June 28, 2026); the Android build is packaged and pending Google Play submission. Sristi is 100% on-device and privacy-first: no account, no servers, no tracking. A free core covers the health essentials, with a one-time "Sristi Plus" unlock for the premium life-optimization modules.

What's included

  • 13 cited life domains: Sleep & Rest, Hydration, Nutrition & Meals, Movement & Exercise, Mental & Emotional, Social & Relationships, Health Monitoring, Environment & Light, Focus & Productivity, Financial Wellness, Spiritual & Purpose, Life Transitions, and Safety & Emergency
  • Free core: a personalized daily plan, a guidance Library with tappable sources, Stats across day/week/month, and the always-free Safety & Emergency module
  • Sristi Plus (one-time unlock, yours forever — no subscription): Focus & Productivity, Financial Wellness, Spiritual & Purpose, and Life Transitions
  • Evidence-based recommendations with tappable citations (WHO, IOM/NASEM, EFSA, ACSM, NSF, AASM, ACC/AHA, APA, CFPB, CDC, NHTSA, and peer-reviewed studies)
  • Voice-first input — speak, tap, or type
  • On-device personalized targets computed from your profile, activity, and climate (e.g., hydration and movement)
  • Optional read-only Health (iOS) / Health Connect (Android) integration for exercise minutes and sleep
  • StoreKit 2 (iOS) and Google Play Billing (Android) — entitlements verified on-device, no account required
  • 100% on-device — no account, no servers, no cloud sync, no analytics, and no tracking; the core works fully offline
  • Native cross-platform builds: SwiftUI (iOS 17+) and Jetpack Compose (Android 8.0+)

What's coming next

  • An intelligence layer — adaptive planning, deeper trends, and on-device guidance — planned as a future add-on
