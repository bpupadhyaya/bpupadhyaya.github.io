Jorrery privacy policy

Math Grade 11: Jorrery, published by EqualInformation, LLC. Last updated 27 November 2026.

In short

The app does not send your study activity to us. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Kept on your device

Answers, progress estimates, review dates, pinned items, build and lab settings, quiz results, text size and your acceptance of the first-run notice are saved on the device so the app can adapt. We cannot see them. If your device or cloud backup includes app data, the provider of that backup holds a copy under its own terms.

"Erase progress" in the grown-up area removes the study record. Removing the app removes its data from the device.

Subscriptions

Optional monthly subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple or Google. We do not receive your card or bank details. The app asks the store which subscriptions are active so it can unlock them. The stores give developers sales and subscription reports, and handle data about your purchase under their own privacy policies. The Android app uses the Google Play Billing library for this.

Store statistics

Apple and Google may provide developers with aggregated installation and crash statistics, according to your device settings and their policies. The app adds no code of its own to collect them.

Web pages you open

After the grown-up check, you can open a cited source or these legal pages in a window inside the app. The website you open receives the usual connection information, such as your network address, and may use cookies, under its own policy. The app does not add information about you to the request.

Writing to us

If you email us, we receive your address and what you choose to write or paste, including any device facts you copy from the app. We use it to answer you and to fix problems. Ask us to delete the correspondence at any time. The app never sends email by itself.

Young people

The app is built to work without collecting personal information from learners. A parent or guardian with a question or a deletion request can write to us.

Changes and contact

If a later version handles data differently, this page and the app store information will be updated, and the app will say what changed. EqualInformation, LLC: support@equalinformation.com.
