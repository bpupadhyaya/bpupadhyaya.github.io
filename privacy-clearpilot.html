AI at Work: Clearpilot - Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC.

Last updated: 2026-11-20.

Effective 2026-11-17. Clearpilot (AI at Work: Clearpilot) is for adults aged 18 and over.

What stays on your device

The app has no account and no server of ours. Your progress, text size and credits are stored only on your device. We do not run analytics.

Ads

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. With your consent, the ad network may use your device advertising identifier and similar data to show relevant ads and measure them. On iOS you are asked for tracking permission; in some regions a consent form appears. You can decline and still use every lesson. You can change choices in the app (Privacy options) or in your device settings.

Subscription

The optional monthly subscription is processed by Apple or Google. We do not receive your payment details.

Citation window

When you open a source link, the page loads in a private browsing session inside the app. We do not read what you view, and no cookies are kept after it closes.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 18.
