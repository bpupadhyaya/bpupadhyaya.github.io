Breathwick Support

Getting help

Email bpupadhyaya@equalinformation.com with your device model, iOS version and a short description. We aim to reply within three working days.

Common questions

  • Text is too small or too large. Use the A- and A+ buttons at the top of any screen, or Settings, Text size. The app also follows your iOS Dynamic Type setting.
  • I feel nothing from haptics. Haptics need an iPhone with a Taptic Engine and are off on most iPads. Check Settings, Cues, Haptics, and iOS Settings, Sounds and Haptics, System Haptics.
  • No sound. Switch on Soft tone in Settings. It follows the ring switch and Silent mode.
  • How do I remove ads? Open Settings, Go ad-free. Subscribe monthly or watch rewarded videos to earn ad-free time. Use Restore purchases on a new device.
  • How do I cancel? In the iOS Settings app, tap your name, Subscriptions, Breathwick.
  • My streak reset. A streak counts consecutive days with at least one finished session of 15 seconds or more.
