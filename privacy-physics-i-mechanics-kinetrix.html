Physics I Mechanics: Kinetrix Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Effective 2026-11-27.

1. Introduction and scope

This Privacy Policy describes how EqualInformation, LLC ("we", "us") handles information in connection with Physics I Mechanics: Kinetrix (the "app"). It applies from the effective date above.

2. Information we collect

A. Information you choose to provide. Diagnostic reports that you choose to send, together with any note you add; feedback and suggestions that you submit (category, message, optional role and level, a source link and rating); and, only if you tick the contact option, an email address so that we can reply.

B. Diagnostic and app-usage information, collected with your agreement or as permitted by applicable law. Device and operating-system information (model, operating-system version, processor, memory, display size, language, region and time zone, accessibility and display settings); app version; performance and memory indicators; crash and error information; a random app identifier; and how the features of the app are used. For this learning app that includes how learning features are used and perform: lessons viewed, questions attempted, whether answers are correct, time taken, hints used, progress estimates, adaptive-learning decisions and their outcomes, and activity in the labs. Numeric answers that you type into question fields are recorded in shortened form, automatically cleaned of recognisable personal identifiers.

C. Information we do not collect. Your name, contacts, precise location, photographs, files, advertising identifiers, or other text that you enter.

3. How we use information

To operate, maintain, secure and support the app; to diagnose problems; to provide and personalise the learning experience; to understand how the app is used; for research and development; for analytics and improvement of our apps and educational content; to read and act on feedback; and to comply with legal obligations.

4. Your choices

When you first open the app a notice is shown with a separate switch for sharing usage information. The switch is off by default in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and where your region cannot be determined. You can change it at any time under More, Privacy and data. Diagnostics and feedback are sent only when you choose to send them. To request deletion of usage data associated with your app identifier, use "Delete my usage data" in the app or write to diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

5. Storage and service providers

Information is stored in our Google Workspace services (Google Sheets and Drive) and notification email, and is processed by service providers acting on our behalf. Kinetrix installed from the App Store or Google Play may produce emails to us containing simulation and problem activity batches, usage records, the acceptance record, crashes, diagnostics, feedback and deletion requests. The mailboxes involved, including any forwarding target, are our own. It is transmitted over encrypted connections. Payments for the optional subscription are handled entirely by Apple or Google; we do not receive your payment details.

6. Retention

Usage and learning information is kept for as long as it is useful for the purposes described above. Deletion on request is performed manually. The "Delete my usage data" request does not wipe anything automatically, and our emailed copies remain unless a person deletes them.

7. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. International users and legal bases

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we rely on your consent or on our legitimate interests in operating and improving the app. Information may be processed in the United States.

9. Children

We design the app to collect only the information described in this policy. If you believe information has been provided to us that you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

10. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, the notice version changes and the first-run notice is shown again.

11. Contact

EqualInformation, LLC. General: bpupadhyaya@equalinformation.com. Deletion and data requests: diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
