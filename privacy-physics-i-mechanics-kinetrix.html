Physics I Mechanics: Kinetrix Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Effective 2026-11-24.

Short version

Kinetrix needs no account. We run no server that stores your study data. Your progress and settings stay on your device.

What is not collected

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, health data or any personal identifier, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell personal data.

Local storage

The app keeps its working data (scores, topic mastery, review queue, text-size and view settings, and whether Plus is active) on your device so the course works offline. We have no access to it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Optional subscription

Kinetrix Plus is an optional auto-renewing monthly subscription. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google; we never see your card details and the app only reads whether your subscription is active. The price is shown by the store before you buy. You can cancel any time in your App Store or Google Play account settings; cancelling takes effect at the end of the month you already paid for.

Children

Kinetrix is aimed at university and advanced school learners and is not directed to children under 13. We knowingly collect nothing from anyone.

Your choices and requests

To ask a privacy question or request access to or deletion of any data we might hold about you, write to bpupadhyaya@equalinformation.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it.
