Privacy Policy: A Level Politics: Ballotwick

1. Scope

EqualInformation, LLC publishes A Level Politics: Ballotwick (the app). This policy explains what information is handled when you use the app from version 1.0.0. It applies to the app only. The app has no user accounts. Contact: support@equalinformation.com.

2. Information you choose to send

Diagnostic report: under Me, then Privacy and data, you can choose Send diagnostics. The report is displayed before anything leaves your device and is sent only when you tap Send. Feedback and suggestions: if you submit feedback, the category, your message and any optional details you add, such as a role, are sent. An email address is sent only if you choose to provide one so that we can reply, and it is used for that purpose only.

3. Usage and diagnostic information

When the optional usage switch is on, or where the law permits, the app can send: app version and build; device model; operating system and version; processor and memory details; display size; language, region and time zone; accessibility and display settings; screens visited; how you move through lessons, quizzes, mocks and labs; the questions you attempt, your answers, correctness and timing; adaptive-learning decisions; purchase-screen interactions; and crash and performance records. These records are linked to a randomly generated app identifier, not to your name.

4. Information the app does not collect

The app does not collect your name, contacts, precise location, photographs, files or an advertising identifier. Notes and essay plans you write stay on your device and are not sent, except text you choose to include in feedback.

5. How information is used

To operate, secure and support the app; to find and fix problems; to personalise your study path; to understand how the app is used; for research and development; to improve our apps and educational content; and to read and act on feedback.

6. Your choices

A notice at first launch explains these practices and offers a separate usage switch. The switch starts off in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and wherever your region cannot be determined, and on elsewhere. You can change it at any time under Me, then Privacy and data, where you can also request deletion of your usage data. Diagnostics and feedback are sent only when you choose.

7. Storage and service providers

Information is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections to a web application operated by EqualInformation, LLC, which records it in Google Workspace services (Google Sheets and Drive) and notification email. "Notification" understates what some messages hold: from copies installed through a store, they can include the full acceptance record, report, feedback, crash detail, deletion request or batch of study and usage activity. They are sent to mailboxes of ours and may be forwarded within them. Subscription purchases are handled by Apple or Google; we receive no payment card details.

8. Retention and security

Usage, learning and diagnostic information is kept while it remains useful for the purposes above; when you request deletion we remove records by hand. This is not automatic, and the emailed versions remain unless they are removed by hand as well. We use reasonable technical and organisational measures, including encryption in transit. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

9. International users and legal bases

Where a legal basis is required we rely on your consent (the usage switch and your decision to send diagnostics or feedback) and on our legitimate interest in maintaining and improving the app. Information may be processed in the United States.

10. Children

We design our apps to collect only the information described in this policy. If you believe information has been provided to us that you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

11. Changes and contact

If we make a material change, the version of the first-run notice changes and the notice is shown again. Questions: support@equalinformation.com. Data and deletion requests: diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

Effective 27 November 2026. EqualInformation, LLC.
