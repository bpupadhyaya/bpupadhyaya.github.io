Helixwick Support

DNA and molecular biology in real 3D

Getting help

Email bpupadhyaya@equalinformation.com with your device model, iOS version and a short description. We aim to reply within three working days.

Common questions

  • How do I rotate or zoom a model? Drag to orbit and pinch to zoom. Tap any part to see its name and role; switch on Find it for a tap-the-part challenge.
  • How do I make the text bigger? Use A+ at the top of any screen or More > Reading size. It stacks on your system text size.
  • Does it work offline? Yes. All structures, labs and questions are built in. Only ads and the subscription need a connection.
  • Restore my subscription: More > Ad-free > Restore purchases.
  • Where do the structures come from? From the RCSB Protein Data Bank (CC0). See More > Sources and licences for every entry and citation.
