Osteowell: Bone Anatomy 3D

Privacy Policy. Effective 8 November 2026. Publisher: Equal Information LLC.

Summary

Osteowell is a bone anatomy study app. It works fully offline. We run no accounts and no servers for this app, so we do not receive your study history, quiz scores or settings. They stay on your device.

Data kept on your device

Quiz results, which bones you have studied, your daily streak, the text-size setting and the state of your ad-free choices are saved locally in the app's storage. Deleting the app deletes them.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To choose and measure ads, Google may use your device's advertising identifier, approximate location derived from your network address, and information about how you interact with ads. On iOS the app first asks whether it may track you across other companies' apps and websites. Where the law requires it, it then shows a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform. You can decline both and keep using the app; the ads will then be less personalised. Google explains its practices at policies.google.com/technologies/ads. You can remove the ads with the monthly Ad-free subscription, or earn ad-free time by watching rewarded videos.

Subscription

The optional monthly Ad-free subscription is sold and billed by Apple through your Apple ID. We never see your payment details. You can manage or cancel it in your Apple ID subscription settings.

What we do not collect

Osteowell does not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, health data, microphone or camera input, or precise location, and it does not ask for those permissions. We do not sell personal data.

Children

Osteowell is a general education app and is not directed to children under 13.

Education only

The app is a study aid for learning bone anatomy. It is not a medical device and gives no diagnosis, treatment or clinical advice.
