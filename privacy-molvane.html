Chemistry AP: Molvane - Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC.

Last updated: 2026-11-20.

What we collect

We run no server or accounts for this app. Your answers, ledger, settings, exam history and age answer stay on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Your age answer

On first launch the app asks whether you are 18 or older, 13 to 17, or under 13. The answer is stored only on your device and decides how ads behave. If you are under 13, no ads and no advertising code run. If you are 13 to 17, ads are non-personalised, are limited to a teen content rating, and the app does not ask for tracking permission. You can change the answer in Settings.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. For adults, with your consent (the Google consent form and, on iPhone, the tracking prompt), advertising partners may use your device's advertising identifier to show and measure ads. You can decline and still use every question and exam.

Sources

When you open a source such as OpenStax or NIST, the page loads inside the app from the publisher's site and is subject to that site's policy. College Board pages open in your browser.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple or Google. We receive only whether a subscription is active, never payment details.

Children

A study tool for high-school students, not directed to children under 13. Children under 13 who say so on the age screen see no ads.
