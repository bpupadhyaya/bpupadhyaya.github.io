Dimeglow support

Dimeglow is a daily spending glance: type a purchase in one sentence and see today, this week and this month.

How do I add a purchase?

On the Today tab type something like "coffee 4.50" or "lunch 12 with Sam" and tap the arrow. Add "yesterday" or a weekday to log an earlier day. Tap the category chip to change it; Dimeglow learns from the change.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap the plus button and search for Dimeglow. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold the Lock Screen, choose Customize and add a Dimeglow widget under the clock or in the lower row. If a widget says to log your first purchase, add one in the app first.

How do I import a bank export?

Download a CSV from your bank's website or app, then use Settings, Import a bank statement. Money-in lines are skipped, repeats of purchases you already have are switched off, and nothing leaves your device. If the day and month look swapped, the file uses the other date order; tell us the bank and we will add it.

Foreign currency

Type a symbol or code ("EUR 9.50", "£4") and add the rate in Settings, Exchange rates. Rates are typed by you; nothing is fetched online.

Subscription and ads

Settings, Remove ads shows the monthly ad-free subscription and Restore purchases. Manage or cancel it in your Apple Account settings. You can also earn ad-free time by watching a short video.

Erase my data

Settings, Erase all purchases, or delete the app. There is no account and no copy on our side.
