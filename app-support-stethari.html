USMLE Step 1 Prep: Stethari: Support

Questions, content corrections and subscription help: support@equalinformation.com. Include your device model and app version.

Subscription: cancel or manage it in your App Store or Google Play account settings. Use Restore purchases in the Stethari Plus panel if a purchase does not show.

Found an error in a question? Send the question code (shown in its explanation) and the source that disagrees. Each cited source opens inside the app.
