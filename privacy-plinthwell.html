Plinthwell Privacy Policy

EqualInformation, LLC. Effective 27 November 2026.

Introduction and scope

This policy describes how EqualInformation, LLC handles information in the Plinthwell art history study app.

Information you choose to provide

Diagnostic reports you send and any note you add. Feedback and suggestions you submit (category, message, and any optional role, level, source link or rating) and, only if you tick the contact option, an email address used to reply.

Diagnostic and app-usage information

With your agreement, or as permitted by applicable law, the app collects diagnostic and app-usage information: device and operating-system information (model, system version, processor, memory, display size, language, region, time zone, accessibility and display settings), app version, performance and memory indicators, crash and error information, a random app identifier, and how features are used and perform: records viewed, questions attempted, the option chosen, whether answers are correct, time taken, progress estimates, adaptive suggestions and their outcomes, activity in exhibition builds, and subscription-screen interactions. When you first accept the notice, a record of that acceptance is sent.

Information we do not collect

Your name, contacts, precise location, photographs or files; essay drafts, comparison notes, or the words you type into search boxes.

How we use information

To operate, maintain, secure and support the app; to diagnose problems; to provide and personalise the learning experience; to understand how the app is used; for research, development, analytics and improvement of our apps and educational content; to read and act on feedback; and to comply with legal obligations.

Your choices

The first-run notice includes a switch for usage information, which you can change at any time under More, Settings, Privacy and data. It starts switched off in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and where the region cannot be determined, until you turn it on. Diagnostics and feedback are sent only when you choose. You can ask us to delete usage data with the in-app "Delete my usage data" request or by writing to diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

Storage and service providers

Information is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored in our Google Workspace services (including Google Sheets, Drive and notification email), with service providers acting on our behalf. When the copy is a store install, the emails can include what was sent: your acceptance, learning and usage batches, crash records, diagnostic reports, feedback and deletion requests. We receive them in mailboxes of our own and may forward them to a further one. Purchases are processed by the app store you use. Links to sources open in your browser and are governed by those sites' own policies.

Retention

Usage and learning information is kept while it is useful for the purposes above. We carry out requested deletions ourselves, by hand; there is no automatic one behind the in-app request. Copies already emailed stay unless we also remove them by hand.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

International users

Where applicable, we rely on your consent or our legitimate interests. Information may be processed in the United States.

Children

We design our apps to collect only the information described in this policy. If you believe information has been provided to us that you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the in-app notice is updated and shown again.

Contact

EqualInformation, LLC. support@equalinformation.com. Data and deletion requests: diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
