IB Computer Science: Nodewren: Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Contact: support@equalinformation.com. Last updated 2026-11-27.

1. Introduction and scope

EqualInformation, LLC (we, us) publishes IB Computer Science: Nodewren (the app), a study aid for learners preparing for the International Baccalaureate Diploma Programme Computer science course. This policy explains how information is handled in connection with the app from version 1.0 and does not cover other products. The app has no account or sign-in. Its lessons, questions, labs, Internal Assessment planner entries and progress are stored on your device.

2. Information we collect

A. Information you choose to provide. Diagnostic reports: under More, Settings, Privacy and data, Send diagnostics shows what will be sent before anything leaves your device, and it is sent only when you tap Send. Feedback and suggestions: category, message, an optional role, and an email address only if you choose to be contacted.

B. Diagnostic and app-usage information, collected with your agreement or as permitted by applicable law while the optional usage setting is on: app version; device model; operating system and version; processor count and memory; screen and window size; language, region and time zone; network type; text size setting; performance indicators; crash and error information; a random app identifier; and how the app is used, namely sessions and screens visited, lessons opened, questions attempted, the option chosen, whether answers were correct, time taken, progress estimates for each topic, adaptive-learning decisions and their outcomes, mock paper results, activity in labs, and interactions with the subscription screen. From version 1.0.1, answers you type into question fields are not included (the earlier version is described below under Typed answers and search words).

When you accept the first-run notice, a brief acceptance record is always sent: a consent identifier, the time, the app, platform, version and build, the notice version, the state of the usage setting, the random app identifier, and your country, region setting and time zone.

C. We do not collect your name, contacts, precise location, photographs, files, tracking identifiers, or, from version 1.0.1, anything you type into the app, such as question answers, Internal Assessment planning notes and case-study answers, which stay on your device, apart from the feedback and diagnostic notes you choose to send.

Typed answers and search words: current and earlier versions

Version 1.0.1 and later. Nothing you type into a question box, planner or lab is part of the usage information; it stays on your device. Whatever you write in a feedback message or a diagnostic note is different: it is sent only when you choose to send it.

Earlier version still installed. Version 1.0.0 behaves differently until you update it. While the optional usage setting is on, it sends the answers you type into calculation, trace-table and written-answer questions in practice sessions and practice papers (the first 200 characters, with anything recognised as an email address, web link, telephone number or card or identity number removed). It does not send Internal Assessment planning notes, case-study answers or lab entries. The text under the usage switch in that version says that nothing you type is included; for those question answers that statement is not correct. To stop this in version 1.0.0, turn the usage setting off under More, Settings, Privacy and data.

3. How we use information

To operate, maintain, secure and support the app; to diagnose problems; to provide and personalise the learning experience; to understand how the app is used; for research and development; for analytics and improvement of our apps and educational content; to read and act on feedback; and to comply with legal obligations.

4. Your choices

When you first open the app, a notice explains these practices and offers a separate usage switch. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and wherever your region cannot be determined, the switch starts off until you turn it on; elsewhere it starts on. While it is off, nothing further is sent apart from the acceptance record described above. You can change the switch at any time under More, Settings, Privacy and data. Diagnostics and feedback are sent only when you tap Send. To request deletion of usage information, use Delete my usage data in the same place, or write to diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

5. Storage and service providers

Information is transmitted over encrypted connections to a web application operated by EqualInformation, LLC, which records it in our Google Workspace services (Google Sheets and Drive) and sends notification email. For installs from the App Store or Google Play, the email may include the data: coding and question activity batches, usage records, the acceptance record, crash reports, diagnostic reports, feedback and deletion requests. The recipient is a mailbox of ours, from which mail may go on to another of ours. Service providers act on our behalf. Subscription payments are processed by the store through which you subscribe; we do not receive your payment details.

6. Retention

Usage, learning and diagnostic information is kept while it remains useful for the purposes described above. No code of ours deletes records automatically in response to a request; we remove them manually. Emailed copies are retained unless that is also done by hand.

7. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures, including encryption in transit. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. International users and legal bases

Where a legal basis is required, we rely on your agreement (the usage switch and your decision to send diagnostics or feedback) and on our legitimate interest in operating and improving the app. Information may be processed in the United States.

9. Children

We design our apps to collect only the information described in this policy. If you believe information has been provided to us that you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the version of the first-run notice changes and the notice is shown again.

11. Contact

EqualInformation, LLC. Questions: support@equalinformation.com. Deletion and data requests: diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
