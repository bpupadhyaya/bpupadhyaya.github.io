Biochemical Engineering: Zymex Privacy Policy

Biochemical Engineering: Zymex is published by EqualInformation, LLC. This policy was last updated on 2026-11-23.

Summary

Zymex is a study app for a Biochemical Engineering course. This page says what the app stores, what it shares and with whom. There is no registration and no account.

What stays on the device

Study progress, settings and unlock timers live on the device itself. We never receive them, and uninstalling the app clears them.

What we never ask for

We do not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, health data or any identifying details, and we do not sell personal data.

Advertising and rewarded videos

To stay free, the app uses Google AdMob: short ads at natural breaks (never in a timed test), plus optional rewarded videos that unlock Plus features for 24 hours. The order on iOS is the tracking prompt, then Google's consent message where required, then the ad SDK. With your consent Google may use an advertising identifier and ad-interaction details to deliver and measure ads; without it the ads are generic. See Google's advertising data page.

Age

This is an undergraduate-level study app for adults and older students, not for children under 13; we do not knowingly gather data from them.

Monthly subscription

An optional auto-renewing monthly subscription removes ads and unlocks all Plus features. There is no free trial or introductory price. The App Store or Google Play shows the price before you buy and the plan renews until you cancel. Payment is handled by Apple or Google; we never see card details and the app only learns whether your subscription is active. Manage or cancel it in your store account settings. See the terms of use.

Choices, questions and requests

Change tracking permission any time in iOS Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking, and reopen the ad consent message from the app's About screen where available. For privacy questions or requests to access or delete data we might hold about you, write to bpupadhyaya@equalinformation.com; the support page has more help.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
