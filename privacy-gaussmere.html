Gaussmere: AP Physics C E&M - Privacy Policy

Gaussmere works without an account. Your study progress, answers, notes and settings are stored only on your device.

We do not collect your name, email or location and cannot see your progress.

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob. Where required, the app first asks for your consent; on iPhone and iPad it may also ask whether apps may track you. Google may use an advertising identifier to show and measure ads. You can decline and keep studying.

Subscribers see no ads. Payments are handled by Apple or Google; we never see your payment details.

When you open a reference page the app loads it from the publisher's site, which sees your request like any web visit.
