Privacy Policy: Human Geography AP: Glenvane

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Contact: support@equalinformation.com. Last updated 2026-11-27.

1. Introduction and scope

This Privacy Policy describes how EqualInformation, LLC (we, us) handles information in connection with Human Geography AP: Glenvane (the app), version 1.0.0 and later, on iOS and Android. It applies to the app only.

2. Information we collect

Information you choose to provide: diagnostic reports you decide to send, with any note you add; and feedback or suggestions you submit (category, message, optional role, source link, rating) and, only if you tick the contact option, an email address for a reply.

Diagnostic and app-usage information collected with your agreement or as permitted by applicable law: device and operating-system information (model, system version, processor, memory, display size, language, region and time zone, accessibility and display settings), app version, performance and memory indicators, crash and error information, a random app identifier, and how features of the app are used. For learning features this includes lessons viewed, questions attempted, whether answers were correct, time taken, progress estimates, study suggestions made to you and what you did next, and activity in the labs.

Information we do not collect: your name, contacts, precise location, photographs, files, advertising identifiers, or the answers you type into free-response boxes, which stay on your device. Purchases are processed by your store; we do not receive payment details.

3. How we use information

To operate, maintain, secure and support the app; to diagnose problems; to provide and personalise the learning experience; to understand how the app is used; for research and development; for analytics and improvement of our apps and educational content; to read and act on feedback; and to comply with legal obligations.

4. Your choices

When you first open the app you see a notice and a switch for sharing usage information. The switch is off by default in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and wherever your region cannot be determined, until you turn it on. You can change it at any time under Settings, Privacy and data. Diagnostic reports and feedback are sent only when you choose to send them. To request deletion of your usage information use Delete my usage data in the app, or write to diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

5. Storage and service providers

Information is stored in our Google Workspace services (Google Sheets and Drive) and notification email, and is processed by service providers acting on our behalf. Copies of Glenvane installed from an app store can have what they send emailed to our own mailboxes: your agreement record, learning and usage batches, crash and diagnostic material, feedback and requests for deletion. An email may be forwarded to a different mailbox that we also hold. It is transmitted over encrypted connections.

6. Retention

Usage and learning information is kept while it is useful for the purposes above. What a deletion request produces is a logged note and an email to us; removal itself is manual. Previously emailed records remain unless they are removed by hand too.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. International users and legal bases

Where applicable law requires a legal basis we rely on your consent or on our legitimate interests in operating and improving the app. Information may be processed in the United States.

9. Children

We design our apps to collect only the information described in this policy. If you believe information has been provided to us that you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

10. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes the notice shown when you first open the app changes version and is shown again.

11. Contact

EqualInformation, LLC, support@equalinformation.com. Deletion and data requests: diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
