Hemoflow: Hemodynamics Lab - Privacy Policy

Equal Information LLC. Last updated 9 November 2026.

What stays on your device

Hemoflow is an offline education app. Your lab notebook, quiz scores, streak and text-size choice are saved only on your device. We do not run accounts and we cannot see this data.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. With your permission (Apple's tracking prompt and, where required, Google's consent form) AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier and ad interaction data to show relevant ads and measure them. If you decline, you still get ads, but they are not personalised. The monthly ad-free subscription removes ads.

Purchases

The ad-free subscription is handled by Apple. We receive no payment details.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email, location, health information, contacts, photos or microphone data. Hemoflow does not use or ask for any of these.

Children

Hemoflow is for students and general learners and is not directed to children under 13.

Contact

Questions or error reports: support@equalinformation.com. See also the support page.

Education only. Hemoflow is not medical advice and not a tool for judging any real patient or scan.
