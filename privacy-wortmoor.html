Wortmoor Privacy Policy

German Language AP: Wortmoor · EqualInformation, LLC · effective 27 November 2026

1. Introduction and scope

This policy explains how EqualInformation, LLC handles information in the Wortmoor app, a practice app for AP German Language and Culture, on Apple and Android devices.

2. Information we collect

Information you choose to provide. Diagnostic reports you send and any note you add; feedback and suggestions you submit (category, message, optional role, level, source link and rating); and, only if you tick the contact option, an email address for a reply.

Diagnostic and app-usage information collected with your agreement or as permitted by applicable law:

  • device and operating-system information such as model, system version, processor, memory, display size, language, region, time zone, accessibility and display settings;
  • app version, performance and memory indicators, crash and error information, and a random identifier created by the app;
  • how learning features are used and perform: lessons, sources, labs and commissions opened, questions attempted, whether answers are correct, time taken, explanations opened, estimated strength by skill, study sessions, suggested next steps and what you do with them;
  • answers typed into grammar question fields and in-app search terms, shortened and automatically cleaned of recognisable personal identifiers.

A record that you accepted the first-run notice (including the app version, language and region, and a random identifier) is sent when you accept it, including when the optional usage switch is off.

Information we do not collect: your name, contacts, precise location, photographs, files, advertising identifiers, your essays, outline notes or commission drafts, or your voice recordings.

3. Microphone, recordings and speech

The microphone is used only while you tap Record. Recordings are stored in the app's private storage on your device, are not sent to us or to anyone else, and can be deleted in the app at any time; removing the app removes them. German audio is produced by the speech engine installed on your device; your device or system provider's own terms apply to that engine.

4. How we use information

To operate, maintain, secure and support the app; to diagnose problems; to provide and personalise the learning experience; to understand how the app is used; for research and development; for analytics and to improve our apps and educational content; to read and act on feedback; and to comply with legal obligations.

5. Your choices

  • The first-run notice includes a separate switch for optional usage information. It is off by default in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland until you turn it on.
  • You can change it at any time under Settings, Privacy and data.
  • Diagnostics and feedback are sent only when you choose to send them.
  • You can ask for deletion with the in-app “Delete my usage data” request or by writing to diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

6. Purchases

Wortmoor Plus subscriptions are processed by Apple or Google. We do not receive your payment card details. The app asks the store whether a subscription is active.

7. Storage and service providers

Information is transmitted over encrypted connections, stored in our Google Workspace services (Google Sheets and Drive) and notification email, and processed by service providers acting on our behalf. A record arriving from a copy of Wortmoor installed through an app store may also be set out in an email to us. This includes vocabulary and study batches, usage information, the acceptance record, crash entries, diagnostic reports, feedback and deletion requests, which go to our mailboxes and may be forwarded to a further mailbox of ours. It may be processed in the United States.

8. Retention and security

Usage and learning information is kept while it is useful for the purposes above; deletion requests are answered by deleting records by hand. The in-app request erases nothing automatically; mailed copies stay with us unless manually removed. We use reasonable safeguards, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

9. Legal bases and international users

Where the law requires it, we rely on your consent; otherwise on our legitimate interests in operating and improving the app. Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct or delete information; contact us to exercise them.

10. Children

We design our apps to collect only the information described in this policy. If you believe information has been provided to us that you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

11. Changes

We will post material changes here, and when the first-run notice changes the app shows it again.

12. Contact

EqualInformation, LLC. General questions: support@equalinformation.com. Deletion and data requests: diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
