Support AllIn Wallet

Your keys. Your coins. Multi-chain self-custody for everyone.

AllIn Wallet is a fully non-custodial multi-chain crypto wallet. Your private keys never leave your device. We never have access to your funds.

What AllIn Wallet Does

Multi-Chain Portfolio

Manage BTC, ETH, SOL, ADA, DOGE, XRP, DOT, POL, LINK, JUP, USDC, and USDT across 8 blockchains from a single app. Real-time prices, balances, and portfolio allocation.

Cross-Chain Swaps

Swap any supported token via Li.Fi (EVM + Solana) or THORChain (BTC, DOGE, XRP). Configurable slippage. ERC-20 approval safety checks with validated spender addresses.

Hardware Key Security

First-class Solana Seeker and Saga Seed Vault integration. Your private key lives inside the phone's secure element and never leaves the hardware — even during transaction signing.

Production-Grade Security

Private keys zero-filled after use, bcrypt-hashed credentials, exponential lockout, biometric auth, 15-minute auto-lock, and WalletConnect v2 for safe dApp connectivity.
