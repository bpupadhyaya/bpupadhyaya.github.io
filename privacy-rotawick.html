Rotawick Privacy Policy

Effective 8 November 2026. Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC.

Your rota stays on your phone

Rotawick is a shift schedule planner. The names, shifts, hourly rates, notes and settings you enter are stored only on your device. There is no account and no sign-in, and we cannot see your rota. If you share a calendar file, it goes only where you choose to send it.

Reminders

If you turn on shift reminders, the app asks for notification permission and schedules reminders locally on your device.

Ads

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device advertising identifier and approximate location to choose and measure ads. On iOS the app asks for your permission before tracking, and where the law requires it shows a Google consent form so you can choose how ad data is used. You can decline and still use every feature; ads are then not personalised. Google explains its practices at policies.google.com/technologies/ads. You can remove ads with the optional monthly ad-free subscription or by earning ad-free time in the app.

Subscription

The optional ad-free subscription is handled by Apple. We never receive your payment details. Manage or cancel it in your Apple Account settings.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal data, collect your contacts, photos, microphone or camera, or build a profile of you. Rotawick works fully offline.

Not legal or payroll advice

Overtime and rest warnings and pay figures are planning aids based on limits you can change. They are not legal, tax or payroll advice.

Children

Rotawick is a work planner and is not directed to children under 13.
