QR Scanner & Maker: Quadwick: Support

Quadwick is a QR code scanner and generator app for iPhone, iPad and Android. Quadwick scans QR codes and barcodes and creates QR codes and barcodes, entirely on your phone. No account is needed.

Common questions

  • Is what I scan sent anywhere? No. Scanning, the link safety check, history and code creation all happen on your phone.
  • What can I scan and create? Scan QR codes and common barcodes. Create QR codes for links, text, Wi-Fi, contacts, email, SMS, phone numbers, locations, events and more, plus Code 128, EAN-13, UPC-A and Code 39 barcodes. Export PNG, SVG or PDF.
  • Will my printed code scan? The live "will it scan" meter checks contrast, quiet zone, size and logo coverage, and does a real test decode. Always test a printed code with a phone before using it widely.
  • How do I delete my scan history? Open History in the app and delete entries, or clear all.
  • What are credits? Scanning and basic black and white codes are free. Styled, high-resolution, SVG and PDF exports use 1 credit per design. You start with 2 credits; watch a short ad to earn another, or go ad-free.
  • How do I remove ads? Open the purchase screen in Quadwick (Settings or More, or the ad-free prompt) and choose the ad-free monthly subscription or the one-time Unlock. Both remove ads and credit limits.
  • How do I restore a purchase? Open Settings (or the purchase screen) and tap Restore purchases, using the same Apple Account or Google account you bought with. This is needed after reinstalling or changing device.
  • How do I cancel the subscription? iPhone and iPad: Settings, your name, Subscriptions. Android: Google Play, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel it.
  • Text too small? Use A- and A+ on the screens, or change the system text size.
  • Can I change my ad choices? Yes. Use the privacy choices for ads in the app where shown, or your device's privacy settings.
