Jigsaw Atelier privacy policy

Last updated 7 November 2026

The short version

Jigsaw Atelier is a jigsaw puzzle app published by EqualInformation. You do not create an account, and the app never asks for your name, email address, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.

Stored on your device only

Your finished puzzles, stars, records, daily streak, settings and the table you last left are saved in a small file inside the app. That file never leaves your device and we cannot read it. Every picture is painted by the app itself while you play, so nothing is downloaded to build a puzzle.

Advertising

The app is free and is supported by advertising from Google AdMob (banners, and short videos between puzzles). When ads are on, Google may process your device's advertising identifier, approximate location and basic usage data to choose and measure ads. On first launch iOS asks whether the app may track you; if you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalised. Where the law requires it, a consent form from Google's Funding Choices appears first, and you can reopen it from Settings.

Ad-free options

You can switch ads off for a month by watching rewarded videos, or by subscribing to the monthly ad-free pass. Payments are taken and managed by Apple. We never see your card or Apple ID details; we only learn from StoreKit whether an entitlement is active.

Data we collect ourselves

None. EqualInformation runs no server for this app and receives no analytics from it.

Children

Jigsaw Atelier is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13.

Your controls

  • Tracking: iOS Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking.
  • Subscription: Settings, your name, Subscriptions.
  • Erase everything: delete the app.
