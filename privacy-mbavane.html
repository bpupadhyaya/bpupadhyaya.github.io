GMAT Focus Prep: Mbavane: Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Contact: support@equalinformation.com. Effective 26 November 2026.

1. Introduction and scope

This Privacy Policy describes the information handled by GMAT Focus Prep: Mbavane (the app), published by EqualInformation, LLC (we, us). It applies to the iOS and Android versions of the app. The app is a study aid for candidates preparing for the GMAT Focus Edition examination.

2. Information we collect

A. Information you choose to provide

  • Diagnostic reports that you choose to send from Settings, and any note you add to them.
  • Feedback and suggestions that you submit: the category, your message, an optional role and level, an optional source link, an optional rating and, only if you tick the contact option, an email address for a reply.

B. Diagnostic and app-usage information collected with your agreement or as permitted by applicable law

  • Device and operating-system information: device model, operating-system version, processor and memory indicators, display size, language, region, time zone, accessibility and display settings, and the app version and build.
  • Performance, memory, crash and error information.
  • A randomly generated identifier of the installation of the app, which is not your name, your contact details or an advertising identifier.
  • How the app is used, including the screens and features you open and how long you use them, and the state of the optional subscription (free or Plus).
  • How the learning features are used and perform: lessons and guided builds viewed, practice sets, quizzes and mock examinations started and finished, the questions you attempt, the option you choose, whether the answer is correct, the time taken, hints and explanations opened, estimated progress by topic, the recommendations the app makes to you and what follows them, activity in labs, and settings such as text size.
  • That a coverage search was made and how many results it showed. From version 1.0.1 neither the search words nor any typed answer is included (the earlier version is described below under Typed answers and search words).

C. Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, contacts, precise location, photographs or files, advertising identifiers, payment details (these are handled by Apple or Google), or, from version 1.0.1, anything you type into the app, such as answers, search words and notes, apart from the feedback and diagnostic notes you choose to send.

Typed answers and search words: current and earlier versions

Version 1.0.1 and later. What you type into the app, including answers to build steps and the words you search for on the coverage screen, stays on your device and is not part of the usage information. While the optional usage setting is on, the app records only that a coverage search was made and how many results it showed. Whatever you write in a feedback message or a diagnostic note is different: it is sent only when you choose to send it.

Earlier version still installed. Version 1.0 behaves differently until you update it. While the optional usage setting is on, it sends the answers you type into build steps, and on Apple devices also the words you type into the coverage screen search box (from three characters, as the text changes). Each is cut to 200 characters, with anything recognised as an email address, web link, telephone number or card or identity number removed. In that version the text under the usage switch mentions answers to questions but not search words, and the first-run notice says that nothing you type other than answers is collected; on Apple devices, where search words are also sent, those statements are not correct. To stop this in version 1.0, turn the usage setting off under Settings, Privacy and data.

3. How we use information

We use information to operate, maintain, secure and support the app; to diagnose and correct problems; to provide and personalise your study experience; to understand how the app is used; for research and development; for analytics and improvement of our apps and educational content; to read and act on the feedback you send; and to comply with legal obligations. Information may be transferred as part of a financing, merger, acquisition or similar business transaction, subject to the commitments in this policy.

4. Your choices

When you first open the app, a notice is shown and you may accept it. The switch for sharing usage information is off until you turn it on if you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, and on by default elsewhere; you can change it at any time under Settings, Privacy and data. Diagnostics and feedback are sent only when you choose to send them. To request deletion of usage information associated with your installation, use Delete my usage data in Settings, or write to diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

5. Storage and service providers

Information is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored in our Google Workspace services (Google Sheets and Drive) and notification email, and is processed by service providers acting on our behalf. Purchases are processed by Apple or Google, which handle your payment details under their own policies. Source links open public web pages that apply their own privacy practices.

6. Retention

Usage and learning information is kept for as long as it is useful for the purposes described above, and deleted on a valid request. Diagnostic reports and feedback are kept until they have been reviewed and for as long as they remain useful.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. International users

Information may be processed in the United States. Where the law requires a legal basis, we rely on your agreement for optional usage information and on our legitimate interests in operating and improving the app for the other purposes described.

9. Children

We design the app to collect only the information described in this policy. If you believe that information has been provided to us that you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If the notice shown on first use changes, it is shown again. The effective date above shows the latest version.
