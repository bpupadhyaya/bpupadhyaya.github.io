GMAT Focus Prep: Mbavane: Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Contact: support@equalinformation.com. Last updated 24 November 2026.

What stays on your device

Your answers, review schedule, concept levels, mock results, build progress and settings (including text size) are stored only on your device. The app has no account system and does not send them to us.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect personal information, does not track you, and does not share data with other companies.

Mbavane Plus

If you buy Mbavane Plus, the purchase is handled by Apple or Google. We see only whether a subscription is active on your device, never your payment details.

Sources you open

Source and reference links open public web pages inside the app. Those sites load from their publishers and apply their own privacy practices.

Audience

The app is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed to children.
