Spacecraft Systems: Solquint Support

Spacecraft Systems: Solquint is a Spacecraft Systems course study app. Published by EqualInformation, LLC.

Restore a purchase

Reinstalled the app or changed device? Sign in with the same Apple Account (or Google account on Android), open More and tap Restore Purchases. No new charge is made.

Manage or cancel the subscription

Apple or Google manages the subscription. On iOS go to Settings, your name, Subscriptions; on Android open Google Play, Profile, Payments and subscriptions. Cancel at least a day before the renewal date to skip the next charge; the store handles refunds.

Get help

Email bpupadhyaya@equalinformation.com with your device model, system version and a short description of the problem. We usually reply within a few days. Privacy questions and data requests go to the same address.

Report a content error

Found a wrong answer, a typo or an unclear explanation? Email bpupadhyaya@equalinformation.com with the topic and question text (a screenshot helps) and, if you can, the source you checked. Confirmed mistakes are fixed in an update.

Common questions

How does the adaptive path work? It tracks what you know topic by topic and chooses what to practise or review next from your mistakes, using only data on your device. You can move to the course-order path at any time and keep your progress.

Can I choose how long a quiz is and how hard? Yes, you set the quiz length and the level yourself.

What are Plus features? The extra labs, levels, modes and tools marked with a lock in the app. The monthly subscription unlocks all of them. You never need it to study the free core course.

Does the app need an internet connection? No. Lessons, questions, worked solutions, mock exams and the interactive labs work offline. A connection is needed only to buy or restore the subscription.

Where do the further-reading links lead? To the sources the lesson cites. We link to them rather than copy them, so their licences remain with their owners.

Is this a replacement for my course? No, Solquint is a companion for study and practice, not a substitute for a class or instructor.
