IB Geography Prep: Cartovane: Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Contact: support@equalinformation.com. Last updated 2026-11-27.

1. Introduction and scope

This Privacy Policy explains how EqualInformation, LLC ("we", "us") handles information in connection with IB Geography Prep: Cartovane (the "app"), version 1.0 and later, and applies to the app only. The app has no account system and works offline: lessons, case studies, questions, simulations and your progress are stored on your device.

2A. Information you choose to provide

Diagnostic reports: if you open Settings, Privacy and data and choose Send diagnostics, the app shows you the full content of the report, and it is sent only after you tap Send.

Feedback and suggestions: if you choose to send feedback, a suggestion or a problem report, we receive the category and your message (up to 2,000 characters, from which email addresses, telephone numbers and links are removed automatically) and, where you supply them, a role (for example IB student, teacher or tutor, parent or guardian, or other), a level or context, a source link and a rating from 1 to 5. We receive an email address only if you tick the option allowing us to reply, and we use it only for that reply.

2B. Diagnostic and app-usage information

With your agreement, or as permitted by applicable law, the app can send diagnostic and app-usage information while the optional usage setting is on. This comprises: app version and build; device model; operating system and version; processor details; graphics processor name; memory; screen size; language, region and time zone; text-size setting and a summary of accessibility and display settings; network type; a battery-level range; whether the free or Plus entitlement is active; a randomly generated app identifier; session timing; the screens you open and the time spent on them; lesson activity such as scroll depth and time; your quiz, practice, unit-test and mock-paper attempts (the question, its theme, topic and specification point, whether the answer was correct, time taken, the option chosen and hints used); answers you type into response fields, shortened and automatically cleaned of recognisable email addresses, telephone numbers, links and card or identity numbers; progress estimates for each part of the course; the adaptive-learning decisions the app makes and their outcomes; your use of the Classical and Real-World views, study-plan pins, focus and exam-date choices; use of simulations and guided builds (control names and value ranges); the maps, graphs and sources you open; interactions with the subscription screen; performance indicators such as render times and dropped-frame ranges; and crash and error information.

When you accept the first-run notice, a short acceptance record is always sent, whatever the setting: a consent identifier, the time, the app, platform, version and build, the notice version and a hash of its text, the state of the usage setting, the random app identifier, and your country, locale and time zone. A notification email about that record is sent to EqualInformation.

2C. Information we do not collect

The app does not collect your name, your contacts, your precise location, your photographs or files, or other text you write in the app, such as notes and fieldwork drafts. Those remain on your device.

3. How we use information

We use information to operate, maintain, secure and support the app; to diagnose problems; to personalise your revision and the adaptive-learning features; to understand how the app is used; for research and development; for analytics and improvement of our apps and educational content; to read and act on feedback and suggestions; and to comply with legal obligations.

4. Your choices

When you first open the app, a notice describes these practices and offers a separate usage switch. The switch is off by default in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and wherever your region cannot be determined, until you turn it on, and is on elsewhere. You may change it at any time under Settings, Privacy and data, where you can also read the first-run notice again. Diagnostics and feedback are sent only when you tap Send.

To request deletion of your usage data, use Delete my usage data in the app or email diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

5. Storage and service providers

Information is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections to a web application operated by EqualInformation, LLC, which records it in our Google Workspace services (Google Sheets and Drive). In addition, our mailboxes can receive the substance of records from store copies of Cartovane, among them fieldwork and quiz activity batches, usage events, the acceptance record, crashes, diagnostics, feedback and requests for deletion. Forwarding to another mailbox of ours is possible. Google acts as a service provider on our behalf. Apple or Google processes subscription payments for Cartovane Plus, and we do not receive your payment details. Source and reference links open public web pages inside the app; those sites apply their own privacy practices.

6. Retention

Usage, learning and diagnostic information is kept for as long as it is useful for the purposes described above. Requests made as in section 4 are processed manually. Because deletion is never automatic and email is kept apart from stored records, mailed copies survive unless we delete them by hand.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information, including encryption in transit. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. International users and legal bases

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we rely on your agreement (the usage switch and the actions you take to send diagnostics or feedback) and on our legitimate interest in maintaining and improving the app. Information may be processed in the United States.

9. Children

We design our apps to collect only the information described in this policy. If you believe information has been provided to us that you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If a change is material, the version of the first-run notice changes and the notice is shown again.

EqualInformation, LLC. Questions: support@equalinformation.com. Deletion and data requests: diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
