Support Kindly

Help us make mental wellness accessible to everyone, everywhere.

AI-powered mental wellness for everyone — mood tracking, guided journaling, breathing exercises, coping strategies, and crisis resources. Free core tools, with Pro and Clinical tiers for deeper care.

Kindly is an AI mental wellness companion designed for two audiences: individuals seeking daily mental health support, and healthcare professionals who need clinical tools for patient care. It is built and maintained by a single developer who believes that mental wellness tools should be accessible to every person on the planet, regardless of income or location.

Every feature is designed with privacy at its core — all data stays on your device with no accounts, no cloud sync, and no tracking. Kindly is available on iOS and Android. Core wellness tools (mood tracking, AI chat, journaling, breathing exercises, affirmations, coping toolkit, gratitude journal, daily check-in, and crisis safety resources) are free forever. Pro features ($4.99/month) unlock advanced analytics, sleep tracking, habit tracking, and more. Clinical features ($19.99/month) provide patient management, clinical notes, outcome tracking, and institutional analytics for healthcare professionals.

Why Kindly Exists

Nearly 1 billion people worldwide live with a mental health condition. In the US alone, 1 in 5 adults experience mental illness each year, yet fewer than half receive treatment. The global shortage of mental health professionals means millions of people have no access to care at any price.

Therapy costs $100–$250 per session in the US. Mental health apps typically charge $60–$120 per year for basic mood tracking. Many people who need support the most cannot afford these costs.

Kindly takes a different approach: free, evidence-based wellness tools for everyone, sustained by subscriptions from professionals and advanced users. The free tier includes 9 fully functional features — not a crippled trial. The Pro tier offers deeper self-care tools. The Clinical tier provides professional-grade patient management that healthcare providers can use in their practice.

Kindly uses CBT, DBT, and ACT techniques conversationally. It is not a therapist and always recommends professional help for serious concerns. If it detects signs of crisis, it immediately surfaces the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline and other emergency resources.

Disclaimer: Kindly is a wellness companion, not a substitute for professional mental health care. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or mental health condition. If you are experiencing a mental health crisis, please contact a qualified professional or call 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline). Always consult a licensed healthcare provider for medical advice.
