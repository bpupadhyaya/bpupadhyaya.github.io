Rotawick Support

Rotawick is a weekly shift schedule planner for shift workers and small teams. It works offline and needs no account.

Quick help

  • Add a shift: tap an empty cell on the Week board, or the plus button. Quick-pick buttons fill in Early, Day, Late or Night.
  • Swap or give away: press and hold a shift, or open it and choose Swap or give away.
  • Overtime flags: set the weekly limit under More, Overtime and rest limits. The limits are yours to set.
  • Share to a calendar: More, Share to calendar (.ics) opens in any calendar app.
  • Bigger text: use A- and A+ at the top of every screen. Rotawick also follows your iPhone text size.
  • Remove ads: More, Remove ads. Restore purchases is on the same screen.
