Background Remover: Peelwick: Privacy Policy

Peelwick: background remover and sticker maker. Effective 14 November 2026. Publisher: Equal Information LLC.

Peelwick removes the background from a photo, lets you refine it with brushes, adds a backdrop and makes sticker packs, entirely on your phone.

What stays on your device

Your photos never leave your device. Peelwick removes backgrounds locally with a small cut-out model that is bundled inside the app, so there is no server, no upload and no account. A finished picture or sticker pack leaves your device only when you tap Share, Save, or add a pack to a messaging app on your phone, and we never receive it; once a file is handed to another app, that app's own policy applies. Credits and settings are stored on your device.

Permissions

You pick a photo with the system picker. On iPhone and iPad the app asks for add-only permission to save finished pictures to your library when you choose Save. To add a sticker pack to a messaging app, the app passes the pack to that app on your device through the system. The app does not use the camera, microphone, contacts or location.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob (a banner on the home screen and a short full-screen ad after finished actions). Your photos are never used for ads or sent to AdMob. An optional short video can be watched to earn one more credit. On iPhone and iPad the system first asks whether the app may track you (App Tracking Transparency); in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some US states a Google consent message also appears (Google User Messaging Platform). On Android the Google consent message appears where the law requires it. You can decline and keep using every feature. With your permission, AdMob and its partners may use your device advertising identifier, approximate location derived from your IP address, and ad interaction and diagnostic data to choose, show and measure ads. See Google's advertising policies. You can change your choices later in the app (privacy choices for ads, where shown) and in your device settings. Ads are not served to people who have an ad-free purchase active.

Purchases

The optional ad-free monthly subscription and the one-time Unlock are handled by Apple or Google Play. We never see your payment details. Credits, purchase status and your settings are stored on your device.

What is collected

Collected by us: nothing, and your photos are never collected. Collected by Google AdMob in the free version: device identifiers (advertising identifier), approximate location from IP address, and ad interaction and diagnostic data, used for third-party advertising and measuring ads.

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information, build profiles about you, run our own analytics, or ask you to create an account. No personal data is sent to us.

Children

Peelwick is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Nothing is sent to us in any case.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new effective date.
