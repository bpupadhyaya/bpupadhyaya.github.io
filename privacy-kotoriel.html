Kotoriel Privacy Policy

Japanese Language AP: Kotoriel · EqualInformation, LLC · Effective 26 November 2026

1. About this policy

This policy explains what information the Kotoriel study app (the app) handles. The app is published by EqualInformation, LLC (we, us). It applies to the iPhone, iPad and Android versions. The app has no user account and does not ask for your name to use it.

2. What stays on your device

Your study history, skill estimates, plan, settings, flash-card schedule, self-scores, the text you type in writing tasks and planner notes, and your voice recordings are stored in the app on your device. Typed compositions, notes and recordings are not sent to us. Recordings are made only after you tap Record and grant microphone access; you can delete them one at a time or all together. Removing the app removes this information from the device. Listening exercises use the speech voice installed on your device; your device maker's terms apply to that voice.

3. Information sent to us

The record that you accepted the first-run notice

When you accept the notice, the app sends a record of that acceptance: a record number, the time, the app and version, the notice version and a fingerprint of its text, your sharing choice, a random installation identifier, and your device's region, locale and time zone. If sharing is on at that moment, the record also includes the text of the notice and the device details listed below.

Usage, learning and diagnostic information, when sharing is on

  • Device and software details: model, operating-system version, processor, memory, display and window size, language, region, time zone, accessibility and display settings, network type, battery level range, app version and plan (free or Plus).
  • Use of the app: screens, lessons, source sets, journeys, studio tools, curriculum-map and source views, settings and subscription-screen interactions, session length.
  • Learning activity: which question you answered, whether it was correct, the choice selected, time taken, mock section results, the app's estimate for each skill, what it recommended, whether you followed it and what happened afterwards.
  • Performance and fault information: loading and scrolling measures, errors and crash reports.

These records carry the random installation identifier. They are not linked by us to your name, and we do not use an advertising identifier or follow you across other companies' apps or websites. We do not collect your contacts, photos, files or precise location.

Information you choose to send

  • Diagnostic report: you see a preview first, and it is sent only when you tap Send. It includes device and app details, storage and memory figures, a short technical event log, recent crash signatures and any note you add.
  • Feedback and suggestions: the category and message you write, an optional rating, role, level and source link, the screen or item concerned and, only if you tick the contact box, an email address used to reply to you. The address is sent and kept together with that message and the random installation identifier. Please do not put personal details in messages.

4. Why we use it

To run, secure, fix and support the app; to adapt practice and study how well the adaptive features work; to research and improve our apps and educational material; to read and respond to feedback; to keep a record of acceptance of the notice; and to meet legal obligations. If our business or the app is reorganised, financed, merged or acquired, information may pass to the successor, which remains bound by this policy until it gives notice of a change.

5. Your choices

Usage sharing has its own switch on the first screen and under More, Privacy and data. It is off until you turn it on in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and where your region cannot be determined; elsewhere it is on when first shown, and you may turn it off before or after continuing. With sharing off, the app still sends the short acceptance record, and sends diagnostics, feedback or a deletion request only when you ask it to. To ask us to delete usage information for your installation, use Delete my usage data in the app or write to diagnostics@equalinformation.com. Depending on where you live you may also have rights to access, correct, delete or object; contact us at the same address.

6. Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple or Google through your store account. We do not receive your payment card details. The app asks the store whether a subscription is active so it can open the matching features.

7. Where it is kept and who handles it

Information sent to us is transmitted over encrypted connections and held in our Google Workspace services (spreadsheets, file storage and email). Google processes it on our behalf as a service provider. The services may process information in the United States and other countries. Where the law requires a legal basis, we rely on your consent for optional sharing and for what you choose to send, and on our legitimate interests in keeping a record of acceptance and keeping the app secure and working.

8. Retention and security

We keep usage and learning information while it remains useful for the purposes above, acceptance records for as long as we may need to show what was agreed, and messages while we deal with them and afterwards where useful for support. We act on valid deletion requests unless the law requires us to keep something. We take reasonable measures to protect information, although no system is completely secure.

9. Children

We design the app to collect only the information described here. If you believe information has been provided to us that you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

10. Changes and contact

If we change this policy materially, we will update this page and, where the first-run notice changes, show the notice again. Contact: EqualInformation, LLC, support@equalinformation.com. Data and deletion requests: diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
