Kotoriel privacy statement

Version of 26 November 2026, applying from that day

Who this is about

This statement is for the Kotoriel app on iPhone, iPad and Android. EqualInformation, LLC is responsible for it. Kotoriel has no sign-up and never asks who you are in order to work.

Kept on your own device

Your answers history, skill estimates, study plan and exam date, settings, card schedule, self-scores, anything you write in the writing tasks and planner, and your voice recordings are saved by the app on your device. Removing Kotoriel from the device removes these along with it. The microphone is used only after you press Record, and you can remove recordings singly or all at once. Spoken Japanese in listening tasks is produced by the voice your device provides, under its maker's terms.

The record made when you accept the notice

Pressing the agree button sends us a short record: a number for that acceptance, when it happened, the app and its version, which wording of the notice was shown (a version label and a fingerprint of the text), whether sharing was on, a random number made up for this installation, your region, language-region setting and time zone, and whether the copy of the app came from a store. If sharing was on, the record also holds the wording itself and the device details described next.

Study and device records, only while sharing is on

  • About the device: model, system version, processor type, the graphics name where the device reports one, memory, screen, text-size and accessibility settings, kind of network, rough battery level, language, region and time zone.
  • About use: sittings and their length, screens opened and how quickly they draw and scroll, lessons and how far they were read, studio benches and the numbers set on their controls, curriculum-map and source openings, plan changes (that a change happened, not the date or words involved) and the subscription screen.
  • About learning: which question was answered, the option picked, whether it was right, how long it took, assessment totals, the app's estimate for each skill, what it recommended and what came of it.
  • About faults: the type of error and where in the program it happened. The wording of an error is left out.

Each record is filed under the installation number. We do not attach your name to it. Nothing you type or search for is part of these records, and neither is any sound. The app uses no advertising identifier, does not track you across other companies' services, and does not read your contacts, photos, files or exact whereabouts.

Things you send yourself

  • A diagnostic report. The whole report is shown first. It holds device, memory and storage figures, app settings, a short list of recent app events, notes of recent faults, the installation number and any note you add. Nothing in it travels until you press the send button.
  • Feedback. Your topic and message, and, if you choose to give them, a role, a course or level, a source link and a rating, with the screen or question it concerns. If you tick the box asking to be contacted, the email address you type travels with that message and the installation number. A press of Helpful or Not helpful sends that choice about that explanation.
  • An erasure request. It carries the installation number so that we can find the records.

These work whether sharing is on or off. What you write to us should be about Kotoriel; keep out anything that would identify you or someone else.

What the information is for

Running the app and keeping it safe; finding and mending faults; shaping practice to the learner and checking that this works; improving Kotoriel, our other apps and our teaching material, including research and development; reading and answering what you send; showing what was agreed; and meeting legal duties. Information may pass to a successor if our business or this app is financed, reorganised, merged or sold, and stays subject to this statement until the successor gives notice of a change.

Your say

The first screen has a switch for the study and device records, and it stays available in the app under More, Privacy and data. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, and wherever the region cannot be told, it starts off; elsewhere it starts on and you can turn it off before agreeing or at any later time. Turning it off also discards records that had not yet left. To have earlier records erased, use Erase my usage records in the app or write to diagnostics@equalinformation.com. The law where you live may give you rights to see, correct, erase, restrict or object to the use of information about you; write to the same address.

Store payments

Apple or Google handles the subscription purchase and billing. Your card details are not given to us. The app only asks the store which subscriptions are active.

Handling, storage and time kept

Messages travel over encrypted connections and are stored in our Google Workspace spreadsheets, file storage and mail, where Google acts for us. This may involve servers in the United States and elsewhere. Where a legal basis is needed, sharing and your own messages rest on your consent; the acceptance record and keeping the app working and secure rest on our legitimate interests. Study records are kept while they remain useful for the purposes above, acceptance records while we may need to show what was agreed, and messages while they are being dealt with and as long as they help with support. We honour valid erasure requests unless a law obliges us to keep something. We protect information with reasonable care; no method is wholly safe.

Young learners and families

The app asks for as little as its features need, as set out above. A parent or guardian who believes something has reached us that should not be held can write to us, and we will remove it when the request is verified as far as the installation number allows.

Updates and questions

A material change will appear on this page, and a changed first-use notice is shown again in the app. For anything about Kotoriel itself, write to support@equalinformation.com; for anything about the records described here, to diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
