Privacy Policy — PlainDental

Effective Date: April 19, 2026

  • /

1 Who We Are

PlainDental is provided by EqualInformation, LLC.

If you have privacy questions or requests, you may contact us at:
Email: bpupadhyaya@equalinformation.com
Contact page: equalinformation.weebly.com/contact.html | bpupadhyaya.github.io#contact

2 Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the PlainDental app (iOS bundle identifier: com.equalinformation.plaindental.app; Android package name: com.equalinformation.plaindental.app). PlainDental is available on iOS (App Store) and Android (Google Play).

3 Information PlainDental May Access or Store

PlainDental is a dental health education app. Depending on how you use the app, PlainDental may store information on your device, including:

  • In-app purchase status (premium unlock state)
  • App settings and preferences
  • Developer mode state (debug builds only, stripped from production)

PlainDental does not collect or store health records, personal data, or user-entered content.

4 How We Use Information

PlainDental uses information to:

  • Provide dental health education content
  • Display appropriate content based on purchase status (free vs premium)
  • Maintain app preferences locally

5 Local-First Design

PlainDental does not collect, transmit, or store any user data on servers. All content is bundled within the app. There is no cloud sync, no accounts, and no user tracking.

6 No Analytics or Tracking

PlainDental does not use any analytics SDKs, crash reporters, or tracking tools. No data is sent to third-party analytics services.

7 App Store and Google Play Billing

PlainDental may use Apple App Store In-App Purchase (on iOS) or Google Play Billing (on Android) for eligible in-app purchase functionality. Apple or Google may process payment and transaction information under their own respective policies. PlainDental may receive limited purchase-status or product information needed to unlock purchased content on your device. EqualInformation, LLC does not receive your full payment card number through PlainDental.

8 Dental Content

All dental health content in PlainDental is bundled locally within the app. No external API calls are made for content delivery. The content provided is for educational purposes only and is not a substitute for professional dental advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

9 Permissions

PlainDental may request the following device permissions:

  • Internet: Used only for in-app purchase verification

PlainDental does not require camera, microphone, location, contacts, or other sensitive permissions.

10 Sharing and Sale of Data

EqualInformation, LLC does not sell personal or sensitive user data. We do not share users' data with data brokers or for advertising purposes. The company does not intentionally transmit personal or sensitive PlainDental user data to its own servers or unrelated third parties.

11 Data Retention and Deletion

Purchase-related unlock state may be restorable through App Store or Google Play for eligible purchases. Uninstalling PlainDental removes all locally stored data from your device.

12 Security

PlainDental relies on iOS and Android platform protections and app sandboxing as part of its local-first design. However, no method of storage or electronic transmission is completely secure, and the company cannot guarantee absolute security.

13 Your Choices

You may choose to purchase premium content or not. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data from your device.

14 Children's Privacy

PlainDental is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. Kids dental content within PlainDental is educational only. If you believe information has been provided inappropriately, please contact the company.

15 Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the updated version on this page and revise the Effective Date above.
