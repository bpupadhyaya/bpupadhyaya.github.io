Taylorine: AP Calculus BC - Privacy Policy

About this policy

Taylorine: AP Calculus BC is a practice app for AP Calculus BC from EqualInformation, LLC. This policy explains what the app does with data.

Your study data stays on your device

Your answers, progress, notebook, plan, streak and settings are stored only on your device. There are no accounts, no sign-up and no servers that receive your study data. You can export or delete your progress in the app.

Ads

The free version shows ads supplied by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and ad interaction data under its own policies. On first use the app asks for consent where the law requires it (Google User Messaging Platform) and, on iPhone and iPad, for tracking permission. Until you consent, ads are non-personalised. The app is not directed to children under 13. Ads are never shown during timed tests, mocks, labs or free-response practice.

Purchases

An optional monthly ad-free subscription is processed by Apple or Google. We do not receive your payment details. Cancel any time in your App Store or Google Play account settings. If you cancel, you keep ad-free until the end of the period you already paid for, and you will not be charged again.

Permissions

The app needs the internet only to load ads, show optional reference pages and process purchases. It needs no camera, microphone, contacts or location access.
