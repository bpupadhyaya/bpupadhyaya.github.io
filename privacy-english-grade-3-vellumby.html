English Grade 3: Vellumby privacy policy

Effective 28 November 2026. Publisher: EqualInformation.

This policy covers the app English Grade 3: Vellumby, a study aid used by children with a grown-up.

What stays on the device

Progress, settings and anything typed in the app are saved on the device only. We do not receive them. A device backup made by you may include them. A grown-up can erase them in the Grown-ups area.

What is not collected

No account, no name, no microphone, camera or location. No advertising, analytics or tracking software. Nothing is sent to us automatically.

What reaches us only if a grown-up sends it

A grown-up may email diagnostics or feedback from the Grown-ups area. The message is shown before it is sent from your own mail app. It may contain your email address, the words you write, and for diagnostics the app version, device model, system version, window size, app settings, subscription state and the last app error. We use it only to answer you and to fix and improve the app. We do not sell it or use it for advertising. Please do not include a child's name or other personal details.

Keeping and deleting

Email you send is kept in our mail system while needed to deal with it. Write to support@equalinformation.com to ask for a copy or for deletion.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to that.

Web pages opened from the app

Source links and these legal pages open only after a grown-up check. The sites shown have their own privacy policies and, like any website, receive ordinary connection information such as an IP address.

Changes and contact

We will post changes on this page with a new date. Contact: support@equalinformation.com.
