FELLWICK

Privacy policy

Effective 7 November 2026. Fellwick is published by Equal Information LLC. This page says what the app does with information.

Your trails stay with you

The habits you create, the steps you log, your camps and your reminder times are stored on your device only. Fellwick has no accounts and no servers of its own, and it does not upload your habits or your log. Trail Notes are worked out on the device from that log.

Widgets and the Lock Screen

The Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets and the Live Activity read a copy of your trails that the app keeps in a keychain item shared only between Fellwick and its own widget extension. Nothing in it leaves your device.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications created on your device after you switch them on. They are never sent through our servers because we have none.

Advertising

Fellwick is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers, advertising data, product interaction data and coarse location to choose and measure ads, and may use them to personalise ads if you allow tracking. The app asks for tracking permission through Apple's prompt and, where the law requires, shows Google's consent form. You can answer either way and the app works the same. You can change your tracking choice in the Settings app.

Subscription and rewarded videos

An optional monthly subscription removes ads. Apple handles the purchase and the app learns only whether the subscription is active. You may also watch short rewarded videos to switch ads off for a while; the counters for that live on your device.

Children

Fellwick is a general-audience habit tool and is not directed to children under 13.

Deleting your data

Retire a trail from its edit screen, or delete the app to remove everything it stores on your device. Email us with any question about this policy.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and its date.
