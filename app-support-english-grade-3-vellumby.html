Contact

Email support@equalinformation.com. In the app, a grown-up can also use Grown-ups, then Send diagnostics or Feedback and suggestions.

Common questions

How do I open the Grown-ups area? Tap Grown-ups and type the answer to the multiplication written in words.

How do I restore a subscription? Grown-ups, then Subscriptions, then Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

How do I cancel? Grown-ups, then Manage subscription, or your Apple Account settings.

Does one subscription include the other? No. Course view and Build view are separate.

How do I change spelling? Grown-ups, then Settings, then Spelling and terms.

Does it work without a connection? Learning does. Buying, restoring and opening source or legal pages need a connection.
