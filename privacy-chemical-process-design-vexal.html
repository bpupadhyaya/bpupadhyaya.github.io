Chemical Process Design: Vexal Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-24. Chemical Process Design: Vexal is published by EqualInformation, LLC.

1. Introduction and scope

This policy explains how EqualInformation, LLC ("we", "us") handles information in the Chemical Process Design: Vexal app for iPhone and iPad ("the app"). The app needs no account and has no sign-up. The first time the app opens it shows a notice, and that notice forms part of the Terms of Use.

2A. Information you choose to provide

If you send a diagnostic report, the report contains the technical information listed in the preview screen that the app shows you before you tap Send, together with any note you add. If you send feedback or a suggestion, we receive its category, your message, your optional role and course or year, an optional source link, an optional rating, and, only if you tick the contact option, an email address for a reply. Please do not put personal information in a message; email addresses, telephone numbers and links written in a message are removed automatically.

2B. Diagnostic and app-usage information

With your agreement, or where applicable law permits, the app collects diagnostic and app-usage information that is permissible under applicable law. This comprises device and operating-system information (model, operating-system version, processor, memory, display size, language, region and time zone, and accessibility and display settings), the app version, performance and memory indicators, crash and error information, a randomly generated app identifier, and how features of the app are used. Because this is a learning app, it also records how the learning features are used and perform: the lessons you view, the questions you attempt, whether your answers are correct, the time taken, hints used, topic-level progress estimates, the adaptive-learning decisions the app makes and their outcomes, and activity in the labs, simulations and builds. When you accept the first-run notice, a short acceptance record (the notice version, the time, your choice, app and device basics, and the country and time zone) is always sent so that we can show that the notice was presented.

2C. Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, your contacts, precise location, photographs or files, or words you type into the app, including searches. Text you choose to send with feedback, a content suggestion or a diagnostic report (including an optional note, source link, course or year and reply email) is received when you tap Send. For practice questions, the option you choose is recorded with usage information when sharing is on. The app does not use your information to track you across other companies' apps or websites and does not ask for tracking permission. Your study progress, mastery estimates and settings are also kept on your device so the app works offline.

3. How we use information

We use the information to operate, maintain, secure and support the app; to diagnose problems; to provide and personalise the learning experience; to understand how the app is used; for research and development; for analytics and for improving our apps and educational content; to read and act on feedback; and to comply with legal obligations. Information may be transferred as part of a financing, merger, acquisition or similar business transaction, subject to the commitments in this policy.

4. Your choices

You may decide whether to share usage information when the notice first appears, and you may change that choice at any time under Settings, Privacy and data. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the setting is off until you turn it on. A diagnostic report or a piece of feedback is sent only when you tap Send. To request deletion of the usage information associated with your installation, use "Delete my usage data" in Settings, Privacy and data, or write to diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

5. Storage and service providers

Information is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored in our Google Workspace services (Google Sheets and Drive), with notification email, and is processed by service providers acting on our behalf. Subscription purchases are handled by Apple, which tells us only that a subscription is active.

6. Retention

Usage and learning information is kept for as long as it is useful for the purposes in section 3 and is deleted on request as described in section 4. Feedback and diagnostic reports are kept while they are needed to review and act on them.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. International users and legal bases

Information may be processed in the United States. Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we rely on your consent for the optional usage information, and on our legitimate interests in operating, securing and improving the app for reports you send and for the acceptance record.

9. Information you wish to have removed

We design the app to collect only the information described in this policy. If you believe information has been provided to us that you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

Monthly subscription

Subscribing is optional. Vexal Plus is one auto-renewing monthly plan. The price is displayed by the App Store before you confirm, billing is handled by Apple, and you can cancel at any time in your App Store subscription settings; cancelling takes effect at the end of the month you have paid for.

10. Changes to this policy

If we make a material change we will update the date above, and the notice version in the app changes so that the notice is shown again.

11. Contact

EqualInformation, LLC, support@equalinformation.com. Deletion and data requests: diagnostics@equalinformation.com. See also the support page.
