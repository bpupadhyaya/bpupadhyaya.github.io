Chemical Process Design: Vexal Privacy Policy

Chemical Process Design: Vexal is published by EqualInformation, LLC. This policy was last updated on 2026-11-23.

What this policy covers

This policy explains how Vexal, a Chemical Process Design course app, handles information. In short: there is no sign-up, and we run no account system or user database.

Your data stays on your device

Your answers, mastery levels, review dates, chosen study path and settings are stored only on your device so the app works offline. They are not uploaded to us, and deleting the app deletes them.

What the app does not collect

The app never requests location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, health information or identity details. Personal data is not sold.

Advertising and tracking

The app shows no advertising and collects no advertising data. It does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, does not use an advertising identifier and does not ask for tracking permission.

Age

This is an undergraduate-level study app for adults and older students, not for children under 13; we do not knowingly gather data from them.

Monthly subscription

Subscribing is optional: Vexal Plus Monthly is one auto-renewing monthly plan that unlocks all Plus features. The price is displayed by the store before you confirm, billing is done by Apple or Google, and you can cancel at any time in your store subscription settings; cancelling takes effect at the end of the paid month. We receive only the active or inactive status.

Your choices and data requests

Questions, access requests and deletion requests go to bpupadhyaya@equalinformation.com; see also the support page.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
