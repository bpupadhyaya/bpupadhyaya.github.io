Chemical Process Design: Vexal Privacy Policy

Chemical Process Design: Vexal is published by EqualInformation, LLC. This policy was last updated on 2026-11-23.

What this policy covers

This policy explains how Vexal, a Chemical Process Design course app, handles information. In short: there is no sign-up, and we run no account system or user database.

Your data stays on your device

Your answers, mastery levels, review dates, chosen study path, rewarded-ad unlock times and settings are stored only on your device so the app works offline. They are not uploaded to us, and deleting the app deletes them.

What the app does not collect

The app never requests location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, health information or identity details. Personal data is not sold.

Advertising and rewarded videos

Advertising comes from Google AdMob and appears at natural breaks, never while a timed test or mock exam is running. Watching a rewarded video is optional and unlocks Plus features for 24 hours. Before any ad is requested, iOS asks for tracking permission and, where needed, Google's consent form lets you accept or limit personalisation. Allowed, Google gets an advertising identifier and ad-interaction data; refused, you see non-personalised ads. Details are on Google's advertising data page.

Age

This is an undergraduate-level study app for adults and older students, not for children under 13; we do not knowingly gather data from them.

Optional subscription

An optional auto-renewing monthly subscription removes ads and unlocks all Plus features. There is no free trial or introductory price. The App Store or Google Play shows the price before you buy and the plan renews until you cancel. Payment is handled by Apple or Google; we never see card details and the app only learns whether your subscription is active. Manage or cancel it in your store account settings. See the terms of use.

Your choices and data requests

You can switch tracking off at any time under iOS Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking, and revisit the consent message from About when it is available. Questions, access requests and deletion requests go to bpupadhyaya@equalinformation.com; see also the support page.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
