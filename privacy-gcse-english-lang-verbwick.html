GCSE English Lang: Verbwick: Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Contact: support@equalinformation.com. Last updated 2026-11-22.

What stays on your device

Your answers, mastery estimates, marks in the annotation lab, writing plans, drafts, test reports, settings and unlock timers are stored only on your device. The app has no account system and no server of ours that receives this data. Your writing is never uploaded and never read by us. Deleting the app, or using Reset all progress, erases it. We do not collect your name, email address or contact details.

No AI

The app contains no AI feature. Counts shown next to your writing are calculated on the device.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: banners on browse screens, an occasional full-screen ad after a finished drill, test or lab check, and optional rewarded videos that unlock features for 24 hours. Because the audience is school age, the app asks Google for non-personalised ads for everyone, limits ad content to teen ratings, and does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps. Where the law requires it, Google shows a consent form. AdMob may still process device and usage data to deliver and measure ads, under Google's own privacy policy.

Subscription

One optional monthly subscription removes ads and unlocks every feature. Purchases are processed by Apple or Google; we never see your payment details. You can cancel any time in your App Store or Google Play account settings and keep the benefit until the paid period ends. There is no free trial.

Links you open

Source links open public web pages in your browser. Those sites apply their own privacy practices.

Age

The app is intended for ages 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Your choices and contact

You can reset the app at any time, change ad privacy choices where your region provides them, or write to support@equalinformation.com with questions.
