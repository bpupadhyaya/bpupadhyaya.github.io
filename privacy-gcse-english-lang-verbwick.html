GCSE English Lang: Verbwick: Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Contact: support@equalinformation.com. Last updated 2026-11-23.

What stays on your device

Your answers, mastery estimates, marks in the annotation lab, writing plans, drafts, test reports, settings and purchase status are stored only on your device. The app has no account system and no server of ours that receives this data. Your writing is never uploaded and never read by us. Deleting the app, or using Reset all progress, erases it. We do not collect your name, email address or contact details.

No AI

The app contains no AI feature. Counts shown next to your writing are calculated on the device.

Subscription

Verbwick Plus is one optional monthly subscription that unlocks the Plus half of the app. Purchases are processed by Apple or Google; we never see your payment details. You can cancel any time in your App Store or Google Play account settings: cancelling takes effect from the next billing period, so you keep Plus until the month you have paid for ends and are not charged again. There is no free trial.

Links you open

Source links open public web pages in your browser. Those sites apply their own privacy practices.

Age

The app is intended for ages 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Your choices and contact

You can reset the app at any time or write to support@equalinformation.com with questions. The app collects no data about you and does no tracking.
