Discrete Mathematics: Lemmora Support

Discrete Mathematics: Lemmora is an undergraduate discrete mathematics course (logic, proofs, counting, graphs and number theory). Published by EqualInformation, LLC.

Get help

Email bpupadhyaya@equalinformation.com with your device model, system version and a short description of the problem. We usually reply within a few days. Privacy questions and data requests go to the same address.

Restore a purchase

On iPhone or iPad, open About in the app and choose Restore Purchases while signed in with the Apple Account that bought the subscription. On Android, use Restore in About with the same Google account.

Manage or cancel the subscription

The optional monthly ad-free subscription is managed by the store, not by us. On iOS: Settings, your name, Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play, Profile, Payments and subscriptions. Cancel at least a day before renewal to avoid the next charge. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google.

Report a content error

Found a wrong answer, a typo or an unclear explanation? Email bpupadhyaya@equalinformation.com with the topic and question text (a screenshot helps) and, if you can, the source you checked. We review every report and fix confirmed mistakes in an update.

Common questions

Does Lemmora need an internet connection? No. Lessons, questions, worked solutions, mock exams and the interactive labs, including the graph explorer all run offline. A connection is only needed to show ads and to buy or restore the subscription.

How does the adaptive path work? On the Personalised path, Lemmora tracks your mastery topic by topic, notices the pattern behind a wrong answer and chooses your next question and review from that, all on your device. Prefer a fixed order? Switch to the Traditional syllabus path at any time without losing progress.

Are ads shown during exams? No. Ads appear only at natural breaks, never during timed tests or mock exams. Worked solutions are free and never hidden behind ads.

What do the interactive labs do? They let you change parameters, predict what will happen and then see the result. Lemmora includes the interactive labs, including the graph explorer.

Is this a replacement for my course? No. Lemmora is a practice and study aid that goes alongside a course, textbook or instructor; it does not replace them.

Will I lose my progress if I change phone? Progress is stored on the device and is not uploaded, so it does not move automatically to a new phone. Backups made by your phone's own backup feature may restore it.
