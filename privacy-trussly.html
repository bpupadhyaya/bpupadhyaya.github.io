Privacy policy

PE Civil Exam Prep: Trussly, published by EqualInformation, LLC.

Effective 27 November 2026. This policy describes how EqualInformation, LLC ("we") handles information in the app PE Civil Exam Prep: Trussly.

Information we collect

Information you choose to provide. Diagnostic reports you send from the app, with any note you add; feedback and suggestions you submit (category, message, optional role and rating) and, only if you tick the contact option, an email address for a reply.

Diagnostic and app-usage information, collected with your agreement or as permitted by applicable law. Device and operating-system information (model, operating-system version, processor, memory, display size, language, region and time zone, accessibility and display settings), app version, performance and memory indicators, crash and error information, a randomly generated app identifier, and how the app is used: screens and features used, study sessions, lessons viewed, questions attempted, whether answers are correct, time taken, topic-level progress estimates, the adaptive decisions the app makes, lab and project activity, and interactions with the subscription screen. Text you type, such as search terms or notes, is not collected, apart from any feedback you choose to send.

Information we do not collect. Your name, contacts, precise location, photographs, files or advertising identifiers.

How we use information

To operate, maintain, secure and support the app; to diagnose problems; to personalise the learning experience; to understand how the app is used; for research and development; for analytics and improvement of our apps and educational content; to review and act on feedback; and to comply with legal obligations.

Your choices

When you first open the app you see a notice and a switch for sharing usage information. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and where the region cannot be determined, the switch is off until you turn it on. You can change it at any time in More, Privacy and data. Diagnostic reports and feedback are sent only when you choose. You may ask us to delete your usage information with the Delete my usage data control in the app or by writing to diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

Storage and service providers

Information is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored in our Google Workspace services (Google Sheets and Drive) and notification email, and processed by service providers acting on our behalf. For a Trussly installed from the App Store or Google Play, we may be sent by email the exam practice and usage batches it uploads, its acceptance record, crash records, diagnostic reports, feedback and deletion requests. These stay in our own mailboxes, forwarding among them included. Information may be processed in the United States. Purchases are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play); we do not receive payment details.

Retention and security

Usage and learning information is kept while it is useful for the purposes above, and deletion requests are handled on request, by hand. The Delete my usage data control files a request only, and any copy we received by email remains unless we delete it manually. We use reasonable measures to protect information; no method is completely secure.

Information you ask us to remove

We design our apps to collect only the information described in this policy. If you believe information has been provided to us that you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

Changes and contact

If this policy or the first-run notice changes materially, the notice is shown again. Contact: EqualInformation, LLC, support@equalinformation.com; data requests: diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
