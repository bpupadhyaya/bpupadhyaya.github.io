CFA Level I Prep: Hurdlewell - Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Contact: support@equalinformation.com. Last updated 2026-11-20.

What stays on your device

Your answers, review schedule, notes, flags, progress, timed-run results, credits, streak and settings are stored only on your device. The app has no account system and no server that receives this data. Deleting the app or using Reset in the app erases it. We do not collect your name, email address or contact details.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: an occasional full-screen ad at natural breaks and optional rewarded videos that earn credits. No ads appear while you answer questions in a timed run or mock. AdMob may use your advertising identifier, approximate location and device and usage data to select and measure ads, under Google's own privacy policy. On iOS the app asks for your permission before tracking across other companies' apps; where the law requires it, Google shows a consent form. Declining gives you non-personalised ads. You can change ad privacy options in the app where your region requires them.

Subscription

The optional monthly plan only removes ads. Purchases are processed by Apple or Google; we never see your payment details. You can cancel any time in your App Store or Google Play account settings and keep the benefit until the paid period ends.

Sources you open

Source and reference links open public web pages inside the app. Those sites load from their publishers and apply their own privacy practices.

Audience

The app is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed to children.
