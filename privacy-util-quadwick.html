QR Scanner & Maker: Quadwick: Privacy Policy

Quadwick: QR code scanner and generator. Effective 14 November 2026. Publisher: Equal Information LLC.

Quadwick scans QR codes and barcodes and creates QR codes and barcodes, entirely on your phone.

What stays on your device

Everything runs on your phone: scanning, the safe-link check, creating and styling codes, and exporting. There is no account. What you scan, the codes you create and your scan history are stored only on your device, and you can delete them in the app. Nothing you scan or create is sent to us. If you tap a link in a scanned code, your browser or the relevant app opens it, and that site's own policy applies; Quadwick shows a safety check first, which is done offline on the device.

Permissions

The camera is used only to scan codes live; the picture is not saved or sent anywhere. You can also scan from a picture you choose. On iPhone and iPad the app asks for add-only permission to save finished codes to your library when you choose Save. On Android, adding a Wi-Fi network uses the system add-network dialog, and on iPhone and iPad the app shows the network details and copies the password for you to join yourself; contacts and calendar events open in the system editors where you confirm them. Payment codes are shown as a preview only; Quadwick never makes a payment. The app does not use the microphone, contacts, or location.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob (a banner on the home screen and a short full-screen ad after finished actions). Your scans and codes are never used for ads or sent to AdMob. Ads and the consent message are not shown in your first session. An optional short video can be watched to earn one more credit. On iPhone and iPad the system first asks whether the app may track you (App Tracking Transparency); in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some US states a Google consent message also appears (Google User Messaging Platform). On Android the Google consent message appears where the law requires it. You can decline and keep using every feature. With your permission, AdMob and its partners may use your device advertising identifier, approximate location derived from your IP address, and ad interaction and diagnostic data to choose, show and measure ads. See Google's advertising policies. You can change your choices later in the app (privacy choices for ads, where shown) and in your device settings. Ads are not served to people who have an ad-free purchase active.

Purchases

The optional ad-free monthly subscription and the one-time Unlock are handled by Apple or Google Play. We never see your payment details. Credits, purchase status and your settings are stored on your device.

What is collected

Collected by us: nothing, and nothing you scan or create is collected. Collected by Google AdMob in the free version: device identifiers (advertising identifier), approximate location from IP address, and ad interaction and diagnostic data, used for third-party advertising and measuring ads.

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information, build profiles about you, run our own analytics, or ask you to create an account. No personal data is sent to us.

Children

Quadwick is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Nothing is sent to us in any case.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new effective date.
