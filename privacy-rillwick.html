Rillwick privacy policy

Effective 7 November 2026. Rillwick is a drink log published by EqualInformation, LLC.

Your log stays on your device

Every drink you log, your daily goal, body weight (if you choose to enter it), bedtime and reminder hours are stored only on your iPhone or iPad. We run no server for Rillwick, there is no account, and we cannot see or recover this information. "Erase everything" in Settings deletes it from the device.

What Rillwick does not use

Rillwick does not read your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or Apple Health data, and it has no analytics SDK of its own.

Reminders

Gentle reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device inside the hours you choose. They are optional and can be switched off in Settings or in iOS.

Ads

Rillwick is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect your device advertising identifier, an approximate location derived from your network address, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads. If you allow tracking when iOS asks, ads may be personalised; if you decline, they are not. Where the law requires it, Google's consent message appears first, and you can reopen it from Settings, "Privacy choices for ads". See Google's advertising technologies page.

Subscription

The optional monthly ad-free subscription is handled by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment details. Rillwick only checks on your device whether the subscription is active.

Children

Rillwick is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Not medical advice

Goals and caffeine estimates are everyday planning figures, not medical advice.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes we will update the date above. Questions: support@equalinformation.com.
