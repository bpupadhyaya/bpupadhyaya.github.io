GRE Physics Test: Fluxarc privacy policy

Who we are

Fluxarc (GRE Physics Test: Fluxarc) is published by EqualInformation, LLC. Effective 15 November 2026.

What the app stores

Your answers, levels, review schedule, mistake notebook, mock test results, streak days, credits and settings are saved only on your device. There is no account, no sign-up and no server of ours that receives this data. You can export your progress to a file you control, import it again, or reset it in Me. Deleting the app deletes the data.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: an adaptive banner on some screens, an occasional full-screen ad after a finished practice block, and optional rewarded videos that earn credits. There are no ads in mock tests, simulations or while a timer is running. AdMob may use your device advertising identifier, approximate location and device information to choose and measure ads. On iOS the app asks permission before tracking across other companies' apps and websites, and where the law requires it, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform is shown. You can refuse and keep using the app. See policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Purchases

The optional ad-free plan is an auto-renewing monthly subscription handled by Apple or Google. We receive no payment details. Manage or cancel it in your store account settings.

References and web pages

The References control can open public pages (for example OpenStax, ETS, NIST, MIT OpenCourseWare) inside the app. Those sites receive the usual request data such as your IP address when you open them. The app limits the embedded view to the cited site.

Practice content and independence

Fluxarc is independent: it is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by ETS. GRE is a registered trademark of ETS, used here only to describe the kind of test this practice suits. All questions and explanations are original and written for this app. Score ranges are rough practice estimates from your own answers, not predictions of any GRE score.

Children

The app is an education tool for graduate-school applicants and is not directed to children under 13.
