Privacy Policy: Music Theory AP: Cadenmoor

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Contact: support@equalinformation.com. Last updated 2026-11-26.

1. Scope

This policy explains how EqualInformation, LLC handles information in Music Theory AP: Cadenmoor, version 1.0 and later, on phones and tablets. The app has no account.

2. What stays on your device

Your study progress, mastery estimates, review schedule, pins, study plan, exam date, build notes, sight-singing self-ratings and settings are stored on your device. All sounds are synthesised on your device. The app does not use the microphone and does not record you.

3. Information we collect

Information you choose to send: diagnostic reports, with any note you add; and feedback or suggestions (category, message, and optionally your role, level or context, a source link and a rating). If you tick the contact option, we also receive the email address you give for a reply.

Diagnostic and app-usage information, collected with your agreement or as permitted by applicable law: device model and system version, processor, memory, display size, language, region and time zone, accessibility and display settings, app version, performance and memory indicators, crash and error information, a random app identifier, and how the app's features are used. For learning features this includes lessons viewed, questions attempted, the option chosen, whether it was correct, time taken, explanations opened, topic progress estimates, practice and exam results, study suggestions made to you and what followed, activity in labs and builds, words typed into the in-app coverage search (shortened, with recognisable personal details removed), and purchase-screen interactions.

We do not collect your name, contacts, precise location, photographs, files, audio, advertising identifiers or your build notes. Your app store processes purchases; we do not receive your payment details.

4. How we use information

To operate, maintain, secure and support the app; to diagnose problems; to provide and personalise the learning experience; to understand how the app is used; for research and development; for analytics and to improve our apps and educational content; to read and respond to feedback; and to meet legal obligations.

5. Your choices

The first time you open the app you see a notice and a separate switch for sharing usage information. The switch is off until you turn it on in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and wherever your region cannot be determined. You can change it at any time under More, Privacy and data. Diagnostic reports and feedback are sent only when you choose to send them. To ask us to delete your usage information, use Delete my usage data in the app or write to diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

6. Storage and service providers

Information we receive is kept in our Google Workspace services (Sheets and Drive) and notification email, and is processed by service providers acting on our behalf. It is sent over encrypted connections.

7. Retention

Usage and learning information is kept while it is useful for the purposes above. Deletion requests are handled when we receive them.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

9. International users and legal bases

Where the law requires a legal basis, we rely on your consent or on our legitimate interest in running and improving the app. Information may be processed in the United States. Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct or delete information; contact us to use them.

10. Children

We design our apps to collect only the information described in this policy. If you believe information has been provided to us that you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

11. Cited web pages

When you open a cited source or this policy from the app, the page loads from its publisher's own website, whose privacy terms apply there.

12. Changes and contact

If we make a material change, the first-run notice receives a new version and is shown again. EqualInformation, LLC, support@equalinformation.com. Data and deletion requests: diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
