Privacy policy

LSAT Prep: Syllogra

Last updated 24 November 2026.

Syllogra works fully offline and needs no account. Your answers, drafts, scores, settings and text size are kept only on your device. We do not receive them, and the app does not collect personal information, track you, or share data with other companies. It is intended for adults aged 18 and over.

If you buy Syllogra Plus, the purchase is handled by Apple or Google. We see only whether a subscription is active on your device, never your payment details. Opening a source link loads that website, which has its own privacy policy.

Deleting the app deletes your study data from your device. Questions about privacy: support@equalinformation.com.
