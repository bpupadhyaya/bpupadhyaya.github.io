Privacy Policy

LSAT Prep: Syllogra ("the app"). Effective 27 November 2026.

1. Introduction and scope

EqualInformation, LLC ("we", "us") provides the app. This policy describes the information the app collects, how it is used and the choices available to you. It applies to the app on iOS and Android.

2. Information we collect

A. Information you choose to provide. Diagnostic reports that you choose to send, together with any note you add; and feedback and suggestions that you choose to submit (category, message, optional role and level, optional source link, optional rating) and, only if you tick the contact option, an email address for a reply.

B. Diagnostic and app-usage information collected with your agreement or as permitted by applicable law: device and operating-system information (model, system version, processor, memory, display size, language, region and time zone, accessibility and display settings), app version, performance and memory indicators, crash and error information, a randomly generated app identifier, and how features of the app are used. This includes how the learning features are used and perform: drills, reading passages, argument maps, writing practice and timed mocks that you open, the questions you attempt, whether your answers are correct, the time taken, topic-level progress estimates, study sessions, purchase-screen interactions and the adaptive-learning decisions made by the app and their outcomes.

C. Information we do not collect: your name, contacts, precise location, photographs, files, advertising identifiers, or other text you enter, including the text of your writing practice and drafts.

3. How we use information

To operate, maintain, secure and support the app; to diagnose problems; to provide and personalise the learning experience; to understand how the app is used; for research and development; for analytics and improvement of our apps and educational content; to read and act on feedback; and to comply with legal obligations. Information may be transferred as part of a financing, merger, acquisition or similar business transaction, subject to the commitments in this policy.

4. Your choices

The first-run notice explains these choices. The usage-information switch under Me, Privacy and data is off by default in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland until you turn it on, and you may change it at any time. Diagnostic reports and feedback are sent only when you choose to send them. To request deletion of usage information, use "Delete my usage data" in Privacy and data or write to diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

5. Storage and service providers

Information is stored in our Google Workspace services (Google Sheets and Drive) and notification email, and is processed by service providers acting on our behalf. Where Syllogra was installed through a store, a notification can contain the entire record. LSAT practice and usage batches, notice acceptance, crash and diagnostic reports, feedback and deletion requests may be emailed in this way to our mailboxes, and forwarded to a further one we own. It is transmitted over encrypted connections. Purchases are handled by Apple or Google; we receive only whether a subscription is active on your device.

6. Retention

Usage and learning information is kept for as long as it is useful for the purposes described above. A person here carries out a requested deletion; the software removes nothing automatically. That leaves emailed copies in our mail unless the person also deletes them.

7. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. International users and legal bases

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we rely on your consent or on our legitimate interests in operating and improving the app. Information may be processed in the United States.

9. Children

We design the app to collect only the information described in this policy. If you believe that information has been provided to us that you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

10. Changes to this policy

We will update this page and the first-run notice when material changes are made; a new notice is shown again at the next launch.

11. Contact

EqualInformation, LLC, support@equalinformation.com. Data and deletion requests: diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
