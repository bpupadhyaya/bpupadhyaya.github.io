Write to support@equalinformation.com with your device model and the app version (More tab, About). We usually reply within two business days.

Common questions

Is my progress saved? Yes, on your device. Reinstalling or choosing Reset erases it, and there is no cloud backup of our own.

How do I cancel the monthly subscription? In your App Store or Google Play account, under Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel it.

How do I restore a purchase? More tab, Restore purchase (or open the subscription screen on the same store account).

The text is too small or too large. Use the A- and A+ buttons; they scale lessons, questions and equations from 50 to 300 percent.

Found a mistake? Please tell us the question number shown in the app. Corrections ship in updates.
