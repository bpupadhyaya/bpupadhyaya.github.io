Lexiesta Privacy Policy

Lexiesta: Spanish Flashcards. Effective 13 November 2026. Publisher: Equal Information LLC.

Lexiesta teaches Spanish to English speakers with original flashcards, example sentences, quizzes and offline pronunciation.

What stays on your device

Your study progress, streaks, daily goal, accent choice, text size and settings are stored only on your device. There is no account, no sign-in and no server of ours that receives your progress. Pronunciation uses the Spanish voices already installed on your device. The app does not use the microphone, camera, contacts, photos, location or speech recognition.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob alongside the free packs. People who subscribe to Lexiesta Plus or buy Remove Ads see no ads and the ad service is not started. An optional short video can be watched to open a locked pack for 24 hours. On iPhone and iPad the system first asks whether the app may track you (App Tracking Transparency); in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some US states a Google consent message also appears (Google User Messaging Platform). On Android the Google consent message appears where the law requires it. You can decline and keep using every feature. With your permission, AdMob and its partners may use your device advertising identifier, approximate location derived from your IP address, and ad interaction and diagnostic data to choose, show and measure ads. See Google's advertising policies. You can change your choices later in the app under Settings (privacy choices for ads, where shown) and in your device settings. Ads are not served to people who have an ad-free purchase active.

Purchases

Lexiesta Plus (monthly subscription, with a 7-day free trial where offered) and Remove Ads (one-time purchase) are handled by Apple or Google Play. We never see your payment details. The app checks your entitlement with the store to unlock packs and remove ads.

What is collected

Collected by us: nothing. Collected by Google AdMob in the free version: device identifiers (advertising identifier), approximate location from IP address, and ad interaction and diagnostic data, used for third-party advertising only.

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information, build profiles about you, run our own analytics, or ask you to create an account. No personal data is sent to us.

Children

Lexiesta is a general-audience language tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and we do not collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new effective date.
