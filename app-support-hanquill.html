Hanquill Support

Chinese Language AP: Hanquill · EqualInformation, LLC · Effective 26 November 2026

Contact

Email support@equalinformation.com with your device model, app version (Me, then About) and what happened. You can also use Me, then Privacy and data, to send a diagnostic report or feedback from the app.

No Chinese voice or silent listening

Listening uses your device's text-to-speech. Add a Mandarin (China) voice in your device's speech or spoken-content settings, check the volume, then reopen the set. You can always show the script after answering.

Typing and characters

Type answers with your device's Chinese keyboard. Choose simplified or traditional display under Me, then Settings. Text size can be set from 50 to 300 percent with A- and A+.

Recording

Allow the microphone when you first tap Record, or change it later in your device settings. Takes stay on your device.

Subscription

To manage or cancel Hanquill Plus, use Manage subscription in the app or your store account's subscriptions page. Cancelling takes effect from the next billing period. To get access back on a new device, tap Restore purchases while signed in to the same store account.
