1 Contact us

Email support@equalinformation.com. Tell us your device, its system version and what happened. Please leave out your child's name or other personal details.

2 The two views

Syllabus follows the course in order. Real-World asks the learner to design something, build it with math and test it. The switch is at the top of the screen.

3 Grown-ups area

The first visit gives a short two-unknown puzzle written in words (enter both numbers as four digits and press Enter) and then has you choose a four-digit code. The puzzle is asked again before a purchase, before erasing and before a new code. If the code is lost, removing and reinstalling the app clears it, together with all progress.

4 Plans, restoring and cancelling

Open Grown-ups and scroll to the "Plans and purchases" button near the end. "Restore purchases" brings back a plan bought earlier on the same Apple Account. "Manage or cancel" opens the subscription settings of your Apple Account. If no price appears, check that the device is online and signed in to the App Store, then choose "Ask the store again".

5 Reading and sound

A− and A+ change text size from 50% to 300%. "Read aloud" needs an English voice on the device; if there is none, add one in the device settings.

6 Saved work

Progress is kept only on the device and does not move between devices.
