Synthetic Data: Loopmoor - Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC.

Last updated: 2026-11-20.

Loopmoor is an offline study app. It has no account and no server of ours. Your progress (stars, text size, credits) is stored only on this device.

Ads: the free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google and its partners may use an advertising identifier and similar data to serve ads. On iPhone, the app asks permission for tracking and shows a consent form where the law requires one. You can change your choice in More > Privacy options. Subscribers see no ads.

Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Opening a source link loads a third-party web page inside the app; that site has its own privacy practices. The app keeps no cookies from those pages after you close the sheet.
