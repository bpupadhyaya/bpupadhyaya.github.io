Support PlainWeather

Help us put hyperlocal, offline weather-safety guidance in everyone's pocket — especially the billions without a paid weather service.

Information shared is power shared — PlainWeather gives everyone clear, offline, life-protecting weather guidance, and it works entirely without a live connection.

PlainWeather is a hyperlocal, offline-capable weather-safety app for iOS and Android, built and maintained by EqualInformation, LLC. It provides severe-weather action guides for tornadoes, hurricanes, flash floods, wildfires, blizzards, heatwaves, lightning, and more — each one usable with zero connectivity — plus safety calculators (heat index, wind chill, a lightning flash-to-bang countdown), activity go/no-go checklists for farmers, fishermen, and outdoor workers, and specialized modes for kids, elderly users, and people with chronic conditions.

PlainWeather is not trying to out-forecast the big weather services — its edge is the offline safety-action layer: what to actually do, pre-downloaded and ready before the signal drops. The core safety-critical guidance is free for everyone, permanently; an optional one-time PlainWeather Pro unlock adds convenience features like unlimited saved locations and historical references. PlainWeather is coming soon to the App Store and Google Play. Your support helps us keep the safety-critical layer free, expand hazard coverage, and translate it into more languages.

Why PlainWeather Exists

Every human lives under weather every day, everywhere on Earth, with no exception — but most weather apps assume a live connection and a paid subscription, exactly the two things people often don't have in the moments a storm actually turns dangerous.

PlainWeather's answer is an offline-first safety-action layer: hazard guides, calculators, and checklists you download once and keep working on with zero signal, plus a last-synced forecast that stays usable — with a visible "last updated" timestamp — long after you go offline.

No safety-critical feature is ever paywalled. That line is absolute: whether withholding a feature could put someone at greater risk decides what's free, not what's popular or what's easy to sell.

Information shared is power shared. Life-protecting weather knowledge should reach everyone, in their own language, for free — that is why PlainWeather is part of the EqualInformation portfolio.

Disclaimer: PlainWeather provides general severe-weather safety guidance for educational purposes and is not a substitute for official alerts from your local meteorological or emergency-management authority. Always follow official evacuation orders and call your local emergency services in a life-threatening situation.
