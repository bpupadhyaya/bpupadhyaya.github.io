App Support — Sristi

Get help with Sristi — an AI-first personal life optimizer.

  • Home
  • /
  • App Support — Sristi

Sristi is an AI-first personal life optimizer — "Live by design, not by default." It plans, executes, and evaluates the essentials of a healthy life across 13 cited life domains: Sleep, Hydration, Nutrition, Movement, Mental & Emotional, Social & Relationships, Focus & Productivity, Environment & Light, Health Monitoring, Financial Wellness, Spiritual & Purpose, Life Transitions, and Safety & Emergency. Sristi is voice-first (voice, tap, or text), evidence-based with tappable citations, and 100% on-device — no accounts, no servers, and the core works fully offline. Sristi is coming soon to the App Store and Google Play.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Sristi available yet?

Not yet. Sristi is coming soon to the App Store and Google Play. There is no download link available at this time — check back here for availability.

Is my data private?

Yes. Sristi is 100% on-device with no account, no servers, no cloud sync, no analytics, and no user tracking. Your profile, daily logs, and mood journal are stored locally on your device. We never collect any personal information. See our Privacy Policy for details.

What does Sristi do?

Sristi generates a daily plan that adapts to your logged energy, then helps you execute and evaluate it. It logs hydration, sleep, movement, energy, mood, and meals, and gives you an evening recap plus weekly and monthly reflection — all across the 13 life domains. Recommendations are evidence-based with tappable citations (WHO, IOM/NASEM, EFSA, ACSM, NSF, AASM, ACC/AHA, APA, CFPB, CDC, NHTSA, and peer-reviewed studies).

What features are free?

The free core is available to everyone. Safety & Emergency and the health essentials — including Sleep, Hydration, Nutrition, Movement, Mental & Emotional, Social & Relationships, Environment & Light, and Health Monitoring — are always free, along with the daily plan, logging, evening recap, and reflections.

What does Sristi Plus include?

Sristi Plus is a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase (product id com.equalinformation.sristi.premium, around $6.99 — no subscription). It unlocks the premium life-optimization modules: Financial Wellness, Spiritual & Purpose, Life Transitions, and Focus & Productivity.

How does voice input work?

Sristi is voice-first, but you can use voice, tap, or text — whatever you prefer. Voice input uses Microphone and Speech Recognition permissions, which are optional and requested only when you choose to speak. You can always interact entirely by tapping or typing instead.

Does Sristi read my health data?

Only if you allow it, and only read-only. With your permission, Sristi reads exercise minutes and sleep via Apple HealthKit (iOS) or Android Health Connect to keep the day's metrics accurate. This data stays on your device — Sristi does not transmit it.

Does the app require internet?

No. Sristi's core works fully offline. It does not require location, camera, or contacts, and does not need internet for its core functionality. Billing permission is used only to verify the Sristi Plus in-app purchase.

Is Sristi a replacement for seeing a doctor?

No. Sristi is for education only. It helps you plan and build healthier daily routines, but it does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or condition. Always consult a licensed healthcare provider for medical advice, and in an emergency contact your local emergency services.

The app is crashing or not loading. What should I do?

Try these steps:
1. Force-close and reopen the app.
2. Restart your device.
3. Make sure you are running the latest version once Sristi is available on the App Store or Google Play.
4. If the issue persists, contact us (see below) with your device model, OS version, and a description of the problem.

Availability

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play. Sristi is not yet published, so there are no download links at this time.
