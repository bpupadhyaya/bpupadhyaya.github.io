US History AP: Gavelmoor: Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Contact: support@equalinformation.com. Last updated 2026-11-25.

1. Introduction and scope

This policy explains how EqualInformation, LLC (we, us) handles information in connection with US History AP: Gavelmoor, version 1.0 and later (the app). It covers the app only. There is no account. Topic notes, practice questions, labs, builds and your progress are kept on your own device, and the free part of the app works offline.

2. Information we collect

A. Information you choose to provide. Diagnostic reports: Settings, Privacy and data, Send diagnostics shows the full content of the report before anything is sent, and the report is sent only when you tap Send. Feedback and suggestions: a category, your message (up to 2,000 characters, with email addresses, telephone numbers and links removed automatically) and, if you supply them, a role, a context, a source link and a rating. An email address is received only if you tick the option to be contacted, and is used only for that reply.

B. Diagnostic and app-usage information, collected with your agreement or as permitted by applicable law while the optional usage setting is on: app version and build; device model; operating system and version; processor and memory details; screen size; language, region and time zone; text-size, accessibility and display settings; a battery range; whether free access or Gavelmoor Plus is active; a randomly generated app identifier; session timing; crash and error information; performance indicators; the screens, topics, quizzes, mock exams, labs and builds you use; the practice questions you attempt, whether your answers are correct, the time taken and hints used; progress estimates by topic; the choices the adaptive engine makes and their outcomes; and subscription-screen interactions. In-app search words are included in shortened form with recognisable email addresses, telephone numbers, links and card or identity numbers removed.

When you accept the first-run notice a short acceptance record is always sent: a consent identifier, the time, the app, platform, version and build, the notice version and a hash of its text, the state of the usage setting, the random app identifier, and your country, locale and time zone.

C. Information we do not collect. Essays and other writing you type into the short-answer, long-essay and document-based practice screens, and any notes or drafts, stay on your device and are not sent to us. We also do not collect your name, contacts, precise location, photographs, files, or identifiers used to track you across other companies' apps and websites.

3. How we use information

To operate, maintain, secure and support the app; to diagnose problems; to provide and personalise the learning experience; to understand how the app is used; for research and development; for analytics and to improve our apps and educational content; to read and act on feedback; and to meet legal obligations.

4. Your choices

On first opening, a notice explains these practices and offers a separate usage switch. The switch starts off in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and wherever your region is unknown, until you turn it on; elsewhere it starts on. You can change it at any time under Settings, Privacy and data, where you can also read the notice again. Diagnostics and feedback are sent only when you tap Send. To ask for deletion of your usage data, use Delete my usage data in the app or email diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

5. Subscription and payments

Gavelmoor Plus is an optional monthly subscription, priced at $2.99 per month in the United States (local prices may differ). It renews each month until you cancel. Payment is processed by Apple or Google; we do not receive your payment details. Manage or cancel the subscription in your App Store or Google Play account settings at any time, and use Restore purchases in the app to recover an existing subscription.

6. Storage and service providers

Information travels over encrypted (HTTPS) connections to a web application run by EqualInformation, LLC that records it in our Google Workspace services (Google Sheets and Drive); diagnostic reports and acceptance notices are also delivered by email. Google acts for us as a service provider. Reference links open public web pages in a sheet inside the app, and those sites follow their own privacy practices.

7. Retention

Usage, learning and diagnostic information is kept while it remains useful for the purposes above; deletion requests are handled on request.

8. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures, including encryption in transit. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

9. International users and legal bases

Where a legal basis is required we rely on your agreement (the usage switch, and your decision to send diagnostics or feedback) and on our legitimate interest in running and improving the app. Information may be processed in the United States.

10. Children

We design our apps to collect only the information described in this policy. If you believe information has been provided to us that you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

11. Changes

If this policy changes materially, the version of the first-run notice changes and the notice is shown again.

12. Contact

EqualInformation, LLC. Questions: support@equalinformation.com. Deletion and data requests: diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
