Hustingale: Support

GCSE Citizenship study app from EqualInformation, LLC. Effective 27 November 2026.

Get help

Email support@equalinformation.com with your device type, app version (shown in Settings) and what happened. In the app you can also use Report a fault, or Table an amendment for ideas and corrections.

Subscription questions

Plus is missing on a new device. Sign in to the same store account, open More, then Hustingale Plus, and choose Restore a purchase.

Cancel. Choose Manage subscription, or use your store account’s subscriptions page. Plus stays to the end of the paid month.

Studying

Two views. Syllabus follows the AQA 8100 order and Real-World organises the same points into missions. Switch at the top of any screen; progress and your plan carry across.

Text size. Tap Text at the top and drag from 50% to 300%.

Coverage. More, then Coverage, shows what is mapped to which source and what has not been checked.

Your records

See the Privacy policy. To ask for deletion use Settings or write to diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
