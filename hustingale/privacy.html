Hustingale: Privacy policy

GCSE Citizenship study app from EqualInformation, LLC. Effective 27 November 2026.

Scope

This policy explains what the Hustingale app for iPhone, iPad and Android handles and what leaves your device. EqualInformation, LLC is responsible for it. The app has no account and does not ask for your name.

What stays on your device

Your answers history, progress estimates, plan, pins, mission decisions, working notes, written practice-paper answers, searches and settings are stored on your device. Notes, written answers and searches are not sent to us. Deleting the app removes them.

The acceptance record

When you accept the standing orders at first run, the app sends a record of that acceptance whether or not you share usage records: the time; the version, text and a check value of the orders; how long the screen was open and whether you reached its end; how you marked the sharing ballot; a random number the app made for this installation; the app version, platform, session and technical labels such as build type; and your region setting, locale and time zone. If you chose to share, it also includes app language, device model, operating system and version, screen and window size and text size.

Usage and learning records, only if you share

The ballot is not pre-marked for sharing in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and linked territories, Switzerland, or where the app cannot read a region. Elsewhere it is pre-marked and you can change it before accepting or at any time in Settings. While sharing is on, the app sends:

  • visits: when study begins or pauses, how long, text size, layout, free or Plus
  • screens and lessons used and time on them; layout changes; sources opened; view switches; plan changes and pins; curriculum-map and subscription-screen actions
  • questions answered: which question and point, the option position chosen, whether it was right, time taken, how many times attempted, and the type of set
  • progress estimates for specification points
  • suggestions the app made, that you followed them and what happened next
  • mission steps, benches and practice papers (identifiers, progress and marks totals)
  • a fault record when the app itself hits an error: the error message, technical location, device model and system version

These records carry the random app number, not your name. Region, language and device details come from the acceptance record.

Things you choose to send

Fault report. You tick the sections, read the exact report and press Send. It can include device model and system, region, locale and time zone, memory and storage figures, a list of recent screens, recorded faults, app settings and your own note.

Amendments and ratings. A message to us includes what you wrote, its category, the lesson or question it concerns, and, if you add them, your role, a web link, a score, and an email address if you tick that we may reply. A thumbs up or down on an explanation is sent when you tap it. Please leave names and contact details out of free text.

Deletion request. Asks us to delete records linked to your random app number.

Why we use information

To run, secure and support the app; to find and fix faults; to understand how the course is used and how its adaptive features perform; to research, develop and improve the app and our educational content; to answer you where you asked; and to meet legal obligations. If our business or this app is involved in a financing, merger, acquisition or similar transaction, information may pass to the successor, who must respect this policy.

Where it goes

Messages travel over encrypted connections to a collector we run on Google services. They are stored in our Google Workspace spreadsheets and files, and some are also delivered by email to our own mailboxes. Google processes this information for us. Like any web service, Google may handle technical connection data such as an IP address under its own terms; the app does not put your location or an advertising identifier in any message. The app has no tracking across other companies’ apps or websites.

Your app store and cited pages

Apple or Google handles subscription payment. We do not receive your card details; the app only asks the store whether a subscription is held. Cited pages open inside the app from their publishers’ websites, which have their own privacy practices.

Your choices

Mark the ballot as you wish; change sharing in Settings at any time (turning it off discards unsent usage records); use “Ask us to delete my records” in Settings or write to diagnostics@equalinformation.com. To find records we may need your random app number, which appears in the fault-report preview. Depending on where you live you may also have rights to ask for access, correction, deletion or restriction, to object, or to complain to your data protection authority.

Legal bases, retention and international use

Where a legal basis is required, we rely on your consent for optional sharing and for things you choose to send, and on our legitimate interests in keeping an accurate record of acceptance and a working, secure service. We keep records while they are needed for the purposes above or to meet legal obligations, and delete them on a valid request. Information may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States.

Security

We limit access to the stored records and use encrypted transport. No system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children

We design the app to be used without giving us a name or contact details. A parent or guardian who believes a child has sent us personal information can ask us to delete it using the address above.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the effective date above changes, and a material change to the standing orders is shown in the app again. Contact: EqualInformation, LLC, support@equalinformation.com.
