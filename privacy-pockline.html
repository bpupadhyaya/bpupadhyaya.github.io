Pockline privacy policy

Last updated 7 November 2026 · Pockline for iPhone and iPad, by Equal Information LLC

What stays on your device

Player names, match scores, event schedules, queue sessions, season records and rating notes are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. Pockline has no account, no sign-in and no server of ours that receives this data. Deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect your device's advertising identifier, coarse location (derived from your IP address), and information about how you interact with ads. On iOS, the identifier is available only if you allow tracking when the app asks. Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and some US states) you also see Google's consent form, and you can reopen it from Settings, Ad privacy choices. Saying no still shows ads, but not personalised ones. See Google's description at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Purchases

The optional monthly ad-free subscription is handled by Apple through your Apple ID. We receive no payment details. Manage or cancel it in Settings, your name, Subscriptions. Finishing short rewarded videos can also switch ads off for the rest of a calendar month; the counters for that are kept only on your device.

Speech and haptics

Spoken score calls use the system speech voice on your device. Pockline does not record audio and does not use the microphone or camera.

Children

Pockline is a general-audience sports tool and is not directed to children under 13.

Sharing

Score cards are images that you choose to share through the iOS share sheet. We do not sell personal information.

Contact

Questions or requests: support@equalinformation.com.
