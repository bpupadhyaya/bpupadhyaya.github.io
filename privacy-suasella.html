Suasella Privacy Policy

EqualInformation, LLC. Effective November 27, 2026.

1. Introduction and scope

This policy describes how EqualInformation, LLC handles information in Suasella ("English Lang AP: Suasella"), an independent study app for iPhone, iPad and Android devices. The app has no user accounts.

2. Information you choose to provide

  • Diagnostic reports you send, and any note you add to them.
  • Feedback and suggestions you submit: category, message and, if you give them, a role, level, source link and rating.
  • An email address, only if you select the contact option so that we can reply.

3. Acceptance record

When you accept the first-run notice, the app sends a record of the acceptance: a record identifier, the time, the app, platform, version and build, the notice version and a fingerprint of its text, the action taken, the position of the usage switch, a random app identifier, country or region setting and time zone. If the usage switch is on, device characteristics are included.

4. Diagnostic and app-usage information

With your agreement, or as permitted by applicable law, the app collects:

  • Device and operating-system information (such as model, operating-system version, processor, memory, display size, language, region, time zone, accessibility and display settings), app version, performance and memory indicators, and crash and error information.
  • A random app identifier. It is not an advertising identifier and is not your name.
  • How the app is used and how its learning features perform: screens and lessons viewed, questions attempted, the option chosen and whether it was correct, time taken, progress estimates, adaptive next-step decisions and their results, tests, labs, essay self-scores, study plan changes and subscription-screen interactions.

The essays, theses, plans, try-it responses and mission builds you write are stored only on your device and are not collected. The app does not collect your name, contacts, precise location, photographs, files or advertising identifiers.

5. How we use information

We use this information to operate, secure and support the app; to diagnose problems; to provide and personalize the learning experience; to understand how the app is used; for research, analytics and the improvement of our apps and educational content; to read and respond to feedback; and to comply with legal obligations.

6. Your choices

  • A first-run notice explains the collection, with a separate switch for usage and device information. It is off by default in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and where the region is not known, until you turn it on. Elsewhere it starts on.
  • You can change the switch at any time under You, then Privacy and data.
  • Diagnostics and feedback are sent only when you choose to send them.
  • To ask us to delete usage data, use "Delete my usage data" in the app or write to diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
  • "Erase my learning progress" in the app removes the progress stored on your device.

7. Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple or Google under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not receive your payment card details. The app asks the store whether a subscription is active.

8. Storage and service providers

Information sent from the app is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored in our Google Workspace services (spreadsheets, files and email) and is processed by service providers acting on our behalf. The email element works like this for store-installed copies: what Suasella sends, such as rhetoric practice batches, usage records, your acceptance, crashes, diagnostics, feedback and deletion requests, can be mailed to us with its contents, to our own mailboxes and possibly onward to another of ours. Pages you open from the app, such as cited sources, are operated by their publishers under their own policies.

9. Retention and security

Usage, learning and diagnostic information is kept while it is useful for the purposes above; deletion requests are handled when received, through manual deletion. "Delete my usage data" asks for this and does not perform it, and copies in our email are kept unless deleted by hand. We use reasonable measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

10. International users

Where the law requires a legal basis, we rely on consent or on our legitimate interests in running and improving the app. Information may be processed in the United States and other countries where our providers operate. Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object; contact us to exercise them.

11. Children

We design our apps to collect only the information described in this policy. If you believe information has been provided to us that you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

12. Changes and contact

If we make a material change, the first-run notice is updated and shown again. Questions: EqualInformation, LLC, support@equalinformation.com. Deletion and data requests: diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
