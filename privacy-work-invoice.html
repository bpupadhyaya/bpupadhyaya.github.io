Invoicelume Privacy Policy

Effective 9 November 2026. Publisher: Equal Information LLC.

Your invoices stay on your device

Invoicelume is an invoice and estimate maker. Your business details, clients, line items, payments and the PDFs you create are stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. There is no account and no sign-in, and we cannot see your documents. The app works fully offline. When you share a PDF, it goes only where you send it using the system share sheet.

Ads

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier and approximate location to choose and measure ads. On iOS the app first asks whether apps may track you across other companies' apps and websites, and where the law requires it shows a Google consent form so you can choose how ad data is used. You can decline and still use every feature; ads are then non-personalised. See policies.google.com/technologies/ads. The monthly Ad-free subscription, or watching rewarded videos to earn ad-free time, switches ads off. Your invoice content is never sent to advertisers.

Subscription

The optional monthly Ad-free subscription is processed by Apple; we receive no payment details. Manage or cancel it in your Apple ID subscription settings. Terms of Use: the Apple standard licensed application end user agreement.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal data. Invoicelume does not ask for camera, microphone, contacts, location or photo permission and does not collect your clients' details.

Children

Invoicelume is a business tool and is not directed to children under 13.

Not tax or legal advice

Invoicelume does simple arithmetic on the rates you enter. It does not know your local tax rules. Check your own obligations with a qualified adviser.
