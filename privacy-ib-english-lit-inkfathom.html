Privacy for IB English Lit Prep: Inkfathom

What leaves your device, why, and your say in it. In force from 27 November 2026.

Who is speaking, and about what

EqualInformation, LLC publishes Inkfathom, a study companion for phones and tablets. This page says what leaves your device when you use it. There is no account to open and no sign-in.

What never leaves through the app's own reporting

Whatever you type: answers, notes, outlines, your works list, essay questions and source log, rehearsal writing and search words. These sit in the app's storage on your device, where deleting the app removes them.

What is sent the moment you agree to the first-run page

A short proof of agreement, whichever way the sharing switch is set: reference numbers for the agreement and the installation, when it happened, which app, system and version you have, which edition of the page you saw with a fingerprint and copy of its words, how long it was open and whether you reached the end, the switch position, and your country setting, region format and time zone. With the switch on, a description of your device travels with it.

What is sent while sharing is switched on

About your device and the app: model, system version, processor count and type, memory, screen size, kind of network connection, languages, region and time zone, app version and how the app was installed, together with performance figures and, after a fault, the type of error and where in the program it arose.

About how you learn here: the screens, lessons, benches, sources and commissions you open and for how long; each question you try by its reference, whether you were right, the number of the option you tapped on single-choice questions and the time taken; the app's estimate of your depth on course points; the suggestions it made and what you did next; your view, level and text size; and how you move through the subscription screen and whether Plus is on.

These records carry a random number created for this installation. They do not carry your name.

What is sent only when you press Send

A problem report: the device and app details you see in its preview, recent event names from the app, fault references and any note you add. It can be sent with sharing off.

A comment or error report: its category and wording and, if you choose to give them, your role, course level, a source link, and an email address for a reply when you tick the box asking us to write back. We use that address for the reply and for nothing else. A tap on 'This helped' or 'This did not help' sends that vote and the item it concerns. Please leave personal details out of anything you write.

What we do not ask your device for

Names, contact lists, precise location, photos, files, the camera or the microphone. The app does not follow you into other companies' apps or websites.

Why we want it

To keep the app working and safe, to find and fix faults, to fit the learning path to you, to learn whether lessons and practice do their job, to research, analyse and improve our apps and teaching material, to read and answer comments, and to do what the law requires. If our business or this app were financed, merged or transferred, the information could pass with it, still bound by this page.

Your say

The sharing switch is on the first-run page and under More, then Privacy and data. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, and wherever the region cannot be told, it begins in the off position; elsewhere it begins on. Move it whenever you like. 'Delete my usage data' turns sharing off and asks us to erase what is tied to your installation; you can also email diagnostics@equalinformation.com. Depending on where you live, you may also have legal rights to see, correct, erase or object to the use of information about you.

Where it goes and how long it stays

It travels over encrypted connections to a web service we run and is held in our Google Workspace spreadsheets, files and mail, with providers handling it on our instructions; this may be in the United States. Store payments are handled by Apple or Google under their own policies, and card details do not reach us. We keep usage and learning records for as long as they serve the purposes above, and act on deletion requests when we receive and can match them. Where a legal ground is needed we rely on your consent or on our legitimate interest in running and improving the app. We guard the information with reasonable care, although no system is proof against everything.

Children, changes and contact

The app gathers no more than this page describes. If something has reached us that you want removed, write to us and a valid request will be honoured. When this page changes in a way that matters we update it here, and if the first-run page changes the app shows it again. Write to support@equalinformation.com, or to diagnostics@equalinformation.com about your data.
