IB English Lit Prep: Inkfathom

Privacy Policy. EqualInformation, LLC. Effective 26 November 2026.

Scope

This policy describes how EqualInformation, LLC handles information in connection with the IB English Lit Prep: Inkfathom app for phones and tablets. The app has no user account or sign-in.

What stays on your device

Your course progress, plan, pins, works list, typed answers, working notes, outlines, essay plans, rehearsal writing, source log and search words are kept in the app's storage on your device. Deleting the app removes them. We do not receive them through the app's automatic information.

The acceptance record

When you accept the first-run notice, the app sends a short record whatever the usage setting: a consent identifier, a random installation identifier, the time, the app, platform, version and build, the notice version and a fingerprint of its text, the action you took, the state of the usage setting, your country and region setting and your time zone.

Usage, learning and diagnostic information

While the optional usage setting is on, the app sends diagnostic and app-usage information that is permissible under applicable law, linked to a random installation identifier and not to your name: app version and build; device model, operating system, processor, memory, display size, language, region, time zone and accessibility and display settings; network type and battery level band; performance, crash and error information; screens, lessons, tools and commissions used and for how long; the identifiers of questions attempted, whether each answer was right, the number of the chosen option for single-choice questions, timings; progress estimates for course points; the app's recommendations and what followed them; view switches, plan and level settings, subscription screen interactions and whether Plus is active.

It does not include typed answers, notes, plans, essay writing, search words or anything else you type.

Information you choose to send

Diagnostic report: under More, Privacy and data, Send diagnostics shows a preview of device, app and recent technical event information. It is sent, with any note you add, only after you press Send, including when the usage setting is off.

Feedback and suggestions: sent only when you press Send. It contains the category and message you write and, if you choose to add them, your role, course context, a source link and a rating, together with the screen concerned. If you tick the contact box and give an email address, we receive it and use it only to reply about that feedback. Please keep personal information out of notes and messages.

What the app does not collect

The app does not ask for your name, contacts, precise location, photographs, files, microphone or camera, and it does not use an advertising identifier or track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Why we use information

To operate, secure, support and maintain the app; to diagnose problems; to personalise the learning path; to understand how learning features perform; for research and development, analytics and improvement of our apps and educational content; to read and respond to feedback; and to meet legal obligations. Information may be transferred as part of a financing, merger, acquisition or similar business transaction, subject to the commitments in this policy.

Your choices

The usage setting is shown on the first-run screen and under More, Privacy and data. It is off until you turn it on in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and where your region cannot be determined; elsewhere it starts on. You can change it at any time. Delete my usage data stops sharing and sends a deletion request. You may also write to diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

Storage, providers and retention

Information travels over encrypted (HTTPS) connections to a web application we operate, is stored in our Google Workspace services (Sheets, Drive and mail for notices, reports and requests), and is processed by service providers acting for us. Apple or Google processes subscription payments under their own terms; we do not receive your card details. Usage and learning information is kept while it is useful for the purposes above. Deletion requests are handled when received and verified.

Security and international processing

We take reasonable measures to protect information, though no method is completely secure. Information may be processed in the United States. Where a legal basis is required we rely on your consent or on our legitimate interests in running and improving the app, as applicable, and you may have rights of access, correction, deletion or objection under your local law.

Children

We design our apps to collect only the information described in this policy. If you believe information has been provided to us that you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

Changes and contact

Material changes will be published here and, where the first-run notice changes, shown again in the app. EqualInformation, LLC: support@equalinformation.com. Data and deletion requests: diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
