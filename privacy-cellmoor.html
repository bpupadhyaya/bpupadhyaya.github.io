Biology AP: Cellmoor: Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Contact: support@equalinformation.com. Last updated 2026-11-27.

1. Introduction and scope

This Privacy Policy explains how EqualInformation, LLC ("we", "us") handles information in connection with Biology AP: Cellmoor (the "app"). It applies to the app only. The app has no account system and does not ask for your name or contact details.

Your study progress, answers, mastery estimates, streaks and settings are stored on your device. The sections below describe the limited information that leaves your device, and when.

2. Information we collect

A. Information you choose to provide. When you choose Send diagnostics, the app first shows you exactly what the report contains and sends it only after you tap Send; you may add a short note. When you send feedback, a suggestion or a problem report, we receive the category, your message (up to 2,000 characters, from which email addresses, telephone numbers, links and similar identifiers are removed automatically) and, if you provide them, your role, a course or level context, a source link and a rating from 1 to 5. We receive an email address only if you tick the option that allows us to reply to you.

B. Acceptance record. When you accept the first-run notice, a short record is sent whatever the usage setting: a consent identifier, the time, the app, platform, version and build, the notice version and a fingerprint of its text, the state of the usage setting, a randomly generated app identifier, and your country, locale and time zone. A notification email about that record is sent to EqualInformation.

C. Diagnostic and app-usage information. While the optional usage setting is on, or where otherwise permitted by applicable law, the app can send: app version and build; device model; operating system and version; processor type and cores; graphics processor name; memory and storage figures; screen and window size; language, region and time zone; text-size and display or accessibility settings; network type; a battery-level range; whether the free or the Plus entitlement is active; the random app identifier; session timing; the screens you open and how long you stay; topic reading activity such as scroll depth and time; practice, quiz, test and mock-exam attempts (the question, its topic, whether the answer was correct, the time taken, hints used and the option chosen); numeric answers you type into question fields and in-app search terms, shortened and cleaned of recognisable personal identifiers; topic-level progress estimates; the adaptive decisions the app makes and what followed them; use of labs and simulations (control names and value ranges); interactions with the subscription screen; performance indicators such as render times and dropped-frame ranges; and crash and error information.

D. Information we do not collect. We do not collect your name, contacts, precise location, photographs or files, an identifier shared with other apps or used to follow you across other companies' apps, or other text you enter in the app. The app has no account and does not read your contacts or location.

3. How we use information

We use information to operate, maintain, secure and support the app; to diagnose problems; to provide and personalise the learning experience; to understand how the app is used; for research and development; for analytics and improvement of our apps and educational content; to read and act on feedback; and to comply with legal obligations. Information may be transferred as part of a financing, merger, acquisition or similar business transaction, subject to the commitments in this policy.

4. Your choices

The first-run notice explains the optional usage setting. Under Settings, Privacy and data you can switch usage sharing on or off at any time. It is off by default in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and wherever the region cannot be determined, until you turn it on. Diagnostics and feedback are sent only when you tap Send. To request deletion of usage information associated with your app identifier, use Delete my usage data under Settings, Privacy and data, or write to diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

5. Storage and service providers

Information is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored in EqualInformation's Google Workspace services (Google Sheets and Drive) and in notification email. For installs that came from the App Store or Google Play, that email can hold your records themselves (acceptance, cell biology learning and usage batches, crash and diagnostic information, feedback, deletion requests). It reaches mailboxes we run and may be forwarded to another we run. Service providers that process information on our behalf do so under our instructions. Purchases are processed by Apple or Google; we do not receive your payment details. When you open a source or reference link, the page is loaded from its publisher, whose own practices apply.

6. Retention

Usage and learning information is kept for as long as it is useful for the purposes in section 3. Diagnostic reports and feedback are kept while needed to address them and to improve the app. Deletion requests follow section 4 and are completed by hand. No automatic erasure operates on our side, and emails containing records are retained unless deleted manually. Data stored on your device is removed when you delete the app or reset your progress in Settings.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. International users and legal bases

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we rely on your consent for optional usage information and on our legitimate interests in operating, securing and improving the app for the limited information described in section 2B and 2C that the law permits without consent. Information may be processed in the United States.

9. Children

We design our apps to collect only the information described in this policy. If you believe information has been provided to us that you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When a change is material, the notice version in the app changes and the first-run notice is shown again.

11. Contact

EqualInformation, LLC. General questions: support@equalinformation.com. Deletion and data requests: diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
