Neural Networks: Nodely - Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC.

Last updated: 2026-11-20.

Neural Networks: Nodely keeps your progress and settings only on your device. There is no account and no server operated by us; we do not collect names, emails or birth dates. The app asks only whether you are 18 or older, to configure ads.

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob. If you are 18 or older you may be asked for consent (and, on iOS, for tracking permission) before personalised ads. If you say you are under 18, ads are non-personalised, no tracking request is shown, and ads are limited to teen-suitable content. Google may process advertising identifiers as described in Google's policies.

An optional monthly subscription, billed and managed by Apple or Google, removes ads; we never see payment details. When you tap a citation, the cited public website opens in an in-app sheet; that site has its own privacy practices, and no cookies are kept after the sheet closes. You can delete all local data by uninstalling the app.
