Advanced RL: Policyridge - Privacy Policy

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC.

Updated 2026-11-17.

The app has no account and no server of ours. Your progress, replay queue, settings and credits are stored only on your device.

Ads and consent

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. Where required, Google's consent form asks for your choice; on iOS the system asks whether apps may track you. Ads and the advertising identifier are handled by Google under its own policy. The course works the same whichever choice you make.

Purchases

The optional monthly ad-free plan is processed by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.

Reading cited sources

Tapping a citation opens the cited public web page in an in-app viewer. That site may log your visit; the viewer keeps no cookies after you close it.

Children

The app is general education and does not knowingly collect personal information. On first launch the app asks whether you are 18 or older. If you answer that you are under 18, ads are non-personalised, limited to teen-suitable content and tagged for the under-age-of-consent setting, and the iPhone tracking prompt is not shown.
