Enamelume support

Enamelume teaches preventive dentistry with 3D simulations: plaque disclosing and cleaning on a 3D mouth, a day of eating against enamel (plaque pH), sealants, gum health stages and a caries-risk balance. It works offline and is for education only.

Report an error

Found a wrong value, label or shape? Email support@equalinformation.com. Every claim in the app lists two named sources under More, Accuracy and sources.

Ad-free and restore

Open More, then Go ad-free. The subscription is $2.99 per month and renews until cancelled in your Apple account settings. Use Restore purchases on a new device.

Text size

Use A- and A+ at the top of every screen. Enamelume also follows your system text size.
