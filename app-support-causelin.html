French Language AP: Causelin · EqualInformation, LLC · 26 November 2026

Contact

Write to support@equalinformation.com. Please name your device, its system version and the screen where the problem happened. You can also use More, Privacy and data, Send diagnostics inside the app.

I cannot hear the listening scripts

The app uses the French voice installed on your device. Check the volume and silent switch, then add or download a French (France) voice in your device’s speech or accessibility settings. The transcript can be read in practice mode.

Recording does not work

Allow the microphone for the app in your device settings. Recordings stay on your device and are deleted when you leave the task.

Causelin Plus

Causelin Plus is a monthly subscription with no trial. Cancel at any time in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings; you keep it to the end of the paid period. On a new device, open the Plus screen and choose Restore purchases while signed in to the same store account.

Text is too small or too large

Use A− and A+ at the top of every screen to set any size from 50% to 300%.

Your data

See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. To delete usage data use Delete my usage data in the app or write to diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
