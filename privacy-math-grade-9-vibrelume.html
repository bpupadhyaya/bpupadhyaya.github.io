Math Grade 9: Vibrelume: Privacy Policy

EqualInformation, LLC. Effective 27 November 2026.

In short

The Math Grade 9: Vibrelume app does not send us information about you or your learner by itself. It has no account, no analytics and no crash-reporting code.

Stored on the device

Answers, progress, strength estimates, build settings, text size, the comfort choice in the parent area and your acceptance of the first-run notice are stored on the device only. "Erase all progress" in the grown-up area, or deleting the app, removes them. Your device backup (for example iCloud or another cloud backup) may include app data under your own backup settings.

Diagnostics you choose to send

The grown-up area can prepare a short technical report: app version, platform and system version, window size, text size and any error messages noted since the app was opened. It contains no answers, progress, name or device identifier. It leaves the device only if you send it yourself. If you email it to diagnostics@equalinformation.com we receive your email address and the report, use them only to look into the problem, and delete them when no longer needed or on request.

Purchases

The app store you bought from takes payment for the plans and applies its own privacy policy to it. Your card details never reach us. The app asks the store which plans are active.

On devices other than iPhone and iPad the app includes the app store’s billing library, which contains components that may send the store operator technical information about purchases, the app and the device so that billing works and can be maintained. The operator handles that under its privacy policy; we do not receive it.

Pages you open

Cited sources and these legal pages load from their own websites when a grown-up opens them. Those sites receive the usual connection information, such as an IP address, under their own policies.

Children

The app is made for learners of about 14 and for the adults who help them. It does not ask a learner for personal information. A parent or guardian can write to us about privacy at any time.

Contact and changes

privacy questions: support@equalinformation.com. If a later version handles data differently, this page and the store listing will be updated first.
