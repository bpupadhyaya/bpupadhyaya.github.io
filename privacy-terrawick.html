Privacy Policy: A Level Geography: Terrawick

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Contact: support@equalinformation.com. Last updated 2026-11-25.

1. Introduction and scope

This Privacy Policy explains how EqualInformation, LLC (we, us) handles information in connection with A Level Geography: Terrawick (the app), version 1.0.0 and later. It applies to the app only. The app has no account system.

2A. Information you choose to provide

Diagnostic reports: in Settings, Privacy and data, you may choose Send diagnostics. The app shows you the exact content first and sends it only after you tap Send. Feedback and suggestions: if you choose to send feedback, a suggestion or a problem report, we receive its category, your message (up to 2,000 characters, with email addresses, telephone numbers and links removed automatically) and, if you provide them, a role, context and a rating. We receive an email address only if you choose to let us contact you about your feedback.

2B. Diagnostic and app-usage information

While the optional usage setting is on, or where applicable law permits, the app can send: app version and build; device model; operating system and version; processor cores and architecture; graphics processor name; memory; screen size; language, region and time zone; text-size setting and a summary of accessibility and display settings; network type; a battery-level range; whether the free or Plus entitlement is active; a randomly generated app identifier (not an advertising identifier); session timing; the screens, labs and atlas entries you open and for how long; lesson activity such as scroll depth and time; practice-question and mock-paper attempts (the question, its topic, whether the answer was correct, the time taken and the option chosen); topic-level progress estimates; the study suggestions the app makes and what follows them; use of labs (control names and values); interactions with the subscription screen; and errors and crash information. The app does not collect your name, contacts, location, photographs, files or advertising identifiers, and notes or other text you type for yourself are not collected.

3. How we use information

To operate, maintain, secure and support the app; to diagnose problems; to provide and personalise the learning experience; to understand how the app is used; for research and development; for analytics and improvement of our apps and educational content; to read and act on feedback; and to comply with legal obligations.

4. Your choices

At first launch you see a notice and a separate usage switch. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and where the region is unknown the switch is off until you turn it on. You can change it at any time under Settings, Privacy and data. You can send diagnostics and feedback only when you choose. To request deletion of usage information linked to your app identifier use Delete my usage data in the app or email diagnostics@equalinformation.com.

5. Storage and service providers

Information is stored in our Google Workspace services (Google Sheets and Drive) and notification email, and processed by service providers acting on our behalf, and is transmitted over encrypted connections. Purchases are processed by Apple or Google; we do not receive your payment details.

6. Retention

Usage and learning information is kept while it is useful for the purposes above. Deletion requests are handled on request.

7. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. International users and legal bases

Where required we rely on your consent or on our legitimate interests in operating and improving the app. Information may be processed in the United States.

9. Children

We design our apps to collect only the information described in this policy. If you believe information has been provided to us that you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it on a valid request.

10. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes the notice shown at first launch changes version and is shown again.

11. Contact

EqualInformation, LLC, support@equalinformation.com. Deletion and data requests: diagnostics@equalinformation.com.
