Sumkite Privacy Policy

Quant Speed Maths: Sumkite. Effective 14 November 2026. Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC.

What the app stores

Your practice history, levels, review schedule, mistake notebook, streak days, credits and settings are saved only on your device. There is no account, no sign-up and no server of ours that receives this data. You can export your progress to a file you control, import it again, or reset it in Settings. Deleting the app deletes the data.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on some screens, an occasional full-screen ad after a finished session, and optional rewarded ads that earn a credit. AdMob may use your device advertising identifier, approximate location and device information to choose and measure ads. On iOS the app asks permission before tracking across other companies' apps and websites, and where the law requires it, shows a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform. You can refuse and keep using the app. The first practice session has no ads. See policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Purchases

The optional ad-free plan is an auto-renewing monthly subscription handled by Apple or Google. We receive no payment details. Manage or cancel it in your store account settings.

Practice content

All questions, explanations and shortcut cards are original and are stored inside the app. Sumkite is independent: it is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any exam body or recruiter. Names such as SSC, IBPS, RRB and CAT are used only to describe the kind of exams this practice suits. The practice readiness figure is a rough estimate from your own answers and not a prediction of any exam score.

Children

The app is an education tool for exam candidates and is not directed to children under 13.
