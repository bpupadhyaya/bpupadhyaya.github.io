Privacy Policy: A Level CS Prep: Bytewick

Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC. Contact: support@equalinformation.com. Last updated 2026-11-23.

What stays on your device

Your answers, mastery scores, review schedule, test history, coursework checklist, settings and unlock times are stored only on your device. There is no account, no sign-in and no analytics profile about you. We do not receive your study data.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: an occasional full-screen ad at natural breaks, a banner on list screens, and optional rewarded videos that unlock extra features for 24 hours. Ads never appear while you answer a question. Before any ad, the app asks for consent where the law requires it, and ads are non-personalised for people under 18. Google may process your device advertising identifier and ad interaction data under its own privacy policy.

Subscription and purchases

The optional monthly plan removes ads and unlocks everything. Purchases are processed by Apple or Google; we never see your payment details. You can cancel at any time in your store account settings. There is no free trial.

Links you open

Specification and source links open public web pages in your browser. Those sites apply their own privacy practices.

Children

The app is written for students of A Level age (16 and over) and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@equalinformation.com.
