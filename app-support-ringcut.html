Ringtone Maker: Snipwren: Support

Snipwren is a ringtone maker and audio cutter app for iPhone, iPad and Android. Snipwren cuts and trims audio, makes ringtones, notification and alarm tones, and converts audio, entirely on your phone. No account is needed.

Common questions

  • Is my audio uploaded anywhere? No. Everything is done on your phone and works offline.
  • Which formats can I export? M4A (AAC) and WAV on both platforms, and M4R ringtones (up to 40 seconds) on iPhone and iPad. Many other audio formats can be opened. MP3 export is not offered.
  • How do I set a ringtone? On Android, export and choose Ringtone, Notification or Alarm; allow the system page the first time. On iPhone and iPad, iOS does not allow apps to set ringtones: save the M4R file and follow the on-screen steps with GarageBand or a computer.
  • Can I take the sound from a video? Yes. Pick a video and Snipwren extracts the audio so you can trim it.
  • What are credits? You start with 2 credits. Each finished export uses 1 credit (setting a tone from it is free). Watch a short ad to earn another, or go ad-free.
  • How do I remove ads? Open the purchase screen in Snipwren (Settings or More, or the ad-free prompt) and choose the ad-free monthly subscription or the one-time Unlock. Both remove ads and credit limits.
  • How do I restore a purchase? Open Settings (or the purchase screen) and tap Restore purchases, using the same Apple Account or Google account you bought with. This is needed after reinstalling or changing device.
  • How do I cancel the subscription? iPhone and iPad: Settings, your name, Subscriptions. Android: Google Play, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel it.
  • Text too small? Use A- and A+ on the screens, or change the system text size.
  • Can I change my ad choices? Yes. Use the privacy choices for ads in the app where shown, or your device's privacy settings.
