Zymewell Support

Zymewell: Enzyme Biochemistry. Fifteen clinically important enzymes in real 3D, a Michaelis-Menten and Lineweaver-Burk kinetics lab, and daily questions. Works offline.

Common questions

How do I turn the molecule? Drag with one finger to rotate, pinch to zoom, drag with two fingers to move, double-tap to reset. The Pocket button flies to the drug-binding site.

Can I make the text bigger? Yes. Use the A- and A+ buttons at the top of every screen. Zymewell also follows your device text size.

How do I remove ads? More, then Remove ads. Subscribe monthly, or watch 100 short videos in a month to switch ads off for the rest of that month. Use Restore purchases on a new device.

Is it medical advice? No. Zymewell is for education only.
