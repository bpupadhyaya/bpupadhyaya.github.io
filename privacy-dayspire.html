Dayspire privacy policy

Applies to the Dayspire app for iPhone and iPad by Equal Information LLC. Updated 7 November 2026.

The short version

The countdowns you create stay on your device. We run no accounts and no servers for the app, so we never receive your events, notes or calendar. The only data that leaves the device is what the Google AdMob advertising software collects when ads are shown, and only as described below.

What Dayspire keeps on your device

  • Your countdowns (title, date, scene type, note, repeat setting) are saved in the app's private storage.
  • A trimmed copy (titles and dates, without notes) is placed in a keychain item that only Dayspire and its own widget extension can read, so the Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets can show your events. It is not shared with any other app.
  • Reminder settings, ad-free time you earned and similar preferences are stored locally.

Calendar access

If you tap "Import from Calendar", iOS asks for permission. Dayspire reads upcoming event titles and dates on the device so you can choose which ones to copy into countdowns. Nothing from your calendar is uploaded. You can revoke access at any time in iOS Settings.

Notifications and Live Activities

Reminders and the day-of Lock Screen tracker are created on the device. They do not use any push server.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. At first launch iOS asks whether Dayspire may track you across other companies' apps, and, where the law requires, a Google consent form follows. If you decline, only non-personalised ads are shown. AdMob may collect device identifiers, advertising data, coarse location and how you interact with ads, to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. This is governed by Google's privacy policy. You can remove all ads with the monthly ad-free subscription, or earn ad-free time by watching a rewarded video.

Purchases

The subscription is processed by Apple. Dayspire only learns whether the subscription is active; it never sees your payment details.

Sharing as a picture

When you share a countdown as an image, it is generated on the device and handed to the share sheet you choose.

Children

Dayspire is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Deleting your data

Use "Delete all my countdowns" in the app's settings, or delete the app. Both remove everything Dayspire stored, including the widget copy. Advertising identifiers can be reset in iOS Settings under Privacy and Security, Tracking.
