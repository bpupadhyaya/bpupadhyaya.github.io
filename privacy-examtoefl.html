TOEFL iBT Prep 2026: Larkmere privacy policy

TOEFL iBT practice test prep app. Updated 15 November 2026. Publisher: EqualInformation, LLC.

What stays on your phone

Your progress, answers, notes, writing drafts, credits and settings are stored only on your device. There is no account and no server that receives your practice data. You can export or delete your progress in Settings.

Microphone and speech

Speaking practice uses the microphone only while you record an answer. Recognition runs on your device whenever an offline voice pack is available. Recordings are not saved and are not uploaded. Only if you explicitly choose "use online recognition once" for a single attempt, that recording is sent to the phone maker's speech service (Apple on iPhone and iPad, Google on Android) to be transcribed; the app then returns to offline-only.

Ads and purchases

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob. Where required, you are asked for consent first (Google User Messaging Platform and, on iPhone, App Tracking Transparency). Ad providers may collect device identifiers and advertising data under their own policies. Your practice answers, writing and voice are never used for ads. The optional monthly ad-free plan is processed by Apple or Google; we do not receive your payment details.

Children

This app is for learners aged 13 and over.
