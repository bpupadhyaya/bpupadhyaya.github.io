Tabwick: Split Bills and Tips

Privacy Policy. Last updated 9 November 2026.

The short version

Tabwick keeps the bills, groups, names and amounts you enter on your own device. We do not run an account system and we cannot see your data.

What stays on your device

People's names, items, amounts, groups and history are stored in the app's private storage on your iPhone or iPad. They are not uploaded. Deleting the app deletes them. If you use the Share button, the text you choose to share goes only where you send it.

Ads

Tabwick is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier (if you allow tracking when asked), your approximate location derived from your IP address, and information about how you interact with ads, to choose and measure ads. In regions where the law requires it, a Google consent form asks for your choice first, and you can change it later in More, Ad privacy choices. You can refuse tracking and still use every feature. See Google's description at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Subscription

The optional ad-free subscription is handled by Apple. We receive no payment details. We only learn, through StoreKit on your device, whether the subscription is active.

Crash and performance data

Apple and Google SDKs may collect anonymous crash and performance diagnostics. They are not linked to your identity.

Children

Tabwick is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Your choices

Decline tracking in the prompt or in iOS Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking. Delete the app to remove all stored data.
