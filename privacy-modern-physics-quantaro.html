Modern Physics: Quantaro Privacy Policy

Modern Physics: Quantaro is published by EqualInformation, LLC. This policy was last updated on 2026-11-16.

What this policy covers

This policy describes how Quantaro, a modern physics course (relativity, quantum ideas, atoms, nuclei and particles), handles information. In brief: there is nothing to sign up for, and we operate no account system or database about you.

Your progress stays with you

Answers, mastery levels, spaced-review dates, chosen study path and preferences are saved only on your phone or tablet, so Quantaro works without a connection. They are never uploaded to us. Removing the app removes them.

What we never ask for

Location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, health data, or any identifying details. Personal data is not sold.

Ads and Google

To stay free, the app displays Google AdMob advertising at natural breaks, never in the middle of a timed test. Before ads are requested on iOS, you are asked through the App Tracking Transparency prompt, and in regions that require it Google's consent message lets you accept or limit personalisation. With your permission, Google receives an advertising identifier and ad-interaction details to show and measure ads; without it, ads are generic. Details: Google's advertising data page.

Age

This app is intended for students and adult learners, not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

Optional subscription

An optional auto-renewing monthly subscription removes ads. The price is shown by the App Store or Google Play before you buy, a free trial is included where offered, and the subscription renews until you cancel it. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google; we never see your card details and the app only reads whether your subscription is active. Manage or cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account settings. See the terms of use.

Your choices and data requests

Change tracking permission any time in iOS Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking, and reopen the ad consent message from the app's About screen where available. To ask a privacy question or to request access to, or deletion of, any data we might hold about you, write to bpupadhyaya@equalinformation.com; the support page has more help.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it.
