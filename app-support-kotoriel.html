Kotoriel Support

Japanese Language AP: Kotoriel · EqualInformation, LLC · Effective 26 November 2026

Contact us

Write to support@equalinformation.com and say which device and app version you use. Inside the app, More, Privacy and data offers Send diagnostics (you see exactly what will be sent first) and Feedback and suggestions. Corrections to the Japanese are especially welcome.

No sound in listening exercises

Kotoriel uses the Japanese voice on your device. Check the volume, then add a Japanese voice if needed. iPhone or iPad: Settings, Accessibility, Spoken Content (or Read and Speak), Voices, Japanese. Android: Settings, search for Text-to-speech, open the speech engine settings and install Japanese voice data. Then use More, Test the Japanese voice.

Typing in Japanese

Writing tasks use your system keyboard. Add a Japanese keyboard (kana or romaji input) in your device's keyboard settings.

Recording does not start

Allow microphone access for Kotoriel in your device settings. Recordings stay on your device; delete them under More, My recordings.

Text size and furigana

Use A- and A+ at the top of the screen (50% to 300%). Furigana can be switched on or off on passages and under More.

Subscription

To restore Kotoriel Plus, open More and tap Restore purchases while signed in to the store account that bought it. To cancel, tap Manage subscription or open your store account's subscriptions. You keep Plus until the end of the period already paid for. Billing questions and refunds are handled by Apple or Google.
