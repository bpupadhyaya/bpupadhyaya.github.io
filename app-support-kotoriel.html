Answers to common questions

Getting in touch

Email support@equalinformation.com with your device type and the app version shown at the foot of More. In the app, under More, Privacy and data, you can send a diagnostic report (you read it before it goes) or a feedback message. We are especially glad to hear of mistakes in the Japanese.

Listening is silent

The app speaks with your device's Japanese voice. Turn the volume up first. If no Japanese voice is installed, add one: on iPhone and iPad, in Settings under Accessibility, in the spoken content or read-and-speak voices; on Android, in the text-to-speech settings of your speech engine. Then try Test the Japanese voice under More.

Writing in Japanese

The writing tasks accept your device's own keyboard. Switch on a Japanese keyboard, kana or romaji, in the keyboard settings.

The recorder will not start

Give Kotoriel permission to use the microphone in your device settings. What you record stays on the device and can be deleted under My recordings in More.

Bigger text and readings

The A- and A+ buttons at the top change the text from half size to three times size. Readings above kanji can be shown or hidden on a passage or under More.

Managing Plus

To bring Plus back, open More and choose Restore purchases while signed in to the store account that bought it. To stop it, choose Manage subscription, or open the subscriptions page of your store account; it stays open until the paid month is over. Apple or Google handle billing and refund questions.
