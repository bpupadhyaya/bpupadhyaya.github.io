Helixwick Privacy Policy

DNA and molecular biology study app by Equal Information LLC. Effective 8 November 2026.

What stays on your device

Your quiz answers, streaks, text-size choice and ad-free time are stored only on your device. Helixwick has no accounts and we do not receive your study data. The app works fully offline.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier and approximate location, and information about how you interact with ads, to select and measure ads. On iOS we ask for your permission to track across apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency) and, where required, show a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) for the EEA, UK and some US states. You can decline and still use every feature. You can change your choice any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. See Google's advertising policy.

Subscription

The optional ad-free monthly subscription is handled by Apple. We never see your payment details. Manage or cancel in Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions.

Children

Helixwick is for students and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, health data, or precise location, and we do not use any third-party analytics or AI services in the app.

Contact

Questions or requests: bpupadhyaya@equalinformation.com.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and its date.
