Help for Math Grade 4: Corvadle. If your question is not here, write to bpupadhyaya@equalinformation.com and say which device and which version of the app you use.

Restoring a plan

Open any locked section, pass the grown-up check, and tap “Restore purchases”. You must be signed in to the same app store account that bought the plan.

Cancelling a plan

On iPhone and iPad: Settings, your name, Subscriptions. On other phones and tablets: open your app store, then the subscriptions area of your account. The plan screen’s “Manage or cancel” button opens the right place. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current month ends, or the next month is charged.

A purchase did not unlock anything

Wait a minute and reopen the plan screen. If the store says the purchase is waiting for approval, it opens once a family organiser approves it. Otherwise use Restore purchases, then write to us.

Sending feedback or a diagnostic report

Open the grown-ups area, pass the check and choose “Feedback and reports”. The app prepares a draft in your own mail app; you press Send. Without a mail app on the device, write to diagnostics@equalinformation.com from anywhere.

Erasing progress

Grown-ups area, then Settings, then “Erase all progress”. This cannot be undone.

Text size

The A− and A+ buttons at the top change all text from half size to three times size.

Working without internet

Learning works offline. Buying, restoring and managing plans and opening a cited source page need the internet.

Phones and tablets

The app works on phones, tablets and foldables, in portrait and landscape.
