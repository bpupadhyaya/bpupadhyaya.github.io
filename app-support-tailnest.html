Support

Tailnest keeps shots, medicine, weight, meals, vet visits and photos for every pet in your home.

Typing an entry

Tap the bar at the bottom and write it the way you would say it, for example "Biscuit rabies shot today" or "Luna weighed 9 lb yesterday". Check the preview, then Save.

Why is there no booster reminder?

Tailnest only schedules a repeat when you confirm an interval. It shows a general published interval with its source as a starting point, but your vet sets the real schedule.

Moving to a new phone or sharing with family

In Settings, make a household file and send it with the share sheet. Import it on the other device and the two merge. Photos are not included.

Ad-free

Settings has the monthly ad-free subscription, Restore purchases, and a button to earn ad-free time by watching a video. To cancel, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions.

Not veterinary advice

Tailnest is not veterinary advice. For anything about your pet's health, contact your vet.
