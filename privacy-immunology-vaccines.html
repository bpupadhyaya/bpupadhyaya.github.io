Epitopewell Privacy Policy

Effective 8 November 2026. Publisher: Equal Information LLC.

What the app stores

Epitopewell: Immunology 3D is an offline study app about antibodies, receptors and vaccines. Your quiz progress, streak and text-size choice are saved only on your device. We run no accounts and cannot see this data.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device advertising identifier and approximate location to choose and measure ads. On iOS the app first asks permission before tracking across other companies' apps and websites, and where the law requires it, shows a consent form from the Google User Messaging Platform. You can refuse and keep using the app with non-personalised ads, and you can reopen your choices from the About screen. See policies.google.com/technologies/ads. Ads can be removed with the optional monthly Ad-free subscription or by watching a video to earn ad-free time.

Subscription

The optional Ad-free subscription is billed by Apple. We receive no payment details. Manage or cancel it in your Apple ID subscription settings.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal data, collect names, emails or contacts, use accounts, or request camera, microphone, photo, health or precise-location permissions. The app contains no artificial-intelligence features.

Children

The app is a general education app and is not directed to children under 13.

Education only

Epitopewell explains how antibodies and vaccines work for study. It is not medical advice and gives no vaccination recommendations. Molecular structures come from the RCSB Protein Data Bank (open CC0 data); simulations are illustrative teaching models.
