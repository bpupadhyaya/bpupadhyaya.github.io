Voice to Text: Speakwick: Support

Speakwick is a voice to text transcriber app for iPhone, iPad and Android. Speakwick turns recordings and audio or video files into text, using a speech model that runs entirely on your phone. No account is needed.

Common questions

  • Is my audio sent anywhere? No. Transcription runs on your phone and the app works offline.
  • Which languages work? English, Spanish, Portuguese, French and German, with auto-detect. Hindi is a beta and is less accurate.
  • Which files can I export? TXT, SRT subtitles, Word (DOCX) and PDF, and you can copy or share the text.
  • How long can a recording be? Long recordings are processed in chunks. One credit covers up to 30 minutes of audio, and the cost is shown before you start. On iPhone and iPad, iOS pauses work shortly after you leave the app and the job continues when you reopen it.
  • What are credits? You start with 2 credits. A transcription uses 1 credit per started 30 minutes of audio, only when it finishes (failed or cancelled jobs are free, and exports are free). Watch a short ad to earn another, or go ad-free.
  • How do I remove ads? Open the purchase screen in Speakwick (Settings or More, or the ad-free prompt) and choose the ad-free monthly subscription or the one-time Unlock. Both remove ads and credit limits.
  • How do I restore a purchase? Open Settings (or the purchase screen) and tap Restore purchases, using the same Apple Account or Google account you bought with. This is needed after reinstalling or changing device.
  • How do I cancel the subscription? iPhone and iPad: Settings, your name, Subscriptions. Android: Google Play, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel it.
  • Text too small? Use A- and A+ on the screens, or change the system text size.
  • Can I change my ad choices? Yes. Use the privacy choices for ads in the app where shown, or your device's privacy settings.
