Photo Sign Resizer: Sizewick: Support

Sizewick is a photo and signature resizer for exam forms app for iPhone, iPad and Android. Sizewick fits your photo, signature, thumb impression or declaration to the file size, pixels and format an exam form asks for, entirely on your phone. No account is needed.

Common questions

  • Are my photos and signature uploaded anywhere? No. All processing is on your phone and the app works offline.
  • Will my file be accepted? Rules differ by exam and change over time. Sizewick shows the rule, its source and the date it was checked, but we cannot guarantee acceptance. Check your own notice. If your form is not listed, use Custom size and enter its limits.
  • How does the file-size fit work? Sizewick keeps exact pixels when the form states them, otherwise it searches sizes and JPEG quality to land inside your KB band, and tells you plainly if the limit cannot be reached.
  • Can I use a signature written on paper? Yes. Photograph it on white paper and use the ink cleaner to turn the page into dark ink on white, then trim and fit it.
  • What are credits? You start with 2 credits. Saving or sharing a finished picture for the first time uses 1 credit (saving it again is free). Watch a short ad to earn another, or go ad-free.
  • How do I remove ads? Open the purchase screen in Sizewick (Settings or More, or the ad-free prompt) and choose the ad-free monthly subscription or the one-time Unlock. Both remove ads and credit limits.
  • How do I restore a purchase? Open Settings (or the purchase screen) and tap Restore purchases, using the same Apple Account or Google account you bought with. This is needed after reinstalling or changing device.
  • How do I cancel the subscription? iPhone and iPad: Settings, your name, Subscriptions. Android: Google Play, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel it.
  • Text too small? Use A- and A+ on the screens, or change the system text size.
  • Can I change my ad choices? Yes. Use the privacy choices for ads in the app where shown, or your device's privacy settings.
